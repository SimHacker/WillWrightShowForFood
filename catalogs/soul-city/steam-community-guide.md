# The Steam Community Guide strategy

*Soul City's front door on Steam — a player-authored guide on the
Legacy Collection hub, not a separate store app. Machine-readable spec:
[steam-community-guide.yml](steam-community-guide.yml).*

**In one line:** Steam Community Guides on
[The Sims Legacy Collection hub](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3314070/The_Sims/) —
an informational playbook that channels players to Soul City (web) and
Repo Shows. Not a game, not EA-endorsed, no install required.

The Valve feature that makes it work: Community Hub → Guides → Create a
Guide. You must own the game on Steam; the guide is free, sectioned,
embeds images and YouTube, and links out to the web
([getting The Sims on Steam](../get-the-sims-on-steam.md)).

## Four jobs

1. **Catalogs and tools** — federated pointers to SimFreaks, SimSlice,
   SimProv, Zombie Sims, ModTheSims, TSR, and Maxis metadata; links to
   web tools (SimShow, Transmogrifier, RugOMatic, install helpers);
   Exchange history and Internet Archive indexes. The primary CTA is
   the browser, not a downloaded app.
2. **Repo Shows channel** — guide sections where Legacy Collection
   buyers find the living conversation: the
   [Will Wright premiere hub](../../repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md),
   live and VOD links, the git entry point, and harvest artifacts from
   aired episodes.
3. **Sims and bridges** — shows and playable demos about the Sims
   lineage and Soul City spoke games: Sims 1 UCC, SimProv, procedural
   rhetoric, Exchange revival, and data portability across Minecraft,
   Stardew, Proxi, Spore, and Mind Mirror.
4. **EA alignment** — the guide requires an owned game, drives Steam
   sales, and documents fan culture respectfully. Fan-guide disclaimer
   required; no bundled EA assets, no Plumbob branding, no implied
   endorsement, no vacuuming creator subscriptions.

## Where it sits in the rollout

Web Soul City and the public repo first; the Community Guide plus
ModTheSims cross-links and YouTube second;
[SoulAngel](soul-angel.md) on Steam Software third, once
web federation and catalog sync are live; EA and platform conversations
after traction. A rejected alternative, for the record: wrapping Soul
City in an Electron or Steam app shell (June 2026).
