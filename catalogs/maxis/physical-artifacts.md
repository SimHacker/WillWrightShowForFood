# Maxis physical artifacts — boxes, merch, tour tees

Companion to [`CATALOG.yml`](CATALOG.yml), which indexes *bytes*. This page indexes **things**:
retail boxes, employee merch, and objects that survive in collectors' and alumni closets.

The rule is the same as the rest of the catalog — **metadata and one article per interesting
backstory**, no binaries. Here the "backstory" is usually a caption someone wrote on Facebook
sixteen years ago.

## SIM ROCK 95 — Maxis World Tour tee

![A navy blue t-shirt printed like a rock tour shirt. Across the top, SIM ROCK in red block letters with a yellow 95, and MAXIS WORLD TOUR arcing below in yellow. Beneath the type, four people are photographed in the pose of a band lineup — long-haired, one woman among them, one man in glasses with long hair and a mustache — with a small round Maxis logo overprinted. The lower half lists tour cities in two columns](media/sim-rock-95-maxis-world-tour-shirt.jpg)

A **1995 Maxis staff tour shirt**, styled after a heavy-metal world tour tee: four employees shot in a
band lineup, Maxis logo where the tattoo would be, and a full tour-date city list on the front.

**The city list, verbatim:**

> London · Glasgow · Edinburgh · San Francisco · San Diego · La Jolla, CA · Los Angeles · Pasadena ·
> Santa Monica · Denver · Minneapolis · Madison, WI · Milwaukee · Menomenee Falls · Boston · New York ·
> Philadelphia · Baltimore · Berwyn, PA · Wayne, PA · West Conshohocken, PA · Kansas City · Newark ·
> Shawnee Mission, MO · *Chicago*

**This list is the actual document.** Three UK cities and a run of major metros, and then — Berwyn,
Wayne, and West Conshohocken, Pennsylvania; Menomenee Falls, Wisconsin; Shawnee Mission, Missouri.
Nobody's *world tour* plays three Philadelphia suburbs unless the tour is going somewhere specific in
each one.

**Hypothesis, not established:** this is the itinerary of a real 1995 business road show — most
plausibly the education/institutional sales circuit, since those suburbs are the kind of place school
districts, educational publishers and distributors keep their offices. If that's right, the shirt is
a **sales-team trophy**, and the four "band members" are the people who flew it. Ask before asserting.

- [ ] **Identify the four faces** (the woman second from left is the most identifiable).
- [ ] What was the tour? Education sales? A user-group circuit? A press tour?
- [ ] Who designed and printed it? Maxis merch usually came out of the art department.
- [ ] Is "Menomenee" misspelled on the shirt (the town is **Menomonee** Falls)? If so, keep the typo.
- [ ] Why is Chicago set in italics and last?

Related in-repo: [Charles London](../../characters/charles-london/README.md) ·
[Eric Hedman](../../characters/eric-hedman/README.md) ·
[the 1997 Maxis Employee Resume shirt](../../characters/mike-perry/maxis-employee-resume-shirt.md) —
two years later, and much darker.

## SimCity Graphics Set 2: Future Cities

![A boxed software product photographed on a wooden table. The box is teal with SimCity Graphics in blue block type and a round Alternate Architecture Set 2 badge. A yellow panel reads Graphic Set 2, FUTURE CITIES, Future U.S.A., Future Europe, Moon Colony. Below, cover art shows two hands gripping a fleshy pink handheld console whose screen displays a SimCity skyline, with the SimCity logo above it; embossed stone tiles depicting a castle, a dome, a temple, a pagoda, a windmill and other buildings float up the left and top edges. A white panel at bottom left reads REQUIRES SIMCITY, IBM PC XT AT PS2 and 100 percent compatibles, supports VGA EGA CGA Tandy and Hercules graphics, contains five and a quarter inch disks. The Maxis logo sits at bottom right](media/simcity-graphics-set-2-future-cities-box.png)

**The second SimCity terrain/architecture pack** — Future U.S.A., Future Europe, and **Moon Colony** —
sold as an add-on requiring the base game. 5¼" disks, CGA through VGA, Tandy and Hercules.

Two things worth noting for the archive:

**It's an expansion pack in 1990, for SimCity.** Graphics Sets 1 and 2 are the ancestor of every
Maxis add-on that follows, including [Livin' Large](../../characters/will-wright/sources/2010-03-11-livin-large-team-photo/README.md).
Same commercial move: same simulation, new skins, sold separately.

**Moon Colony.** Maxis was shipping a lunar tile set for SimCity in 1990 and pitching
[LunaSim / Tycho Rising](../../characters/mike-perry/lunasim-tycho-rising.md) — a full lunar colony
sim — six years later. The moon was a recurring Maxis idea long before it was a product proposal.

The cover art is also doing something strange and worth a second look: **the "console" is drawn as
flesh** — pink, soft, gripped in two hands, with the city inside it. Whoever painted that understood
what the game was before the marketing did.

- [ ] Confirm the release year and the Graphics Set 1 contents.
- [ ] Who painted the cover? (Same illustrator as the original SimCity box?)
- [ ] Are the embossed tiles on the box edge from Set 1, Set 2, or invented for the packaging?

## Provenance: Judson Hudson's collection

![A Facebook post to the Maxis Alumni group by Judson Hudson, dated March 23, 2010, thanking former and current Maxis employees, describing himself as an avid fan who started with SimCity Classic as a child and now collects Maxis products, and offering to show the group his collection. A follow-up comment thanks Jenny Martin, the group's creator, for answering his questions about a Maxis pin badge found on eBay and for a fact about the godzilla monster on the original SimCity box](media/2010-03-23-judson-hudson-collector-post.png)

*Source:* **Judson Hudson** to Maxis Alumni, **23 March 2010**. 4 comments.

> "I just want to give all former/current Maxis employees a huge round of applause and a big thank you
> for all that you have given us. Whether you've worked on the epic SimCity series, the various other
> Sim titles, The Sims series and even Spore, you're (in my opinion) **the greatest video game company
> ever to grace the industry.**
>
> I'm an avid fan and **started off with SimCity Classic when I was just a little kid.** Now grown,
> I've accumulated a great deal of your products and continue to collect them. So thanks for creating
> some of the best games on Earth. When I get a moment or two (and if you're interested), **I'd love to
> show you my current collection** 🙂"

And the follow-up, which is where the leads are:

> "While I'm at it, I should also thank the creator of this group — **Jenny Martin** for being so kind
> in answering a stranger's question. She has been a huge help in seeking an answer about **a Maxis pin
> badge I found off of Ebay** and the history behind it, and even gave me **an interesting fact about
> the godzilla monster on the original SimCity box** (which i won a copy off of Ebay earlier today).
> So thank you Jenny!"

**Judson Hudson is not an alumnus — he's a collector who walked into the alumni group and was let
in.** That matters for this archive in two ways. First, the material flows *both* directions: the
company's own history is partly held by fans who bought it back off eBay. Second, **Jenny Martin
answered.** She created the Maxis Alumni group, and her habit of answering strangers is why several
artifacts in this repo exist at all — including her
[Commodore 64 SimCity floppy](../../characters/will-wright/sources/2017-04-28-simcity-c64-floppy/).

### Two open leads from four sentences

- [ ] **The Maxis pin badge.** Which badge, and what history did Jenny give him? Employee pin, trade
      show pin, or sales award? Get a photo.
- [ ] **The Godzilla monster on the original SimCity box.** Jenny told him "an interesting fact" and
      the fact is not in the post. **The monster is the thing to chase** — SimCity's disaster monster
      and its box-art appearance sit right on the Godzilla trademark line, and someone at Maxis or
      Broderbund made a decision about that. Ask Jenny.
- [ ] **Judson's collection.** He offered to show it in 2010. Still standing? Ask.
- [ ] **Rooms needed:** Jenny Martin (creator of Maxis Alumni; C64 SimCity disk; "do you have the
      original demo?"), Judson Hudson (collector).
