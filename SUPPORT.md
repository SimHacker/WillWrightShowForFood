# Supporting Don Hopkins

Everything in this repository is free to use and will stay free: the
[Machine Room](https://hyperties.org/machine/), where a PDP-7, an Apple ][ and a PDP-10 run in
the browser; the restored PIXIE and SYMELEC from 1969; the Repo Shows; the characters and their
sources; the code. Museums, schools and anyone else use it without asking and without paying.

Making it is not free. Most of the code is written with AI models, and the tokens are paid for
out of my own pocket, at thousands of dollars a month
([what the PIXIE restoration cost](packages/cabinet/PIXIE-RESTORATION.md#what-it-cost)). Each
machine, each restored program, each show is weeks of that. If what you find here is worth
something to you, help pay for the next one.

— [Don Hopkins](characters/don-hopkins/README.md)

## How

- **[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/DonHopkins).** Monthly support, with tiers for
  institutions too: a museum, a university, a company or a lab.
- **Hire me.** I am available for work: user interfaces, emulators, games, simulations, tools
  for language models. See [`hire-me.md`](characters/don-hopkins/hire-me.md).
- **A version for your exhibit.** A customized Machine Room for a museum can be arranged. That
  is support, not a condition: the free version stays free. See
  [the letter to museums](packages/cabinet/FOR-MUSEUMS.md).
- **Write to me** at don@donhopkins.com, or [open an issue](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/issues).

GitHub Sponsors is not switched on yet.

## What patrons get

Nothing is ever taken away from the public to make something for patrons. Patrons get things
first and get a say in what comes next; everything reaches everyone on a fixed schedule whether
or not anybody pays. The details are in [PATRONS.md](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/designs/PATRONS.md).

- **Early:** clips, posts and datasets two weeks before everyone, app builds and tours a month.
- **Chat:** the patrons' channel, where the work is talked over while it is still soft.
- **A vote** on a few real decisions: which Repo Show is made next, which talk gets transcribed
  and indexed next, which module is built next when two are equally ready.
- **A room of your own** in the repository, written by you and credited to you, if you want to
  join in rather than watch.

## Other ways to help

- **Use it and say so.** Tell me what works, what breaks, and what you would like to see run.
- **Record a real machine.** Photographs, sound and video of a PDP-7 or its parts: what is
  wanted is in [the letter to museums](packages/cabinet/FOR-MUSEUMS.md).
- **Run the programs on real hardware.** If you have a PDP-7 or an Apple ][, the Machine Room's
  programs can go to tape or disk, and the real machine is the emulator's last test.
- **Pull requests** are welcome, on anything.
