# Agreement: Will Wright

**Status:** drafted, awaiting Will's written reply.
**Scope:** his talks, slides, transcripts, writing, likeness, and portrayal in this repository.
**Does not cover:** the GDC and Long Now *recordings* of those talks, which those organizations
own. See [`../RIGHTS.md`](../RIGHTS.md), and the separate asks in this directory.

He is enthusiastic and cooperative, which is exactly why this gets written down rather than
left as a good feeling. The point of a grant is not distrust. It is that in ten years the
question will be asked by someone who was not in the room, and possibly of someone who cannot
be asked.

## What we want, in plain terms

1. Publish his talks, slides, transcripts and writing in this repository, and quote from them.
2. Point at the official videos with timestamped links, and thread them into playlists.
3. Make short clips and remixes for the personal channel, where those are ours to make or
   permitted by whoever owns the recording.
4. Portray him as a character under [`../schemas/portrayal-standards.md`](../schemas/portrayal-standards.md):
   about him, never as him, no fabricated quotes, and his room is his to correct or delete.

## What he keeps

Everything. The grant is non-exclusive, he retains full ownership and every other right, he
can license the same material to anyone else on any terms, he can review anything before it
goes out, and he can withdraw any item or all of it by saying so once. No lawyers, no notice
period, no argument.
