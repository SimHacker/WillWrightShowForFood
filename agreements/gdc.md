# Ask: GDC / Informa — clip permission

**Status:** draft, not sent.
**What we need:** permission to publish short excerpts from GDC talk recordings with
commentary, on a personal YouTube channel, linking back to the originals.
**What we do not need permission for:** linking, timestamped links, and embedding the official
player. Those are free and are the default. See [`../RIGHTS.md`](../RIGHTS.md).

The rights position: GDC talk recordings are owned by the conference, not by the speaker. Will
Wright's own grant covers his words and slides but not the footage, so the footage needs its
own yes. Note that GDC posts a large amount of its own archive publicly on YouTube, which
means we can do a great deal by pointing alone -- ask for the clips, but do not block on them.

Who to write to: the GDC / GDC Vault content or press contact, and if there is a relationship
with the conference programming staff, use it. A named human beats a form.

```text
Subject: Permission request: short clips from Will Wright's GDC talks, with attribution

Hello,

I am building a free, non-commercial archive and companion guide to Will
Wright's talks and design thinking -- WillWrightShowForFood -- with Will's
own written permission for his material. Several of the essential talks are
GDC recordings, so the footage is yours rather than his, and I would rather
ask than assume.

What I would like permission for: short excerpts, typically under ninety
seconds, published with original commentary and analysis, credited on screen
to GDC and linking to your official upload of the full talk. Every clip
would name the talk, the year, and the venue, and point viewers to you for
the whole thing.

What I am doing regardless, which needs no permission: timestamped links
into your official YouTube uploads, embeds of your player, and curated
playlists that thread your videos in a viewing order with context. That
sends watch time to your channel, which is the outcome I want too -- the
clips exist to get people to the full talks.

If it helps: I am happy to accept conditions, such as a maximum clip length,
required on-screen credit wording, a link in every description, or excluding
specific talks. I am also happy to submit clips for review before posting.

If the answer is no, I will restrict myself to linking and quoting, with no
hard feelings and no re-uploads.

Some context on why this matters to me: I worked on SimCity and The Sims, and
much of what Will taught a generation of designers exists only as conference
video that is getting harder to find and impossible to search. The archive is
transcripts, indexes, and citations pointing at your recordings -- an
argument for their permanence, not a substitute for them.

Thank you for considering it,

Don Hopkins
[contact, and a link to the repo]
```
