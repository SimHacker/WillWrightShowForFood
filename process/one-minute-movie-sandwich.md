## The hook

The 2003 Stupid Fun Club One Minute Movies were :60 spots with the commercial break **built in** — movie, pause-for-ad, completion — and never aired. This format finally puts them to work **the way they were meant**: split at natural cleavage points, around **our own fake commercials**. Sibling of [One-Minute Intense](one-minute-intense.md) (participation instead of ads; this one replaces ads with fake ads that **are** the content).

A show is a **playlist**: 1MMs, fake commercials, and longer segments, interleaved.

## Episode grammar

Cold open phone gag → knock-knock segue → topic → sandwich alternation.

1. Call Will. He answers.
2. Not home — leave a message (answering machine gag).
3. Knock knock — forced reverse knock knock segue.
4. Topic lands in tonight's concept.
5. 1MM intro → fake commercial → 1MM conclusion → longer segment.
6. Alternate: commercial / segment / commercial / segment — mixing other 1MMs and guests.

**House standard:** Empathy and Servitude split clean at **30/30** — two slices of bread of the same thickness.

## Sandwich algebra

| Stack | What |
|-------|------|
| Nothingburger | Buns only — original :60 restored |
| Single | bun / commercial / bun |
| Dagwood | bun / ad / segment / ad / … / bun — arbitrarily high |

Buns pair like parentheses — Empathy(Servitude(segment)) is well-formed. Shows can **mate** (shared segment offspring) and **lay eggs** (fork = genome export).

## Flagship instances

**Julie doll:** Empathy 1MM + fake Julie doll commercial + Will's 1996 clip + Servitude 1MM — empathy/servitude concept match total.

**Tiger Mountain:** Dagwood — Doug Hilsinger story interrupted by Ronco ad; Eno's answering machine message as testimonial; Long Now clips as origin footage. Build before contacting Brian — finished episode is the invitation.

## Show is a playlist

Compose on YouTube; grow longer playlists from shorter ones. **Plywood → mahogany:** popular playlists get high-production rendered versions. **Skip the needle:** prev/next links dig grooves through all content. Implicit voting via watch-through and outro engagement.

**Messages for Will:** Don calls, gets machine, leaves message — show-and-tell down the phone. **No Will needed** for production; reply video is bonus.

## Show hooks

- **Head On repeat:** House commercial when content is about repetition, loops, CA.
