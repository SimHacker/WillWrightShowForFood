## The hook

The game is the prompt. Your mouth is the puppet. The chat is the crew.

One performance space where mute gameplay, generated scripts, celebrity face-cutouts, and vocal-tract instruments share the same stack: human voice + optional video + OBS/Twitch audience participation — **no voice clones**, no synth impersonation of living people.

**Core engine:** one host, a button board, Conan face-holes on distinguished portraits, improv voice acting live — weaving all short clips into one show.

## Convergence threads

Foreign Poet × Drew Carey × Conan face-puppet × script readings × Sims play-along narration × MOOLLM pub episodes × memory palace tours.

## Production values

High production values. Low production costs. Lean into the cheese — Conan face-holes, South Park construction paper. TypeScript + WebGPU browser compositor.

## Show hooks

- **Button board live:** PTT crossfade between character portraits.
- **Memory palace tour:** One puppet per artifact stop.
- **Sims play-along:** Audience footage + guest voice-over improv — see [sims-play-along-narration.md](sims-play-along-narration.md).
