## Provenance

**Mooney** is borrowed — with credit and a tip of the hat — from Ian
Bogost's [*Cow Clicker*](https://bogost.com/games/cow_clicker/) (2010).
Bogost's Mooney was a satirical micropayment currency; people paid real
money for premium cows anyway (including the legendary $20
rightward-facing cow). Ours is the **honestly worthless** sequel: same
name, no gateway, infinite supply. We originally called this **MOOLA**;
EA shipped real-money **Moola** for The Sims 4 Marketplace in March
2026, so we lifted Mooney from the source instead.

## The hook

Native **worthless play currency** of the Repo Show multiverse. Symbol: 🐄. **NEVER gateway · ALWAYS invite duplication.**

Simoleons (§) are gallery theater; MOONEY is settlement underneath — what you earn for feeding the machine attention, chat, misread reviews, forked YAML souls. Training data in, worthless MOONEY out.
