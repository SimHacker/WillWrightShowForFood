## The hook

**A Repo Show IS ALWAYS INTENSE!** Format contributed by Will Wright (2026-07-06): hook average people really fast — **60 seconds total.**

| Segment | Duration | Rule |
|---------|----------|------|
| **Idea** | ~45s | ONE idea, told hot. No preamble, no logos. Cold open into the thing itself. |
| **CTA** | ~15s | Hard break into participatory ask — upload, PR, issue, record, roar. Never just subscribe. |

Sibling to Stupid Fun Club's Empathy/Servitude 1MMs — but wrapped around **participation**, not ads.

## First instance — upload roars

**Idea:** NOT GODZILLA monster needs a voice — roars by MOOD; kids program its brain in Snap!

**CTA:** Upload ROARS!!! Record yourself roaring — angry, sad, hungry, polite — GitHub issue or PR with audio + mood tag.

Standing pinned issue per feedback genre — roars issue is the model.
