## The hook

We want to learn how you actually work — not a sales funnel. **Artisanal programmers** (no AI in the loop) earn extra respect. AI users: declare honestly — we document, compare within class, breed technique into skills/.

## Please include (AI rigs)

- Tools, models, when/why — declare tier AND token budget
- Budget bull ride: wrangling cheap models through hallucinations
- Stick shift: model-switch timeline + Cursor spend export
- Token/spend/wall-clock — cost-to-ship AND solution quality
- MCP servers, Agent Skills, MOOLLM wiring
- What worked, broke, would do differently

After you ship: PR back, lift reusable parts to skills/ and packages/.
