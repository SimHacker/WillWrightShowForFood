## The hook

Standard guest ritual: sell guest artwork to menagerie patrons for **§** before the main show. Your patrons buy everything. You start the show buried in worthless §. **Play money only.**

Optional cold open or pre-show block (20–40 min). Default for guests with visual art, generative artists, gallery walk shows.

## Flow

Menagerie patrons bid on wall-title deeds (Unity screenshots, Flame stills, 77MP combinations, JSON graph frames). Guest wallet ends huge (§500k–§5M+ comedy scale). During show: scatter § to chat, tip patrons, Oblique Strategy bids, audience polls.

Auctioneer: Don Philahue. Ledger: `repo-shows/<show>/AUCTIONS.yml`.

## MOOLA exchange

After opening: guest § pile → MOOLA — burn, dig hole, scatter to chat, fund art-thief repairs. Mantra: **NEVER gateway · ALWAYS invite duplication.**

## Show hooks

- **Hammer beat:** Mimsy buys before JPEG loads.
- **Rosebud inverse:** Guest gives away opening pile on stream.
