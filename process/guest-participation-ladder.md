## The hook

**Constitutional default for every invite:** lead with the easy tiers. Stretch goals are optional, never required. Time zones kill sync — David Rosenthal (+9h Amsterdam, grandparent duty) and Lars Brinkhoff (Swedish July offline) said so plainly. **Default: solo async.** Don weaves in post. Never insist on synchronized arrival.

Don bends over backwards — especially for older guests with grandchildren and better things to do than git. No GitHub account required. Email/phone/video file → Don commits.

## Participation tiers

| Tier | Label | Ask | Effort |
|------|-------|-----|--------|
| **0** | Get familiar | Read docs, watch a short clip, skim your character dir if Don pre-researched | Minimal |
| **1** | Solo video | Record short videos on phone/laptop — walk the repo bundle, tell a story, correct Don's portrayal. Send files; Don adds to git | Low — **default initial ask** |
| **2** | One-on-one chat | Pick a time that suits you; Don breaks into clips and weaves time-shifted | Medium |
| **3** | Accretive rounds | After first weave — reply to audience questions, comment on other guests, add artifacts | Ongoing |
| **4** | Group sync | Multi-person live call — only when guests want it and logistics work | High |
| **5** | Live Twitch | Holy grail — TicketPR chime-ins, play-along, [performance space](performance-space/README.md) | Highest |

## Initial ask template

**Easy path (pick any):**

1. Skim the repo + a short video or two — see if the format clicks.
2. Record solo clip(s) on your phone or computer — send to Don; he'll put them in git for you.
3. Or: one-on-one video chat at a time that works for your time zone — Don adapts.

No synchronized group call required. No git required. Many short passes beat one marathon. After the first weave we can do more rounds.

## Production rhythm

Don records host narrative **after** guests submit solo video or 1-on-1 sessions. Break clips, interlink yaml + transcripts + sources, shotgun-blast ensemble — [guest-prototype.yml](../characters/will-wright/guest-prototype.yml#ensemble_medium).

Guests with access can watch the **private production repo** while artifacts land — same accretive rounds inside the green room.

## Franchise vision

WillWrightShowForFood is **Will's** show — his ideas, audience, guests. The format is a **franchise**: any guest can spin their own public repo + private green room (BrianEnoRepoShow, DrewCareyRepoShow, …). Shared Repo Show infrastructure; owned show per host.

People like Brian Eno and Drew Carey may want their own orbit — that is the stretch vision, not the first ask.
