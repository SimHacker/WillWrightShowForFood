# Retro — PDP-7, PDP-10, Apple ][, Lisp Machine

> **→** Same spec CARD — you bring the emulator; ship SETUP.md so others boot without you.

Retrocomputing Drive challenges are platform instances of one umbrella spec. Lars Brinkhoff on ITS/MACLISP; Thomas Cherryhomes on FujiNet real iron — emulated AI Lab meets tree fort.

Two machines already run in the browser: Heinz Lemke's 1972 PIXIE on an emulated PDP-7 and Type 340, and HyperTIES, the 1988 NeWS hypertext browser, with its Space Telescope database.
