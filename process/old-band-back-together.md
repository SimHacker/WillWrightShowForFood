## The hook

A reunion is a first-class show **format**: gather a crew who once built something great, revive the work in public, let the conversation + artifacts continue. Don has been part of many tight crews across decades — NeWS, Sims, David Levitt ventures, OpenLaszlo, the 2006 Eno circle, Snap!Con Barcelona. Same shape recurs — so "get the band back together" is a format, not a one-off.

Relationships are described from Don's perspective. No claim that any named person has agreed to participate.
