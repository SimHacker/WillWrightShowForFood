## The hook

Alan Kay's black-box critique: SimCity teaches city-feel without exposing mechanisms — crime, traffic, pollution algorithms stay hidden. Will Wright acknowledged educator desire for explicit models (1996 Winograd talk). **North star:** same dreams Alan Kay has voiced since OLPC — live, inspectable, modifiable microworlds. Ship working runbooks, not another proposal.

## Response pillars

| Pillar | What |
|--------|------|
| **Glass box** | Reverse diagrams → live views into the running sim (Chaim Gingold lineage) |
| **Export guts** | Telemetry and spreadsheets from gameplay — data literacy through a game students already relate to |
| **Live scripting** | Snap! + Micropolis — Scheme-level live programming in the browser |
| **MOOLLM tutors** | Constructionist microworlds for LLM sessions — rooms, hero-story cards, play-learn-lift |

Educator anchor: Prof. Upmanu Lall — Columbia Civil & Environmental Engineering; OPTIMUS / SimCity.edu lineage since ~2002.

## Runbooks to ship

**Educator glass-box session:** Play city → export metrics → analyze in spreadsheet → modify rule in Snap! → replay and compare.

**Constructionist microworld checklist:** Low floor / high ceiling / wide walls audit · transparency checklist · data export path · live inspect · no opaque scoring.

## Show hooks

- **Glass box live:** Chaim's reverse diagrams on stream over running Micropolis.
- **Export beat:** Classroom spreadsheet from gameplay — Lall lineage named.
- **Snap! modify rule:** Change one rule live; replay and compare.
