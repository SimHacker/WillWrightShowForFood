## The hook

**Send us your Sims. We'll make shit up over it.** Audience submits screen recordings; guests narrate live by voice — pure improvisation. No script. The game is the prompt.

Drew Carey Show × House Party × Foreign Poet × TicketPR.

## Lineage

**Drew Carey Show:** Live-action Sims parody cold open (S6E01) AND segment built in actual game (Maxis custom assets). Drew was a real fan.

**Foreign Poet:** Stephen Kearin + Gerri Lawlor — speak emotive gibberish; partner interprets. Icons carry meaning; voice carries feeling. **Do not impersonate Gerri Lawlor** (d. 2019) — honor memory; Kearin may explain on air.

**Repo Show spin:** No Maxis custom build required. Anyone sends recording; guests supply Foreign Poet layer live.

## Format

| Input | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Audience screen recording | Any era — Sims 1 aesthetic is spiritual home |
| Guest improv | No prep on specific clip — first watch on air |
| Duration | 3–8 min per clip; stack 2–3 per block |

Playback: gameplay audio low/muted. Guest voice-over LIVE — invent names, relationships, lies. Commit to the bit — wronger is funnier.

## Show hooks

- **Balcony cutaway:** 🤡💀 react between clips.
- **Drew archetype guest:** Whose Line energy over audience house party chaos.
