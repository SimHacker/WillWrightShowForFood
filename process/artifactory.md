## The hook

An **artifactory** is a factory for artifacts — a constructor that builds artifacts, including itself. Give it a spec (tape, class, SKILL.md) and it constructs what the spec describes; in the strong form, it copies the spec and you have self-reproduction.

Also a real product name (JFrog Artifactory). The double meaning is the point.

**Positioning:** abstract like lambda, concrete like Ubik; mystical framing, falsifiable mechanics. If a claim can't be opened, run, diffed, or reverted in the repo, it isn't artifactory — it's incense.

## Calm vs runaway

| Pole | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| **Calm generative** | Builds what is asked, stops, leaves clean provenance |
| **Runaway coprophagic** | Consumes past purpose; junk poisons next build (model collapse) |
