PIXIE is the other production HyperTIES database. This is the door.

1969, Cambridge: ~Heinz Lemke~, Neil Wiseman, and John Hiles. Light pen, radial buttons that travel with the tracking cross, circuits drawn on a ~PDP-7~ and analysed on Titan. The earliest known pie menus.

**Run it:** ~PIXIE live~ — SYMELEC, the 1972 PIXIE program, executing on an emulated PDP-7 with a Type 340 display, in this page. Press and drag on the tube; the mouse is the light pen. The page says which buttons draw a line, and ends with the 1969 film.

**How it got running.** Each of these transcludes the working document itself, so what you read is what is in the repository.

- ~Bug journal~ — every bug found getting here. Two one-digit transcription errors only showed up when SYMELEC ran; one bug is 1972's own.
- ~Transcription report~ — 128 pages of printout into octal: the pipeline, the checkers, what the scribes got wrong.
- ~The listing~ — the 1972 printout itself, and the rules for copying it.
- ~Off by one~ — field notes from the first four rungs: boot, display, tracking, the Titan link.
- ~Tracking~ — how the tracking cross follows a pen that only sees light.
- ~Tiny Titan~ — the Cambridge mainframe as the smallest part on the backplane.
- ~Cabinet design~, ~Web bench~, ~SIMH map~ — how the emulator is built, and what it borrowed.

**The people and the machine.**

- ~Heinz Lemke~ — co-author, 1969; his assembler listing is what the emulator runs.
- ~PDP-7~ — the machine: an 18-bit DEC minicomputer with a vector display.
- ~The emulator~ — the browser PDP-7 and the desktop SIMH bench it is checked against.
- ~Lars Brinkhoff~ — the iron: SIMH work and the Type 340 character glyphs the emulator draws with.
- ~Roy Eagleson~ — leading students at Western University to re-implement PIXIE.
- ~The films~ — the 1969 reels.
- ~The plan~ — everything in the WillWrightShowForFood rooms, transcluded, and what comes next.
- ~Master Index~ — every name in this database.
