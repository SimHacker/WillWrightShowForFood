PIXIE is the other production HyperTIES database. This is the door.

1969, Cambridge: ~Heinz Lemke~, Neil Wiseman, and John Hiles. Light pen, radial buttons that travel with the tracking cross, circuits drawn on a ~PDP-7~ and analysed on Titan. The earliest known pie menus.

**Run it:** ~playground/PIXIE live~ — SYMELEC, the 1972 PIXIE program, executing on an emulated PDP-7 with a Type 340 display, in this page. Press and drag on the tube; the mouse is the light pen.

What else is here:

- ~Heinz Lemke~ — co-author, 1969; his assembler listing is what the emulator runs.
- ~PDP-7~ — the machine: an 18-bit DEC minicomputer with a vector display.
- ~The emulator~ — how the browser PDP-7 was built, and the desktop SIMH bench it is checked against.
- ~Lars Brinkhoff~ — the iron: SIMH work and the Type 340 character glyphs the emulator draws with.
- ~Roy Eagleson~ — leading students at Western University to re-implement PIXIE.
- ~The films~ — the 1969 reels.
- ~The plan~ — the map of what will be transcluded here from WillWrightShowForFood.
