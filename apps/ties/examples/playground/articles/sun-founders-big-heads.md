Educom, October 1988. A Sun workstation on the show floor, rotating NeWS, pie menus, Emacs, and ~HyperTIES~.

~hyperties/Don Hopkins~ showed ~The Founders~ to Steve Jobs and Bill Joy. The picture is three ~Sun~ founders. The heads are ~PopupTarget~ regions — they pop when you point at them, and all at once when you click the background. That is ~Reveal all links~. Bill Joy is one of those heads.

Jobs jumped up and down: "That sucks! That sucks! Wow, that's neat. That sucks!"

The best part was popping all three heads at once: Don held the optical mouse up to his mouth and blew and sucked into it, secretly pressing and releasing the button, so it looked like he was inflating them.

A man hung around through several loops. By the time Don got back to Emacs, the man said he used to use Emacs on ITS. Don asked his username. **"WNJ."** That was Bill Joy. Don had been popping Joy's own head without recognizing him, because Joy had shaved his beard.
