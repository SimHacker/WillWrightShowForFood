~Arthur van Hoff~ built a network-aware ~HyperCard~ in PostScript for NeWS. First name: GoodNeWS. Then ~HyperNeWS~. Then shipped as ~HyperLook~.

Same object, three names. The load-bearing property is ~Browse and edit~ at runtime, in PostScript, on the display server — not a separate design-time tool.
