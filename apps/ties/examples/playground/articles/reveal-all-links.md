---
title: Reveal all links
synonyms:
  - click the background
  - reveal all
  - press background
definition: "Click or hold the page background and every link highlights at once. A failed click is a request for the map."
---

> HyperTIES had a feature that you could click or press and hold on the page background, and it would blink or highlight **ALL** of the links on the page, either by inverting the brightness of text buttons, or by popping up all the cookie-cut-out picture targets at the same time.

Arbitrarily-shaped graphical targets cannot be self-naming the way link text is. An image with hidden targets is a guessing game. The 1991 paper's answer was pop-out highlighting: offset the object, drop a shadow, and let the slight movement catch the eye.

Then: when people sweep an image looking for targets, or click in a non-target area, highlight them all.

A failed click is not an error. It is a request for the map. That is how ~Sun Founders Big Heads~ pops all three heads at once.
