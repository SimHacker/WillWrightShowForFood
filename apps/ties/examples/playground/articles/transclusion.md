From ~hyperties/The Medium wall~, ~hyperties/Don Hopkins~ on how he builds those posts:

> I think of what I try to do as **manually implementing** Ted Nelson's, Ivan Sutherland's, Douglas Engelbart's, and Ben Shneiderman's important ideas about "transclusion".

> Following links is distracting and costly, so most people aren't going to click on a bunch of inline links, read something, then come back... So I aspire to simulate Ted Nelson's and other people's ideals with **the crude stone knives and bearskins that we're stuck with today.**

The irony he names: he had to transclude by hand in order to explain transclusion, which Ted thought should be built in and automatic.

This reader takes a `transclude` fence naming an article — and optionally another database — and splices that article's current segments. Change the source, the illustration changes.

~The Founders~ in this database is a door: its body is a transclude of the 1988 demo. ~Sun Founders Big Heads~ transcludes that door. Nested, live, not copied.

A span still needs a start and an end. Right now a transclude is a whole article. That is the next cut.
