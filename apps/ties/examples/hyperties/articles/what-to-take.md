From ~A Look Back~ and ~Hacker News archive~, into this reader:

- Mandatory per-article definition — ~Definition previews~
- Single click reads, double click goes
- Click the background, ~playground/Reveal all links~
- Pop-out highlight with drop-shadow — motion as the cheapest "this is live" signal
- Dimmed-but-enabled instead of disabled
- One markup language for content and shared definitions
- Authorability chosen over SGML
- Storyboards compiled and dumped as an image — ~The stack~
- Scriptable embedded applets
- Pie menus for link routing — ~Self revealing~
- ~Browse and edit~ as a toggle, not two programs
- Controlled experiments before defaults — ~The blue link~, ~Lift-off~

~HyperTIES~ is the front page. ~playground/XanaTIES~ is the name that rings. Then ~playground/HyperCard~ → ~playground/HyperLook~.
