Development began fall 1983 at the University of Maryland ~HCIL~ under ~Ben Shneiderman~, as **TIES** — The Interactive Encyclopedia System — on IBM PCs. Dan Ostroff implemented the first embedded menus that year.

Renamed **HyperTIES** in 1986 after a cease-and-desist: "TIES" collided with an existing trademark. "By then 'hyper' was a growing term."

The NeWS workstation version, 1988–89, is ~the stack~: C formatter, FORTH markup, PostScript applets, UniPress Emacs authoring. ~Don Hopkins~ wrote that layer. ~William Weiland~ wrote the C index manager the synonyms rest on.

Paper of record: ~A Look Back~. This reader is that browser, pointed at markdown. ~HyperTIES~ is the front page.
