HyperTIES is The Interactive Encyclopedia System. It began in 1983 at the University of Maryland ~HCIL~ as TIES, on IBM PCs. ~Dan Ostroff~ wrote the first embedded menus that year. The name changed in 1986 when TIES hit a trademark.

This website is that system, pointed at markdown. The NeWS workstation browser of 1988–89 is what you are using: a short definition on a single click, the article on a double click, pictures whose pieces pop. ~Ben Shneiderman~ directed the lab. ~Catherine Plaisant~, ~Rodrigo Botafogo~, ~Don Hopkins~, and ~William Weiland~ wrote the 1991 paper that is still the account of this browser: ~A Look Back~.

Click a link once to read what it is. Click twice to go there. Click the paper to see every link.

The 1988 demo that made the argument is Hubble. Point at an instrument. It pops. Click the sky. They all pop.
