In January 2022 ~Don Hopkins~ put a lightly edited pile of HyperTIES discussions and email in one place. Seventy-four minutes. His preface asked forgiveness for the wall and the redundancy: he had not had time to distill it into one sentence.

These nodes are that distillation. Same material, one claim per article, each with a ~Definition previews~ so you can read the abstract without leaving the page you are on.

- ~The blue link~ — Ben's claim, why not red, the path to Tim
- ~Definition previews~ — single click reads, double click goes
- ~playground/Reveal all links~ — a failed click is a request for the map
- ~The stack~ — C, FORTH, PostScript, MockLisp
- ~Browse and edit~ — HyperCard's toggle, the line the web took
- ~Self revealing~ — pie menus lead, follow, or get out of the way
- ~Lift-off~ — select on lift-off, not land-on
- ~Sun Founders Big Heads~ — Jobs and Joy on the Educom floor
- ~playground/Transclusion~ — quote with a start and an end, not a point
- ~Hypertext on Hypertext~ — the 1988 CACM disk
- ~What to take~ — one page

~A Look Back~ is the 1991 paper this archive keeps citing. ~Wikipedia~ is the stub.
