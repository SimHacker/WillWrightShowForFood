Co-author of ~A Look Back~ with ~Ben Shneiderman~, ~Rodrigo Botafogo~, ~Don Hopkins~, and ~William Weiland~.

Touchscreen toggle switches with D. Wallace (1990), later cited as prior art against Apple's Slide to Unlock patents. High-precision touchscreens with Sears and Shneiderman. Adjacent to ~Lift-off~.
