The full text of eight *CACM* papers from the July 1987 Hypertext conference at UNC, published on disk in July 1988, built in ~HyperTIES~. ~Ben Shneiderman~: the world's first electronic journal. ACM sold 4000 copies.

Berners-Lee's Spring 1989 WWW proposal cites that electronic journal as the source of the "hot spots" idea. That is the attested path in ~The blue link~.

*Hypertext Hands-On!* (Shneiderman and Kearsley, 1988) is the world's first electronic book, from the same lab.
