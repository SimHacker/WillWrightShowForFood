The synonyms are alternate identifiers by which the document may be referenced. (see ~how to make a link~)

The synonyms are specified by the author in the storyboard. They follow (one per line) the command ".synonym", (or ".synonyms", ".SYNONYM", or ".SYNONYMS".)

A synonym is a string ended by a newline (it can include blanks, punctuation...)
