---
title: the storyboard
synonyms:
  - storyboard
  - storyboards
definition: The text file describing an article, written by the author.
source: doc/storyboard.st0
---

The storyboard files are pure text descriptions of the individual articles (nodes in the hypertext network) of a Hyperties database.  These files are broken into sections, containing the ~title~ , ~synonyms~, ~description~, ~content~, and notes (an optional collection of textual remarks maintained by the author).  The content (and possibly the description) may contain references to other documents, which are simply the titles or synonyms of those documents, embedded in the text (or connected to a graphic), and marked by the author.  Since the storyboard is a pure text document, it uses a special ~markup language~ to indicate page layout, font types, and to specify the inclusion of graphics.
