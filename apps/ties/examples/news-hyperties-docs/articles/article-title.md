---
title: Article title
synonyms:
  - title
  - titles
definition: The name of an article, given by the author.
source: doc/title.st0
---

The title identifies the article.  It appears at the top left corner of each page when the article is displayed.

The title of an article is specified by the author in the ~storyboard~. It follows the command `".title"` or `".TITLE".`

The title of an article is also used to make reference to an article. (see ~how to make a link~).
