synonyms:

definition: A lengthy multi-media piece -- the document proper
source: doc/content.st0
---

The content may contain references to other documents, which are simply the ~titles~ or ~synonyms~ of those documents, embedded in the text (or connected to a graphic), and marked by the author. (See ~How to make a link~).

Since the storyboard is a pure text document, it uses a special ~markup language~ to indicate page layout, font types, and to specify the inclusion of graphics.

The content is part of the ~storyboard~. It follows the ".content" command. (or ".contents", ".CONTENT", or ".CONTENTS")
