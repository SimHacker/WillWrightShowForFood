The storyboard contains all the information necessary to display an article.  The author has to use a markup language to specify all that information.

The command syntax is: <whitespace> `.command<whitespace>[arguments]`

Whitespace consists of blanks, newlines, and/or tabs.

There are four types of commands:

- the ~field commands~

To separate title, synonyms, contents, notes

- the ~component commands~

To separate text, picture, video, sound

- the ~formatting commands~

To specify margin sizes, fonts, paragraphs

- the ~link commands~

To indicate links to other articles
