  - the index article
definition: The list of articles in the database
source: doc/aut-index.st0
---

The index article consists of an alphabetical list of all the articles of the database.

It is the article whose title or synomym is "!index". It is accessed when selecting the INDEX option of the control panel.

This article is created automatically by the ~master index builder~ which creates or updates the storyboard index.st0
