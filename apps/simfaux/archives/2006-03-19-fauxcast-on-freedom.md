## FauxCasting

"Cinematic leap beyond podcasting and blogging" — keyword-tagged text, video, sound bites, simulated characters, interactive widgets; viewer controls what they see and hear. Tell Bill O'Reilly to shut up and go away. Surprising juxtapositions (Frank Zappa and Dick Cheney share a cuss word starting with F).

## Politics (Don, 2006)

SimFaux can parody any side; Don argued conservatives were "fucking up the country" and sending kids to die — impeachment over war lies, Katrina, warrantless wiretap, etc. — versus performative outrage over Clinton. After the festival: host without size cap, more content, **open source** release.

## Design lineage

Inspired by Will Wright's emergent behavior and **Chris Trottier** on tuned emergence / design by accretion — "toilet game" until tuned. Learn **OpenLaszlo**: https://www.openlaszlo.org/ · LaszloMail example.

## Source release

Subversion (March 2006): `svn co svn://SimFaux.com/SimFaux/trunk/SimFaux` — XML-configured characters, video, surveys, pie menus.
