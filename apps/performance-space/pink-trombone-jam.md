# Pink Trombone Jam 🎺👥🗣️

*A bunch of people pink-trombone-ing and singing together, live.*

## What it is

Everyone opens **[Pink Trombone](https://dood.al/pinktrombone/)** — Neil Thapen's playable model of the
human vocal tract — in a browser tab, and sings with their **real voice** on mic. The trombone is a second
voice you conduct with your mouse (or MIDI, or gamepad): drag the tongue, pinch the constriction, set
pitch and voicing. Two to four people at once is chaos and joy.

This is **articulatory synthesis** — a physical model of a tube, not a recording or clone of anyone's
voice. You are playing an instrument, not impersonating a person. That matters for the show's ethics: no
voice clones, ever.

## What you do with it

- **Accompany** — trombone drones or harmonizes under your real singing.
- **Follow** — trombone chases your pitch and vowels a beat behind; call-and-response with yourself.
- **Lead** — the instrument sets the line; you sing to match it (you're the cover).
- **Karaoke fool** — ham it up, no dignity required; aim the silliness at yourself.
- **Affectionate send-up** — gently impersonate other voices and styles, as homage. Keep it nice.

Four trombones plus four voices = an instant absurd choir. Pick a round (*Row, Row, Row*). One player
leads, three follow — musical Simon Says with vocal tracts.

## We Are the Weird

The dream: do a Pink Trombone session on **each guest's own show**, then edit them together into one mass
choir — **as many people trombone-singing at once as possible**, live or async. Anthem energy: *We Are
the Weird.*

Nina Hagen belongs on that choir list — extreme vocal-tract control incarnate; the trombone is a cartoon
of what she does live. Also on the dream roster: Laurie Anderson, Brian Eno, Kevin Lenzo, Zack Qattan,
Alan Kay, Craig Latta.

## Voice → tract (the magic layer)

**[Zack Qattan](../../characters/zack-qattan/README.md)** trained models to map human speech to Pink
Trombone parameters — phoneme classifiers, KNN voice classifiers, the **Vocap** demos, plus
collaborative multi-player Pink Trombone (Croquet-synced), MIDI and gamepad control, variable tract
length, text-to-speech, and lip sync.

Watch his walkthrough: [Pink Trombone Demos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B74tKZDVNS8) ·
[YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ZackQattan)
