# How to Deconstruct Almost Anything Inside the Cube

*After Chip Morningstar. Performed by Captain Kira Ashford, Ph.D.
(Pending), Department of Distressed Bovine Studies. For the Ian Bogost
invitation. Full machine-readable spec:
[gag-how-to-deconstruct-the-cube.yml](gag-how-to-deconstruct-the-cube.yml).*

## Lineage

Chip Morningstar's
["How to Deconstruct Almost Anything"](http://www.fudco.com/chip/deconstr.html)
(1993) is the working engineer's field guide: he attended a postmodern
conference, built a parody from real jargon, and learned the field is
epistemologically challenged but not empty. (Morningstar and Randy
Farmer also built Habitat in 1986 — avatars, First Penguin Award — so
he'd earned the right.) Captain Kira Ashford — the Belter space-pirate
lit-crit ghost from MOOLLM's adventure-4 coatroom — wrote the homage
*How to Incarnate Almost Anything*, and this essay-gag is her third
movement: deconstruct Molyneux's Curiosity **without resurrecting it**.
Bogost's Cow Clicker needs no deconstruction; it already deconstructed
itself.

## Morningstar's three-part test, applied to clickers

1. **Does peeling layers reveal structure that was always there?**
   Curiosity: yes — one winner, hollow center, hype as load-bearing
   wall. Cow Clicker: yes — scheduled death, Mooney, cow JPEG; honest
   architecture. Our cube parody: yes — stolen deeds on faces, MOONEY,
   distressed moos, the horse-is-cow reveal.
2. **Does analysis surface what players feel but cannot name?**
   Curiosity: compulsion without rescue; godhood as deferred
   signifier. Cow Clicker: "It wasn't very fun before" — Bogost named
   the shame out loud. Our parody: filtered far-away moos crying for
   help — procedural guilt as retention.
3. **Can a working builder use this without tenure?** Curiosity:
   fail — promises a life-changing center, delivers liability and a
   podcast. Cow Clicker: pass — criticism you can click; extension
   payments as punchline. Bogost's term for the passing grade is
   *procedural rhetoric* — Morningstar's immune system, Bogost's
   design vocabulary.

## The ask, for Ian

You coined procedural rhetoric. Morningstar wrote the engineer's
survival guide to jargon. Ashford wrote the homage. Fourth voice
wanted, on air:

- Is distressed-moo audio procedural rhetoric or just cruelty with
  reverb?
- Do Morningstar's deconstruction essay and *Persuasive Games* share a
  family tree?
- When satire becomes popular anyway (Cow Clicker), is the second joke
  the product?
- Should Curiosity stay dead and buried — and is
  [our cube parody](../gag-curiosity-cow-cube/gag-curiosity-cow-cube.md)
  honest enough about that?
