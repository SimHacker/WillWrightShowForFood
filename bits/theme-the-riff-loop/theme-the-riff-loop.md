# Theme — The Riff Loop

> *AI and people riffing off AI and people — and you can jump in anywhere to steer it.*


**Shows:** will-wright, jeff-adkins, brian-eno, nina-hagen · **Status:** ready

---

## The pattern

A loop where each pass's output becomes the next pass's input, and **both people and AI** take turns riffing off each other AND off what the loop already made. It never repeats and it never finishes — every round mutates the last. The magic: it's **steerable**. A human isn't outside pushing buttons; they're **in** the loop, nudging from any point.

---

## Four ways in

| Handle | What you do |
|--------|-------------|
| **Into** | Seed it — speak/prompt the material the loop chews on |
| **Over** | Talk OVER it live — your voice becomes part of the mix it feeds back on |
| **After** | Respond AFTER a pass — feed your reaction in as the next input |
| **Before** | Prime BEFORE the next pass — pre-load context that bends where it goes |

Four handles on the same wheel. Steer + mutate from anywhere; no pass is final.

---

## Anchor: Slats' speech feedback loop

Slats' voice-synth stream-of-thought robopoetry loops — output feeding back as input — and people talk into, over, after, and before it to steer the stream. Reincarnate Slats and the loop is live again. **The diff between passes is the drama.**

## Why it matters

Reframes *"AI vs people"* as *"people AND AI in a loop."* Authorship isn't lost to the machine and isn't withheld from it — it's continuously **re-steered** by whoever jumps into the cycle. That's the RepoShow repossession thesis in motion: stay in the loop, keep your hands on the wheel.
