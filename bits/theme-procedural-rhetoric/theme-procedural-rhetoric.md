# Theme — Procedural rhetoric

> *Objects argue — the rules are the rhetoric.*


**Shows:** will-wright, heather-and-steve · **Status:** ready

---

## The thread

Ian Bogost's frame applied to Repo Show inventory: simulations don't just represent — they *argue*. Two poles already live in this repo:

| Pole | Artifact | What it argues |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Inclusion** | [SimProv wedding playset](../../catalogs/simprov/README.md) | Who gets recognized — marriage equality as playable procedure |
| **Farce** | Dumbold Voting Machine | Institutional ridicule — broken democracy as comedy |

**Substrate:** [Soul of the Sims (2008)](../../characters/will-wright/sources/2008-02-10-soul-of-the-sims/) — Will on what sims *mean*, not just what they do.
