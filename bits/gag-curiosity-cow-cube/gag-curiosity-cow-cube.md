# Curiosity Cow Cube — the cube clicker that should stay dead

*Parody of Peter Molyneux's Curiosity crossed with Ian Bogost's Cow
Clicker. Full machine-readable performance spec:
[gag-curiosity-cow-cube.yml](gag-curiosity-cow-cube.yml).*

> Click the cube for worthless MOONEY. Peel layers. The center is a
> cow. It wasn't very fun before. That's a cow.

## Should never ship — that's the point

If there was ever a game that should never be recreated and kept dead
and buried forever, it's Molyneux's cube clicker
[*Curiosity: What's Inside the Cube?*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curiosity:_What%27s_Inside_the_Cube%3F)
(released November 6, 2012; ended May 26, 2013; never delivered on its
grandiose promises). This repo holds a *parody* and social-commentary
beat — scheduled termination communicated upfront, on the
[Cow Clicker](../../characters/ian-bogost/sources/cow-clicker.md)
Cowpocalypse model. We do not resurrect the scam; we put Bogost's cow
in the middle instead of godhood and broken lies.

## The three sources

**Curiosity.** Molyneux's
[Unite 2012 keynote](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24AY4fJ66xA&t=1h08m21s)
(Amsterdam, Westergasfabriek) peaks at 1:48:06 with the crucifix pose:
"We can dynamically put on ANY surface of the cube ANY image we like —
THAT's how we're going to surprise the world."
[Don trolled him afterward](../../characters/don-hopkins/sources/hn-cow-clicker-curiosity-unite-2012.md):
the secret inside the box is a cow. Peter didn't get it. Don explained.
Peter still didn't get it.

**Cow Clicker.** Bogost made the argument on purpose in 2010 —
currency called Mooney, a Cowpocalypse scheduled for the game's first
birthday, extensions purchasable, cows finally raptured in September
2011. "It wasn't very fun before."
[The full story](../../characters/ian-bogost/sources/cow-clicker.md).

**The Berkeley horse.**
["Cows In Berkeley? Moooo"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10JSPdTDFsI)
(Charles Scamahorn, 2008): a shirtless dude rides a horse the wrong way
up Telegraph Avenue while buddies outside the Mediterraneum Cafe,
high on too much coffee, deadpan-commit that the animal is a cow.
"That's a cow. I know the difference." "I've been waiting for that all
my life." Don's YouTube comment, years ago: "It's a cow." The cube's
alternate final layer is this horse. The menagerie insists it's a cow.
Never break character.

## The parody game

*Curiosity: What's Inside the Repo?* — clicks earn MOONEY; collective
clicks peel layers; ANY face can show ANY stolen gallery deed
(Molyneux surface-promise energy). Sometimes — not every click — a
filtered, echoing, far-away **distressed moo** cries for help behind
the next layer; you keep clicking to "rescue" it. The cow is never
rescued until the center — and then it's a JPEG. The distressed audio
is procedural rhetoric: the game admits it's manipulating you.

The Cowpocalypse rules apply: the death date is the feature, every
click deducts time, MOONEY buys supplication (10 for an hour, 4,000
for a month), and at the end the cows are raptured and the MOONEY pile
is burned. And the anti-Curiosity mantra: **NEVER gateway, ALWAYS
invite duplication** — Curiosity's original sin was one person winning
the center; here everyone gets the cow JPEG, freely copyable, not a
single-winner NFT.
