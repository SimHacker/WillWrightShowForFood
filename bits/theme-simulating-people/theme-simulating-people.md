# Theme — Simulating people

> *Impersonation ≠ simulation — Sims players have done this for 26 years; AI raises the stakes.*


**Shows:** will-wright, drew-carey, all · **Status:** ready

**Constitutional detail:** [portrayal-standards.md#simulation](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

---

## The question

*Is it ethical to simulate people?*

**Thesis:** A sharp line separates **impersonation** (machine replaces your authorship — voice clone, ChatGPT script, opaque *"is that really them?"*) from **simulation** (models of characters and people — fictional, historic, or yourself with consent — clearly framed, author-controlled, playing with each other and with real humans).

---

## Impersonation — forbidden without explicit consent

| What | Rule |
|------|------|
| Machine replaces your authorship | Voice clone, ChatGPT script, opaque identity |
| Drew 2023 | Audience wanted personality back — [ai-contrast-framing.md](../../repo-shows/drew-carey/ai-contrast-framing.md) |

---

## Simulation — allowed when labeled and governed

Game characters, fictional audience, historic figures, and living people who **consent and co-author** their model. AI may animate dialogue and give agents autonomy — within human approval and always labeled as simulation.

**Governance checklist:**

- `SIMULATION` label always visible
- Consent + repo authorship for living models
- Human approves what agents say and do (Player-in-the-Middle)
- Deceased: discuss **about**; never speak **as**

---

## Sims precedent

For 26+ years players have made Sims of partners, friends, bullies — and run experiments on them. Kids and adults both. **Simulating people in dollhouses is core Sims play** — not a Repo Show invention.

Will in 1996: *"Really no hope"* simulating people — prop up a convincing **illusion** instead. The illusion worked better than Will predicted. AI makes illusions cheap; **consent and labeling matter more**.

→ [1996 transcript](../../characters/will-wright/sources/1996-04-26-winograd-interfacing-to-microworlds/transcript.md)

---

## AI opens Pandora's box

LLM agents can converse, decide, and be mistaken for real people at scale. We govern with Engelbart augmentation, PITM, portrayal-standards, memorial mode, no living voice clones on air.

**Most interesting case:** Simulating **yourself** — with consent, direct authorship, and control — so an AI model of you can play with other simulated characters and real people on air, clearly framed. TicketPR, co-edited repo characters, self-sovereign Don. The opposite of ChatGPT impersonating you while you sleep.
