🎻🗣️ **Laurie Anderson** — American avant-garde performance artist, composer, and inventor (b. 1947). "**O Superman**" (1981); the epic *United States I–IV*; a lifelong **inventor of instruments and voice tech** — the **tape-bow violin**, the **talking stick**, the pitch-shifted "**Voice of Authority**" persona. Anti-censorship voice of the NEA-controversy era; later a pioneer of **VR/immersive** storytelling (*Chalkroom*). Spoken word + technology + generative systems on stage — the Repo Show's exact register. Built the **Talking Stick** with Bob Bielecki & **Interval Research** (c. 1999, public record); earlier **STEIM** "invisible instrument" (1992).
