Dear Laurie,

We briefly crossed paths during the **Interval Research** years — I was there in the lab's heyday,
and you've spoken publicly about building the **Talking Stick** with **Bob Bielecki** there (c. 1999):
that six-foot MIDI baton that granularizes and replays audio in real time. That instrument stuck with
me — it's the same instinct I chase in interactive systems: **turn playback into performance**, seams
showing.

I run something now called a **Repo Show** — a live conversation whose stage is a **public GitHub
repository** people can browse and build on. Instead of letting AI flatten people's work, I sit down
with the people who actually made things, we talk in public, and the tools turn ideas into something
real while we go. **Show, don't tell.**

I'd be honored if you'd join one — and there's no homework trap. You'd be the topic. The threads I
keep coming back to (your pick, skip whatever bores you):

- **Invented instruments as performance-hacks** — the tape-bow violin; the Talking Stick; rebuilding
  a browser scrub-by-gesture toy live so the audience can fork it.
- **The Voice of Authority** — voice puppet, persona, and who gets to speak when a machine can shift
  yours.
- **Granular kinship** — your resampling baton and my **Musical Gas** granular CA synth (colliding
  particles firing voice grains) as sibling instruments.
- **Storytelling that commits to git** — *United States* as architecture vs narrative that's live,
  forkable, and time-shifted.
- **VR/immersive work** (*Chalkroom*, *Aloft*) meeting a show built for an audience that can edit
  the story.

More hooks in [`ideas.md`](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/laurie-anderson/ideas.md). No pressure — accepted,
delayed, declined, or no reply all honored gracefully.

— Don Hopkins, Repo Show Class
