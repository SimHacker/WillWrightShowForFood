# Dear Jared (and Laurie!) — an invitation to a Repo Show

*Draft from Don. Consent not yet asked. This page is yours to edit, prune, or delete —
[portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md).*

Jared!

Do you remember how far back we go? Let me jog both our memories, because I'd forgotten half of
this until I dug up the emails.

**March 2005.** Will Wright is the one who pointed me at levitated.net — he loved it and knew I
would too. I sent you my notes from one of Will's presentations; you'd just caught that hour-long
mini-bio on him and told me your girlfriend played SimCity when she wasn't studying architecture.
The word *spore* in my notes got you excited — and I told you Spore's **tidepool level** looked
like your Flash work come to life, multi-legged procedural creatures and all. You were in Seattle
finishing the Boeing work and joked about ordering us both a plane, being just a tad short of the
$270M price tag.

**February 2010.** You and **Laurie** came to Amsterdam for your FITC talk and we finally met in
person. You'd written to **Ben Cerveny** the same day you wrote me. **Will friended you on Facebook
the week I wrote back** — I may have had something to do with that. We had a great time; you left a
gift for me at the Ambassade's front desk with **two laser-engraved sandstones** in it — I still
think about that box. You promised to pull every string you could find to bring some sun next time.
The strings apparently reached further than the weather: four years later you'd pulled Etsy's
success into a whole **Toy Factory**.

**September 2014.** Your Levitated launch letter is one of my favorite things anyone has ever
written about why we do this: *"Since I was first introduced to the personal computer almost 30
years ago, I have used code to explore the mysteries of mathematics and the beauty of geometry…
I did it because it was fun."* And then you built the place — laser cutters, natural materials,
the LifeStar, a team of your friends — to see just how deep you could dig.

**So here's the show.** Will and I are making **Repo Shows** — the product and stage is a public
GitHub repo, not just a video: sources, code, questions, and artifacts accumulate before, during,
and forever after the conversation ([how it works](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/process/FORMAT.md), or see the
[vision](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/process/VISION.md)). **Will Wright** has already accepted the first invitation — the [premiere show](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md). That feels right, since he kicked off our friendship. I'd love a show with you: your arc from
**levitated.net → Complexification → Etsy → the Toy Factory** is the cleanest story I know of
*generative code becoming a life* — the gems mined from math, then manufactured into wood you can
hold. And your Flash work is secretly load-bearing for this whole project: you're the artist who
opened my eyes to **dynamically orchestrating the stage with scripts** — a cast conjured by code —
which is exactly what this repo now does with its characters.

**Laurie is invited too** — as guest, audience, or heckler, her choice. And I'd love Will in the
room for the tidepool reunion.

No deadlines, no live obligation — async at your pace, time-shifted like everything here. Your
directory in this repo is yours: [characters/jared-tarbell/](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/jared-tarbell/README.md). Edit anything. Delete
anything. Or just tell me a story about the wilderness and we'll put it on the show.

Come back during warmer times. We'll rent a boat.

— Don

*P.S. — Paul Haeberli, advising me on OpenGL in 2010, pointed me at your
[levCAWorm](http://levitated.net/daily/levCAWorm.html) as the model for how to render cellular
automata beautifully. Your work keeps showing up in my inbox from every direction; it was only a
matter of time before it got its own show.*
