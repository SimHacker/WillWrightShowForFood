# In memoriam — Dame Stephanie Shirley (1933–2025)

*Memorial mode: we represent and discuss her documented work and publicly shared
story; we do not interview her or speak as her, and we invent no quotes or inner life.*
[Memorial standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode)

Dame Stephanie ("Steve") Shirley CH arrived in Britain as a Kindertransport child
refugee and built one of the defining software houses of the mid-century UK:
**Freelance Programmers** (1962), staffed overwhelmingly by women working from home —
flexible remote work as social design, not a pandemic afterthought. She signed
business letters **Steve** because letters signed Stephanie went unanswered. Her
teams' early work included software for Concorde's black-box flight recorder. The
company grew into FI Group; staff share ownership made many millionaires; later
valuations approached $3bn.

She spent the second half of her public life as a **venture philanthropist**, giving
tens of millions strategically — especially to autism research and care (Shirley
Foundation, Autistica, Prior's Court) and to IT institutions (including a founding
gift to the Oxford Internet Institute). Her own framing: money that isn't working
has a kind of obscenity about it.

We celebrate her documented public work; we do not speak as her.

*This is a collaborative memorial, not a signed work. Those who knew Dame Stephanie
— colleagues, foundation partners, friends — are warmly invited to edit, correct,
and add their own memories, documents, and stories whenever they're ready.*
