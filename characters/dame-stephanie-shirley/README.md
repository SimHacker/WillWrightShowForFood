## Who

**Dame Stephanie ("Steve") Shirley CH** — Kindertransport child refugee; founder of
**Freelance Programmers** (1962), a women-first, home-based software house that
pioneered flexible remote work; later one of Britain's foremost IT-and-autism
philanthropists (Shirley Foundation / Autistica / Prior's Court; Oxford Internet
Institute founding donor).

## Why she's here

A through-line for Repo Show: software as **social infrastructure** — who gets to
build it, from where, under what name the world will answer. Her public story
connects refugee welcome, sexism in mid-century computing, remote work, employee
ownership, and strategic giving.
