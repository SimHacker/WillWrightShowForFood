## What Reese has done

**Reese Jones** — biophysicist; co-founded **BMUG** (1984); founded **Farallon** (PhoneNET,
MacRecorder, Timbuktu) → **Netopia** → Motorola; associate founder & trustee, **Singularity
University**; **Santa Fe Institute** board trustee (2012–2015); **Facebook advisor** (2016–);
chairman, **Cambrian Genomics**. Fifteen public talks cataloged in
[sources/talks-catalog.md](sources/talks-catalog.md) — one recurring thesis: *everything evolves*.

## Shared ground

Don knows Reese personally; they read each other on Facebook. Reese recorded and published
[Sam Arbesman's 2016 *Overcomplicated* book event](sources/overcomplicated-2016-video.md);
Chaim Gingold introduced Sam to Don in 2024 — triangle closed. BMUG-era homebrew and phone-phreak
culture is the soil Don's world grew in too.

## The hooks

### 1. Consciousness in the Cloud — the ALife session
His 2014 Netflix HQ discussion (Biota podcast): **artificial life, multi-agent systems,
evolutionary computation, agent-based modeling, complexity theory**. That's the SimEarth /
SimLife / Spore intellectual neighborhood by name — with a running Micropolis on the table.

### 2. Simulation humility × Will's 1996 "no hope"
Reese: a computer can't fold **one protein in one neuron** — whole-brain simulation is far away.
Will told Winograd in 1996 that simulating people was "really no hope — prop up a convincing
illusion." Same epistemic modesty, biophysicist and toymaker. What *can* honest simulation do?

### 3. The Santa Fe Institute nexus — from the boardroom
SFI **board trustee 2012–2015**. This repo *worships* SFI: it's the chaotic-cellular-automata /
feedback / complexity universal nexus, and the repo's SFI cluster all flows from there —
[Jim "Chaos" Crutchfield](../jim-crutchfield/README.md) (edge of chaos, evolving CA, video
feedback; external SFI faculty), [Owen "OG User Interface Flower Child" Densmore](../owen-densmore/README.md)
(Complex Systems Summer School → Redfish → NetLogo), and Maxis's own SFI thread documented in
Chaim's *Building SimCity*. Reese sat on the **board** of the institution the others orbit — the
complexity science story told from the governance seat.

### 3b. Earth soul — Ground and the worms
Reese's LinkedIn is a manifesto for "**living organisms as applications platforms**," an
"**internet of living things**," astrobiology, and long-range evolutionary futures. Get down to
earth: that's the same living-planet idea as the premiere's
[SimEarth ↔ Gaia ↔ the worms of Ground](../../repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/game-bridge-simearth-gaia-worms.yml)
bridge — Lovelock's self-regulating biosphere, Margulis's evolution-by-merger, Will's SimEarth,
Dave Ackley's self-regulating computational medium. A biophysicist who reads technology as biology
belongs in the segment where we resurrect the ground we walk on. **Earth soul.**

### 4. Blockchains like biology × git as provenance
His APIdays thesis: chains carry information like DNA and evolve by selection. The Repo Show's
substrate is git — a Merkle DAG of attributed provenance. Same argument, different chain.

### 5. Internet as mirror × the soul mover
His TEDx digital-twin talk meets the show's Sims character import/export: your data shadow,
made portable and yours. Will's 1996 data-portability crown jewel is the same claim about toys.

### 6. BMUG — user groups as proto-open-source
Wiring plans in the newsletter, adapters at cost, PhoneNET spun out when demand outgrew the club.
Fork-it-yourself culture before git. This repo is a BMUG newsletter that runs.

### 7. Timbuktu → Twitch, MacRecorder → podcast
Farallon shipped watch-and-drive screen sharing and Mac audio capture decades early. The Repo
Show's whole production stack is his products' grandchildren. He even holds a patent on
**telephone-controlled entertainment** (US 7539293) — TicketPR audience participation, 1990s edition.

### 8. Who makes the content — attribution on the social internet
A theme of his public feed (and his Facebook advisory decade): content provenance and authorship.
The show's answer is radical attribution — every quote sourced, every character consented, yaml
girders all the way down.

### 9. Spinoza's god = nature — theology of simulation
Another feed theme. Deus sive Natura in a repo full of microworlds: if nature computes, toy
universes are devotional instruments. Eno's generative music and Will's generative galaxies
belong in this segment.

### 10. Life Before Earth × long-range forecasts
His Singularity U talk on deep-time origins plus his personal project on long-range evolutionary
futures — the [Long Now braid](../sam-arbesman/sources/long-now-thread.md): Eno × Will 2006,
Sam 2016, git as the long now. (His 2015 complexity talk was at Fort Mason — The Interval's
neighborhood.)

### 11. DNA read/write × character read/write
Cambrian Genomics ("DNA LaserPrinter"), Genome Compiler — CAD and fabrication for living code.
SimObliterator reads/writes 25-year-old Sims souls. Data portability all the way down.

### 12. Record the talk — the archivist beat
His channels are a self-published archive of his own talks and events he thought mattered
(including Sam's book launch). Repo Show does the same with yaml and transcripts. Compare notes.
