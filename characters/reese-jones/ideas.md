## What Reese has done

**Reese Jones** — biophysicist; co-founded **BMUG** (1984); founded **Farallon** (1986 —
PhoneNET, MacRecorder, Timbuktu) → **Netopia** → Motorola; associate founder & trustee,
**Singularity University**; chairman, **Cambrian Genomics**; reported **Santa Fe Institute**
trustee; speaker on evolution, genomics, and technology as biology.

## Shared ground

Don knows Reese personally. Reese recorded and published
[Sam Arbesman's 2016 *Overcomplicated* book event](sources/overcomplicated-2016-video.md);
Chaim Gingold introduced Sam to Don in 2024 — the triangle closed itself. BMUG-era homebrew /
user-group culture is the same soil Don's SimCity/HyperLook world grew in.

## The hooks

### 1. Biology of technology × Overcomplicated × Spore
Reese's evolutionary lens, Sam's field-biologist method, Will's SimEarth/SimLife/Spore — one
argument, three dialects. Natural stack segment with Sam.

### 2. The Santa Fe Institute thread
Maxis × SFI is documented in Chaim's *Building SimCity*; Reese is reported SFI board. Complexity
science as an institution — with the repo's SFI cluster
([Owen Densmore](../owen-densmore/README.md), [Jim Crutchfield](../jim-crutchfield/README.md)).

### 3. BMUG — user groups as proto-open-source
Wiring plans published in the newsletter; adapters at cost; PhoneNET spun out when demand outgrew
the club. Fork-it-yourself culture before git existed — this repo is a BMUG newsletter that runs.

### 4. Timbuktu → Twitch
Farallon shipped watch-and-drive screen sharing decades before streaming. The Repo Show's live
format is Timbuktu's grandchild. MacRecorder → podcasting is the same joke twice.

### 5. DNA read/write × the soul mover
Cambrian Genomics ("DNA laser printer"), Genome Compiler — read/write for living things. The
show's Sims character import/export is read/write for simulated ones. Data portability all the
way down.

### 6. Long-range evolutionary forecasts × the Long Now
Reese's personal project on long-range evolutionary futures meets the repo's
[Long Now thread](../sam-arbesman/sources/long-now-thread.md) — Eno × Will 2006, Sam's 2016
seminar, git as the long now.

### 7. Record the talk — the archivist beat
His channel is a quiet archive of talks he thought mattered. Repo Show does the same with yaml
and transcripts. Compare notes on preserving conversations as primary sources.
