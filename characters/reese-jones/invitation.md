Reese —

Small world, again. I've been building a public show-and-archive around **Will Wright** — he's
accepted the premiere, so his name's on the marquee. Will and I have been friends and
collaborators for decades across a pretty wide range of stupid fun: audience-participation TV
(Bar Karma / StoryMaker), the Stupid Fun Club robots, and a lot of cellular-automata work. While
wiring up **Sam Arbesman's** room I landed on your channel — you filmed his *Overcomplicated*
launch back in 2016. Chaim Gingold introduced Sam and me in 2024, so the triangle was already
two-thirds drawn before I noticed you'd sketched the first edge.

The show runs out of a public GitHub repo — invitations, sources, transcripts, and running
simulation code (browser Micropolis, cellular automata, The Sims tooling) all forkable. I've
written up what I know about you in [README.md](README.md) and the files it links to. Correct
anything wrong, tell me what to add or cut, and say whether you're OK with it staying public.

You'd fit in more ways than one:

- **Biology of technology** — your lens, Sam's *Overcomplicated* naturalist method, and Will's
  SimEarth/SimLife/Spore are the same argument in three dialects
- **Agent-based modeling** — your *Consciousness in the Cloud* session hit artificial life,
  multi-agent systems, and complexity theory; we'll have a live agent-based city simulation
  running on the table
- **Simulation humility** — your one-protein-in-one-neuron point is almost word for word what
  Will told Terry Winograd in 1996: no hope of simulating people, so prop up a convincing
  illusion — I'd love you two on that
- **Santa Fe Institute** — Maxis's SFI thread runs through Chaim's *Building SimCity*; you know
  that institution from the inside
- **BMUG and kit culture** — wiring plans in the newsletter, adapters at cost: the user group as
  the ancestor of open source, told by the person who ran the biggest one
- **Timbuktu** — you shipped screen-sharing decades before "streaming" was a word, and this
  whole show is built on its descendants
- **Teaching computer science to kids** — open-source public education is your kind of turf (Chabot
  Space & Science Center, Edmodo), and it's a live thread here: Micropolis / SimCity in the
  classroom, Logo and Snap!, learning to think by building running worlds
- **Earth soul** — your "living organisms as applications platforms" and "internet of living things"
  land right on our living-planet segment. There's a character named **Palm** who keeps a field
  notebook on the worms of *Ground* (SimEarth ↔ Gaia ↔ soil that keeps its own books) — I think
  you'll enjoy it: [Palm on Worms — field notes](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/examples/adventure-4/pub/stage/palm-nook/study/palm-on-worms-fieldnotes.yml)
