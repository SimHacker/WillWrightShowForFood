## What Sam says (the thesis, in his words)

- Technologies — software, the internet, urban infrastructure, embedded devices — have evolved
  an almost **organic level of complexity**; even the experts who build them don't fully
  understand them (Toyota unintended acceleration, IRS systems from the Kennedy era).
- So **look to biologists**: collect specimens like the naturalists of old (bug databases as
  butterfly collections); study **pairwise interactions** like ecologists in a jungle rather
  than demanding total comprehension; build slow, iterative field understanding.
- Machine learning makes it urgent: millions of fitted parameters, powerful predictions,
  unclear insides.
- Opening line: *"We should operate as outsiders to our own programs."*
