## Recurring theses

- **Everything evolves** — networks, blockchains, genomes, and technologies are one substrate
  under natural selection; information flows like waves
- **The internet is a mirror** — your data shadow is a digital twin; treat it accordingly
- **Simulation humility** — a computer can't fold one protein in one neuron; whole-brain
  simulation is far away (his blockchain interview) — rhymes with Will's 1996 "no hope,
  prop up the illusion"
- **Agent-based modeling & artificial life** — the Netflix/Biota session is squarely the
  SimEarth/SimLife/Spore intellectual neighborhood
