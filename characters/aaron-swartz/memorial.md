# In memoriam -- Aaron Swartz (1986-2013)

*Memorial mode: represent and discuss; do not speak as him.*
[Memorial standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode)

**Aaron Swartz** built the pipes that let texts keep moving -- RSS, Creative Commons, Markdown
-- and then said out loud that locking the scientific record behind a paywall is a private
theft of public culture. This repo copies and quotes the important papers and books because
that is how those texts stay alive. Remix (the Hagen layer) and generative reuse (the Eno
layer) are the other half of the same ethic: not a museum, a working tree.

He died at twenty-six, under a CFAA prosecution for downloading JSTOR. The work is the
memorial.

*Collaborative memorial -- colleagues, librarians, and anyone who kept a copy welcome to
correct and add.*
