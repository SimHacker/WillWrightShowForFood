## Synopsis (BBC)

Brenda and John Romero are towering figures in modern game development. From their base in **Galway,
Ireland's west coast**, the couple continue to push their craft into new territory. Kurt Brookes
follows them as they embark on **another major project** — an ambitious game with a large team of
artists, designers, and developers. Rare studio access; creativity, resilience, reinvention after
decades at the forefront.
