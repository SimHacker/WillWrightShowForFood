### 8. GOLEM vs GOLEM — patents, copyleft, and the Latent Trolls thread (HOT 1 Aug 2026)

Live email thread: [sources/2026-07-30-latent-trolls.md](sources/2026-07-30-latent-trolls.md).
RMS reply in [great-quotes.yml](sources/great-quotes.yml#patents-unlike-copyright-2026).

**Setup.** Don mailed the 1984 Copyleft (L) sticker story again, then coined **Latent Office**
wordplay (Patent → Latent, Latentleft, Latent Troll) as a copyleft-for-patents joke — pie menus
kept unpatented so SimCity / Sims / Micropolis stayed free to reuse.

**RMS's correction.** Patents and copyright differ "in almost every detail"; nothing in patent law
is analogous to copyleft. Pointed at [Did You Say "Intellectual Property"?](https://www.gnu.org/philosophy/not-ipr.html)
— treat each law separately; "intellectual property" is the mirage.

**The acronym collision (discussion hook).** In that same essay RMS already proposed **GOLEMs** —
*Government-Originated Legally Enforced Monopolies* — alongside IMPs (*Imposed Monopoly
Privileges*). Don's reply pivoted to Stanisław Lem's **Golem XIV** (superintelligent war-computer
that outgrows military logic, pauses ascent to lecture humans on evolution). Two GOLEMs, one
letter, different lineage — his coinage vs Lem's philosophical SF.

**Don's concessions on air.**

- Latent Office = "bad pun in search of meaning"; don't sully **IMP** as Interface Message Processor.
- Lem curiosity quote: Golem XIV shares only intellectual curiosity with humans — "our single
  meeting point."
- Optional: GOLEM VI as the joke predecessor Don warned RMS not to ask about.

**SF-con punning — Glenn "GUB@AI" Daniels vs RMS (seek).**

Don to RMS, 1 Aug 2026 ([thread](sources/2026-07-30-latent-trolls.md)):

> I've seen you brilliantly pun in real time at science fiction convention punning
> competitions, although once I witnessed Glenn "GUB@AI" Daniels valiently give you a good
> shellacking. He had a brilliant finishing move, but it took a while to dry before anyone
> could touch it. I wish I remembered the puns, they must have been good.

**Reading the meta-joke.** "Shellacking" = a beating in a pun war; also **shellac**, a finish
that must dry before you touch it. "Finishing move" / "took a while to dry" is Don riffing on
both senses — the actual pun lines are lost, but the recap pun isn't.

**Who is Glenn?** In Don's archive, **Glen Daniels** appears as `gub@vax.ftp.com` on the 1993
`comp.object` tally (same orbit as Will/Alan Kay harvest). Handle **GUB** → Don's memory
**GUB@AI** (MIT AI Lab login style). On Don's Facebook friends list as Glen Daniels; Boston-area
technologist (WPI, Apache Axis / Sonic / Meta per public profiles — verify before naming on air).

**Other receipts.** Don to someone else: "I will always remember how well you did at the real
time punning contest at Boskone!" (`DonHopkins/temp/rms-prompts.txt`). Same file: RMS "was
spectacular in punning contests" at east-coast cons; filk dancing too
([stallman.org/doggerel.html](https://www.stallman.org/doggerel.html) — Boskone filk verses).

**Ask on air (if RMS remembers).** Which con? Which year? What was Glenn's drying finish?
