## Segment map

### 1. ITS · TECO · MACLISP — the environment

- What made ITS different from "a time-sharing system."
- TECO as the soil Emacs grew from.
- MACLISP manuals, shared code, the printer story — lab ethic before GNU.

### 2. Tourist policy & Marvin's open community

- Minsky co-founded the lab; the **tourist policy** as intentional hospitality.
- Don as ARPANET tourist — KMP as `:TEACH;LISP` mentor — parallel witnesses in **separate**
  episodes unless RMS approves a panel.
- Why openness was worth defending — and what was lost.

### 3. Remembering Marvin (memorial discussant)
