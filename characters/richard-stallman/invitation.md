# Repo Show invitation — Richard M. Stallman

*Draft — not sent. Richard M. Stallman may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time.*  
[Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

Richard —

We have known each other a long time. I would like to record a **Repo Show** conversation whose
stage is a public GitHub repository — so your words stay **in full context**, linked to primary
sources, not clipped for outrage or misquoted in a thread. You choose the shape.

**Format options (your pick):**

1. **Written Q&A first** — I send questions; you answer by email at your pace; we publish the
   exchange as the episode (optional Jitsi or landline addendum later).
2. **Jitsi Meet or BigBlueButton** — recorded to **WebM** or **Ogg** only.
3. **Landline audio** — via `rms-assist@gnu.org` if that is easier.

No Zoom, Skype, Discord, or mobile. No surprise guests on the call unless you approve them in
advance. One session, one thesis — or a short series of narrow sessions.

**What I would love to explore — your pick, any order, skip whatever bores you:**

### The MIT AI Lab you loved

- **ITS, TECO, MACLISP** — what made that environment work; what we lose when it is gone.
- **The tourist policy** — the open community Marvin Minsky catalyzed; ARPANET pilgrims; shared
  source and printed manuals; [my Medium piece on the tourist policy](https://donhopkins.medium.com/mit-ai-lab-tourist-policy-f73b77075631) as a starting point for your corrections.
- **Why the lab's collapse mattered** — the path from that culture to GNU (only if you want to go
  there).

### Remembering Marvin Minsky (memorial — discuss, not impersonate)

- Your memories of Marvin and the lab he co-founded.
- **Minsky's humor** — his take on jokes, cognitive bugs, and the unconscious; the 1980/81 paper
  *Jokes and their Relation to the Cognitive Unconscious* (AI Memo 603). I hold a digest in the
  repo for attribution; your perspective as someone who lived in that lab.
- Optional cross-link (separate episode, not this call): Henry and Margaret Minsky as family
  discussants — only with your comfort.

### Copyleft envelope — primary source on air

- The **68000 manual** I borrowed from you and mailed back in 1984.
- The **Copyleft (L)** sticker on the envelope — you adopted the term; I am glad it helped.
  Artifacts: [`copyleft-envelope-1984.yml`](../don-hopkins/media/pantomime/copyleft-envelope-1984.yml)

### Emacs — only if you want

- TECO → Emacs → GNU Emacs; extensibility as freedom.
- The **1993 thread** — kabuki-west natalism, `doctor.el` answering the flame
  ([primary source](http://www.art.net/studios/hackers/hopkins/Don/text/rms-vs-doctor.html)).
- **Unipress / Emacs wars** — only on your terms; I will not ambush you with old Usenet unless
  you ask to open that box.

**What this is not:** a debate about unrelated current politics, a multi-guest panel, or a
"gotcha" interview. The repo holds receipts; you hold the editorial veto on what airs.
