# Repo Show invitation — Richard M. Stallman

*Ready to send. Richard M. Stallman may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time.*

Richard --

We have known each other a long time. I would like to record a **Repo Show** conversation
whose stage is a public GitHub repository -- so your words stay in full context, linked to
primary sources, not clipped for outrage. You choose the shape, the medium, and the pace.

**Any medium you are happy with:** email (async Q&A), landline via `rms-assist@gnu.org`,
Jitsi Meet or BigBlueButton (WebM/Ogg only), or whatever free path you prefer. No Zoom,
Skype, Discord, or mobile. No surprise guests. You hold the editorial veto on what airs.

**Your rider and communication preferences:** we are glad to accommodate them. Tea with milk
and sugar if we ever meet in person; no spicy food; soft keyboards if typing is involved;
SSH/email for work; free recording formats only; say free software and GNU/Linux. I have
studied your speaking info packet and the rider archive at
[ddol/rre-rms](https://github.com/ddol/rre-rms) -- including the famous parrot clause
([Gizmodo summary](https://gizmodo.com/please-do-not-buy-richard-stallman-a-parrot-and-other-r-5853729)).
We will not buy you a parrot. We will not surprise you with one.

**What we *will* offer:** an ethical **virtual** parrot -- Copyleft -- that you can train by
email, voice, or any free medium you like. It can say anything you want it to say. It has
one carefully limited time-travel trick (retrognuification): when someone says "Linux"
meaning the whole operating system, it hops back ~0.4 seconds and inserts "GNU/" -- with
mandatory disclosure, and with your own bug fix already applied (it must NOT rewrite
kernel-only uses or Linux-libre). We can teach it new tricks together, within those
limits. I already wrote you about this; I would love to discuss the rider, real parrots,
and the virtual one.

**Topics -- your pick, any order, skip whatever bores you:**

### Parrots, rider, and the virtual familiar
- Why the friendly-parrot clause exists; what hosts get wrong; what they get right.
- Training Copyleft: phrases, corrections, time-travel interventions you approve.
- The copyleft name -- the 1984 sticker on the envelope that returned your 68000 manual.

### The MIT AI Lab you loved
- ITS, TECO, MACLISP -- what made that environment work.
- The tourist policy Marvin Minsky catalyzed; ARPANET pilgrims; shared source.
- Remembering Marvin -- humor, cognitive bugs, AI Memo 603 -- as memorial discussant, not impersonation.

### Optional drawers (only if you open them)
- Emacs lineage; Unipress; the 1993 doctor.el reply to kabuki-west.
- A warm "Morning Sam. Morning Ralph." reconciliation seed with James Gosling
  (MockLisp vs Emacs Lisp) -- friendship after the punch-out, not prosecution.
- Your 1989 note that the way to fix a problem is for someone who knows how to fix it
  simply to do so -- discussion as last resort.

**What this is not:** a debate about unrelated current politics, a multi-guest panel, or a
gotcha interview. The repo holds receipts; you hold the veto.
