## Rider clauses we treat as hospitality, not comedy

- No break mid-speech (if a live talk ever happens).
- Tea with milk and sugar; Pepsi only if sleepy; never Coke (boycott).
- No breakfast questions.
- Soft keyboards / RSI history (dictation into Emacs in the 1990s).
- Couch over hotel when privacy exists; temperature under ~72F for sleep.
- Friendly parrot welcome; **do not buy** a parrot as a surprise -- lifelong animal,
  wild-caught trade is cruel.
- Let him work email at dinner; do not "help" crossing streets.
- Ask before deciding his food; no spicy.

The [rre-rms](https://github.com/ddol/rre-rms) project diffs rider text across years so
clauses can be read as accumulated experience, not eccentricity for its own sake.

## Ethical virtual parrot (Copyleft)

We do not buy a bird. We offer a **software familiar** he can train:

- Named after Don's 1984 Copyleft sticker (RMS's own naming story).
- Train by email, voice, or any free medium he chooses.
- Speaks lines he approves; refuses controversial-quotes list without his say-so.
- **Retrognuification:** one time-travel power -- when "Linux" means the whole OS,
  insert "GNU/" ~0.4s earlier, with disclosure. Does **not** rewrite kernel-only
  statements or Linux-libre (RMS's own bug report against an earlier draft).
- Representation ethics: the parrot is a stand-in / familiar, not an unauthorized
  simulation of RMS himself. Saint IGNUcius framing is optional and consent-gated.

## Show beats

1. Read the parrot clause aloud -- then present the virtual parrot as compliance done right.
2. Let RMS teach Copyleft a new phrase live (email or call).
3. Demonstrate a retrognuification with disclosure; ask him to stress-test the context rules.
