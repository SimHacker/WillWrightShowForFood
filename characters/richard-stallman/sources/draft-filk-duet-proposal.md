# Filk duet proposal — Don to RMS (draft)

Status: draft for email or Repo Show segment. RMS doggerel is CC-BY 3.0;
he invites performance: [stallman.org/doggerel.html](https://www.stallman.org/doggerel.html).

## Email body (RMS mode — plain text)

Richard,

Your doggerel page says anyone may sing or perform the songs — you would be
delighted. I would like to take you up on that.

Proposal: we sing some filk together sometime — in person if Amsterdam or another
overlap works, or recorded for the Repo Show if you prefer, WebM/Ogg, no surprise
guests, questions sent in advance.

I thought we could start with "My Ronnie" — you wrote it around 1987 to the tune
of "My Bonnie" (stallman.org/doggerel.html#MyRonnie). I wrote an affectionate
counterpart filk, "My Donnie", same tune, same four-line shape, about me mail-bombing
you with copyleft stickers and bad patent puns instead of Reagan on the radio. You
can sing your verse, I sing mine, or we trade lines.

Other duet candidates from your page that fit us:

- Bardic Circle — you wrote it about Boskone filk circles; I was there too.
- Debugging — punched cards and random fixes; ITS/AI Lab soil.
- Boot It! — still true; stronger now with the last verse.
- APL — rho rho rho; short and deadly.
- I've Been Answering My Email — self-explanatory.
- Si la face ay pale — MIT tooling; problem sets; nurd anthem.
- Limerick for Limerick — you wrote it for University of Limerick (2021):
  Zoom would doom our freedom; "by Big Blue Button I'll preach." If we record
  remotely, I will use BBB or Jitsi, not Zoom.

I will not pick spicy food, will not buy you a parrot, and will not say "open source"
without correction. Tea with milk and sugar if we meet.

If you are willing, we could also invite Mitch Bradley to sing his Open Firmware
song — to the tune of The Flintstones. He wrote IEEE 1275, Sun OpenBoot, OLPC
firmware, and the song; I worked with his Forth at Sun and on OLPC SimCity.
"Hack Forth / Using Emacs on the keys" — you may appreciate that verse.

-Don

## My Donnie (draft filk by Don Hopkins)

To the tune of "My Bonnie Lies Over the Ocean" — counterpart to RMS's
[My Ronnie](https://www.stallman.org/doggerel.html#MyRonnie) (circa 1987).

Don's original. Performance OK; attribute both filks. Not published as RMS's words.

Verse A (copyleft thread):

My Donnie lies over the email.
My Donnie lies over the net.
My Donnie lies over and over —
Copyleft on the manual yet.

Verse B (Latent Trolls / Aug 2026):

My Donnie lies over the Latent.
My Donnie lies over the pun.
My Donnie lies over and over.
Patent jokes aren't copyleft, son.

Verse C (pie menus / SimCity):

My Donnie lies over the pie menu.
My Donnie lies over the grid.
My Donnie lies over and over.
SimCity went GPL, kid.

Pick one verse for a duet, or sing all three as a medley after My Ronnie.

Note on "lies": same double sense as My Ronnie — sprawls across (bonnie over the
ocean) and teasing "lies over and over" (persistent mailing / persistent punning).
Not accusing Richard of anything; Reagan target stays in his verse.

## Mitch Bradley — Open Firmware Song (Flintstones filk)

By **Mitch Bradley** (Firmworks; IEEE 1275 Open Firmware). Sung to the tune of
*"Meet the Flintstones"*. Mitch is Don's Sun-era Forth colleague (CAD plug-in, CAM6,
OLPC). Lyrics archived at [osxwiki.net](http://osxwiki.net/media/ofwsong-lyrics.txt);
also an **Open Firmware easter egg** — a Forth word `OpenFirmwareSong` prints the lyrics
then reboots (HN threads cite OLPC / Sun L1-A Forth).

**Invitation:** third voice on the filk session — Mitch sings lead; Richard and Don
harmony or call-and-response on the chorus ("We'll have an FCode time!").

```
Firmware
Open Firmware
It's the appropriate technology,
Features
FCode booting
Hierarchical DevInfo tree.

Hack Forth
Using Emacs on the keys,
Save in
NVRAM if you please.
With your
Open Firmware
You can fix the bugs in no time
Bring the kernel up in no time
We'll have an FCode time!
```

Character room: DonHopkins `characters/mitch-bradley/` (OLPC SimCity thread; Forth archive mail with Don).
Contact hint from harvest: `wmb@firmworks.com`.

**Ensemble map:** RMS filk (My Ronnie, Bardic Circle, Debugging, Boot It!) +
Mitch filk (Open Firmware Song) = Sun/MIT/Forth/Emacs/GNU triangle — same soil,
different boot ROMs.
