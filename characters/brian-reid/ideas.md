# Brian Reid — show hooks

## The 1985 laser-lovers definitive history

**March 2, 1985** — `fa.laser-lovers`, "PostScript and Interpress: a comparison." Brian was consultant to PARC during Interpress; thesis advisor **Bob Sproull** (Interpress architect with Lampson and Warnock). Saw **JAM** at Stanford (1981). 2,603 views when archived — the Hacker News of its day.

**First-order claim:** PostScript and Interpress are nearly identical vs all other page-description approaches; *differences only make sense with history.*

**Philosophical split (MOOLLM-relevant):**

> Interpress guarantees useful properties; PostScript provides the means to achieve them if you want them.

MOOLLM explicitly takes the PostScript stance (optional protection, empowerment over enforcement). Documented in MOOLLM `designs/postscript/BRIAN-REID-POSTSCRIPT-HISTORY.md` and `LINGUISTIC-MOTHERBOARD.md`.

## Warnock's linguistic motherboard

From the same historical thread (via Owen Densmore → Don):

> PostScript is a linguistic "mother board" with "slots" for "cards" — graphics first; font, networking cards considered.

## Scribe and semantic markup

Brian's **Scribe** (1980) — bold/italic as concepts, not device commands — same era as Interpress/PostScript wars; influences LaTeX/HTML thinking. Optional tangent if the show wants "documents as programs" vs "documents as data."
