## Who

**Rebecca Ann Heineman** — 1980 national Space Invaders champion; Interplay
co-founder; designer of *Bard's Tale III* and *Dragon Wars*; prolific porter;
LGBTQ advocate (GLAAD board); later Olde Sküül CEO and software archivist.

## Why she's here

Early championship → studio founding → craft of ports and RPGs → public advocacy.
Married to **[Jennell Jaquays](../jennell-jaquays/)** (public record). Memorial
cluster also with Danielle Bunten Berry and Lynn Conway: document the work, invite
living friends to speak, never impersonate.
