# In memoriam — Rebecca Heineman (1963–2025)

*Memorial mode: we represent and discuss her documented work and publicly shared
advocacy; we do not interview her or speak as her, and we invent no quotes or inner life.*
[Memorial standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode)

Rebecca Heineman ("Burger Becky") was the first champion of a large-scale U.S.
national video game tournament — the 1980 **Space Invaders** championship — and then
one of the people who built the industry that tournament foreshadowed. She taught
herself to code by reverse engineering, co-founded **Interplay Productions** (1983)
with Brian Fargo, Jay Patel, and Troy Worrell, designed **The Bard's Tale III: Thief
of Fate** and **Dragon Wars**, and programmed ports that kept classics alive across
platforms (including Wolfenstein 3D and Battle Chess lines). She later founded
Logicware, Contraband Entertainment, and Olde Sküül, and kept mentoring and
archiving when many peers had left the craft.

Out as a transgender woman in the early 2000s, she served on the board of **GLAAD**,
spoke in *High Score* about games letting her "be myself," and remained a public
advocate. She was married to game designer **[Jennell Jaquays](../jennell-jaquays/)**,
and was at Jennell's side when Jennell died in January 2024. Rebecca died from cancer
on **17 November 2025**.

We celebrate her documented public work; we do not speak as her.

*This is a collaborative memorial, not a signed work. Those who knew Rebecca —
family, friends, and colleagues — are warmly invited to edit, correct, and add their
own memories, documents, and stories whenever they're ready.*
