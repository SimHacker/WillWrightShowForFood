# Juliana "Julie" Minsky 🎬

*Invitation portrayal — NOT Julie Minsky. [Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)*

Daughter of [Marvin Minsky](../marvin-minsky/) 🕯️ and Gloria Rudisch; **twin of
[Henry](../henry-minsky/)**; sister of [Margaret](../margaret-minsky/). One of the three children of
the **first wired family** (NYT, 1996/97) — all online once the AI Lab joined the ARPAnet.

With Henry and two MIT friends — Michael Fredkin and **Brian Fox** (author of GNU Bash) — she
co-founded **Universal Access Inc.** (~1994, Santa Barbara), serving as VP of marketing for
**META-HTML**, a scripting language for building Internet and intranet applications. The twins did a
coast-to-coast "meet the Minskys" press tour to launch it.

Her *Roll 'em* homepage was the most playful of the family's pages: an enigmatic clock with links at
12, 3, 6 and 9 — daily life in Japan, the Mystical Smoking Head of 'Bob, HotWired's *Suck* — plus
postage-stamp wallpaper sketches.

**Why invited:** with Julie, all three of Marvin's children can share the memorial stage. Her
META-HTML story is also a natural prehistory beat for the [OpenLaszlo reunion](../../repo-shows/openlaszlo/)
— the web-application-language idea, eight years before LZX.

This room is deliberately small: the public record is thin by her choice. Like every portrayal here,
she may edit, expand, reduce, or delete it anytime.
