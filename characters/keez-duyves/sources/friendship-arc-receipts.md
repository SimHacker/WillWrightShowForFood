## Confirmed — Keez Duyves / PIPS:lab

### ~2009 — first meet: **Trouw** performance

**Don’s memory (2026):** first met Keez at a **PIPS:lab performance at Trouw**, not at Canvas. **Ben Cerveny** may have been there too — verify with Ben/Keez.

**8 Jun 2009 mail** (Don → Paul Asmuth, “Re: Dude!”) — same week/scene, earliest dated write-up: café **Canvas** on the **Volkskrant** roof (across from Trouw); met PIPS:lab, who regularly performed at Trouw, just after their 10-year anniversary of live video performance art. Don to Arthur van Hoff (Jaunt), 8 Mar 2017: *“Yesterday I met with Keez Duyves, of PIPS:LAB, and experienced his VR video for six people (although I was the only one, he set it up and orchestrated it so I could see what it was like).”* Called him *“an amazing genius computer artist who’s been doing 360 video for a long time”*; picked his brain on production/implementation; wanted Keez on Jaunt’s JavaScriptable 360 player.
