# 2011-07 — Owen van Dijk (dpdk) 360 streaming gig → PIPS:lab referral

*Network receipt. Don declines the freelance; recommends PIPS:lab.*
[Portrayal standards](../../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

## Ask (22 Jul 2011)

**Owen van Dijk**, Interactive Director at **dpdk** (Rotterdam), offered Don a short freelance:
Windows software for a camera that **stitches realtime spherical panoramic video** and pushes
it to a transcoding appliance over ethernet (C++ / DirectShow / OpenGL / H.264 streaming).
Coffee in Rotterdam.

## Don (22 Jul 2011)

Busy on other projects; declined. Added: that sounds like the fun kind of thing
**PIPS:LAB** could do cool stuff with.

## Owen (23 Jul 2011)

Sorry Don’s unavailable; **“I love the PIPSlab collective!”** — had invited them to talk
about their work at a conference he put together. Asked if Don knew anyone with C++ /
DirectShow / OpenGL / streaming video (H.264).

## Why it matters

By mid-2011, Don’s Amsterdam network already treated **PIPS:lab** as the local answer for
realtime panoramic / performance video — two years after first meeting Keez at Trouw, six
years before the Anyways / Jaunt conversation with Arthur.
