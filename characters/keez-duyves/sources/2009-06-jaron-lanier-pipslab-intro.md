## Context

Jaron mailed his list about a free Grace Cathedral labyrinth concert (Diana Stork + Jaron,
12 Jun 2009, San Francisco). Don was already in Amsterdam and could not attend.

Don wrote that he had met “very interesting video performance art geeks” he wanted to
introduce Jaron to — using **Quartz Composer**, yearning for something as fun and powerful
as **Body Electric**. Pointed at:

http://www.pipslab.nl

## Why it matters for Keez’s room

Same week as the Canvas / Trouw write-up to Paul Asmuth (8 Jun 2009). Don was already
trying to bridge **Amsterdam participation-video art** to **VPL / Body Electric** lineage —
years before Anyways and the Jaunt era.
