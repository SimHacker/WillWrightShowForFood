## Hooks

### 1. Die Space — show Will this first
Slogan: interactive SNS for people who already passed away — chat when you're dead. TEDx mass upload: personal LED under the seat → write given name letter-by-letter → surname initial → forehead polls (“life after death?” / “life before death?”) → ages in light → “diespace cloud.” Still index: [`sources/tedx-amsterdam-2012-diespace.md`](sources/tedx-amsterdam-2012-diespace.md). Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ApyDSq_DbQo

### 2. Participation, not observation
Urban Nation line. Crowd as instrument. Dim/shine chorusing. Drawing-field etiquette (“don't fuck it up for your neighbors”). Repo Show parallel: audience PRs vs toxic comment landfill.

### 3. Lumasol → Luma2solator
Photo long-exposure → video with *live* self-view. Why live feedback changes the art. 500k paintings as proof of scale. Kin to projection-mapped pie menus / self-revealing gesture (Don 2020 paper share).

### 4. AnywaysVR — multi-user 360 cinema
Don (2017): never seen anything like it; wanted Jaunt JS player flexible enough for Keez-class work. How one take-per-person + seamless audio weave works. What tools still don't exist.

### 5. Unity: seeds vs Island demo
Messenger jam: World-object bloom from data vs brittle instance-space demos; gaffer tape on *tapeable* objects; pie menus laid out in 3D. Keez: “a little inbetween gaffertape and messaging objects.”

### 6. Die Space ↔ Mind Mirror
MOOLLM inherits Timothy Leary’s *Mind Mirror* (1985) for personality simulation ethics. Die Space is the afterlife *community* twin — chat after death vs simulated minds. Segment: [mind-mirror SKILL](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/skills/mind-mirror/SKILL.md) beside TEDx stills.

### 7. NDSM / Parker / Will
Studio visit path via Parker (Will’s son). Amsterdam inventiveness as show texture — SO DUTCH. Optional stack with [Ben Cerveny](../ben-cerveny/) (Amsterdam public-code) and [Jared Tarbell](../jared-tarbell/) (FITC Amsterdam era).

### 8. Closing pill
TEDx closer: not heaven, not hell — diespace.nl. Repo Show punchline / credit slate.

### 9. Analytic-cubism paint-cam (Don jam)
A video camera with a motion-capture device attached so you can place the video-stream surface frames into 3D space and **paint in 3D with analytic cubism** — Die Space’s “faces + gestures → cloud” move generalized: every frame is a plane you can tilt, stack, and stroke through. Show sketch / instrument idea to jam with Keez (not a claimed PIPS product).

### 10. Massively multiplayer CV (HN 2024)
Don on raised-hand polls: nod/shake with face tracking; Die Space as the 2012 worked example. Harvest: [`sources/2024-03-hn-diespace-massively-multiplayer-cv.md`](sources/2024-03-hn-diespace-massively-multiplayer-cv.md).
