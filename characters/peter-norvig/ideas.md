## What Peter has done

Peter Norvig — Director of Research at Google and one of the most widely read teachers in AI. With Stuart Russell he wrote *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (AIMA), the standard AI textbook; he also wrote *Paradigms of Artificial Intelligence Programming* (PAIP, 1992), a deep tour of AI techniques in Common Lisp. Earlier he led the Computational Sciences Division at NASA Ames. In 2011 he and Sebastian Thrun taught a free online AI course to ~160,000 students — an early spark of the modern MOOC wave. Essayist: [*Teach Yourself Programming in Ten Years*](https://norvig.com/21-days.html), [*Python for Lisp Programmers*](https://norvig.com/python-lisp.html), [*How to Write a Spelling Corrector*](https://norvig.com/spell-correct.html), [*I've Consed Every Pair*](https://medium.com/@peternorvig/ive-consed-every-pair-54ef5d9d93b6), the *Gettysburg PowerPoint* parody. Don's friend; writing an AI book and offered a chapter on Don's work.
