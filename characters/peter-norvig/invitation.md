Peter —

Remember the thread we had going last summer? [I've Consed Every Pair](https://medium.com/@peternorvig/ive-consed-every-pair-54ef5d9d93b6) and Vint Cerf's RFC1121 poetry, the Pervert's Guide to Computer Programming Languages, the Sims 4 synthetic-data vision models we built for Leela, the agents demonstrating chat and gossip protocols, Alan Cypher being the first to break the frame (with Brad scolding him for breaking the fourth wall), Rocky the Pet Rock's cult following, and McLuhan and Lakoff as constitutionally influential characters who designed their own protocols. **I'd love to give you the demo we talked about — the Vulcan Screen Meld — and turn the
conversation into an episode. Or several.**

and writings — the Ten Years essay, PAIP, whatever you choose — and watch it hold Coffee Talk
with the others. You see the portrayal standards, the consent machinery, and the puppetry all the
way down. Then we talk about whatever it stirs up. The whole stage is [this public GitHub repository](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood) — artifacts, portrayal standards, consent machinery, all browsable without an account.

**Episode directions, all yours to pick from:**

authoring and *teaching* AI behavior by building shareable things. 
- MOOLLM's extensions to Anthropic skills.

I'd love to bring in **Henry Minsky** and other **Leela** colleagues — Society-of-Mind ideas alive in modern AI is right in the middle of all this.
