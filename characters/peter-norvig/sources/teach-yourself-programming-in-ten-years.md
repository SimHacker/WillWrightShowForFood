# Teach Yourself Programming in Ten Years — Peter Norvig (2001)

**Author:** [Peter Norvig](https://norvig.com/21-days.html) · **Also known as:** 21 Days / Ten Years essay

---

## The hook

Deliberate practice beats "Teach Yourself X in 24 Hours" hype. Peter's thesis: nobody becomes Mozart
in three weeks; programming mastery takes about **ten years** of serious work — and the click-bait
URL (`21-days.html`) is itself the punchline.

## Why Don loves it

Don forwarded this to Paul in 2009 with the line: "Hang in there! 10 years will go by really fast if
you're having fun programming!" It became a touchstone for the Repo Show's constructionist frame:
skills and microworlds are what you practice *with*, not shortcuts around the work.

## Show hooks

- **Are MOOLLM skills** the constructionist artifacts we practice with for the next decade?
