## The hook

Peter met a hacker excited about Clojure who seemed to know Peter only as a Python programmer, not as
someone with fifteen years of Lisp as a primary language. He wrote a **Johnny Cash-style road song**
listing Franz Lisp, MacLisp, Emacs Lisp, Symbolics, Racket, R7RS, and every `cons` in the land.

Don posted it to Hacker News; Daniel compared it to Guy Steele's AI Lab hacker songs of the late 1970s.

## Show hooks

- **Perform it on air** — or ask Peter to read a verse.
- **The Lisp/Python identity joke** as warm-up before a serious MOOLLM skills conversation.
- **Pair with Don's XML song** ("Twenty Twenty Twenty Four Escapes to Go") as the Repo Show's musical interlude tradition.
