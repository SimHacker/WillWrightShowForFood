## Worm pipeline Don described

Worms as cursors — even dual cursors like selections (point and mark). Input mouth (dot), output
anus (mark): `eat()`, `transform()`, `poop()`, move forward. Chain worms; worms within worms.
Castings return home through a wormhole so they don't leave poop on the data they're eating
(nondestructive by default). Write-only numbered `.001` `.002` `.003` output files for batch
processes — map/reduce agents or pipes. "It's like save-excursion in Emacs!"

Tagline: **"Worm Pipelines: We're Better Than Boring, We're WORMS!"**
