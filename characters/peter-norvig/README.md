## Who

**Peter Norvig** is Director of Research at Google and one of AI's most widely read teachers. With
Stuart Russell he wrote *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* — the textbook a large share of
the world's AI students learned from — and on his own he wrote *Paradigms of Artificial Intelligence
Programming* (PAIP, 1992), a deep, hands-on tour of classic AI in Common Lisp. Before Google he led
the Computational Sciences Division at NASA Ames. In 2011, with Sebastian Thrun, he taught a free
online AI course to roughly 160,000 students — one of the sparks of the modern MOOC era.

He is also an essayist whose short pieces have shaped programming culture:
[*Teach Yourself Programming in Ten Years*](https://norvig.com/21-days.html) (deliberate practice
beats "learn X in 24 hours" hype — the URL is the joke),
[*Python for Lisp Programmers*](https://norvig.com/python-lisp.html),
[*How to Write a Spelling Corrector*](https://norvig.com/spell-correct.html),
[*I've Consed Every Pair*](https://medium.com/@peternorvig/ive-consed-every-pair-54ef5d9d93b6)
(Johnny Cash meets every REPL in the land),
and the *Gettysburg PowerPoint* parody.

## Why a Repo Show

Peter is a friend of Don's. He's **writing a book about AI** and has offered to include a **chapter
about what Don is doing** — which makes a Repo Show a perfect two-way fit: a recorded conversation
that's fun on its own and useful as source material for his chapter.

In January 2026 Peter and Don were already aligned on the **worm/Ground** architecture before
Palm's field expedition made it public: scruffy AI on a neat substrate, Ground as a character,
worms as map/reduce cursors, *The Ground Truth* as the ML publication. Palm's
[field notebook](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/examples/adventure-4/pub/stage/palm-nook/study/palm-on-worms-fieldnotes.yml)
is the friendly invitation to read that lore over Palm's shoulder.

The topic is **his to choose** — [MOOLLM](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm), skills, worms,
or AI in general — and Don would love to bring in **Leela colleagues** like
[Henry Minsky](../henry-minsky/README.md). The *Ten Years* essay is a natural frame: what does
**deliberate practice** mean in an age of LLMs?
