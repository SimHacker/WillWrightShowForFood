# Maurice Wilkes — show hooks & threads

*Memorial mode: prompts for a remembrance **about** him, not an interview of him.*

## Segment hooks

- **The statute story.** Heinz Lemke's first-hand account of Wilkes invoking an old
  Cambridge statute to admit a university-less, unfunded mathematician to a PhD —
  told on air by Heinz. The human root of PIXIE.
- **EDSAC as a *usable* machine.** Wilkes' instinct that a computer is only as good
  as what you can get people to do with it: subroutine libraries, the first
  programming textbook, time-sharing on Titan. Direct ancestor of this show's
  "run the old code" premise.
- **Microprogramming & caches.** How two of his ideas quietly became universal.
- **The Titan and the PDP-7 link.** The distributed setup (Wiseman's data-link) that
  PIXIE ran on — bridge to the [Lars Brinkhoff emulation segment](../../repo-shows/pixie-pie-menus-pdp7/README.md).
