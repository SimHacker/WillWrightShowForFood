# Repo Show invitation — Brian Harvey 🐢

*Public draft — not sent. Brian Harvey may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.yml)

**Today I'm sending two matching invitations** — yours and **Jens Mönig's** ([Jens's invitation](../jens-monig/invitation.md)) — for one **pair show**. Each letter is written for the person receiving it; both describe the same show from your two angles, and each of you can read the other's.

Brian —

I'd love to have you on a **Repo Show** — a friendly, low-friction conversation whose stage is a public
GitHub repo people can clone, run, and build on. You and **Jens** are both invited; I've written him his
own letter alongside this one ([Jens's invitation](../jens-monig/invitation.md)). You two aren't a couple —
but you're **colleagues joined at the heart** by Snap!, so the **default is a pair show** —
[`snap-logo-brian-jens`](../../repo-shows/snap-logo-brian-jens/README.md) — telling the shared
Snap! / Logo / BJC story once, together. **No synchronized sitting required** — two one-on-ones at each of
your best times, woven together in post, is completely fine.

**What I'd love to draw out from you:**

- **The books** — *Computer Science Logo Style* (3 vols) and *Simply Scheme* (with Matthew Wright, foreword
  by Hal Abelson): teaching real computer science through Logo and Scheme.
- **Berkeley Logo** and a lifetime of Logo for learners — the through-line from Papert's turtle to today.
- **The Beauty and Joy of Computing** — the CSforALL curriculum your 2024 Karlstrom award (with Dan Garcia)
  recognized: reaching kids who were never invited to computing before.
- **Snap!'s pedagogy** — why first-class everything matters for *beginners*, not just theorists; the design
  and documentation you brought to what Jens builds.
- **Optional:** **Micropolis × Snap!** in the classroom — an open, modifiable city sim (and CAM6 cellular
  automata) reprogrammed from blocks (from our 2018 thread).
