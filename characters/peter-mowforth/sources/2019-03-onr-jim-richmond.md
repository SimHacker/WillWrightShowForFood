# 2019-03 — Jim Richmond / ONR double-pay story

**From:** Peter Mowforth → Don Hopkins (18 Mar 2019), HyperLook source-code hunt thread (Cc Arthur, Tim, Doug Scoular).

Context: Don seeking HyperLook source; Jim Richmond was said to have kept tapes in his garage; Pete: "Jim died. Worth a try." Then the story.

## Story (Pete's telling)

1. Large US military delegation visited Turing about a Personnel Management System — wined, dined, happy return to Washington.
2. Weeks later, another bunch from **US Office of Naval Research** — same hospitality; team puzzled ("assumed they liked rain and golf").
3. Ridiculously expensive proposal sent.
4. Panic call to Jim from Washington: "serious problem with this proposal" — Jim expected the worst.
5. Problem: *two* Washington groups each expected to pay for the project. Near end of financial year — spend the money or lose it. Suggestion: **double the numbers**. Done. Everyone happy — especially Jim.
6. Bummer: paid in dollars, didn't convert to pounds when £1 ≈ $1; later ~£1 = $2; salaries in pounds → "unexpected bonus" evaporated.
7. Punchline: project ran short; finished with cheap programming help from **Soviet researchers** then at Turing. "Just as well the guys in Washington never found out."
