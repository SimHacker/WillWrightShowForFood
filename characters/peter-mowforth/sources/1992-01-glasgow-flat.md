# The Glasgow flat — January 1992

*A 33-year-old email surfaced 29 July 2026 when Don forwarded it back to Pete Mowforth and
Arthur van Hoff. Turing Institute domestic lore, verbatim.*

**From:** Arthur van Hoff `turing.ac.uk!arthur@cygnus.com` — **To:** `don@toad.com` —
**10 January 1992:**

> Hi Don,
>
> We have found some accomodation for you. There is a flat near the center of Glasgow which
> you can rent starting in february. You might have to share with Marcel (a student and
> working for the UI group). The rent will be 250-300 pounds. It is a small but very nice
> flat.
>
> The flat is owned by Jill, the wife of Pete Mowforth (one of our directors). She is
> looking for a buyer for the flat so you might have to move out at a months notice. It is
> also important the flat is kept tidy since potential buyers will want to see it now and
> then. Let me know what you think.
>
> Have fun,

Don's 2026 reply asks for the address to retrace his walk to work down **Sauchiehall
Street**, past the **Glasgow School of Art** (whose library has tragically burnt twice
since), and remembers the Tank Girl themed **"Tankin'"** club at the art school student
union — "great music and fun to hang out with freaky artists. I'll always have great
Glasgow memories!"
