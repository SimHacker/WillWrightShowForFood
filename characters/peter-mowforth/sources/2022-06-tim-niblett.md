# 2022-06 — Pete announces Tim Niblett's death

**Canonical memorial room:** [`../../tim-niblett/`](../../tim-niblett/)

Pete mailed the Turing orphans (13 Jun 2022): Tim died of cancer Sunday **5 June 2022**, aged **66**. Father Bryan, sister Mary, stepmother Daisy said goodbye. Funeral 29 Jun 2022, South Oxfordshire Crematorium near Wantage. Contact Mary Blackmore.

Pete's framing (paraphrase): Tim's original thinking, project success, and research direction were numerous; he rarely took credit for innovations that were his. Kept illness quiet; hoped to meet again once medication worked — didn't.
