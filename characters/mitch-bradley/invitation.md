# Repo Show invitation — Mitch Bradley

*Mitch may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time — or take direct edit access. [Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md) — also covers quiet mode and onboarding.*
**Subject:** Your PDP-7 Forth, running, and a show about forty years of Forth

---

Mitch —

Thank you again for the PDP-7 Forth. It runs in the cabinet now, drawing with the turtle on the
Type 340, and it has become the language I use to put new devices on the machine:

- [Your Forth, live, with the turtle](https://hyperties.org/cabinet/forth/)
- [The Ultimate Machine](https://hyperties.org/cabinet/ultimate/), written in your Forth: a box
  whose only function is to turn itself off
- [ULTIMATE HAND](https://hyperties.org/cabinet/ultimate-hand/): your webcam hand becomes the
  Ultimate Machine's hand, through a DataHand device on the PDP-7
  ([ultimate-hand.fs](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/packages/cabinet/tapes/pdp7forth/ultimate-hand.fs))
- [The Turing engine](https://hyperties.org/cabinet/turing/), running Minsky's universal machine
  and Christopher Cherniak's busy beavers, driven from Forth
- [PIXIE's ring structures built from Forth](https://hyperties.org/databases/pixie/cabinet-forth/)

The write-up of how it came about, with your notes on the design, is
[PDP7-FORTH.md](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/packages/cabinet/reference/PDP7-FORTH.md).
There's also a [23-minute tour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lo8kdY-5i6c) of the cabinet, which
ends with your Forth drawing on the 340.

**Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the [premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md) and more.**

I'd like to invite you to a **Repo Show**: a recorded conversation whose stage is a public GitHub
repository, where the audience can run what we talk about. I'd like to do one about your Forths,
from Sun to the PDP-7:

1. Sun Forth for hardware diagnostics, and how it became OpenBoot and Open Firmware. The device
   tree outlived the Forth that defined it.
2. The summer of 1987: CADroid, pie menus and Forth under NeWS, and how you came to suggest the
   name "pie menu."
3. Writing a Forth in a day with Claude: what it got right, what you had to steer, and why the
   PDP-7 turned out to be a good target, down to the three-instruction NEXT.
4. The OLPC XO firmware, and putting a Forth prompt in front of a child's laptop.
5. What to build next on the PDP-7 in Forth: a metacompiler, Open Firmware's device tree for the
   cabinet's devices, or something stranger.

We could do it live, coding in the cabinet, or as a relaxed conversation. Remote is fine. No
preparation needed.

— Don
