# In memoriam — Jennell Jaquays (1956–2024)

*Memorial mode: we represent and discuss her documented work and publicly shared
advocacy; we do not interview her or speak as her, and we invent no quotes or inner life.*
[Memorial standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode)

Jennell Jaquays made non-linear adventure design into a verb. Her Judges Guild
modules **Dark Tower** and **Caverns of Thracia** (1979) taught generations of
referees to build dungeons with loops, multiple entrances, and real choice —
**Jaquaysing** the map. When *Dungeon* magazine listed the thirty greatest D&D
adventures for the game's 30th anniversary, *Dark Tower* was the only entry not
published by TSR.

She co-founded *The Dungeoneer*, illustrated widely (including the cover of TSR's
*Dragon Mountain*), converted arcade classics for Coleco, and later designed levels
for **Age of Empires**, **Quake II**, **Quake III Arena**, and **EVE Online**. She
was also a public transgender rights advocate (including work with the Transgender
Human Rights Institute in Seattle and support for Leelah's Law). She was married to
fellow game developer **[Rebecca Heineman](../rebecca-heineman/)** ("Burger Becky"),
who was at her side when she died and who is also remembered in this repo (2025).
After Jennell's death, the community's **Return to Perinthos** memorial game jam /
megadungeon supported her family and Trans Lifeline.

Friend-phone tribute clips from colleagues are welcome when people who knew her
choose to share them.

We celebrate her documented public work; we do not speak as her.

*This is a collaborative memorial, not a signed work. Those who knew Jennell —
family, friends, and colleagues — are warmly invited to edit, correct, and add their
own memories, documents, and stories whenever they're ready.*
