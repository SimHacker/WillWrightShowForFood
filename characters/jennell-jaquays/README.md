## Who

**Jennell Allyn Jaquays** — tabletop RPG designer and artist (*Dark Tower*,
*Caverns of Thracia*); video game level designer (Age of Empires, Quake II/III,
EVE Online); transgender rights advocate. Died 2024-01-10 from complications of
Guillain–Barré syndrome.

## Why she's here

Spatial design that trusts players with **choice** — the same grain as good
simulation toys and open worlds. Memorial adjacency to Danielle Bunten Berry and
Lynn Conway: public pioneers whose work and advocacy belong in the show's ethical
spine. Colleague memories (especially Quake-era and Judges Guild) welcome when
offered.
