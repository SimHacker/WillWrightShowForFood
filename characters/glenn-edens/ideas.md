## 1. GRiD Compass — the first laptop that mattered

**1982 GRiD Compass** — clamshell, magnesium alloy, **Dynabook-inspired** (Alan Kay lineage). Pre-IBM PC portable wave; NASA shuttle use; niche military/field — but Glenn stresses **~75% sold to banks, audit firms, engineering, oil exploration**, not primarily military.

**Smalltalk on GRiD:** real 8086 16-bit; Kay and Ingalls both played with it — "pretty good for the day"; Alto II slightly faster.

**Customers (stories):**
- **Steve Jobs** — first GRiD (a bet)
- **William F. Buckley** — second
- **Larry Ellison** — sailing race computer

CHM profile: https://computerhistory.com/profile/glenn-edens/

## 3. The 1982 mouse crisis → Lisa

Commercial mouse **unavailable** in 1982 — only **Tat Lam** manufacturing; production went to **Xerox**. Glenn **"appropriated"** a mouse from **PARC** for the **Apple Lisa** (Xerox was an Apple investor — tacit permission). **Trip Hawkins** also tells this story.
