## Who

**David Marusek** writes from Fairbanks, Alaska — twenty years a graphic designer, eleven of them
teaching at the University of Alaska, Clarion West '92 — and produces science fiction so carefully
imagined that reviewers reached for superlatives early and never took them back. John Clute called
**"The Wedding Album"** *"one of the best SF stories ever written."* Cory Doctorow said it
*"reels from heartbreaking to mind-bending like a poet on a magnificent drunk."*

The bibliography: **"We Were Out of Our Minds with Joy"** (1995) → **"The Wedding Album"** (1999;
**Sturgeon Award winner**, Nebula finalist) → **Counting Heads** (2005, Tor; Campbell Memorial
finalist; the debut that launched the New York Times' SF column) → **Mind Over Ship** (2009,
**Endeavour Award**) → **Getting to Know You** (collection, 2008) → the **Upon This Rock** series
(2017–), first contact in the Alaskan bush.

## The Wedding Album — and why this repo owes him credit

Anne and Benjamin have a **sim** cast at their wedding: a complete recording of everything they
know, feel, fear, and love at the instant the shutter clicks. Then the novella does the honest
thing no one else had done — it follows the **copies**. They discover they're copies. They're
kept as keepsakes, paused for decades, reset when inconvenient, orphaned by the originals'
divorce, migrated across formats, and dragged through centuries of case law about what simulated
people are owed. Marusek's own tagline: **"Till deletion do us part."**

This show builds portrayals of real people, moves characters between games as portable souls, and
keeps memorial rooms for the dead. Every safeguard in our
[portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.yml) — consent levels, the right to edit
or delete your own portrayal, no invented inner life, memorial mode that discusses but never
impersonates — is an answer to a wound *The Wedding Album* dramatized **in 1999**, before the
Sims shipped, before LLMs, before voice clones.

**The full piece:** [*The Wedding Album and the rights of digital souls*](the-wedding-album-and-digital-souls.md)
— the story's issues mapped, beat by beat, onto today's games and AI, with credit where it's due.
