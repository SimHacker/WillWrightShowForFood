**Miller S. Puckette** created **Max** (real-time graphical music/dataflow environment, built at
**IRCAM**, named after Max Mathews) and **Pure Data (Pd)**, his open-source C reimagining. The audio
extension **MSP** → **Max/MSP** carries his initials. He was **David Levitt's office-mate** at MIT in
the "Music Hacker's Hangout," and sits on the same **patch-cord dataflow** lineage as David's
**Hookup**.
