## Kay's side-note

Alan was a grad student at **University of Utah** from end of **1966** through **September 1969**.

Toward the end of that period, **Andrew Armit** visited for **several months, if not longer** —
with his **Coons patch interactive system**. Kay was *"quite in awe of what he was able to do on
that."* Believes Armit worked on a **PDP-7**.

This was the **"Ivan" period** — Sutherland and Harvard grad students venturing to Salt Lake
(*"pretty much in the middle of nowhere!"*).

### 4×4 matrix multiplier

The proposed **4×4 matrix multiplier** was originally intended for the **head-mounted display**,
but could also be used for **Coons patch** work. Kay forgets how it finally turned out — he
received his PhD in **1969** and left for **Stanford**.
