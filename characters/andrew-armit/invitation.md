## Why you

**Alan Kay** wrote on that thread (10 July 2026) that you were one of the best extended visitors at
**Utah** when he was a grad student — months with a **Coons patch** system on a **PDP-7**, work he
was *"quite in awe"* of. He asked us to **find the film**. Your reply (16 July) is the best lead we've
had in years: you still have the **octal listings**, the manuals, notes from when the film was made,
and the memory of showing it on a US tour with **Henri Gouraud**.

**Heinz Lemke** names your Cambridge graphics as another example of work the American textbooks
never properly saw — the same week Roy Eagleson's students at UWO want to **re-implement PIXIE** on
a real PDP-7 emulator. Your **Multipatch** archive is the natural second demo: same machine class,
same era, different genius.

And then there's the line you gave Lloyd's Register management when you rewrote the **ICON** proposal
over a weekend to be fully **3D**:

> *"You have to understand that while you are paying for it, I am making it for myself."*

Alan answered that email calling you the definition of *"single minded and dedicated."* We'd like
that exchange on record — not as legend, but with the barn door open and the listings on screen.

Your public site ([Andrew Armit Designs](http://armit.com/)) traces the same through-line we care
about: the **1966 oscilloscope** that bootstrapped its own x-axis scan, interactive **3D CAD** when
the field was new, **picoamp** electron amplifiers, and still building — **TAKT**, *Mind My Machine*.
We sourced it respectfully here: [`sources/armit-com-profile.md`](sources/armit-com-profile.md).

## What I'm asking

One or more **recorded conversations** (video call, your schedule, your pace):

1. **Walk the barn** — Multipatch, Multiobject, ICON, TAG, PGM: what survives, what should be scanned
   first, what the **Multipatch film** was.
2. **Utah ~1968–69** — Coons patches, PDP-7 constraints, what Alan and Ivan's lab felt like from
   inside.
3. **Simulation path** — how your octal listings could meet **Lars Brinkhoff**'s PDP-7 emulator and
   Roy's students (optional: **CARS 2027 Berlin**, 29 June — Heinz proposed an HCI session).
4. **Optional:** *Mind My Machine* and the 1966 CRT story — if you want that human arc on air.

**Async is fine.** Record a segment when convenient; we assemble with scans and emulator footage.
Heinz mentioned visiting near Cambridge — if timing works, a two-person segment with him would be
wonderful, not required.
