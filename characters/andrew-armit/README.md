# Andrew Armit 📐 *(Cambridge 3D CAD · PDP-7 graphics · hardware design)*

*Portrayal of a real correspondent, written by Don — not Andrew, and not his words. Andrew may

## Who

**Andrew Armit** — Cambridge **Mechanical Sciences** (1965); PhD on **interactive 3D CAD** when
the field barely existed. Graphics pioneer on the Cambridge **PDP-7** stack (**Multipatch**,
**Multiobject** + **Titan**); **ICON** with [**Heinz Lemke**](../heinz-lemke/) for Lloyd's Register
(ships, oil rigs, NASTRAN data); **TAG** for aerospace clients; **Parallel Graphics Machine**. Extended
visitor at **University of Utah** (~1968–69) — Coons-patch interactive CAD that left **Alan Kay**
*"quite in awe."*

Today: [**Andrew Armit Designs**](http://armit.com/) — signal amplifiers, electron microscopy
hardware, environmental sensors, and consumer products including the [**TAKT**](https://www.takt-metronome-watch.co.uk/)
metronome watch (gait metronome). Autobiography ***Mind My Machine***. Lives near Cambridge.

Public profile digest: [`sources/armit-com-profile.md`](sources/armit-com-profile.md) ·
[`sources/takt-metronome-watch-profile.md`](sources/takt-metronome-watch-profile.md)

## Why he's here

**Alan Kay** (10 Jul 2026, PIXIE thread) asked Don and Heinz to find **film of Andrew's Utah system**
— *"quite impressive."* **Heinz** named Armit's Coons-patch work as another case of Cambridge graphics
**ignored in the US canon**.

**16 Jul 2026** — Andrew replied on the same thread with a **barn archive manifest**: full **octal
listings** for Multipatch and Multiobject, PDP-7/340 manuals, **Titan** extracode, **ICON** sources,
confirmation that a **Multipatch film** existed (shown on a US tour with **Henri Gouraud**), plus
**TAG**, **VG3400** hardware, and a **Parallel Graphics Machine** that still runs with video.

Alan (same day): *"If anyone is qualified to define 'single minded and dedicated', it would most
certainly be you!"*

**29 Jul 2026** — architecture note: **Multipatch** ran entirely on the PDP-7 (~4K, no Titan,
ASR-33 language, paper tape); **Multiobject** used Titan like PIXIE (16K→24K, 100-word link).
Andrew's corollary: Multipatch is the **easier recreation** — thesis command sequence + miniature
screenshots as the acceptance test. Digest:
[`sources/2026-07-29-multipatch-vs-multiobject.md`](sources/2026-07-29-multipatch-vs-multiobject.md).

## The ICON line

When Lloyd's Register wanted a 2D system, Andrew refused — rewrote the proposal over a **weekend**
to be fully **3D** (PDP-11/45 + Series 3 Vector General), and told upper management:

> *"You have to understand that while you are paying for it, I am making it for myself."*

Structured beat: [`sources/icon-lrs-3d-proposal.yml`](sources/icon-lrs-3d-proposal.yml)

## 1966 → 2026 — one builder

The [TAKT About page](https://www.takt-metronome-watch.co.uk/about) tells how a **50µA army-surplus
meter** became a multimeter, then a **self-bootstrapping 3-inch CRT oscilloscope** (1966) — the spot
on screen generating the x-axis scan used to finish the scope. Same person later squeezed interactive
3D graphics onto a **PDP-7**, then designed amplifiers for **half a picoamp** electron currents with
**8 million-to-one** gain range.
