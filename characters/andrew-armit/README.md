# Andrew Armit

**Status:** stub · **Consent:** not_yet_asked · **Invitation:** not_offered

Cambridge graphics visitor at **University of Utah** (~1968–1969) — **Coons patch** interactive
system on **PDP-7**, during the **Ivan Sutherland** period. Remembered by **Alan Kay** (grad
student Utah 1966–1969) as one of the best and most unusual extended visitors.

## Why he's here

Alan Kay (10 Jul 2026, PIXIE thread) asked Don and Heinz to **sniff out film/video** of Armit's
system working — *"quite impressive."* Parallel to PIXIE: another PDP-7 graphics story the US canon
under-documented. Heinz: *"Andrew Armit's graphic system on Coon's patches is another example"* of
Cambridge-area work ignored in American textbooks.
