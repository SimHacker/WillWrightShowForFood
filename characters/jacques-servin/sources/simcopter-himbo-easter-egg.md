# SimCopter himbo easter egg -- source dossier

The event: Maxis programmer Jacques Servin inserted an easter egg into SimCopter (1996) that
filled cities with men in swim trunks, holding hands and kissing, on his birthday, his
boyfriend's birthday, and Friday the 13th. A trigger bug made them appear far more often than
intended. Discovered after ship; Servin fired late 1996; content patched out. ACT UP called a
boycott; Servin called it "absurd." RTMark claimed sponsorship (as culture jamming). Servin
became Andy Bichlbaum of The Yes Men.

## Why it matters here

Don's Draft 3 review of The Sims (7 Aug 1998) cites "the SimCopter fiasco and the lip service
that Maxis publically gave in response about not being anti-gay" as the reason the team would
be expected to do better -- and references "the guys in SimCopter" in the proposed romance
model. See [same-sex-relationships-the-record.md](../../patrick-j-barrett-iii/same-sex-relationships-the-record.md)
and [same-sex-design-arc.yml](../../don-hopkins/sources/qgcon-inclusivity-paper/same-sex-design-arc.yml)
(`simcopter_context`).

Framing note: the two stories are complementary, not equivalent. Servin's egg was unauthorized
visibility that cost him his job; The Sims' same-sex romance was reviewed, promised in writing,
and shipped. Back door, then front door.
