## The himbo easter egg

In 1996, Servin inserted an easter egg into Maxis's **SimCopter**: on certain dates -- his
birthday, his boyfriend's birthday, and Friday the 13th -- the game's cities filled with men in
swim trunks, holding hands and kissing. Partly a protest over working conditions, later framed
as an [RTMark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTMark)-sponsored act of culture jamming. A trigger
bug made the himbos show up far more often (and in far greater numbers) than intended, players
noticed after ship, and Servin was fired in late 1996.

**ACT UP** called for a boycott of Maxis until he was rehired. Servin called the boycott
["absurd"](https://archive.wired.com/culture/lifestyle/news/1996/12/915) and had no desire to go
back. Maxis patched the content out -- and publicly insisted it was not anti-gay.

As **Andy Bichlbaum**, Servin went on to co-found **The Yes Men**, the culture-jamming duo
behind the 2004 BBC Dow/Bhopal hoax and the Yes Men films. The easter egg is a canonical entry
in the [LGBTQ Video Game Archive](https://lgbtqgamearchive.com/2015/08/31/gay-sims-easter-egg-and-controversy-in-simcopter/).

## Why he belongs in this repo

Don's **7 August 1998 Draft 3 review** of The Sims cites the episode by name, in writing:

> "We are going to be expected to do better than that after the **SimCopter fiasco** and the
> lip service that Maxis publically gave in response about not being anti-gay."

...and even uses him as a data point in the proposed romance model: "monosexual homosexual
(like **the guys in SimCopter**)". The fiasco is part of why written, front-door inclusion won
in The Sims. Full context: [the record](../patrick-j-barrett-iii/same-sex-relationships-the-record.md).

**The arc in one line:** 1996, queer representation goes in through the back door as an easter
egg and gets its author fired. 1998-2000, it goes in through the front door -- flagged in
design review, promised in writing, implemented by [Patrick Barrett](../patrick-j-barrett-iii/),
approved by Will -- and ships on day one.

## Show beat: the back door and the front door

A prequel segment for [*Did The Sims Make You Gay?*](../../repo-shows/did-the-sims-make-you-gay/README.md):
same company, same years, two ways representation gets into a simulation -- sabotage vs design
-- and what each one cost. Servin's voice would make it sing; the story stands on the published
record either way.
