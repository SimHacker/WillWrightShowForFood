## The bug, diagnosed

`research.htm` was last modified in 2004 and its three door links point at
the old Santa Fe Institute URLs — `www.santafe.edu/~cmg`, `~dynlearn`,
`~evca` — which now 404 (redirecting through `sites.santafe.edu` to
nothing). **The doors moved to Davis:** working copies live on Jim's own
current server, and his newer pages' navbar already links to them:

| Door | Dead (2004 link) | Alive |
|------|------------------|-------|
| #1 Computational Mechanics | santafe.edu/~cmg | [csc.ucdavis.edu/~cmg](http://csc.ucdavis.edu/~cmg) |
| #2 Dynamics of Learning | santafe.edu/~dynlearn | [csc.ucdavis.edu/~dynlearn](http://csc.ucdavis.edu/~dynlearn) |
| #3 Evolving Cellular Automata | santafe.edu/~evca | [csc.ucdavis.edu/~evca](http://csc.ucdavis.edu/~evca) |

Follow-up material: the fix is a three-line edit to a 2004 page — a
LOCKSS-flavored teaching moment, and a natural on-air beat for the show's
archival thread (Don found the doors *by walking around the building*).

## Facts verified

- **Internet Archive Europe HQ:** Oudeschans 16, 1011 KZ Amsterdam — a
  17th-century canal house on the city's old eastern rampart, opened
  September 19, 2025, with Brewster Kahle presiding. Reading room hours,
  lectures, meetups.
  [IA Europe's own reflection](https://www.internetarchive.eu/2025/09/22/oudeschans-old-ramparts-and-new-guardians-reflections-on-the-internet-archive-europe-open-house/)
- **The Seventh Sally** — the Cyberiad story where Trurl builds a
  miniature kingdom-in-a-box for the deposed tyrant Excelsius, and the
  question of whether simulated subjects suffer becomes unavoidable. The
  Cyberiad's Electronic Bard already anchors the
  [Rhetoric Organ](../../don-hopkins/rhetoric-organ-semantic-modulator-keyboard.md);
  the Seventh Sally is its ethics-of-simulation sibling — and the obvious
  bridge from Jim's evolving CA to The Sims.
- **The boat, again:** Ben Cerveny took Don out on the canals in August 2026
  and they stopped at the IA Europe dock on the Oudeschans to pick up more
  riders — seven years after the [2019 boat ride](../don-and-jim-history.md)
  that Jim was on. The hook: come back to Amsterdam, the boat now stops at
  the Internet Archive.
