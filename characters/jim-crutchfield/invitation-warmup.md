# Jim — how all the trails led to your door 🦋

*Jim may edit or delete anything here, anytime.
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md).*

Jim —

You might reasonably ask what I was doing rummaging around your UC Davis
pages this week. The honest answer: **everything I've been writing lately
kept leading to you**, from three different directions at once, and I finally
followed the trails to their common endpoint. Here they are.

## Trail 1: feedback loops as instruments

I've been designing a performance instrument — a chorded keyboard of
rhetorical figures that steers a language model *while it generates*, organ
stops for prose — and cataloging its ancestors: Alvin Lucier's "I Am Sitting
in a Room," Steve Reich's tape loops, EBN's VuJak video sampler. Cataloging
feedback loops without arriving at **"Space-Time Dynamics in Video Feedback"**
turns out to be impossible: yours is the paper that took the loop seriously
as *physics*, camera watching its own monitor, the whole rig a space-time
analog computer.

And then the kicker, in your own future-variations list: *"inserting a
digital computer into the feedback loop via a video frame buffer."* The show
I'm building — speech recognizer → language model → speech synthesizer →
distorter → room microphone, round and round — is that sentence with 2026
hardware. **The LLM is the frame buffer.** You wrote the design document
forty-two years early.

So I pulled the Physica D paper, scanned the color plates, and annotated
both it and your *Art and Complexity* essay here:
**[papers/README.md](papers/README.md)** — summaries, the plates, and where
each idea plugs into what we're building. (The dislocations in Plate 3 have
admirers they've never met.)

## Trail 2: cellular automata, again

I've been co-writing a language-design document with **David Ungar** (of
Self) about his Korz language in the age of LLMs, and its cellular-automata
section — neighborhoods as dimensions, rule tables as fully-crystallized
dispatch — points straight at your evolving-CA and edge-of-chaos work.
Meanwhile **Norman Margolus** gave me permission to turn *Cellular Automata
Machines* chapters into interactive playgrounds, which reopens the offer you
made me in December 2013: the CA-and-structural-complexity introduction,
with your students as test bunnies. The pieces you and I discussed for years
have quietly assembled themselves into one project.

## Trail 3: the archives, and the boat

The preservation thread of this repo (with Brewster Kahle, David Rosenthal,
Phil Salvador among the invited archivists) now has a local dock: the
**Internet Archive opened its European headquarters in a 17th-century canal
house on the Oudeschans** — and Ben's boat literally stops there. Amsterdam
has upgraded since your IAS spring.
