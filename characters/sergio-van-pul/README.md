## Who

**Sergio van Pul** is a Dutch game designer and educator based in Hilversum, with an **MA in Game
Design and Development from HKU** (Hogeschool voor de Kunsten Utrecht). As a freelancer (2007–2012)
he designed interactive media and education projects for clients including the **Eduverse
Foundation**, the **University of Utrecht**, and the **Scratchweb Foundation**, where he coached
workshops teaching children programming and game design with **Scratch**. That work became
**"Scratch 2.0 Game Development Hotshot"** (Packt, 2014, with Jessica Chiang) — ten complete game
projects teaching most genres in Scratch. He later built games at Talpa Digital and Gamica, and
paints and scratch-builds tabletop miniatures.

## The 2008–2010 connection

Sergio was a core participant in the **Micropolis Eduverse Project** — the Kennisnet/IBM-backed push
to bring open source Micropolis into Dutch education — a named regular at the ABC Treehouse
meetings alongside Don, Warner Dijkhuizen (IBM), Michael van Wetering (Kennisnet), and David van
Gent (IBM).

When Don wrote everyone on 1 October 2010 to say he was no longer associated with Robert Shepherd,
Sergio answered within a day, candid and warm. The foundation had, from his perspective,
*"evaporated"* — but:

> "The only lasting connection I took away from it was meeting Helen and the rest of the 9-box
> development team. We're currently testing the game on a small scale in schools, are developing a
> game design studio around the 9-box concept and educational games in general…"

And he signed off:

> "Have lots of stupid fun creating some crazy concepts with Will Wright. And perhaps we'll meet
> again sometime in the future."

Sixteen years later: it's the future, and the crazy concepts with Will Wright are a repo.

## Why reactivate him

- **He took the wreck and built something.** The Eduverse collapse cost everyone, but Sergio walked
  out with the 9-box team, school pilots, and eventually a published book on teaching game
  development to kids. That's the resilient half of the story, and he owns it.
- **Triple-fit for the [educators' track](../../repo-shows/ideas/themes/educators-track.yml):**
  HKU alum (the exact Dutch game-jam host school the track names), Scratch teaching (bridges
  straight to the Brian Harvey / Jens Mönig Snap! bench), and lived Eduverse experience — he ran
  the teacher-plus-open-source-city-sim experiment before the tooling existed.
- **He closed with an open door.** "Perhaps we'll meet again sometime in the future" is the easiest
  reactivation in the roster: no repair needed, just news.

## The approach

Catch-up interview, **no ask**. "Hey — it's on. Micropolis runs in the browser now, AI plays it,
and there's an educators' track. What happened with 9-box? What are you building these days?"
Interview first; collaboration only if he's curious. LinkedIn (the msn address is sixteen years
old).
