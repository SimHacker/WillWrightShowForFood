# Charles London 💎

*Portrayal from published record only — not Charles London. Not yet contacted.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · authored by Don Hopkins

**He made the Plumbob.** By his own account, as placeholder art that a process rule then forbade him
to improve.

> "As I recall it was a performance decision at first, and when the issue came up again near ship
> we'd hit **art lock**, which our EP was very stringent about. So stringent that **the placeholder
> art I'd made for the active character indicator was forbidden to be updated**, regardless of any
> amount of my cajoling. **We call it the Plumbob now and it's the symbol of the whole franchise.**"
>
> — Charles London, [Maxis Alumni, 31 May 2021](../will-wright/sources/2021-05-31-brain-ui-beta-screenshots/README.md)

Asked directly whether Don deserved the credit for it, he said: *"Nope, it was me."* And: *"The
plumbob replaced the arrow."*

made the thing:

buried in the shipped assets are all the same artist.**

## The scooter caricature

*Source:* **Sean Baity**, Maxis Alumni, **6 February 2015** —
[post](media/2015-02-06-sean-baity-post.jpg) · [comments](media/2015-02-06-comments.png). 15
reactions, 3 comments.

> "Couldn't find the Sims V1 voice talent for Mackraz but **found this is a zip file**. LOL"
> — Sean Baity
>
> "**I drew that.**" — Charles London *(5 likes)*

![A hand-painted caricature displayed inside a grey Windows dialog box with a single OK button. A man with glasses and a goatee, in a pink shirt, green trousers and yellow shoes, rides a red kick scooter against a blue background. Four tiny Sims-scale figures climb on him — one standing on his head with arms raised in triumph, one on his shoulder, one on his hand, one on the handlebar](media/scooter-caricature-in-dialog.png)

Watercolour or gouache, scanned, and **wired to an OK button** — this is about-box or easter-egg art
sitting in a shipped-or-shipping asset bundle, found by accident fifteen years later by someone
looking for something else.

The composition is the joke: **a Maxis person being climbed on by his own Sims**, one of them
standing on his head in the Plumbob's position, arms up. Drawn, as it happens, by the man who made
the Plumbob.

**Subject unidentified here.** Sean's caption says he was digging for the Sims 1 voice-talent
recording [Jim Mackraz had asked for](../will-wright/sources/steve-and-gerri-simlish-adlib/README.md#how-it-surfaced-jim-mackraz-asked-for-it-in-2015),
and does not say who is on the scooter. Razor scooters were a 1999–2000 office fixture, which is
consistent with the Sims 1 era but proves nothing. **Ask Charles, ask Sean.**

## The DECO caricature

**Eric Hedman** ([room](../eric-hedman/)) answered in the same thread —
*"I see your scooter and I raise you… **DECO!!!**"*

![A painted caricature of a man in a white belted overcoat, purple shirt and yellow tie, wearing round glasses, seated against a night-time city skyline with falling snow. He holds up a green diamond shape like a cocktail glass. Beside him are a garden gnome in a red hat and a wooden artist's mannequin holding a club](media/deco-caricature-from-eric-hedman.png)

Not Charles's work — Eric's, or at least Eric's to post — but it belongs beside the scooter as the
matching half of a pair of colleague caricatures, and it is **made of Sims objects**: the figure
toasts with what reads as a **green Plumbob** held like a martini glass, flanked by a **garden gnome**
and a **wooden artist's mannequin** wielding a club. A Maxis person drawn as a nightlife character
attended by the game's own décor.

*"DECO"* is unexplained — a nickname, an art-asset name, or the subject's own. Filed with the
question open.
