# 2026-08-21 — Jim corrects the polished-turd quote

Jim Mackraz, replying to the invitation, 21 Aug 2026:

> FYI, the quote is "Never underestimate the power of a polished turd."
> The turd refers to documents, ppt decks, etc. (SimWorld Report being
> the likely context.) There is no concept of polishing turd code.
> There might be for the related "lipstick on a pig," but I don't know
> that one.

## What this corrects

Don's long-standing telling — including a public Hacker News comment —
had Jim, as CTG lead, telling Don his job was *turd polishing* after
Don called the code a turd: "clean it out and polish your turd, and
you'll be golden." Jim's correction:

- **The real quote:** "Never underestimate the power of a polished
  turd."
- **The real referent:** documents and PowerPoint decks — the
  **SimWorld Report** being the likely context — not code.
- **Explicitly:** there is no concept of polishing turd code. The
  code-flavored cousin would be "lipstick on a pig," which Jim
  doesn't claim.

Don's memory of being told to clean up and polish the code (the
de-Macification episode) stays on record as Don's recollection; the
quote itself now carries Jim's version. How the two memories merged
into one story is itself a good on-air question.
