# Remembering Dave Evans (1924–1998)

**David C. Evans** built the Utah ARPA graphics school — the environment where Alan Kay, John
Warnock, and a generation of SIGGRAPH founders learned what interactive pictures could be. With
[Ivan Sutherland](../ivan-sutherland/) he co-founded **Evans & Sutherland**, flight-sim and
real-time 3D before the industry had a name for it.

Kay's FLEX path starts here: Evans introduced him to Ed Cheadle (Yoot People.md).

We cite him; we do not speak as him.
