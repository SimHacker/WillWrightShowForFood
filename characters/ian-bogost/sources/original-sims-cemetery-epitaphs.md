# The Original Sims cemetery — harvested epitaphs (Wayback, 2004)

**Source:** [Wayback capture of originalsims.com/cemetery, 2004-11-12](https://web.archive.org/web/20041112005837/http://www.originalsims.com/cemetery).
At capture time: **364 tombstones**; by the
[September 2005 article](../../will-wright/sources/2005-09-22-halloween-tombstones-original-sims/article.md)
the count had passed **1200 memorials of people and Sims**. Users engraved
tombstones (Halloween with ghosts, or Solemn with flowers), uploaded photos
of the deceased, downloaded the working Sims object, and emailed links to
friends. Epitaphs below are verbatim, spelling preserved — patina is the
point.

## Sincere grief, a month old at capture

> **Christopher Reeve** — September 25, 1952 – October 10, 2004. Whether it
> was on the silver screen or campaigning to advance spinal cord injury
> research, Chris was truly a Superman.

> **Jam Master Jay** — He Was The Heart And Soul Of Hip-Hop

## Sims lore, mourned like family

> **Misty LaBelle** — Born September 1999 – Died December 2001. She had the
> distinction of being the first Sim to die of a broken heart. She lived
> well in her short life.

> **Ken Lover** — Died on october-31-2004. Born on october-27-1567. Lived
> many sims years and now i am gone to Meet my sim manchion never to exist
> again…

## Community in-jokes, grief as fan fiction

> **Ginnie** — Here lies Ginnie our Updating Queen, She forgot to post one
> day and the groups turned mean. She got them back by updating thrice one
> day, Then she took the Downloadoholics Anonymous way. The Withdrawal
> killed her!

## Pop culture, buried with honors

> **Frankenstein** — May God Have mercy upon his creator's soul

> **Vlad Tepes** — Here lies Dracula, But not for long!

> **Nancy Thompson** — Died Febuary 1987. Died in her sleep. Daughter of
> Marge & Donald Thompson. *(A Nightmare on Elm Street, played straight as a
> local obituary.)*

> **Hubert Cumberdale** — Tasted like soot and poo. *(Salad Fingers, 2004's
> creepiest flash cartoon, interred beside Superman.)*

> **Ron Weasley** — He was a good child and a great friend. His family will
> miss him.

## Kids writing their own myths

> skateboarding zack stumbled upon a rail he was trying to board slide when
> his board broke, he lost balance and knocked ino an old lady and died

## Pets

> **Dona** — She was an excellent cat.

## The museum label

Every register of mourning the internet has, on one page, without a
moderator: a real Superman a month dead, the first Sim to die of a broken
heart, an updates-forum martyr, Dracula, a skateboarding self-mythology
with the spelling intact, and an excellent cat. Strangers wrote each
other's grief with care and jokes and nobody was cruel — in 2004, on an
open UGC site with a free-download button. That's the show beat: read
these aloud, then ask what this page knew that the platforms that came
after didn't. The cemetery is the counterfactual internet.
