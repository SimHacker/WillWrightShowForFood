## The finding to lead with

**Media Streams' grammar and the modern computer vision task taxonomy are the same schema,
arrived at independently thirty years apart.**

| Media Streams, 1993 | Computer vision, now |
|---|---|
| `subject-action-object` | human-object interaction detection — the `<human, verb, object>` triplet |
| `subject-relative-position-object` | scene graph generation |
| `camera-movement-object` | camera motion estimation + tracking |

Checkable from both sides, and it says the representation work anticipated a structure the
field had to rediscover. Details: [`../golan-levin/media-streams-icon-language.yml`](../golan-levin/media-streams-icon-language.yml)
and [MEDIAFLOW.yml](https://github.com/SimHacker/MicropolisCore/blob/main/apps/screen-angel/MEDIAFLOW.yml).

## The 1993 argument that got sharper, not weaker

> "an accurate and readable time-indexed representation of simultaneous, sequential,
> overlapping and contained actions (natural languages are not very good at this task)"

That is the case against free-text description as the representation for video content,
written thirty-three years before people started asking a language model to describe a clip
and storing the paragraph. Worth putting to him directly: **models solved the *labour* of
annotating and left the *representation* problem exactly where he left it.** Does he think
that is right?

## Topics

- **Semasiographic writing** — why a visual language rather than words, and what a system of
  signs that doesn't encode speech buys you. The deepest idea in the thesis and the least
  borrowed since.
- **Glommable compounds** — a completed compound returns to the palette as a primitive.
  Vocabulary that grows through use; directly relevant to a schema meant to be extended by
  people who aren't its authors.
- **The three interaction constructs** — Director's Workshop, Icon Palettes, Media Time
  Lines. Which held up? Which would he build differently?
- **What the dialects still cover that vision doesn't** — cinematic operations, camera motion
  as *intent* rather than measurement, character relationships over time.
- **Garage Cinema** (CACM 1997) — "professional video by hobbyists with camcorders."
  Arguably arrived; arguably arrived as something else entirely. His read.
- **Automatic media production at Interval** — the teams he led, and what the blocker really
  was. The standing hypothesis here is that annotation cost was the blocker and the
  representation was fine, which is a claim he can confirm or demolish.
- **Retrieval and repurposing** — the thesis title's second and third words. Most people cite
  the annotation and skip these, and repurposing is the part that maps onto generative media.
