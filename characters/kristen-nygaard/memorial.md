# Remembering Kristen Nygaard (1926–2002)

**Kristen Nygaard** (1926–2002) — with **Ole-Johan Dahl** created **Simula**;
    later participatory design and Norwegian computing politics. Turing Award 2001 with Dahl.
    Paired memorial with ole-johan-dahl for the Simula root Kay cites.
