# Liam Proven 📰

*Press contact and long-running correspondent — not an invitation portrayal.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · consent: warm correspondent · authored by Don Hopkins

**Liam Proven** is the Linux and FOSS reporter at **[The Register](https://www.theregister.com/)**,
writing from the Isle of Man. He first contacted Don in **January 2023** after Don's
[Alan Kay / NeWS conversation](https://donhopkins.medium.com/alan-kay-on-should-web-browsers-have-stuck-to-being-document-viewers-and-a-discussion-of-news-5cb92c7b3445)
made the rounds, and the correspondence never really stopped: the **X-at-40 / NeWS history**
thread (PSIBER Space, HyperLook SimCity, pie menus, the "Axis of Eval," and an introduction to
Duncan Mak's NeWS-in-browser work), the **Yoot Tower licensing** research (Fair Core vs. Commons
Clause vs. Polyform, alongside Heather Meeker's advice), and in **February 2026** Don's full
**MOOLLM** brief — *LambdaMOO meets The Sims* — which Liam forwarded to his San Francisco
colleague **[Thomas Claburn](../thomas-claburn/README.md)** in June 2026.

**The story now:** the February brief was about MOOLLM; the center of gravity has moved to
**WillWrightShowForFood** — Will accepted, the Repo Show as MOOLLM's first full-scale real-world
application, [MicropolisCore](https://github.com/SimHacker/MicropolisCore) as the running code,
and the [Soul City](../https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/tree/main/catalogs/soul-city/README.md) create/publish/share bridge — all free
and open (MOOLLM free, WWSFF free, Micropolis GPL). Liam and Tom get the first look, with
publication timing coordinated around the premiere
([quiet mode](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#stealth-mode)).

**The standing offer:** participate, not just report — dig in at any depth, write about whatever
aspects he pleases, and if it's fun, record the conversation as a show in either direction: Don
on Liam's show, or Liam on the Repo Show (a journalist cross-examining the repo on camera is the
format working as intended). There's a meta layer built for a GitHub-native reporter: MOOLLM is
also the **production tool** — the show's invitations, ethics enforcement, and editorial
workflow run as skills in the same public repos the audience browses.
