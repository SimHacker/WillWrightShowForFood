## Who

**Leo Joskowicz** (born 1961) — professor at the **Hebrew University of Jerusalem**; faculty member
of **ELSC** (Edmond and Lily Safra Center for Brain Sciences). Founder and director of the **CASMIP
Lab** (Computer-Aided Surgery and Medical Image Processing, 1996). Former **president of The MICCAI
Society** (2019–2022). **IEEE Fellow** (2013) for computer-assisted surgery and medical image processing.

Education: **Technion** BSc (1983); **NYU Courant** MSc (1984) and PhD (1988, Ernest Davis — geometric
reasoning in mechanical devices). **IBM Watson** (1988–1995) computer-assisted surgery group before
returning to Israel.

Public profiles: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Joskowicz) ·
[ELSC](https://elsc.huji.ac.il/people-directory/faculty-members/leo-joskowicz/) ·
[HUJI CS](https://www.cs.huji.ac.il/~josko/)

## Why he's here

**Heinz Lemke** (15 July 2026, PIXIE thread) Cc'd Joskowicz for a proposed **CARS 2027 Berlin**
special **HCI session** on **29 June 2027** — same date as the 60th anniversary of Heinz's first
Cambridge PIXIE lecture. Heinz hopes Joskowicz will present on HCI activities related to **Joe
Weizenbaum's ELIZA**, which Heinz says Joskowicz *"studied extensively in the earlier part of his
career."*

**Verification note:** The ELIZA research history is **Heinz's attribution** (Jul 2026 email), not
yet confirmed from Joskowicz's public CV or publications. Joskowicz's documented career centers on
medical imaging, surgical navigation, and robotics. The show bridge is still strong via **CARS**
(Joskowicz: organizing committee 2007–2026, program committee 2000–2026 per [CV PDF](https://www.cs.huji.ac.il/~josko/publications/joskowicz-leo-cv-full.pdf)) and the **Weizenbaum ↔ Heinz** line (PIXIE demo at MIT 1969; Weizenbaum at TU Berlin decades later).

Heinz notes Roy Eagleson met Joskowicz at **CARS 2026 Nagoya**.
