# John von Neumann -- sources

Hard-to-find texts, transcriptions, and Don's HN corpus, formatted for humans.

- [three-kinds-of-universal-constructors.md](three-kinds-of-universal-constructors.md) --
  The big picture: von Neumann's three universal constructors at three levels of reality
  (deterministic mathematical, physical mechanical, probabilistic quantum mechanical), with
  Don's transcription of p. 99 of *Theory of Self-Reproducing Automata* and the full HN link
  inventory.
- [29-state-implementation-and-buckley.md](29-state-implementation-and-buckley.md) --
  The nuts and bolts: how the 29 state rule and the universal constructor actually work.
  Construction arm huffman codes, the full cell state table from Don's CAM6 implementation,
  the three signal crossing organs (real time crossing, coded channel crossing, Buckley's
  autoinitializing exclusive-or) with runnable initial configurations, the Factorio analogy,
  and the umbilical-cord boot sequence of self-reproduction. Links to working copies of
  Buckley's Automata-2008 paper (the Wikipedia citation is a dead link).
- [hn/](hn/README.md) -- readable local copies of the unique HN comments that are not
  subsets of those two write-ups (hacker emblem, 1948 prediction, Robust First tour,
  Factorio/SimCity/Kay, Omega Point, billiard-ball heat, etc.). Backup if HN goes away.

Primary book: [*Theory of Self-Reproducing Automata*](https://archive.org/details/theoryofselfrepr00vonn_0)
(von Neumann, edited and completed by Arthur W. Burks, 1966) -- page 312 has "the sexy playboy
centerfold spread of John von Neumann's 29 state transition rule"
([hn/19354538-page-312.md](hn/19354538-page-312.md)).
