# Alan Turing -- sources

Hard-to-find texts Don typed in, formatted for humans.

- [morphogenesis-collected-works.md](morphogenesis-collected-works.md) -- Prefatory texts from
  *Collected Works of A.M. Turing, Volume 3: Morphogenesis* (P.T. Saunders, ed., 1992):
  P.N. Furbank's preface, the chronology, Saunders's preface to the volume, and Saunders's
  introduction (the "defeat the argument from design" line; D'Arcy Thompson; why Turing did
  *not* invent cellular automata). Plain-text original:
  [donhopkins.com/home/archive/Turing/Morphogenesis.txt](https://donhopkins.com/home/archive/Turing/Morphogenesis.txt).

The daisy drawing that sat inside the front cover:
[../media/hockey-or-watching-the-daisies-grow.jpg](../media/hockey-or-watching-the-daisies-grow.jpg)
(*Hockey or Watching the Daisies Grow*, Ethel Sara Turing, 1923).

The 1952 paper itself is still in print at the Royal Society:
[The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rstb.1952.0012).
