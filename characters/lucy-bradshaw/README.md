# Lucy Bradshaw 🏙️🎮

*Invitation portrayal — not Lucy Bradshaw.* [Standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

**Who:** **Lucy Bradshaw** — studio leader at **Maxis** (1997–2015): executive producer of **The Sims
2**, production leadership on **The Sims**, **SimCity 3000/4**, and **Spore** with Will Wright;
**General Manager** then **SVP of Maxis**. LucasArts credits include *Monkey Island II* and *Indiana
Jones and the Fate of Atlantis*. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Bradshaw_(game_developer))

**Why Sims Fest:** Few people held the whole Maxis label together across the Sims explosion, Spore's
long gestation, and the online era. Production culture, team empowerment, and what "shipping The Sims
franchise" meant from the GM chair — reunion gold alongside engineers and designers.

**Show:** [`building-the-sims` / **Sims Fest**](../../repo-shows/building-the-sims/README.md)
