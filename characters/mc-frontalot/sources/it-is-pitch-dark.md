## One sentence

Second-person status-line anxiety as nerdcore: if you forgot the lamp, the grue is on you — and the TRS-80 in your brain never quite reboots clean.

## Why it’s a masterpiece (for this show)

Not “rap about games” as novelty — a **dense Infocom citation engine** with flow: Zork/grue, Babel fish ear puzzle (*Hitchhiker’s*), *Planetfall* mop/dropship, *Nord & Bert*, *Leather Goddesses*, *Suspended* six robots, *A Mind Forever Voyaging*, XYZZY/PLUGH, Flood Control Dam, mailbox pamphlet → `>run`. The video is a museum of machines; Meretzky’s cameo is the implementor seal.

## Show hooks

1. **Cold open before any Adventure / Infocom talk** — 30–60s chorus, then cut to Get Lamp or Scott Adams
2. **Transcript as parser poetry** — put lyric lines next to actual Zork/HHGG transcripts
3. **“Never get misunderstood by the NPCs inhabiting Earth”** — MOOLLM many-voices beat
4. Pair Frontalot (living nerdcore) with Jason Scott (living archivist) if either episode lands
