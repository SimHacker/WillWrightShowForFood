# Sketchpad and descendants — north star lineage

**Ivan Sutherland's Sketchpad** (1963) is a **north star** for Will Wright Show For Food: direct
manipulation, constraints, pen-driven model building, and software that teaches by letting you
*see* relationships hold.

---

## Sketchpad (1963)

- **Machine:** MIT Lincoln Laboratory **TX-2**
- **Input:** Light pen; **master–slave** copying; **constraints** (geometry stays true when you drag)
- **Idea:** Interactive drawing as engineering — the user specifies intent; the machine maintains structure

[Turing Award citation](https://amturing.acm.org/award_winners/sutherland_3467782.cfm) ·

## Educational line: The Geometer's Sketchpad

**The Geometer's Sketchpad** (GSP) — **Nick Jackiw**, Key Curriculum Press — brought dynamic geometry
into schools (1980s onward):

- Drag a constructed point; dependent objects update in real time
- Exploration and proof-like reasoning for students
- The clearest **classroom** descendant of Sketchpad's constraint spirit

Successors and cousins: Cabri, Cinderella, [**GeoGebra**](https://www.geogebra.org/). We name GSP
because Roy Eagleson's students meet Sketchpad *through* that educational tradition before PIXIE
enters the syllabus.

## Cambridge: PIXIE (1969)

Six years after Sketchpad, [**PIXIE**](../heinz-lemke/README.md) (Wiseman, Lemke, Hiles):

- **PDP-7 + Type 340** + light pen; **radial menus**
- Interactive **graph-theoretic models** on the satellite; simulation on **Titan**
- Complete PDP-7 listing recovered (Heinz, July 2026) — [`../heinz-lemke/pixie-source-recovery.md`](../heinz-lemke/pixie-source-recovery.md)

Same pen-and-beam era; different application domain — but one lecture arc from Sutherland to Heinz.
See [`../roy-eagleson/sketchpad-to-pixie-lineage.md`](../roy-eagleson/sketchpad-to-pixie-lineage.md).

## Mother of All Demos (1968) — neighbor, not parent

**Douglas Engelbart**'s NLS demo (1968) shares the light-pen decade with Sketchpad and PIXIE.
Different lineage (augmentation, hypertext, mouse) — often taught beside Sketchpad in HCI courses.
