# Kent Pitman on Hacker News

Kent posts on HN as [netsettler](https://news.ycombinator.com/user?id=netsettler),
usually when one of his own papers comes up, and usually to add the context
the paper couldn't carry.

## Kent, in his own words

- **Lisp1 vs Lisp2**, under his 1988 paper with Dick Gabriel,
  "Technical Issues of Separation in Function Cells and Value Cells"
  ([the 2016 HN discussion of the paper](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=12799227)):
  "As it happens, RPG and I disagreed completely on whether Lisp1 or Lisp2
  was a good idea. Most readers read this like a Rorschach, assuming that the
  article confirms their own beliefs, not stopping to think that the article
  just presents two sides of an issue."
  ([12801023](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=12801023)).
  In the same thread, on `FUNCALL`: "It is my preference, not something I
  bargain down to." ([12827361](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=12827361)).
  On Scheme vs CL macros: "You never get anything for free--you just trade one
  thing for another." ([12820558](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=12820558)).
- **ISLISP and the standards process**, under "Common Lisp: The Untold Story"
  ([the 2016 HN discussion of the article](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=11789288)),
  explaining islisp.info and why standards get reissued
  ([11797858](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=11797858),
  [11798026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=11798026)).
- **Where ANIMAL came from**, under the elizagen post (2024): he didn't
  originate it; writing it in BASIC primed him for Lisp
  ([39486285](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39486285); the whole
  story is in [ANIMAL, the Pope, and the apology](animal.md)).

## What others cite

- The **condition system**. phoe, author of *The Common Lisp Condition
  System*: "Kent M. Pitman (also known as kmp) is the father of the condition
  system as it exists nowadays" ([23845935](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=23845935)).
  His paper [Condition Handling in the Lisp Language Family](http://www.nhplace.com/kent/Papers/Condition-Handling-2001.html)
  is linked whenever exceptions come up.
- The **HyperSpec**. kragen: Kent "chaired the error-handling subcommittee of
  the ANSI Common Lisp committee, was the Project Editor, and prepared the
  Common Lisp HyperSpec" ([22345209](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22345209)).
- [The Best of Intentions: EQUAL Rights—and Wrongs—in Lisp](http://www.nhplace.com/kent/PS/EQUAL.html),
  on why there is no one true equality or copy.
- [Special Forms in Lisp](https://www.nhplace.com/kent/Papers/Special-Forms.html)
  (1980), cited every time fexprs come back.
- His comp.lang.lisp posts on what made Genera good, which EdwardCoffin keeps
  recommending ([39059298](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39059298)).
- His list of what Lisp is used for, ending "...just because these are the
  only things they happened to list."
