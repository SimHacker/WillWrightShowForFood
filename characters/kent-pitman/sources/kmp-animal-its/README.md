Single-line MacLISP s-expression: the persisted `*MEMORY*` decision tree from Kent Pitman's **ANIMAL II**
on MIT-MC (spring 1978 MacLISP). Players taught the game new animals by answering yes/no questions and
supplying distinguishing sentences; the parser turned natural language into internal question forms and
hung new terminals on the tree.

This snapshot is the **shared lab database** (dozens of MIT-MC userids appear as `AUTHOR` on nodes).
**A2DEH** (Donald E. Hopkins) appears 42 times — Don's ARPANET tourist contributions are woven into the
same tree.
