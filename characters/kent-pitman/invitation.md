Hi Kent,

You've been teaching me Lisp since I was a kid on a 300 baud terminal, and
I've never properly thanked you for it. `:TEACH;LISP`, the duel where you set
my output base to Roman numerals from inside your own Lisp, ANIMAL making me
apologize to it about the Pope: those are the reasons I became a programmer
who thinks programs should have characters, and they are all over the work
I'm doing now.

I've put the stories together in one place, with your code and your own
words where they're already public, so other people can find them:

https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/tree/main/characters/kent-pitman

Your Facebook reply about `ESCE` sent me digging, and it turned out you
remembered it exactly: `SYS3; TS ESCE`, "Call me an elevator", July 1982,
"The deed is done!" That one is written up here:

https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/kent-pitman/elevator-and-door.md

**The ask.** I'd love to interview you on a Repo Show: a recorded
conversation whose stage is a public GitHub repo, where everything we
mention gets linked and the ideas get built and credited in the open. Will
Wright is doing the premiere. I'd like to have you on more than once if
you're up for it, alone and with friends, as often or as rarely as you
like.

Some places we could start (there are more on
[our page of topic and show ideas for you](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/kent-pitman/ideas.md)):

- Your Lisp origin story, from BASIC ANIMAL to the HyperSpec, with every
  artifact linked as you go.
- Weizenbaum's point, "how easily we were confused", and what it says about
  LLMs.
- The condition system as a design for agents that hit errors: signal,
  handle, restart, ask a human.
- Special forms and fexprs, and why I've ended up building something
  fexpr-like on purpose.
- Standards as social engineering: X3J13, Lisp1 vs Lisp2, and turning a
  standard into a hypertext.
- MOO, which you mentioned being interested in, and MOOLLM, which is MOO
  ideas plus LLMs: https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm
- Group shows: the ITS reunion with RMS and Lars Brinkhoff, the emulation
  roundtable (door emulator optional), ANIMAL and The Sims with Will.

Format is yours: live, recorded, text, one long talk or a few short ones.
"Later" or "no" are fine answers too, and you can edit or delete anything in
your directory whenever you like.

Thanks for everything, Kent.

Don
