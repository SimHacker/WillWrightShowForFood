## Who

**Albert Theo Hofkamp** (b. 1967, Leeuwarden) is a software craftsman at **TU Eindhoven**
(Mechanical Engineering, Systems Engineering group), where he has worked since the late 1990s on
real-time systems, the **Chi** modeling language, and the **Eclipse ESCET** supervisory control
engineering toolkit — software that helps run Dutch bridges, locks, and ASML lithography machines.

In open source he is **"Alberth"** — an **OpenTTD** developer credited as the project's **GUI expert
from 0.7 through 1.9**. OpenTTD is the open-source Transport Tycoon Deluxe: the same species of
project as open-source Micropolis — a beloved classic sim, liberated, then kept alive for decades by
craftspeople like him.

## The Micropolis connection

In the winter of 2008–09, right after the SimCity GPL release, Albert volunteered on **Micropolis**
and did some of the most valuable work in the codebase's history: methodical cleanup of the TCL/Tk-era
C code through the C++ transition, renaming and normalizing variables and functions, **Doxygen**
comments throughout, deep build-system and architecture analysis, and bug fixes. Don at the time:
*"such high quality work, prioritizing your efforts well."* Don credited him publicly in the
**HAR 2009** talk, and he's in the Micropolis manual credits.

His emails are quotable engineering literature. On build systems:

> "In general, build and install is one *big* *mess*. … In other words, you can safely give up
> trying to understand it all. Nobody does."

On documentation:

> "In OpenTTD I have found out that writing a few lines documentation is worth whole functions :)"

And when Don mentioned applying for grant money to support development, his entire reply was:

> "We need money?"

Three words that define the volunteer archetype the whole
[educators' track](../../repo-shows/ideas/themes/educators-track.yml) wants to cultivate.

## Why invite him

- **He's done this twice.** Micropolis cleanup *and* fifteen years of OpenTTD GUI work. Nobody in the
  Netherlands knows more about the long game of keeping a liberated classic sim alive and clean.
- **Overhauling Micropolis/SimCity code is the show.** MicropolisCore is the C++ he cleaned up,
  now compiled to WebAssembly and driven by AI. A Repo Show walking from his 2008 Doxygen comments to
  today's LLM-navigable codebase would be a perfect before/after arc — his documentation instinct
  ("the first step towards understanding the program") is exactly what makes code legible to AI agents.
- **OpenTTD × Micropolis.** Two GPL'd classics, two communities, one conversation about open-source
  game stewardship — with someone who's a credited developer on both sides.
- **TU Eindhoven.** A Dutch university affiliation on the educators' bench, and living proof that
  serious engineering careers and volunteer game work feed each other.
