# Albert Hofkamp × Micropolis — 2008–09 email digest

*Digest of threads from Don's mail archive. Quotes verbatim; personal contact
details omitted. Full originals stay private.*

## "Coding style" — 25 December 2008

Albert and Don, deep in the post-GPL-release cleanup. Albert's working method and philosophy, in his
own words:

- On documentation: "In OpenTTD I have found out that writing a few lines documentation is worth
  whole functions :) It is also the first step towards understanding the program, which is why I do
  it. By picking a standard like Doxygen, others can profit from it too."
- On build/packaging systems, a small essay ending: "In general, build and install is one *big*
  *mess*. … Everybody packaging author tries to find an overall system and include as much features
  as possible, and they all fail at some point. … In other words, you can safely give up trying to
  understand it all. Nobody does."
- He asked the right architecture question early: is the goal a cleaned-up C++ core, or something
  bigger — "If the latter, what is the route to that goal?"

## "Next steps" — 12 January 2009

Don introduces **Robert Shepherd** and the **"Micropolis Eduverse"** project:

> "We're working on getting some grant money from Kennisnet and IBM to support the project. Our goal
> is to develop a web based version of Micropolis…"

Albert's complete reply to the grant-money sentence:

> "We need money?"

Don invites him to the **21 January 2009 meeting in Amsterdam with Kennisnet and IBM**; Albert can
only make Amsterdam in the late afternoon, so Don offers to meet on a Friday or weekend instead.

## What the collaboration produced

Surface cleanup and renaming/normalizing across the TCL/Tk-era code and the C++ transition, Doxygen
comments throughout, build-system and architecture analysis, and bug fixes. Don, at the time: "such
high quality work, prioritizing your efforts well." Credited in the HAR 2009 talk and the Micropolis
manual credits.
