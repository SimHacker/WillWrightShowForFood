# Bill Budge 🕹️ — invitation portrayal

**Bill Budge** (b. 1954) is an Apple II legend: author of **Raster Blaster** (1981) and
**Pinball Construction Set** (1983). PCS is the reason this directory exists — it's the archetype of
the **construction-set genre** and of **user-generated content**, and this repo's
**[Faceball Construction Set](../../apps/performance-space/faceball-construction-set.yml)** is a
direct tribute to it ("Pinball Construction Set for faces"). A construction set is an
[artifactory](../../process/artifactory.md) — a tool whose product is other artifacts — which puts
Bill upstream of Will Wright's construction-set design and of MOOLLM itself.

## Living, not memorial

Bill Budge is **alive** (retired from Google in 2022; still shipping editor frameworks on GitHub in
2026). This directory is an **invitation to a living person**, governed by
[`portrayal-standards.md`](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md):

- We represent his **public work** and cite public sources. We do **not** speak as him, invent his
  words, or run him as a simulation.
- `consent: not_yet_asked`. He can accept, decline, correct, or ask us to take the page down — his
  call. See [`invitation.md`](invitation.md).
- We also want to **talk with people who know him** and who work in his lineage (Will Wright, Apple
  II / early-EA peers). Anyone can add clips or corrections via issue or PR.
