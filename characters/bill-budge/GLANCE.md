🕹️ **Bill Budge** — Apple II legend; author of **Raster Blaster** (1981) and **Pinball Construction Set** (1983). PCS turned a game into a *tool to build games* — the archetype of the **construction-set genre** and **user-generated content**. Published via BudgeCo then **Electronic Arts** (the glove guy in the 1983 "Software Artists" photo). Career-long **tools & frameworks** builder: 3DO, Sony (Lead Tools Programmer), Google (retired 2022), and still shipping editor frameworks on GitHub.
