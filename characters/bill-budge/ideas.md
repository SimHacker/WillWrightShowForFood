### 1. The construction set as a genre — the flagship
**Pinball Construction Set** (1983) turned "play this pinball game" into "build any pinball game."
Drag parts onto the table, wire their behavior, play. That's the birth of the **construction-set
genre** and of **user-generated content** as a first-class idea — the through-line to SimCity, The
Sims, LittleBigPlanet, Minecraft, Roblox. Great opener with **Will Wright**, who treats construction
sets as a design philosophy. *Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Budge),
[MobyGames](https://www.mobygames.com/person/742/bill-budge/).*

### 2. Pinball Construction Set is an *artifactory*
A construction set is literally an **artifact factory** — a tool whose product is other artifacts.
That's the [`artifactory`](../../process/artifactory.md) concept this repo (and MOOLLM) is built
on: read/write/edit/play, and the output is more things to play with. PCS in 1983 is one of the
cleanest early examples: not the artifact, the *engine that makes artifacts*. Bridges directly to
the **[Faceball Construction Set](../../apps/performance-space/faceball-construction-set.yml)**.

### 3. What's actually inside PCS — the engineering
To ship PCS, Bill had to build a **mini paint program**, a **mini sound editor**, and a **save/load
system**, on an Apple II, in assembly. A construction set is a stack of little editors. Rich, honest
territory for a tools person — the plumbing behind the magic. *Source: Wikipedia.*

### 4. Raster Blaster came first (1981)
Before the construction set, the pinball game itself — hand-tuned Apple II physics. Some of PCS's
components were built for Raster Blaster first. Good origin beat: the specific game that taught him
the general tool.

### 5. The EA "Software Artists" era
Early **Electronic Arts** (1983) credited programmers like rock stars — the "**We See Farther**"
campaign, the famous photo where Bill is **the one wearing the glove**. He'd published through his
own **BudgeCo** first, discussed EA with **Woz**, and signed on with **Trip Hawkins**. A window into
when software authorship was culture. *Source: Wikipedia, MobyGames.*

### 6. A life of tools & frameworks (still going)
"My passion is building tools and frameworks." Raster Blaster → PCS → **3DO** (3D engine for Blade
Force) → back to EA → **Sony** (Lead Tools Programmer) → **Google** (retired Jan 2022) → and still
shipping on **GitHub**: Diagrammar, **WebEditorFramework** (TypeScript, active into 2026),
JSStatecharts. The through-line of a whole career is *empowering other makers*. *Source:
[github.com/billbudge](https://github.com/billbudge), [Game Developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-conversation-with-bill-budge).*

### 7. The Faceball tribute — Pinball Construction Set for faces
Show the repo's homage: snap on mouth/eyes/props, wire behaviors, play the physics — the same verbs
as PCS, aimed at face puppets. A chance to close the loop live: the man who invented the construction
set, looking at a construction set built in his honor.
