# Golan Levin 🎛️

*Portrayal of a real invitee, written by Don — not Golan.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

**Golan Levin** is an interactive / computational media artist, engineer, and educator
(b. 1972, NYC; Pittsburgh). Professor of Electronic Art at
[Carnegie Mellon](https://art.cmu.edu/people/golan-levin/) (courtesy Design, Architecture, CS, ETC);
former Director of the [Frank-Ratchye STUDIO for Creative Inquiry](https://studioforcreativeinquiry.org/).
MIT Media Lab alum. Archive: **[flong.com](https://flong.com/)** — *“We fling flongs all day long.”*
Co-author (with Tega Brain) of *Code as Creative Medium*.

At **Interval Research** he worked in the same Camelot-era circle as
[Don Hopkins](../don-hopkins/) and [Paul Debevec](../paul-debevec/) — including
**[Mouther](https://www.flong.com/archive/projects/mouther/)** (1995–96) with Malcolm Slaney,
artwork in **[Tom Ngo](../tom-ngo/)**’s **Embedded Constraint Graphics** editor, and
*Rouen Revisited* with Debevec.

## Why Golan

- **Mouther** — speech-driven cartoon faces on ECG; still the clearest demo of drag-the-feature animation ([flong page](https://www.flong.com/archive/projects/mouther/))
- **flong.com** — live, attributed archive of interactive work (Repo Show kin)
- **Code as Creative Medium** — teaching practice adjacent to Repo Show *homefun*
- Gestural / audiovisual works: AVES, *Messa di Voce*, Manual Input Workstation, *Eyecode*, …
- Interval colleague — natural pair with [Tom Ngo](../tom-ngo/) and optional [Paul Debevec](../paul-debevec/) (*Rouen Revisited*)

Dream segment: **[Tom + Golan — ECG & Mouther](../../repo-shows/tom-and-golan-ecg-mouther/)** —
do the cartoons still exist? Reverse-engineer vs recover; whiteboard SVG / Canvas / WebGPU ECG;
drive morph-target faces for [Faceball](../../apps/performance-space/faceball-construction-set.yml).
**Talk only** — nobody has to reimplement on air.
