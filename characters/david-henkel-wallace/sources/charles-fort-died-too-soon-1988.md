# Charles Fort died too soon (December 1988)

**[David Henkel-Wallace](../README.md)** ("**Gumby**"), then at MCC (`Gumby@mcc.com`), a year
before co-founding **Cygnus Solutions** — and three years before he
[nominated Don for the unix-haters list](hn-interactions.md).

## What it is

A legendary **mailing-list flame-essay**. **Michael Travers** had ruled that "usenet weenie"
talk was off-topic on `SubGenius@mc.lcs.mit.edu` unless tied to "genuinely bogus spiritual
violence." Gumby's rebuttal: **Unix is exactly on-topic.** There are those who allege the
Church of the SubGenius is a hoax — so what better defense than linking it to a *genuine*
hoax, the Unix "operating system"?

The centerpiece is a **two-column proof by analogy** — **Catholicism ↔ Unix** — row after row,
each pair a punchline: sendmail as the Dark Ages, Bill Joy and Dennis Ritchie as Borgia Popes,
no error-checking as the selling of indulgences, the refusal to version the filesystem as
papal infallibility. The title invokes **Charles Fort** (1874–1932), collector of "damned
facts" and obsessive parallel-drawer: *Fort would have had a field day documenting Unix as
phenomenon.* The opening blames the phone company; the closing PS films a Unix convention as
H.G. Wells's Morlocks.

Signed **The most Right-shifted Rev. Bit-Rot, C.O. (Computationally Orthodox)** — a
right-shift `>>` joke wearing monastic orders. Genre: SubGenius satire × unix-haters ×
Charles Fort × 1988 mailing-list performance art. Not a serious argument — **satirical
overload**, and all the better for it.

The theology has since been forked and upgraded: the SubGenius tradition Gumby was ordained
into lives on in Don's **Sub⇒Eval** liturgy
([SubGenius → EvalGenius, and why I hate SUBMIT buttons](../../don-hopkins/witty-diatribes.yml)) —
which is why [Gumby's invitation](../invitation.md) proposes re-ordaining him **E.O.
(Evaluationally Orthodox)** on air.

## Provenance and circulation

Sent 20 December 1988, 01:36 PST, from `Gumby@mcc.com` (XANTHIPPE.cyc-west.mcc.com — the Cyc
project's machines at MCC in Texas) to Michael Travers at the MIT Media Lab, Cc
`SubGenius@mc.lcs.mit.edu` and `unix-haters@media-lab.media.mit.edu`. Forwarded the same day
by **Martha S. Rose** (`strata@FENCHURCH.MIT.EDU`) to `elbows@BLOOM-BEACON.MIT.EDU`, which is
the copy preserved here. Same cultural bloodstream as the later *UNIX-HATERS Handbook* (1994),
whose X-Windows Disaster chapter [Don wrote](../../don-hopkins/README.md).

Original wording and spelling preserved below ("eunich," "Inscense," "facistic," "Morlocs" —
the patina is load-bearing); headers trimmed to essentials and the thread arranged in
chronological reading order. The parallels table is rendered as a table for readability.

### Gumby — Charles Fort died too soon (20 December 1988)

> **From:** `Gumby@mcc.com`
> **To:** Michael Travers `mt@media-lab.media.mit.edu`
> **Cc:** `SubGenius@mc.lcs.mit.edu`, `unix-haters@media-lab.media.mit.edu`, `jtw@xx.lcs.mit.edu`
> **Date:** Tue, 20 Dec 88 01:36 PST
> **Subject:** Charles Fort died too soon

Oh come on. There are those who allege that the Church is a hoax, that the SubG are no
different from your everyday Pink. What better way to keep them guessing than by linking the
Church with a genuine hoax: the unix "operating system."

Observe that once the phone company lost its monopoly hold on expensive, crappy phone
service, they immediately strangled the microcomputer world by cramming unix down its throat.
Now you can buy cheaper, better phone service, but you can hardly walk by a small computer
without smelling the unwashed stench of some unix zealot. Now, as computing power gets
better, the rate of advance is slowed by the feebleness of the operating system you're given
to run on it. And who gets a royalty on each one? The same damned phone company!

I'd believe that it was another coup by the trilateral commission if it weren't for the
obvious hand of the roman pontiff in all of this. I wonder how much of the Vatican's wealth
is in ATT stock? Consider the parallels:

if you really think Unix shouldn't be discussed on the SubG list!
