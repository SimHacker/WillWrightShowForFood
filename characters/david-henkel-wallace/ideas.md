## Hooks

### 1. Cygnus as first COSS — 1989 to now

Explain free software to customers who thought it was radical. What survived, what risks David sees for COSS today (OCS 2019 talk).

### 2. BFD — porting GNU to every chip

How a "not such a BFD" library enabled embedded bring-up under NDA — connects to Don's cross-platform shipping history (NeWS, Sims, web).

### 3. TLGnet + FidoNet — community before the web

Pair with Tom Jennings: reseller-friendly ISP vs store-and-forward BBS network — two Bay Area models of distributed access.

### 4. MIT AI Lab / Atari Cambridge — the gestural system

Watch Cynthia Solomon's videos on air: touch screens, force sensitivity, the *Rocky's Boots*
button box — with Margaret Minsky, Danny Hillis, Daniel Huttenlocher, Radia Perlman.
[Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Wq6SQTVM9M) · [`sources/hn-interactions.md`](sources/hn-interactions.md)

### 5. Charles Fort died too soon (Dec 1988)

Gumby's SubGenius/unix-haters **two-column proof**: Catholicism ↔ Unix. Michael Travers wanted usenet-weenie talk off the SubG list; Gumby argues Unix *is* the hoax religion. AT&T conspiracy, `man sh` as Inquisition, Borgia Popes = Joy & Ritchie, signed Rev. Bit-Rot C.O. Predates Cygnus; same bloodstream as Don's unix-haters X-Windows chapter.

→ [`sources/charles-fort-died-too-soon-1988.md`](sources/charles-fort-died-too-soon-1988.md) · live table-building on air

### 6. unix-haters, under oath

Gumby nominated Don for the unix-haters list in 1991 ("a much better ranter than many who DO
send to it"); Don wrote the X-Windows Disaster chapter. Settle the **Space Cadet
nitrous-and-parens rumor** on air — Don has been publicly asking Gumby to confirm it on HN
since 2016. Bring a Hefty bag as a prop.

### 7. The Sub⇒Eval upgrade ritual

In 1988, Gumby was *Rev. Bit-Rot, C.O. (Computationally Orthodox)* of the Church of the
SubGenius. MOOLLM now hosts the **Church of the Eval Genius** — "Bob"⇒"Val" Dobias,
Slack⇒declared Bias, ShorDurPerSav⇒ShorDurPerEval, X-Day⇒E-Day, SUBMIT⇒EVALUATE. Two
ordained reverends, thirty-eight years apart, compare sacraments on air; Don performs the
upgrade and re-ordains him **E.O. (Evaluationally Orthodox)**. For a Lisp hacker, EVAL and
APPLY were always the liturgy — McCarthy wrote the metacircular mass.
