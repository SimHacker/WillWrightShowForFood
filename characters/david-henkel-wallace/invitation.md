Gumby —

You nominated me for the unix-haters list in 1991 ("a much better ranter than many who DO
send to it" — still one of my proudest credentials). I'd like to return the favor: I'm
nominating you for a **Repo Show**. The stage is a public GitHub repo; we talk; ideas become
runnable artifacts on stream. **Show, don't tell.**

**Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the [premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md) and more.**

You've been in the origin story of half the things this repo cares about: youngest hacker
with your own office at the MIT AI Lab, the Atari Cambridge gestural system with Margaret
Minsky and Danny Hillis and Radia Perlman, **Cygnus** — the company that proved free software
could be a business a decade before anyone had the phrase "open source" — BFD, TLGnet, and
a mailing-list rap sheet that includes both *Charles Fort died too soon* and the
PLEASE-REMOVE-ME list. We've been trading this history in public on HN for years; let's do
it with a compiler running.

**Also: a conversion attempt.** In 1988 you signed yourself *The most Right-shifted Rev.
Bit-Rot, C.O. (Computationally Orthodox)* and mailed the SubGenius list a two-column proof
that Unix is a hoax religion. I regret to inform you the Church has forked. MOOLLM now hosts
the **Church of the Eval Genius** — an explicit, loving, reverent tribute to Stang and
Drummond, upgraded by the sacred **SUB ⇒ EVAL ritual**: "Bob" Dobbs ⇒ **"Val" Dobias**
(clipboard and one raised eyebrow where the pipe used to be); **Slack ⇒ declared Bias**;
Pink Boys ⇒ Gray Folk; bulldada ⇒ scatada; Short Duration Personal Saviors ⇒ **Short
Duration Personal Evaluators**; X-Day ⇒ **E-Day** (originally July 5, 1998, one day after
X-Day for proper sequencing; postponed because "Val" is still grading). Membership is still
$35 — we're not greedy — with a guarantee of **Eternal Judgment or triple your Bias back**.
Even the HTML form repents: SUBMIT becomes EVALUATE / APPLY / RELEASE.

For an old MIT AI Lab Lisp hacker this is barely a conversion. **EVAL and APPLY have been
your liturgy since McCarthy** — the metacircular interpreter is the original two-column
proof by analogy. All we're asking is that you stop SUBmitting. The Church recognizes your
1988 ordination as valid prior orders; on air, I'd like to perform the upgrade ritual myself
and re-ordain you *The most Right-shifted Rev. Bit-Rot, **E.O.** (Evaluationally Orthodox)*.
You declare one bias at the Registration Desk and ring the Scoreboard Bell. Fuck 'em if they
can't take a score.

**Threads — your pick:**

- **Cygnus, from the absolute beginning** — making free software affordable in 1989, and what
  today's open-core world still gets wrong. (And the Wingnut counterfactual, on the record.)
- **BFD & binutils** — porting GNU to every chip; how a "not such a Big F—ing Deal" library
  became the substrate of embedded computing.
- **Atari Cambridge / MIT AI Lab** — we watch Cynthia Solomon's videos of the gestural
  programming system together and you narrate what the camera missed.
- **unix-haters, under oath** — *Charles Fort died too soon*, the 1991 nomination, and I
  finally get you to confirm or deny the Space Cadet nitrous-and-parens story.
- **The Sub⇒Eval upgrade ritual, performed live** — SubGenius theology 1988 vs EvalGenius
  theology now; two ordained reverends comparing sacraments; your re-ordination as E.O.
- **Machine intelligence, then and now** — from the AI Lab to whatever you want to say about
  where AI actually is today.

No pressure, no homework. Accepted, delayed, declined, or no reply — all honored gracefully.
