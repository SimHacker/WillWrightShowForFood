# Warren Spector

Guest room for **Warren Spector** — immersive-sim designer (System Shock, Deus Ex, Thief), game preservationist, and the person Don has owed **The Sims** design archive to for decades.

Years ago Warren asked Don whether he had Sims design notes or materials, and they discussed Don donating his design docs, notes, and code for academic research — Warren's own papers at the [UT Videogame Archive](https://repositories.lib.utexas.edu/communities/4bf6571f-a6c3-4cd2-8258-1fa5f0d76355) were the model. Don found text notes and a fat folder of printed bug reports, object-database printouts, and handwritten diagrams — then life happened. He uploaded scanned PDFs to [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/collections/4050497/The-Sims) (Dec 2012) and published [94 PDFs on donhopkins.com](https://donhopkins.com/home/TheSimsDesignDocuments/), but the paper backlog and email `.pst` archives remain largely unprocessed.

**Aug 2026:** Warren [retired from game development](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/deus-ex-director-warren-spector-is-retiring-after-43-years) and said he is open to **lecturing and consulting**. Don sent a LinkedIn connect request to reopen the donation conversation — and to invite him into the repo's archivist company: [Phil Salvador](../phil-salvador/) (VGHF), [David Rosenthal](../david-rosenthal/) (LOCKSS), [Brewster Kahle](../brewster-kahle/) (Internet Archive), and the [PIXIE restoration](../../repo-shows/pixie-pie-menus-pdp7/README.md), where a 1969 Cambridge archive request became digitized films and is becoming running PDP-7 code.

The repo's archivist discipline — provenance policy, export pipeline, portrayal standards — is at [`process/entryways/archivist.md`](../../process/entryways/archivist.md).
