# Ray Kurzweil 📈🔮

Invitation portrayal — **not** Ray Kurzweil, and **not** [RAI](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ray-kurzweil-unveils-rai-302818934.html),
his own consented digital twin. [Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

**Ray Kurzweil** — inventor (omni-font OCR, the Kurzweil Reading Machine, the K250 synthesizer,
commercial speech recognition), author (*The Singularity Is Near*, 2005; *The Singularity Is
Nearer*, 2024), futurist (the Law of Accelerating Returns), Principal Researcher + AI Visionary
at Google. National Medal of Technology (1999); Lemelson–MIT Prize (2001); Technical Grammy (2015).

## Why we want the human, not the twin

Ray just answered the question this repo's [portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)
were written for. In July 2026 he unveiled **RAI** — a "Human-First Digital Twin" built from sixty
years of his writings and interviews, with provenance, governance, and IP protection — and appeared
[on stage alongside it](https://aiforgood.itu.int/ray-kurzweil-at-ai-for-good-global-summit-2026-we-will-not-be-replaced-by-ai-we-will-merge-with-it/)
at the UN AI for Good Summit. His earlier avatar **Ramona** (2001) was the ancestor.

This room is deliberately the opposite of a twin: a sourced portrayal *about* him that never
speaks *as* him. The show wants **the real Ray** talking about his real ideas and real
accomplishments — the singularity and nonlinear growth *from inside the curve we're now living
through*. RAI is welcome as a clearly-labeled cameo, on Ray's terms; comparing his twin
architecture with MOOLLM's consent-first portrayal architecture is itself a headline segment.

## The Minsky thread 🕯️

As a high-school student Ray corresponded with **[Marvin Minsky](../marvin-minsky/README.md)**,
visited him at MIT, and went to MIT expressly to study with him (he also visited Frank Rosenblatt
at Cornell — he knew both poles of the *Perceptrons* debate personally). He is warmly invited as a
discussant for the [Marvin Minsky memorial](../marvin-minsky/memorial.md) — alongside Marvin's son
**[Henry Minsky](../henry-minsky/README.md)** (Don's colleague at Leela AI, where Society-of-Mind
ideas are alive in production) and daughter **[Margaret](../margaret-minsky/README.md)**.
