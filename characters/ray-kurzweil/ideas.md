## What Ray has done

Built the first omni-font OCR and the Kurzweil Reading Machine (1976, first production user:
Stevie Wonder), founded Kurzweil Music Systems (K250, 1984) and Kurzweil Applied Intelligence
(speech recognition). Wrote *The Age of Spiritual Machines*, *The Singularity Is Near*, *How to
Create a Mind*, *The Singularity Is Nearer*. Proposed the Law of Accelerating Returns. National
Medal of Technology, Lemelson–MIT Prize, Technical Grammy. Principal Researcher + AI Visionary
at Google. Unveiled RAI, his own digital twin, at the UN AI for Good Summit (July 2026).

## The hooks

1. **This is it — the curve from the inside.** He published the Law of Accelerating Returns in
   1999/2001 and named a date. In 2026 we are visibly *on* the knee of the curve he drew.
   A 2026 checkpoint in his own words: what landed early, what's late, what surprised him.
   His own "How My Predictions Are Faring" self-audit vs. the Forbes/IEEE Spectrum scorecards —
   the prediction ledger as a live segment.

2. **Digital twins with consent — RAI meets the portrayal standards.** This repo's rule is
   "portrayal about, never speaking as." Ray shipped the other answer: a consented twin built
   from his own archive, with provenance and governance. Ramona (2001) → RAI (2026). What does
   he require of anyone representing a real mind? Direct dialog with MOOLLM's
   representation-ethics — the show about the show.

3. **The kid who wrote to Marvin** 🕯️. High-school correspondence with Marvin Minsky → MIT to
   study with him; visits to Rosenblatt at Cornell — both poles of *Perceptrons* known
   personally. Memorial-mode segment for [Marvin Minsky](../marvin-minsky/memorial.md) with
   Henry, Margaret, and Gary Drescher. Don works with Henry at Leela AI, where Society-of-Mind
   ideas run in production — Pattern Recognition Theory of Mind × Society of Mind × Made-Up
   Minds, one MIT lineage on one stage.

4. **Invention timing as surfing.** His documented creative method: most inventions fail because
   the timing is wrong, so anticipate the enabling technologies and catch the wave. Reading
   machine (CCD scanner + TTS arriving just in time), K250 (Stevie Wonder's lament → sampling
   synthesis), OCR → LexisNexis. Will Wright's possibility-space design vocabulary is a natural
   rhyme — invention as navigating what's about to become possible.

5. **Music machines.** The K250 story — from I've Got a Secret (1965, piano piece composed by
   his homebuilt computer) to the Technical Grammy (2015). Pairs with the show's music thread
   (David Levitt — MIT AI music lineage; Jerry Martin).
