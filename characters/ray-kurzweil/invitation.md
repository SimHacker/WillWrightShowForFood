## Draft (message shape)

Dear Ray,

I'm Don Hopkins — I worked with Will Wright on The Sims at Maxis, and before that on SimCity,
NeWS, and pie menus. I'd like to invite you onto a **Repo Show**: a friendly recorded
conversation whose stage is a public GitHub repository — rooms, characters, and ideas as files,
carried through to working code and credited ideas in the open. **Will Wright has signed on for
the premiere.**

Two invitations in one:

**1. The real you, on the record, from inside the curve.** You described the Law of Accelerating
Returns decades ago; we're now visibly living through the nonlinear part. I want the human Ray —
not a summary, not an avatar — talking about the real ideas and real accomplishments: the reading
machine and Stevie Wonder, the K250, the prediction ledger (your self-audit *and* your critics',
side by side), and what 2026 looks like against the 1999/2005 charts.

There's also a meta-conversation we may be uniquely positioned to have with you. Our show keeps
public "portrayal" directories about guests — sourced, consent-first, never speaking *as* anyone
([standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md)).
You just shipped your own answer to that problem: RAI, with provenance and governance built in.
Ramona → RAI is a 25-year arc. We'd love to compare architectures — and if you'd like RAI to make
a clearly-labeled cameo on your terms, that's a segment nobody else can do.

**2. Remembering Marvin Minsky.** You wrote to Marvin from high school, visited him, and went to
MIT to study with him. We keep a [memorial room](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/marvin-minsky/memorial.md)
for Marvin — we represent and discuss him, never interview or impersonate — and we invite the
people who knew him to tell the real stories. I work at **Leela AI with Marvin's son Henry**
(co-founder/CTO) and Milan Singh Minsky, where Society-of-Mind ideas run in production code;
Margaret is in the cast too. A conversation with you, Henry, and Margaret about Marvin — from
the kid-who-wrote-a-letter to *How to Create a Mind* — would honor him the way he'd want:
with ideas, and jokes.
