## Who

**Dr.-Ing. Mario A. Cypko** — Head of **Medical AI and Modeling (MedAIM)** at [**Hahn-Schickard**](https://www.hahn-schickard.de/en/digital-health-research-night)
(Freiburg); lecturer at **University of Freiburg**. Tagline on [mariocypko.com](https://www.mariocypko.com/):
*machine intelligence-assisted technology, properly applied, can improve patient safety and quality of life.*

PhD (**University of Leipzig / ICCAS**, 2017) on multidisciplinary oncology decision support with
**Bayesian networks** — supervisors **Heinz U. Lemke** and Prof. Andreas Dietz, MD. Published by
[Springer](https://www.springer.com/de/book/9783658325930). Diploma (**Freie Universität Berlin**, 2013,
medical informatics + psychology minor) — diploma thesis also under Lemke.

Postdoctoral stations: **ESA** Human Research (Noordwijk), **German Heart Center Berlin**, **Matters of
Activity / FU Berlin Human-Centered Computing**. Visiting professor, **Tokyo University of Agriculture
and Technology** (per mariocypko.com).

## Why he's here

**Heinz Lemke** (15 July 2026) Cc'd Cypko on the PIXIE thread and will encourage him to present on
**Human–AI Model Interaction** at the proposed **CARS 2027 Berlin HCI session** (29 June 2027).

Longer arc in this repo:

- **PhD/diploma lineage** from Heinz — Model-Guided Medicine and **Medical Model Science**
- **CARS** organizer/program committee; MGM workshops with Heinz (e.g. CARS 2024 Barcelona)
- **Barn video** — patient-modelling filmed at Heinz's Black Forest farmhouse ([`../heinz-lemke/correspondence.yml`](../heinz-lemke/correspondence.yml) — consent `not_yet_asked`)

Cypko advocates **transparent, inspectable clinical models** with **human-in-the-loop** — connecting
Heinz's 1969 *graphical man-machine communication* (PIXIE) to today's model-guided medicine.
