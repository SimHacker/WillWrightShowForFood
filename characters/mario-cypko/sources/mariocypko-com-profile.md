## Current role

**Head, Medical AI and Modeling (MedAIM)** — Hahn-Schickard, Freiburg (from Apr 2023)  
**Lecturer** — University of Freiburg

MedAIM focus (about-me page):

- Knowledge modelling for **cancer care** and **transplant follow-up**
- Continuous patient monitoring via **smart wearables**
- **Trustworthy clinical AI**, multimodal patient representations, **Model-Guided Medicine**
- **Medical Model Science** — term from doctoral supervisor **Heinz Lemke**
- **LLM-guided knowledge modelling** with human oversight and model governance
