## Medical Model Science / Model-Guided Medicine (MGM)

Cypko ([about-me](https://www.mariocypko.com/about-me)) advocates **Medical Model Science** as coined/
developed by his doctoral supervisor **Heinz U. Lemke** — treating clinical models as inspectable
scientific objects (validation, lifecycle, governance, human-AI interaction), not only ML by-products.

## Heinz Lemke editorial (2024)

**Title:** *Model guided medicine and the search for truth*  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s11548-024-03097-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-024-03097-z)  
**Journal:** International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery

Notable beats:

- Traces model-driven intelligence to **Maurice Wilkes** and 1960s **CAD** — parallel to PIXIE show spine
- Quotes **Joseph Weizenbaum**: *"Der Mensch, nicht die Maschine, ist das Maß!"* / *"The Human Being, not the Machine, is the Term of Reference!"*
- CARS 2024 dedicated MGM sessions and workshop on *Model Guided Medicine and the Search for Truth*
- References **Wilhelm D, Cypko M (2024)** on MGM and AI containment (CARS 2024 proceedings supplement)

## KI-Compass project

[Uni Freiburg press release](https://www.uniklinik-freiburg.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/detailansicht/6855-ki-gestuetzte-nachsorge-nach-krebstherapie-soll-herz-probleme-frueher-zeigen.html):

- **KI-Compass** — AI-supported follow-up after esophageal cancer therapy
- Smartwatch vitals + clinical records → **explainable digital patient models**
- Consortium: Hahn-Schickard, BiSigma, Uni Freiburg clinics, Digestive Cancers Europe
- **Overall project lead:** Mario Cypko (Hahn-Schickard)
- BMBF-funded (Förderkennzeichen 16SV9610)
