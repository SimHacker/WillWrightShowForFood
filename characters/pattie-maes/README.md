## Who she is

Pattie Maes is a professor at the MIT Media Lab, where she founded the Software Agents group and
now leads the Fluid Interfaces group. Before the Media Lab she did her PhD at the Vrije Universiteit
Brussel's AI Lab and worked on behaviour-based action selection with Rodney Brooks's group at the
MIT AI Lab. Her 1994 *CACM* paper "Agents that Reduce Work and Information Overload" set out the case
for learning interface agents, and her group's collaborative-filtering work became Firefly, which
she co-founded and which Microsoft acquired in 1998.

In 1997 she debated Ben Shneiderman on direct manipulation versus interface agents, at IUI 97 in
January and again at CHI 97 in March. The *interactions* transcript of the IUI session became the
field's standard citation for a dichotomy, though it ends with the two of them agreeing.

## Why she's here

Her own list of agent UI requirements from that debate (make the user model available, continuous
feedback, variable autonomy that the user decides, user programming of the agent, the language of
the application) is the program of [interface to agency](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/designs/INTERFACE-TO-AGENCY.md),
and its "user programming" bullet is programming by demonstration, the synthesis the debate never
named. Her lab neighbours in this repo: [Henry Lieberman](../henry-lieberman/README.md) (cited by
her from the IUI 97 stage), [Marvin Minsky](../marvin-minsky/README.md), [Tod Machover](../tod-machover/README.md),
and [Cyrus Shaoul](../cyrus-shaoul/README.md), who was at her talk.
