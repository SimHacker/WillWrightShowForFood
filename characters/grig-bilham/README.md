# Grig Bilham 🌐

*Invitation guest — consent not yet asked. [Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md).*

**Laszlo alum who went all-in on public goods.** Grig's current constellation reads like
an infrastructure wish-list for a healthier internet: **Universal Manifest** (the
permissions and control layer the internet is missing), the **Open Spatial Lab** at
peers.social — where he designed, built, and shipped the SIGGRAPH proof of concept for
the **Web of Worlds** standard (W3C-published, developed at the **Metaverse Standards
Forum**) plus the **spatial fabric** standard and a new open-source rendering engine for
spatial fabrics and WASM widgets — **peers.social** (an open stack for building your own
platform while sharing network effects), **localartist.network** (hyper-local digital
signage for creatives), and **Distributed Creatives**, the nonprofit keeping it all
pointed at communities.

**The preservation thread.** **EverArchive** is building a foundation for **200–1000 year
storage** of creative work with self-sovereign publishing tools — he did a panel with
**Brewster Kahle** at the first Distributed Creatives conference (held on Forbes Island,
towed to the San Francisco Delta for the occasion), and works with **Bram Cohen** on Chia
standards for intermediary-free digital lending via atomic peer-to-peer swaps. This show
does preservation by RUN; Grig does preservation by MILLENNIUM. Same religion, different
liturgy.

**The kicker.** Jul 2026, closing a warm reconnection thread: *"I was working on a
circular menu today, and of course thought of you and the study you did."* A man putting
circular menus into an open metaverse is already halfway on the show.
