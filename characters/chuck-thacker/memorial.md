# In memoriam — Chuck Thacker (1943–2017)

*Memorial mode: represent and discuss documented work; do not speak as him.*
[Memorial standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode)

**Charles P. Thacker** designed the **Xerox Alto** — the personal computer that made Smalltalk
and the modern desktop feel real — and **MAXC**, PARC's homebrew PDP-10. With Butler Lampson he
turned Alan Kay's "kiddicomp" budget into working iron (first unit nicknamed **Bilbo**). Later
work at DEC and Microsoft (including Tablet PC hardware) continued the personal-computing line.
Turing Award 2009.

We remember him by inviting people who shared the Alto years — Alan Kay, Butler Lampson, Dan
Ingalls — to tell the real stories.
