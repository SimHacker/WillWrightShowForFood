Brenda —

We'd be **honored** to have you (and **Rob**, if you're both up for it) on a **Repo Show** — a live,
friendly conversation whose stage is [this public repository](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood).
**Show, don't tell:** ideas become running code, archives, and playable moments on stream.

I was at **Interval Research** in the Camelot years — and before I joined **Maxis** for **The Sims**, I
watched your **games-for-girls** research with real admiration. It was enlightening work, worth doing on its
own terms — and it still informs how I think about **The Sims**, **Purple Moon**, and **computers as theatre**.

**Threads — your pick (solo, with Rob, or both):**

- **Placeholder** — *Landscape and Narrative in a Virtual Environment* with Rob and **Rachel Strickland**
  (Terry Winograd's **CS547** archive still has that talk on tape). What would you build differently in 2026?
- **_Computers as Theatre_** — dramatic structure for interfaces; the Repo Show as a **medium for telling
  creators' stories** (not a podcast about stories).
- **Purple Moon & gender + technology** — honest post-mortem: research value vs market; what **The Sims**
  accidentally got from a different path (ambiguity, user content, Simulator Effect).
- **Interval reunion** — reignite the community; **make NEW videos and share them this time** (not the lost
  tapes). Who else from that era should be in the room?
- **Utopian Entrepreneur** — design research that ships; what's still missing in "new software worlds."

No homework trap. Remote is fine. Accepted, delayed, declined, or no reply — all honored gracefully.

— Don Hopkins *(User Interface Flower Child)*
