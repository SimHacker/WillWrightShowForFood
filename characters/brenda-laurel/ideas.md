## What Brenda has done

**Brenda Laurel** — author of **_Computers as Theatre_**; Interval Research **gender + technology**
research; founder of **Purple Moon** (games for girls); **_Utopian Entrepreneur_**. A foundational voice
for **dramatic, participatory HCI** — interfaces as staged experience, not control panels.

## Shared ground

Don at **Interval Research** (~mid-1990s) in the same circle as Brenda and **Rob Tow**. Don saw Purple Moon
research **before Maxis** and admired it ([Jenkins / Barbie–Mortal Kombat note](../will-wright/sources/2004-01-10-barbie-mortalkombat-jenkins/article.md)).

### 2. Computers as Theatre → Repo Show as medium
Dramatic structure for interaction; **direct engagement**; the show as theatre where creators **see their
own faces** (HyperCard test, Alan Kay thread).

### 3. Purple Moon vs The Sims — two design ethics
Research-driven "games for girls" vs Will's **Simulator Effect** / ambiguity / user content. Don's honest
take: both experiments worth learning from. Co-guest **Will Wright** (careful framing).

### 4. Gender + technology — what shipped, what didn't
Four-year Interval research → spin-off; pink boxes; market vs insight. No dunking — **archive and learn**.

### 5. Interval reunion — NEW videos this time

**David Levitt**, **Coco Conn**, **Laurie Anderson** (Talking Stick bridge).
