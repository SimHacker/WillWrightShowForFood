# Remembering Larry Tesler (1945–2020)

**Lawrence Gordon Tesler** made editing *stay* — modeless interaction and the cut/copy/paste
verbs that ordinary people now treat as physics. At Xerox PARC he worked in the Smalltalk /
UI culture; in 1979 he demoed Smalltalk to Steve Jobs's Apple team. He then helped carry those
ideas into Lisa and Macintosh.

We cite him; we do not speak as him. Discussants on air: living colleagues who knew the work
([Alan Kay](../alan-kay/), [Dan Ingalls](../dan-ingalls/), [Adele Goldberg](../adele-goldberg/)).
