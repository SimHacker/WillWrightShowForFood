**Bill St. Clair** — Lisp hacker (MIT-orbit career; now Knoxville; Mammudeck / Elm for fun). The
person who confirms Don was not dreaming: at **Atari Cambridge Research** (Q1 **1984**) he turned a
**Star Wars** arcade cabinet into a **turtle-graphics output device** for a **Symbolics Lisp Machine**
(new ROM; original ROM swapped back to play the game), plus a **3D turtle** eye-steering system.

That cabinet's industrial design is **[Mike Jang](../mike-jang/)**'s Star Wars cockpit — memorial
bridge: coin-op iron → research Lisp Machines.
