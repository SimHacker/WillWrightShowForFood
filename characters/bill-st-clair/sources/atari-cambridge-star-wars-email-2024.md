## Public-safe quotes (Bill)

> I was hired to turn the Star Wars arcade game into a turtle graphics device. I did this by
> burning a new ROM for the machine. But we put the original ROM back in the machine often, and
> played the Star Wars arcade game.

> The hardware provided vector graphics, with a display that stored the output for half a second
> or so, so you had to redraw your display list often.

> I also wrote a 3D turtle system for the Lisp Machine. You could steer the eye around and see
> what it was looking at.
