## Who

**Andrew Quitmeyer** — **Stupid Fun Club intern**; **Georgia Tech** (studied under **Janet Murray**);
founder of [**Digital Naturalism Laboratories**](https://www.youtube.com/@DigitalNaturalism) ("hiking
hacker who likes to play in nature"). Known for playful ubicomp — especially [**Mark Your Territory**](sources/mark-your-territory.md)
(**Pee-to-Check-In-to-Foursquare**, released [Oct 31, 2011](https://www.quitmeyer.org/updates/mark-your-territory-release/)):
open-source [Android ADK + Arduino](https://github.com/quitmeyer/Mark-Your-Territory---Pee-to-Check-In-to-Foursquare)
bodily Foursquare check-in. [Explainer video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rRdfeb7vDWg) — Don commented
*"Badass. Great idea."* Featured [Instructables](https://www.instructables.com/id/Pee-to-Check-In-to-Foursquare-Mark-Your-Territor/);
[Pop-Up City](https://popupcity.net/insights/pee-to-check-in-to-foursquare-and-mark-your-territory/) reports Foursquare called it
*"possibly the best API app ever!"*

Posted [**Nancy the Van Seat**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fvXG4gQ2FWI) (Aug 2011) — a driveable
Stupid Fun Club creation. Don commented on the video.
