# Mark Your Territory

**Pee-to-Check-In-to-Foursquare** — Andrew Quitmeyer's open-source ubicomp art project (Oct 2011).
Arduino moisture sensor + Android ADK + Foursquare API. Bodily check-in as territory marking.

## What it is (verified)

- **Released Oct 31, 2011** — Andrew prepping paper for **TEI 2012** ([release post](https://www.quitmeyer.org/updates/mark-your-territory-release/))
- **Stack:** Android phone + **Arduino ADK** board + moisture-sensing probe → Foursquare check-in
- **Markers:** laser-cut absorbent paper stakes (litmus cabbage juice, spray-paint reveal, embedded seeds)
- **Open source:** Java Android app + Arduino firmware on GitHub (3 commits, ~2011)

## Design features (from Instructables / project docs)

Andrew's stated affordances of *physical* vs purely digital check-in:

| Feature | What |
|---------|------|
| **Check-in gradient** | Amount of urine → Foursquare rating strength |
| **Unbiased profile** | Urine pH reveals diet — "description you cannot cheat" |
| **Cross-species communication** | Animals read the mark; squirrels don't know Foursquare |
| **True ephemera** | Physical reputation maintenance is demanding |
| **Indirect environmental restructuring** | Seeds in markers regrow after decomposition |

## Press & reception

**Pop-Up City** (Joop de Boer, Dec 2012) reports Andrew said Foursquare officials contacted him, loved the app, and called it **"possibly the best API app ever!"**

Andrew, quoted in Pop-Up City on cross-species legibility:

> "squirrels know nothing of your Foursquare status but can tell where you peed"

**Don Hopkins** commented on the [explainer video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rRdfeb7vDWg): *"Badass. Great idea."*

Instructables (**blorgggg** = Andrew): Featured; contest wins — Hack It!, Pocket-Sized, 2nd Epilog Challenge.
