## Who he is

James Fogarty was in the first cohort of PhD students at Carnegie Mellon's Human-Computer
Interaction Institute and has been on the faculty of the University of Washington since 2006, where
he is a Professor in the Paul G. Allen School and helped build its DUB group.

Much of his work changes software from outside. With Morgan Dixon he built
[Prefab](https://prefab.github.io/), which recovers an interface's structure from its pixels so any
application can be enhanced without its source (CHI 2010 Best Paper). With Xiaoyi Zhang, Anne
Spencer Ross and others he developed interaction proxies (CHI 2017), which repair the accessibility
of mobile apps while they run, and led the first large-scale assessment of accessibility in 10,000
Android apps. He also studies how people track their own health data, work that has informed
features people use every day. He was elected to the SIGCHI Academy in 2025
([UW news](https://news.cs.washington.edu/2025/04/23/allen-school-professor-james-fogarty-inducted-into-sigchi-academy-class-of-2025)).

## Why he's here

Prefab and interaction proxies are the research behind
[Screen Angel](https://github.com/SimHacker/MicropolisCore/blob/main/apps/screen-angel/ACCESSIBLIFY.yml)'s
plan to recognize interfaces from pixels and repair their accessibility from outside.
