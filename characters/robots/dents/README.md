# Dents 🤖💔

*Sniff:* [CARD.yml](CARD.yml) · [GLANCE.yml](GLANCE.yml)

**Fictional character** — not a human guest invitation.

![Dents on an Oakland sidewalk during the Empathy hidden-camera shoot — fallen robot, cameraman on tripod](../slats/slats-oakland-broken-robot-street.png)

**Dents** is the broken robot from Will Wright's Stupid Fun Club **One Minute Movie — *Empathy***
(2003). Fallen on an Oakland sidewalk, pleading *"Help me — I require Human Assistance"* while
passersby fumble. Don Hopkins drove Dents live from the **FMC Motorcoach** across the street — an
ensemble crew talking and typing together behind shaded windows.

**Slats** (*Servitude*) is the sibling robot — eager waiter, validation junkie. Same teleoperation
rig, different body, different film. See [Slats](../slats/README.md).

**Primary source:** [one-minute-movie-empathy.md](one-minute-movie-empathy.md) · **Overview:** [One Minute Movies](../slats/one-minute-movies.md)

## The voice

Frightened, literal, escalating distress meeting confused human concern — the empathy **mismatch**
is the joke. When r/shittyrobots met the films in 2022, **Dents outscored Slats forty to one**
([receipts](../../will-wright/sources/stupid-fun-club-one-minute-movies/2022-04-28-reddit-shittyrobots.md))
— the broken robot ran the better con.

*I'm scared. Are you scared of me?*
