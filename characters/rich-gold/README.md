## Why he matters to the bridge work

He designed a person who lives on a floppy disk and whose personality persists across power cycles,
in 1985, and then argued that you should not be able to interact with him. Crane overruled him and
shipped the interactive version. Fifteen years later Will Wright made the operable version of the
same idea and took feedback from Gold while doing it.

The Federation's headwater target is that 1985 person: 256 bytes of brain in a known block, unique per
physical disk because Activision serialized every copy at the duplicator and used the number as the
seed. Reading him out and giving him neighbours is [the tribute](memorial.md#the-tribute), and it is
also the oldest working test of whether a soul bridge is real.

## The argument he lost, which is now everyone's problem

Ambient companion you watch, or operable simulation you drive. Gold wanted the fishbowl. Crane wanted
the interaction. That is the same argument as agents-versus-direct-manipulation, thirty years of it,
and these two ran the experiment on one house with room for one man and one dog.
