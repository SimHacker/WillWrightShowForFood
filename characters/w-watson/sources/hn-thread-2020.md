# The Pervert's Guide on Hacker News — April 2020

**Story:** The pervert's guide to computer programming languages (2017) [video]

https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22910702

Posted April 2020. Backstory: Don found the talk, emailed Watson, and dang liked it enough to
post the video to HN himself. Don then talked Watson into joining the thread, where he answered
questions under his own name.

## The digest that opened the thread

A top commenter condensed the taxonomy so well Watson's readers preferred it to the talk:

> Psychopaths: I don't care about rules, types, or beauty, just get it done (JS)
>
> Obsessives: a little monotony now avoids a lot of pain later (go, java)
>
> Masochists: Look at how disciplined I am! (C)
>
> Sadists: Look at how impotent everyone else is! (perl, regex)
>
> Hysterics: Look at how beautiful this code is! (python)
>
> Fetishists: Have you tried more X? (objects, abstractions, types) (smalltalk, erlang)
>
> Melancholy: Oh, I miss the days when I could write an entire application in 200 lines of Lisp...

From the video's own slides: Psychopath (JavaScript, PHP, VBA, SQL), Masochist (C, Assembly,
Ada, Brainfuck, Factor), Sadist (Perl, Clojure, Erlang, Regex), Fetishistic (Erlang, Smalltalk,
Elixir, Factor), Obsessional (Java, C++, Haskell, Scala, Go), Hysterical (Python, Ruby, C#,
Prolog, Elixir), Depression (COBOL), Melancholy (Lisp, Scheme, Smalltalk).

## Watson in the thread

He answered as himself. Highlights:

- **Erlang as sadist, defended:** "The sadist (causing anxiety to the other in order to get the
  other to announce the rules) wants the other to admit their impotence... The erlang community
  wants the other to admit that they can't develop software that has nine nines of availability."
- **Mind maps:** hand drawn and made with SimpleMind (Loomio is a voting tool, resolving Don's
  question).
- **Journal publication:** hadn't published anywhere; open to suggestions.
- On why he'd take language-choice psychoanalysis seriously: language choice is wrapped up with
  power and ideology, Žižek's home turf.

## Don in the thread

- Quoted Bruce Fink's *Clinical Introduction to Lacanian Psychoanalysis* to explain that
  "pervert" is not derogatory in Lacan, and that Lacan classifies the **Marquis de Sade as a
  masochist** — so the resident Perl/Regex sadists are probably "the good kind."
- Retold the Rees objectology menu and the "Pervert's Guide to Objectology" wish that led him to
  the talk in the first place.
- On Perl and Bash as job security: "optimized for" writing obscure unmaintainable code that
  forces employers to admit the impotence of firing you — "while on the other hand, Java is
  designed to appeal to management by making software developers fungible easily disposable
  worker-bees."
- Melancholy correction: "Obviously Lisp, Scheme, and Smalltalk are not melancholy languages
  because you are only melancholy about them when you are not using them."
- Turbo Pascal as the first ex you truly cared about.

## Thread treasures

- "As someone who has primarily worked with Perl, JavaScript, SQL during their career, and who
  has a penchant for regular expressions, I'm a sadistic psychopath."
- Hoon (Urbit): "a sadistic language implemented by a hysterical personality." Rejoinder:
  "Subjecting Urbit to Lacanian/Žižekian psychoanalysis is like feeding Zippy the Pinhead quotes
  into ELIZA, just without the computer."
- Clojure's move from melancholy to sadism: "Injects itself into the corporate scene, then
  subverts from within." ("Hickey's glasses, probably.")
- Ada as "bondage and discipline language" — a joke traced in-thread to Richard Riehle, ~1993.
- The Perl-vs-Java merger story: Perl team shipped the XML integration in three weeks; the Java
  company hadn't scheduled the design meeting; the Perl company nearly doubled turnover and
  absorbed the other.
- Someone linked **Deldo** ("Vibration Control and Teledildonics Mode for Emacs") as the better
  explanation of which languages correspond to which fetishes — the same Kyle Machulis lineage
  as [Tom Lord's Buttplug thread](../../tom-lord/teledildonics-hn-2020.md).
- Žižek's sex-toy joke: bring the toys on dates, let them do their thing while the humans
  discuss philosophy and sip tea.
- Timothy Leary escaping prison by acing his own psychological test (the Leary Interpersonal
  Behavior Inventory) into a gardening assignment.
- **dang, personally moderating the bottom of the thread:** "I haven't listened to the talk but
  please let's not overreact to the title. It's a clever reference, and I'd rather not have to
  change it to 'Psychoanalysis and Software', which might be just as baity anyhow."
