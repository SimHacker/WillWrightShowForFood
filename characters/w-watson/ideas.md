# W. Watson — show ideas

*Draft — nothing leaves the workshop without Watson's consent.*

## 1. The Pervert's Guide to Objectology 🛋️ (the headliner)

Watson's own standing offer from April 2020. Rees' OO à la carte menu as the text; Lacanian
categories as the lens; Don's objectology archive as the material evidence:

- NeWS and PostScript (a Lisp in drag) — what was the object of desire?
- HyperLook, prototypes vs classes — Self envy as a clinical category
- The Sims' object system — SimAntics, advertisements, the libidinal economy of *literal*
  objects advertising enjoyment to simulated people (this is not even a metaphor)
- JavaScript's prototype system claiming Self as inspiration while missing the point —
  the 2006 Tom Lord conversation as read-aloud evidence

Live segment: **diagnose the audience.** Chat names a language; Watson charts it; Don supplies
the archaeology.

## 2. The paper, republished

With Watson's blessing: extract the 2017 PDF into markdown in this repo, full credit, all
diagrams, deep-linkable headers — the thing Don asked for in his first 2020 email ("everybody
will want to link to the one about their favorite (or hated) programming language").

## 3. Software ideology, the heavier session

Four Elements of Software Ideology + The Plague of Programming Languages. Slower, more serious
episode; maybe pair with a philosopher guest.

## 4. Worker co-ops and Loomio

Vulk as a worker co-op; Austin Software Co-operatives; group decision software; ideology
critique applied to how software shops organize themselves. Connects to the show's
anti-sharecropping instincts.

## 5. Lacan for programmers

His "How to Read Lacan" review series and mind maps. The Partially Examined Life community
hates Lacan; Watson went in anyway. Don plays the resistant analysand.

## 6. Tom Lord, in absentia

The introduction Don made in June 2020, the argument that never got finished. Watson +
Don discuss Tom's extension-language ideology through the Lacanian lens — with Trina's
blessing, per memorial standards.
