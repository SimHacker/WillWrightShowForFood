## Performance vs demo — you wrote the doctrine

Your Mar 1990 director's notes are still the clearest statement of why
**Empowered** existed:

- The audience should leave asking *"what if I could do that myself?"* — not
  *"when will Windows do that?"*
- Short, emotional, inexplicable in enumeration — papers and the demo track
  explain mechanisms; the performance forum explains **trust**.
- Practical UI on stage beats VR-only spectacle for answering *"what does it do
  for me?"*

The Space Inventory is the same ethic with different costumes: SimCity + CA
cabaret instead of pie menus + PSIBER. Ask Craig whether Repo Show segments
should be tagged **performance** or **demo** — and who decides.

## Glenn Gould and "every user an artist"

You closed the director's notes with Gould on art making itself unnecessary.
MOOLLM's pitch is the same: directories as rooms, characters as prototypes,
every player an author. Does Craig still believe the CHI'90 audience left as
artists? What would falsify that?

network effects. What broke when scaling from 500-seat Steinbeck Forum to
multi-night repeats?
