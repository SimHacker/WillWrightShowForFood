## Who

**Craig Hubley** is a Toronto-based consultant and long-time SIGCHI community
organizer who, in 1989–1992, invented and ran CHI's **Interactive Performance**
venue — live human–computer theatre for an audience of HCI professionals. He
commissioned Don Hopkins's **Empowered** show at CHI'90 Seattle after seeing
Don's blindfolded pie-menu virtuoso act at CHI'88.

## CHI'90 — Empowered

As **CHI'90 Performance Chair**, Craig negotiated travel, Sun/Barco logistics,
and — critically — the *dramatic* shape of Don's act. His Mar 1990 director's
notes rejected demo framing and imposed drumrolls, audience chants, and the
**gesture interface** thesis:

> *"By pushing the pie-menu interface to the limit you get a gesture interface
> that ties the tools so tightly and personally to their user that they
> effectively become one cooperative entity."*

Ben Shneiderman photographed Don on stage ([photo](../don-hopkins/sources/1989-05-25-hubley-chi90-interactive-performers-cfp/assets/don-hopkins-chi90-empowered-shneiderman.png)).
Full archive:
[CHI'90 Empowered provenance](../don-hopkins/sources/1989-05-25-hubley-chi90-interactive-performers-cfp/README.md).

**Hubley (2007 LinkedIn):**

> *"Don's software development and testing methodology is nothing short of
> performance art. I literally put him on stage in front of most of the user
> interface designers in the world in 1990…"*

**Don (2007 reply):**

> *"I really appreciate your help organizing my thoughts, focusing my
> presentation, and giving me the confidence required to go up on stage and do
> all that risky stuff in real time."*

## Why he matters to the show

Craig is the original **co-designer of performance-as-HCI** — the person who
insisted the forum was emotional, not technical, and that audiences should
leave believing every user could be an artist. Repo Show is the same bet on a
forkable public stage. Inviting him as **format co-designer**, not merely guest.
