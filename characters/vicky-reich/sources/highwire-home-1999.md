# Vicky Reich's home page at HighWire Press (1999 capture)

A summary of her Stanford page as captured by the Wayback Machine on 2 February 1999
([the capture](https://web.archive.org/web/19990202071752/http://highwire.stanford.edu/%7Evreich/);
115 captures, 1996–2017). Her contact details on that page are left out here.

**Position then:** Assistant Director, HighWire Press and HighWire Marketing Group, Knowledge
Enterprise Division, Stanford University Libraries and Academic Resources.

**What she described doing:** working with scholarly societies to put high-impact science and
medical literature on the web, with the aim of improving scholarly communication and its economic
models; directing part of a Mellon Foundation grant to put Stanford University Press's Latin
American monographs online, with a focus on business models and interfaces; serving as the
Libraries' expert on copyright and intellectual property, and advocating fair use in the digital
environment; and earlier, working on the Stanford Digital Library Project (NSF/ARPA/NASA) and the
Computer Science Technical Reports project (ARPA).

**Talks, 1994–98 (selection):** "Electronic Publishing: The HighWire Experience" (NASIG, 1998);
"Stanford University's HighWire Press — The Academy Fights Back" (UC Digital Library Seminar, 1998);
"The Stanford Digital Library Project" for the Gore–Chernomyrdin Telecommunications Working Group
(Moscow, 1998); keynote at the Sun Microsystems Library Conference (Shanghai, 1997); "Digital
Library Initiative Project Overview," with Rebecca Lasher and Terry Winograd (1995).

**Publications (selection):**

- Vicky Reich and Mark Weiser, "Libraries Are More Than Information: Situational Aspects of
  Electronic Libraries," *Serials Review* 20(3), 1994, pp. 31–37; also Xerox PARC CSL-93-21.
- Vicky Reich and Terry Winograd, "Working Assumptions About the Digital Library," Stanford Digital
  Library Project.
- Rebecca Lasher, Vicky Reich and Greg Anderson, "The Computer Science Technical Report (CS-TR)
  Project: Considerations from the Library Perspective," STAN-CS-TR-95-1554.
- Carl H. Gotsch and Vicky Reich, "Electronic Publishing of Scientific Journals: Effects on Users,
  Publishers, and Libraries," Council on Library Resources, 1997.
- Library of Congress work in the 1980s, including an optical disk pilot study (*College and
  Research Libraries*, 1986) and the 1982 online public catalog users survey.
- Manfred Kochen, Victoria Reich and Lee Cohen, "Influence of Online Bibliographic Services on
  Student Behavior," *JASIS* 32, 1981.

**Previous positions, as listed:** Chief, Serials and Acquisitions, Stanford; Chief, Serials
Department, Stanford University Libraries; Head, Acquisitions Branch, National Agricultural Library;
Planning Librarian and Science Reference Librarian, Library of Congress; Head Librarian, Mental Health
Research Institute, University of Michigan; Natural Science Library, University of Michigan;
Literature Searcher, The Upjohn Company.
