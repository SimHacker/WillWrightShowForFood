## What is a Repo Show?

The center of gravity is a **GitHub repo**, not just an mp3 or mp4. I
announce a show and open the repo: questions, artifacts, code, papers,
links, videos. The audience participates and learns *before* the
conversation, follows along live or time-shifted, and keeps contributing
forever after. **Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the premiere and
more**, and the guest list is filling with people whose work intersects
yours in ways you'll enjoy discovering.

## What I'd love to talk about (your pick, any subset)

- **Lem, Kandel, and translation.** Your "One hundred and thirty-seven
  seconds" and the translator's essay; how Kandel pulled off the
  Electronic Bard; Polish as the source language of half my bookshelf;
  what gets carried and what gets left at the border.
- **Bitic literature.** Lem coined the term for machine-written books —
  and we've been making some, honestly labeled, with the process shown:
  a machine-written review of a Lem book that doesn't exist
  ([COVFEFE-FUTURES](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/designs/COVFEFE-FUTURES.md)).
  I want the opinion of someone who has actually *translated* Lem on what
  counts as apocrypha and what counts as theft.
- **Pac-Man reverence.** Your doodle put Pac-Man on the world's front
  page; I put a giant PacBot in SimCity and built it a church. The shared
  ethic: how to touch a classic without spoiling it — the exact thing you
  told me you feared with the doodle and I feared with SimCity.
- **Keyboards and input.** Shift Happens, 150 years of keyboards; my
  decades of pie menus. Two people who each picked one input device and
  refused to let go.
- **Writing about technology with love and receipts.** Your Medium era,
  underlines, fonts at Figma, the typewriter museum in Spain — and how
  long-form, deeply-linked writing survives platform weather (this repo's
  answer: a repo).
