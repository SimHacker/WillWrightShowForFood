At KDE you said why windows overlap is "a long story… Buy me a beer and we'll talk about

named: windows, files, and that invisible clipboard. Your "a little bit like pie menus if
you're familiar with that" made me smile.

## What is a Repo Show?

The center of gravity is a **GitHub repo**, not just an mp3 or mp4. I
announce a show and open the repo: questions, artifacts, code, papers,
links, videos. The audience participates and learns *before* the
conversation, follows along live or time-shifted, and keeps contributing
forever after. **Will Wright is in — he's signed
on to do the [premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md) and more.**

**Your room:** https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/tree/main/characters/scott-jenson

## What I'd love to talk about (your pick, any subset)

- **The long story**: why windows overlap, the 342-pixel Mac, and the 1985 *Methodology
  of Window Management* workshop that called tiling vs overlapping "merely options".
- **Style, structure, strategy, stuff**, and what's in the stuff layer we should dig up.
- **The clipboard**: your visual and per-document clipboards next to Ted Nelson's decades
  of complaint and David Rosenthal's ICCCM selections. We're putting together an
  "Unnatural Selection" panel on exactly this, and you'd be a great fourth chair.
- **Spatial, associative, episodic**: your three prototypes, and some wild window-scaling
  ideas of mine you're welcome to shoot down.
- **Clippy, Bob, and the I-beam**: if WinFS wasn't proof the idea was wrong, maybe Clippy
  wasn't either. I think the I-beam is the anti-Clippy.
- **Small local models under the UX**: the ultimate right click, and "boring is good".
- **How to actually try**: the Ink & Switch model, and whether a repo can be the lab
  notebook for one of your three projects.
