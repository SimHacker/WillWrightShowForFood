# Scott Jenson — GLANCE

🍺 **The Desktop Explorer**: first member of Apple's Human Interface group (System 7,
Newton, the HIG), then Symbian, frog design and Google (mobile UX, the Physical Web),
author of **The Simplicity Shift**, now doing UX for Mastodon and Home Assistant. His two
talks, [Ubuntu Summit 25.10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1fZTOjd_bOQ) and
[KDE 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V7AfAcQwLW0), ask the free desktop to stop
copying and start exploring: "We don't need a 37th window manager", and "all I want us to
do is to try."
