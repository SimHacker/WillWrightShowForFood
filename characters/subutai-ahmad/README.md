## The two arcs

**1. Video — motion tracking + background removal (the part Don touched).**
At **Interval Research** (1993–97) Subutai built foundational real-time computer vision (and one of the
first DVRs, for TV-news indexing). Don **helped him with real-time motion tracking + background removal
on Windows**, then did **his own version on the PowerPC Mac**. The Interval spin-off **ePlanet** turned
it into the first consumer CV product — the **IntelPlay Me2Cam** (Mattel & Intel, 1999): track a kid's
body/head/arms at 30 fps on a plain USB webcam, drop them into scenes with a **"Virtual Blue Screen."**
**Intel bought the spin-off** and shipped the webcam. This is the **direct ancestor of the show's
browser video-motion-tracking puppet input** (Bounce "play any gesture," Faceball motion-drive).

**2. AI — the neocortex (a great conversation with Will).**
Now **Chief Scientist at Numenta** and an initiator of the **Thousand Brains Project**: cortical
columns, **reference frames**, **sparse distributed representations**, active dendrites — a
neuroscience-first bet that reads as a pointed counterpoint to today's LLMs. Perfect three-way with Will
about how minds and models actually work.
