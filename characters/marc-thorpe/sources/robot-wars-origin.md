## Genesis (1992)

> I don't know whether I came up with the idea for Robot Wars or the idea came up with me.

LucasToys senior designer; pitched fighting vehicles to a toy company — *"Some day someone is going to figure out how to do this."*

RC vacuum on tank → removed vacuum → *"dangerous toy with battery powered tools"* → stage events, invite competitors, **merchandising / licensing** revenue. Star Wars model. Trademark **Robot Wars** — Lucasfilm did not oppose.

## Promotion

- Ads in RC magazine + *Art Week* — 70 requests, 17 entries
- Fort Mason dates canceled for lack of funds (1992–1994)
- **New Year's Day 1994** — flu, doubts; sent package to **Wired**
- Photo panic: bought chainsaw, mounted on tank for Bill Zemenek shoot — didn't need to work
- **Jeff Raskin** wrote article; Feb 1994 issue → email flood

## First event (Aug 1994)

> When the first featherweight match began and I heard the excitement of the crowd, I knew at that moment that my idea was indeed as good as I had envisioned it to be.

**First competitors:** Mark Setrakian, Caleb Cheung, Mike Winter (X1), Charlie Tilford (The Mauler), Scott LaValley (DooLittle), **Will Wright (JulieBot)**.

2 ft arena railing; no injuries; *"charmed."*
