# Stupid Fun Club — Will Wright fansite (Marc Thorpe named)

Source: https://www.will-wright.com/stupidfunclub.php

---

## People

> The club is funded entirely by Wright and has two full-time employees: Filmmaker **Michael Winter** and conceptual design consultant **Marc Thorpe** (both he met at the RobotWars TV show).

Berkeley warehouse, 3,800 sq ft, Amtrak tracks. Fox first-look deal (Mike Darnell).

## Projects

**M.Y. Robot** — feudal Japan puppets, 8" scale village, robot bigger than a person; anime overlay secret script.

**Reality Robot** — 250 lb remote-controlled; University Ave cafes; *"what do people do when they encounter robots?"*

**Sad Robot experiment** — car-hit pose on footpath:
- Women: creeped out, walk away fast
- Men: ignore talking, **strip for parts**
- Mixed couples: pick up, converse, try to reset

**Robot waiter** — restaurant scene; people normalize within 3–4 minutes.

> "Although there will be comedy, this show will use the robot as a tool to explore human psychology."

**Chatbots** — robot brain copied to laptop; conversation with self → toys talking (Simpsons, Powerpuff Girls, Muppets). *"Toy Story come to life."*

## Will on robotics

> For me the interesting problems in robotics are more software problems… in trying to build one of those things, you learn a lot about humans.
