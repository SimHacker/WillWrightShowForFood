# Repo Show invitation — Morgan Dixon

*Morgan may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time — or take direct edit access. [Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md) — also covers quiet mode and onboarding.*
**Subject:** Prefab, thirteen years later, with LLMs

---

Morgan —

I wrote to you around 2013, after James Landay pointed me to Prefab in reply to a proposal Ben
Shneiderman had forwarded. I was trying to build aQuery, a scripting layer over accessibility APIs
for selecting, scripting and extending other applications' interfaces from outside, and Prefab was
the missing half: recognizing interfaces from pixels where the accessibility tree is missing or
wrong. I've been pointing people to your work on Hacker News ever since, as proof that it works on
real software.

The project is being built now, renamed Screen Angel. It reads accessibility trees, has a selector
language and a click-through overlay, and the pixel side is next. Its plan for recognizing widgets
says "Prefab's prototype matching is the documented approach and it worked on real interfaces.
Start there rather than at a model."
([ACCESSIBLIFY.yml](https://github.com/SimHacker/MicropolisCore/blob/main/apps/screen-angel/ACCESSIBLIFY.yml)).
I also kept a [Prefab bibliography](https://github.com/SimHacker/MicropolisCore/blob/main/apps/screen-angel/sources/prefab-bibliography.md),
checked against Crossref, with corrections to my own earlier citations.

My view is that Prefab was pre-LLM pattern recognition, and it is more relevant with LLMs. What
stalled in 2013 wasn't the selector engine or the event model; it was understanding what a widget
is for. A vision model can now say "this is the volume slider," but it can't find the slider's exact
bounds sixty times a second. Prefab can. The two together look like the right design, and you're the
person who knows where it breaks.

**Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the [premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md) and more.**

I'd like to invite you to a **Repo Show**: a recorded conversation whose stage is a public GitHub
repository, where what we discuss can be built afterward. Possible threads, your pick:

1. Prefab and LLMs: which parts a model now does better, and which still need prototypes.
2. "What if every GUI were open source?", revisited: Prefab Layers and Annotations, and modifying
   software you don't control.
3. Running Prefab's demos again, and what it would take to bring the code back to life.
4. James Fogarty could join, as could Richard Potter, whose Triggers drove Mac applications from
   pixels in 1991.

I'm Don Hopkins; I worked on pie menus with Ben Shneiderman, on NeWS at Sun, and on SimCity and
The Sims. Remote is fine. No preparation needed.

— Don
