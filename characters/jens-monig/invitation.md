# Repo Show invitation — Jens Mönig 🧩

*Public draft — not sent. Jens Mönig may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.yml)

**Today I'm sending two matching invitations** — yours and **Brian Harvey's** ([Brian's invitation](../brian-harvey/invitation.md)) — for one **pair show**. Each letter is written for the person receiving it; both describe the same show from your two angles, and each of you can read the other's.

Jens —

I'd love to have you on a **Repo Show** — a friendly, low-friction conversation whose stage is a public
GitHub repo people can clone, run, and build on. You and **Brian** are both invited; I've written him his
own letter alongside this one ([Brian's invitation](../brian-harvey/invitation.md)). You two aren't a couple —
but you're **colleagues joined at the heart** by Snap!, so the **default is a pair show** —
[`snap-logo-brian-jens`](../../repo-shows/snap-logo-brian-jens/README.md) — telling the shared
Snap! / Logo / BJC story once, together. **No synchronized sitting required** — two one-on-ones at each of
your best times, woven together in post, is completely fine.

**What I'd love to draw out from you:**

- **Snap! internals** — first-class lists, procedures, and continuations (enough to build the Y combinator
  out of blocks), plus prototype objects; how far the blocks metaphor goes without losing beginners.
- **Morphic.js** — the live, malleable world Snap! runs on: direct-manipulation objects all the way down,
  in the browser, no install.
- **Micropolis × Snap!** — driving an open, modifiable city sim (and CAM6 cellular automata) from Snap!
  blocks, from our 2018 thread.
- **Optional solo verticals** — schemas for Snap! (your kind of thing; my Snap!Con Barcelona talk),
  distributed messaging / dataflow / actor systems, and the GP-under-Alan-Kay lineage.
