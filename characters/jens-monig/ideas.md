## What Jens has done

Jens Mönig — Architect and lead programmer of Snap! (formerly BYOB, "Build Your Own Blocks"), the block-based visual language used in UC Berkeley's *Beauty and Joy of Computing* curriculum, with design input + documentation by Brian Harvey. Built it on his own Morphic.js framework; Snap! has first-class lists, procedures, AND continuations (enough for the Y combinator), plus prototype-based objects. Previously on the MIT Scratch team and worked under Alan Kay on the GP language (with John Maloney + Yoshiki Ohshima). Research Expert at SAP, consultant at UC Berkeley; a fully qualified German lawyer who returned to programming via Scratch/Squeak.
