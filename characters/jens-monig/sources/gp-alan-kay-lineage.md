## The hook

Jens was on the MIT Scratch team, worked under Alan Kay on the **GP** language (with John Maloney
and Yoshiki Ohshima), then architected **Snap!** on his own [Morphic.js](morphic-js.md) — with
Brian Harvey's pedagogy and documentation shaping what "first-class everything" means for learners.

## Biographical note

Fully qualified German lawyer who returned to programming via Scratch/Squeak — a career path that
proves Brian's "beauty and joy" thesis isn't marketing copy.
