## The hooks

### 1. Maxis Labs 2 — the design brief Sam already wants
Not fanfiction. Institution charter: what gets funded, what gets open-sourced, what ships as toys.
Sam names it; Don runs it; Will (premiere guest) stress-tests it. [Digest](sources/microcosm-maxis-labs.md).

### 2. Microcosm meets Soul City
Simulation toys ↔ liminal soul-files between game worlds. Export json as almost-soul-file; federation
without EA trademark traps.

### 3. Overedge × Repo Show
New types of knowledge-creation orgs — inspect the yaml before you partner. Anthology by PR, not pitch deck.

### 4. Catalogs as media
Sam's list-of-lists instinct ↔ Palm's field notebooks ↔ character rooms as cast window. Linnaean pass
on the whole repo graph — what species of show object exist?

### 5. Complexity science on glass-box cities
*Overcomplicated* meets SimCity rules you can read. CAM6 jam with Dave Ackley / Scott Draves thread;
Sandspiel family reunion with Chaim.

### 6. The Magic of Code — sorcery demystified on air
Code-as-magic rhetoric vs runnable microworlds. Show the trick, then show the repo.

### 7. Half-life of facts in simulation lore
What Maxis "facts" decayed (Exchange, SliceCity promises) vs what git preserves. Teaching update curves
with city saves.

### 8. Interval / Infernal Research echo
Mail thread — Interval Oompa-Loompa year, SFC robot films, Gallium, Bell Labs piece, *CAM Machines*.
[Thread](sources/infernal-research-thread-2024.md). Sam's Lux seat as contemporary echo.

### 9. Humanistic Computation × resonant computing
Manifesto energy meets MOOLLM cauldron harvest — PLAY → LEARN → LIFT after each show.

### 10. Three-way stack with Chaim (optional)
*Building SimCity* historian + complexity scientist + toolchain author — Maxis Labs 2 from three necessary views.
Will already interviewed Chaim live on [ROMchip, Jul 2024](../chaim-gingold/sources/romchip-will-wright-2024.md);
Sam had Chaim on his podcast. Every edge of this triangle is warm.
Chaim pacing: async, new-parent-friendly — [his invitation](../chaim-gingold/invitation.md).

### 11. Maxis vision primary source

### 12. Epistemix / FRED vs home-cooked sim
Sam flagged platform roots in intro mail — contrast institutional sim stacks with Robin Sloan meals.
