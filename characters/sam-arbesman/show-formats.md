## Tier 1 — Solo (default ask)

**One person. One video. No Don in the frame.**

Guest records on phone or laptop — walk a topic, correct the portrayal, riff on Maxis 2.0 — and
sends the file. Don weaves in post. This is the easiest path and the constitutional default.

Sam solo → Maxis Labs 2 / Microcosm / *Magic of Code* / Long Now, as he chooses.

---

## Tier 2 — One-on-one

Scheduled video chat: Don + one guest. Don clips and intercuts. Higher coordination cost than solo;
use when the guest wants dialogue, not when solo would do.

---

## Tier 3 — Stack

Multiple guests each submit **solo** videos (Sam, Will, Chaim, …). Don edits into one weave. No
shared calendar required.

---

## Tier 4 — Live

As many as can make one slot — Twitch, audience participation. Optional stretch; never a blocker
for Tiers 1–3.
