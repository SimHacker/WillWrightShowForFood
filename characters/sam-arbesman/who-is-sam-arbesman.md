## How he thinks (his public philosophy)

> The world is combinatorially weird and fractally interesting. Therefore **omnivorous curiosity**
> is the only proper response.

Orthogonal bets: when prediction fails, optimize for the **interesting** collision.

## Origin story (self-described)

**Commodore VIC-20** → typed-in programs from magazines with his father → **HyperCard** → Martin
Gardner → science fiction → LEGO. *The Magic of Code* is the book he wishes he'd had at age seven.

## Maxis connection (why he's in this repo)

- Lifelong **SimCity** fan; compiles **Microcosm Industries** in that spirit
- Publicly advocates **Maxis 2.0** — HyperCard-for-simulation, customizable sim platforms
- **Chaim Gingold** introduced him to Don (July 2024) after Chaim's *Building SimCity* podcast
- Warm mail thread: CAM6, Snap!/Micropolis, Overedge, cellular-automata coffee-table books

## Long Now — same orbit, different night

Sam is a **Long Now Research Fellow** ([people page](https://longnow.org/people/arbesman/)). The
repo's seed event is **26 June 2006** — [Brian Eno + Will Wright, *Playing with Time*](sources/long-now-thread.md):
generative art meets generative simulation; audience Q&A planted **open-source SimCity**; Don was
**in the room**.

Sam's documented Long Now **speaker** appearance is **August 2016** — *Technology at the Limits of
Comprehension* (his *Overcomplicated* era). We have **no record** he attended the 2006 talk; worth
asking him — fellow travelers sometimes overlap years later at the Interval.
