## Who

**Chuck Blanchard** was the **lead designer/programmer of Body Electric** at **VPL Research** — the
real-time visual-programming language for VR, music, and hardware that powered VPL's virtual worlds and
virtual instruments, and the ancestor of David Levitt's **Bounce**. The custom tools that made Body
Electric feel alive — including the **Swivel 3D transform-tree editor** built right into the
environment — were Chuck's work.

For years the story got told with Jaron Lanier's name on the marquee. Jaron himself set the record
straight in a **1999 email** to Don (see the [primary source](../don-hopkins/body-electric-1999-jaron-email.md)):

> "The problem was that Chuck Blanchard wasn't credited as the lead designer/programmer of BE/Bounce
> when David brought the program to Interval. Chuck's name was reduced in stature…"

This repo credits Chuck plainly. Jaron founded VPL and dreamed the vision; **Chuck built the thing.**
