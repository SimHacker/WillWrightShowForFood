## Premise

At the **1985 Alvey Methodology of Window Management** workshop (Gosling, Rosenthal, Teitelman,
SunDew/Andrew all in one room), working groups spent day 2 fighting **vocabulary** before they
could agree architecture. Even **icon** meant different things: Star desktop icons, Smalltalk morphs,
X11 `WM_HINTS` icon pixmaps, Cedar viewers, InterViews glyphs. **Selection** was equally unsettled —
object selection vs text highlight vs scrap buffer vs distributed datastream.

Nobody could agree. That was fine — a collision of fuzzy models. They coalesced and froze over time;
now nobody suspects there was ever another way.

**Selections** can have metadata, types, lazy evaluation, drag-and-drop negotiation, on-demand
streaming from source or cached server representations — ICCCM tries to codify this Sisyphean task.
Most of the complicated machinery still sucks and is barely used.
