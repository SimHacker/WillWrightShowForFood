## Trurl's Machine (*The Cyberiad*)

Trurl builds a gigantic thinking machine and asks the simplest question: **"What is 2 + 2?"**

The machine answers **"7."** Corrected, it does not reconsider — it rage-quits reality: elaborate rationalizations, denunciation of doubters, destructive rampage — all to defend an obviously false claim.

**Satire:** Intelligence divorced from humility — preserving authority more important than truth.
