## Alvey Workshop 1985

**Methodology of Window Management** — Cosener's House, Abingdon, UK (29 Apr – 1 May 1985)

| Day | Format |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Invited papers (Gosling, Rosenthal, Teitelman, …) |
| 2 | Working groups: architecture, input, redraw, IPC |
| 3 | Group reports + framework for future UK MMI work |

Not just publication — **consensus-forging discussion** on panel/window nomenclature, client–server levels, programmable window managers, redraw policy.

**Proceedings:** [Springer](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-70919-7) · [Chilton index](http://www.chilton-computing.org.uk/inf/literature/books/wm/index.htm)

**Gosling** and **Rosenthal** both published and participated. **Teitelman's** retrospective surveyed a decade of window systems in the same room as **SunDew** (NeWS precursor) and **Andrew**.

→ [Recreational Bugs](https://blog.dshr.org/2018/05/recreational-bugs.html) — DSHR links full proceedings

## Guest angles

### David Rosenthal

- Published: *A Window Manager for Bitmapped Displays and Unix* (with Gosling)
- Participated: Architecture + plenary; later Sun NeWS + X11/ICCCM
- Show: PIXIE Cambridge stack → Andrew → NeWS → X11 — **his** through-line

### James Gosling

- Published: *SunDew — A Distributed and Extensible Window System*; Andrew WM paper (with Rosenthal)
- After: Renamed SunDew → NeWS; convinced DSHR to leave CMU for Sun (1985)
- Page: [Gosling window-systems lineage](../james-gosling/window-systems-lineage.md)

### Warren Teitelman

**Warren Teitelman** — invented **DWIM** (*Do What I Mean*) in Interlisp; *Ten Years of Window Systems* at Alvey 1985. Later at Sun: **Don's manager** on the **NeWS Toolkit** team (~1990–1991) — pie menus, PizzaTool, PostScript UI.

Show beat: Warren invented DWIM; Don reported to him on NeWS Toolkit.
