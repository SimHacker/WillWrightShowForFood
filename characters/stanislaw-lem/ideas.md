# Stanisław Lem — threads for a remembrance

*Memorial mode. About his published work; never in his voice.*

## The Cyberiad

- The Seventh Sally and SimCity, with Will Wright: the kingdom in a box, and Klapaucius's
  objection.
- Trurl's machine that insists 2+2=7, and LLMs that are confidently wrong (David Rosenthal).
- The Electronic Bard and machine poetry; how Michael Kandel translated the poems.
- The Polish-only tale missing from the English edition.

## Philosophy

- *Summa Technologiae* (1964): phantomatics, intellectronics, and the search engine before there
  was a web.
- *Golem XIV*: a machine that outgrows its makers' questions and stops answering.
- *A Perfect Vacuum* and *Imaginary Magnitude*: reviewing and introducing books that don't exist,
  as a way to explore ideas without building them.

## Lem and Dick

- Lem's 1975 essay praising Philip K. Dick as the American exception, and Dick's 1974 letter to
  the FBI calling Lem a committee. Told fairly, with Dick's 1974 crisis as context.
