# 📐 Said Achmiz — glance

**Interface designer and implementor.** gwern.net's design and much of its JavaScript/CSS since 2017,
per the site's own credit line — `sidenotes.js`, `image-focus.js`, and with Shawn Presser the
generalized popup system. Themes and much of the frontend JS for GreaterWrong. Gwern's word for the
relationship: **"tech co-creator."**

**Why invited:** Don read the popup source and found his own architecture. Pinning sets a **flag on the
same window object** — identical to TNT OPEN LOOK pin-up menus, opposite of the olwm/ICCCM rewrap
dance. Two lineages, decades apart, same answer.

**Register:** implementor to implementor. Addressed to the interface work, not to the writing.
Invited **in his own right**, in parallel with but independent of [gwern](../gwern/README.md).

**Standing rule:** do not divide the design between the two of them. The public record does not
support it. Same credit line in both directories.

**Scope:** the published interface work. See `CHARACTER.yml` → `scope`.
