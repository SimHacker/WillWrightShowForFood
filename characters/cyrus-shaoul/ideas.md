# Interview hooks — Cyrus Shaoul

Draft hooks for a solo episode, a Leela crew round-table, or a Cyrus+Henry duo.
Cyrus picks; nothing is locked.

## 1. Factory floor as stage

Physical AI with receipts — expand from one line to three, Monterrey
learnings, bench prep for Axis P-3265 cameras. What does "real industrial
AI" look like when the customer is watching?

## 2. Pixels vs relations

Onlab 2021: don't count pixels, understand relations between people,
objects, and environment — learn like humans, 100× less data. Bridge from
psycholinguistics (n-grams, formulaic sequences) to concept-aware video.

## 3. The myth of cognitive decline

2014 paper: older adults' test scores reflect memory-search load from
experience, not decay. Parallels to a plant that accumulates operational
history — and to Leela learning new behaviors in days, not months.

## 4. Neuro-symbolic since Day 1

Leela's hybrid from the start; the Vincent Lextrait Claude Code post Cyrus
shared — generative grammars behind the curtain, LLM slapped on 1980s syntax
editors. What Leela does differently on the shop floor.

## 5. AI-readiness is baloney

Automation World / Rick Rider: meet manufacturers where they are. Visual
intelligence with minimal deployment demands vs the reference-architecture
fairy tale.

## 6. Digital Garage → Leela

Eccosys, Joichi Ito's email that started it, evangelizing the net in Shibuya,
Infoseek Japan dominance, IPO — then PhD, postdoc, Landmark College, Leela.
"The customer is king" in Japan and everywhere.

## 7. Schneider Electric Athens DC

Operational visibility case study — perceived bottleneck vs actual constraint,
27% capacity in non-throughput activity, 44%→50% efficiency, $146K Q1
quality incidents avoided. Schedules vs reality.

## 8. IA not AI

David Tamés — Intelligence Augmentation for humans. Cyrus's public framing
alongside Leela Assistant as augmenting operators, not replacing them.

## 9. Kaizen and the hose on the floor

Mark Graban thread Cyrus amplified — improvements operators choose (pinch
points, tripping hazards) that never show up in cost-savings reports. Visual
intelligence that sees the hose.

## 10. MIT 6.004 and the jet engine

JARVIS Challenge — LLMs not ready for engineering design; the real world is
messy. Hands-on MIT lore vs hype cycle.

## 11. Leela crew constellation

Group show with Henry (schema factory / Society of Mind), Milan (product),
Steve (verifiable ML / grid world), Isabella (vision eval + policy), Art
(WAIS / Internet Archive / Connection Machines). Founding myth: MIT students
→ Somerville.

## 12. Natural language on the shop floor

NVIDIA Automate whiteboard — Leela's natural language interface live. Alvin
Clark scribbling. What operators actually ask.
