## What is a Repo Show?

The center of gravity is a **GitHub repo**, not just an mp4. I announce a
show and open the repo: questions, artifacts, code, papers, links, videos.
The audience participates and learns *before* the conversation, follows
along live or time-shifted, and keeps contributing forever after.
