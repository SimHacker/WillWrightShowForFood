# Cyrus Shaoul 🏭🧠🇯🇵

Invitation portrayal — **not** Cyrus Shaoul. [Standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

Co-founder and **Chief Customer Officer** at **[Leela AI](https://www.leela.ai/)**
(visual intelligence for manufacturing). Computational cognitive scientist:
MIT Brain & Cognitive Science (psycholinguistics under Steven Pinker), PhD
Alberta (word n-grams, Chris Westbury), postdoc Tübingen (Harald Baayen).
Japan internet pioneer — co-founder/CTO of **Digital Garage** (Infoseek Japan
era through IPO) after **Eccosys** with Joichi Ito. Co-author on Leela's
grid-world learning paper (2020). Open source: **HiDEx**, **NDL** R package.

The CCO on the factory floor: deployment videos, Schneider Electric case
studies, Axis camera partnerships, and a public voice for neuro-symbolic
AI, IA-not-AI, and the thesis that manufacturers don't need to "clean up
their data" before visual intelligence works.
