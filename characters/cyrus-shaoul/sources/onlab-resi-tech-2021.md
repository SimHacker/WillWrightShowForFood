# Onlab Resi-Tech interview — Cyrus Shaoul / Leela AI (Aug 2021)

Source: [Open Network Lab — "AI with Concept Awareness. How Leela AI Lead the PoC to Success"](https://onlab.jp/en/onlab-journal/ai-with-concept-awareness-how-leela-ai-lead-the-poc-to-success/) (Digital Garage Group accelerator, 2nd Batch, Dec 2020–Apr 2021).

Summarized for the invitation portrayal. NOT a transcript.

## Profile block (as published)

CEO at time of interview. MIT BSc; Alberta MSc/PhD. Co-founder/CTO Digital Garage until 2001. Neural networks for NLP from Alberta and Tübingen work.

## Key quotes and claims (Cyrus, paraphrased closely)

**Why less data:**
> If you look at existing camera systems, they look at each pixel… billions or trillions of pixels. Our system focuses on a higher level — people's bodies, where objects are. Relations between people, objects, and environment. Learn much more like a human being.

**Finger color vs hot pot:**
> Humans don't think about what color my finger is. We care: am I touching a hot pot or a cold glass?

**Custom events in 1–2 days:**
During Resi-Tech PoCs (office building, shopping mall, construction sites), when a customer wanted a new behavior detected, turnaround was **one or two days** — "extremely fast for high quality machine vision."

**Japan vs US:**
- Safety "very, very important" in Japan — sometimes more than profit
- Japanese customers invested more in high-quality cameras
- Cultural acceptance of workplace cameras higher in Japan ("cameras are okay… especially when the camera will be making my life safer")

**Onlab's role:**
Matchmaker, translator, progress tracking — "I don't think we could have had the success we did without their help." Remote from Boston during COVID; Slack collaboration.

**Small bird on elephant:**
Startup-enterprise collaboration risk; Onlab chose partners experienced with foreign startups.

**Investor impact:**
American investor impressed by Japan PoC success → invested in Leela.

**Next steps (2021 horizon):**
v1 release Japan + US simultaneously; robotics in 2–3 years; Japan office planned — "the customer is king."
