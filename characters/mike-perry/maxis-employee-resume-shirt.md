## Why this is a real document

It's a gag shirt, and it is also **the most candid available account of what went wrong at Maxis in
the mid-1990s** — written by the company about itself, worn at a picnic.

**The flops are named on the record, by the people who shipped them.** *Crystal Skull*, *Zaark*,
*ScruTiny in the Great Round*, *El Fish*, *Mind Warp*, *Kid's Studio*, *Chug-a-Lug*, *Daring to
Fly* — Maxis's non-Sim publishing bets. Company retrospectives usually launder this era into
"diversification." The shirt says: *gave away more Crystal Skull CDs at trade shows than were ever
sold in the U.S.*, and calls the sales team's achievement *getting shelf space for flops*. Being
listed as **Executive Producer of El Fish** is the joke Chris Bennett replies to; being credited
with **27 different ways to release SimCity 2000** is the SKU treadmill that funded all of it.

**The dates are the story.** *1987–1997* is Maxis's entire independent life — founded 1987, acquired
by EA in **1997**, the year of this picnic. A shirt that prints your employer's lifespan as a
resume's work-experience block is a leaving-party artifact whether or not anyone said so. And
**"Guessing Product Slip Dates"** listed as a hobby, with *"Holding my breath for SC3K release by
November… Christmas… 98!"* — SimCity 3000 shipped in **1999**. The joke undershot.

**The references are all liquor** except Will Wright, Bob Derber, Jeff Braun, and "The Brother."
Named non-alcoholic humans: the founder-designer, the executive (Bob Derber), the co-founder (Jeff
Braun) — plus **Fred Gerson**, whose moods are an "interest and activity," and **Andy Derber** and
**Val Garcia**, whose fictional universities award the degrees.

Held against the other 1997 objects in this archive — [Tycho Rising's press
sheet](lunasim-tycho-rising.md#it-was-announced-the-e3-1997-press-fact-sheet) promising Q1 1998, the
[EA legal binder](../melissa-bachman-wood/the-box.md#6-ea-law-legal-checklist-1997), the
[bay cruise where Don thought Luc looked like the cat that ate the canary](../eric-bowman/team-lunch-from-eric-bowman.md)
— 1997 at Maxis reads as a year of people making very funny jokes about their own situation.

## Open questions

- **Whose resume is it?** It reads as a collective self-roast (*"TITLE: Valued Employee"*), but the
  Tony La Russa and El Fish specifics suggest at least one real career underneath. Ask Mike.
- **What does the front say?** Mike thinks *"San Mateo"* — presumably the picnic venue.
- Who wrote it, and who printed it?
- Is *"Zaark"* the correct spelling, and is *"Chug-a-lug"* the game *Chug-A-Lug*?
- **Rooms needed:** Fred Gerson, Bob Derber, Andy Derber, Val Garcia, Chris Bennett.
