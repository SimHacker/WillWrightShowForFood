**Eric Bowman** is a programmer on the original **The Sims** team at Maxis, working on core/systems
engineering. He and Don later worked together again at **TomTom** in Amsterdam.

## Why a Repo Show

A natural voice for the [Sims team reunion](../../repo-shows/building-the-sims/), on the engineering side
— core systems, the **throbbing brain** UI that didn't ship, the long arc from Maxis to
Amsterdam/TomTom, and how pools fit the lot grid (objects vs rooms vs dedicated layer).
