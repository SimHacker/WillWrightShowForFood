# Repo Show invitation — Eric Bowman

*Shared 20 Aug 2026 via chat. Eric may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time.*
[Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md) — also covers direct repo access, the quiet-mode publication plan, and easy onboarding.

Eric —

We worked together on the original **Sims** team — and again later at **TomTom** in Amsterdam. I'd
love you at **Sims Fest** — our Maxis reunion Repo Show: the engineering side of how the thing actually
got built. The stage is a public GitHub repo; we talk; ideas can become runnable artifacts on stream.
**Show, don't tell.**

**Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the [premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md) and more.**

You held the core systems together while the rest of us were inventing motives, behaviors, and UI
experiments that may or may not have shipped. I still think about your **"throbbing brain"** needs
interface — hand-drawn, brilliant, not in the box — and the Amsterdam years after Maxis.

**One thing worth a look first.** With the EA sale in the news, the story of how same-sex
relationships got into The Sims has been circulating again, in a garbled version. I wrote up
[the record](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/patrick-j-barrett-iii/same-sex-relationships-the-record.md)
correcting the "outdated design doc" myth, with the 1998 marked-up design docs and SimAntics
tree-code screenshots [archived in the repo](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/don-hopkins/sources/sims-design-documents/README.md).
You were in the engine room those years — if I got anything wrong, your memory beats my archaeology.

**Want the keys?** Tell me your GitHub account and I'll give you write access — no PRs, we're in
push-to-main mode. Your directory is yours to edit, prune, or grow. And if you have any old code
or documents from the Maxis days — prototypes, design notes, throbbing-brain sketches, runnable or
not — upload them. LLMs make dead code walk now. That's the whole point of the repo: get the great
designs and prototypes out where people and machines can learn from them and bring them back to life.

**Threads — your pick:**

- **Core systems engineering** — what held the original Sims together under the hood.
- **Swimming pools: objects or rooms?** — architecture question from the CTG years; how did the grid
  model actually represent pools?
- **Pool ladders and edges** — pool pieces in the object pipeline; removable ladders and drowning.
- **Pool QA** — pathological lots (urn rings, service AI on pool islands) filed as bugs?
- **The throbbing brain** — your mood/needs UI prototype; what we learned from what didn't ship.
- **"Who did grass?"** — Patrick's story: he barged into a programmer meeting demanding to know who
  did grass, three of you stared, and you finally said "legally I can't ask that." He meant the lawn.
  On air we can finally settle who wrote the lawn code.
- **Amsterdam → Maxis** — TomTom reunion, Sound Garden chance encounters, modding culture.
- **Then and now** — what you'd build differently with today's tools (and what you wouldn't).

No pressure, no homework. Accepted, delayed, declined, or no reply — all honored gracefully.

— Don Hopkins *(User Interface Flower Child)*
