## One sentence

Cyberspace needs **narrative forms that give the interactor agency** inside an immersive procedural world that can **transform** how they see — not just choose-your-own-adventure branches dressed as literature.

## Triad cream (steal these)

| Murray | Plain | Ours |
|--------|-------|------|

## Show hooks

1. **GitHub as holodeck** — issues/quests, branches/timelines, PRs as negotiated canon (private draft already exists; public invite can point at the idea without dumping the email).
2. **Agency vs averaged LLM** — MOOLLM many-voices ensembles vs one hedged narrator.
3. **StoryMaker → field** — Quitmeyer / Mark Your Territory as Murray-line students making narrative *in the world*.
4. **Holodeck ≠ hypertext only** — same Montfort point: simulation underneath, not just lexia hops.
