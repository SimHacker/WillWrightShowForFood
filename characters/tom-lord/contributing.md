# Contributing — for Trina and everyone who knew Tom

This room grows from what Tom published, what friends remember, and what family chooses to
share. **Nothing here reaches a show without Trina's blessing.** Everything else is welcome at
her pace.

## For Trina

Don is in touch with you. Cat photos continue. This memorial room is a gift back: Tom's work
and voice stay findable without you having to become a git expert overnight.

**You are the editor-in-chief.** Correct anything. Remove anything. Add anything. Silence,
crying, and despairing at your own pace are also valid choices.

### What you asked for (March 2025)

> A repository where I can drop stuff as I find it / as I have the heart to look through it.

That is the design. Not "organize everything first." Not "learn Emacs." **Drop when ready.**

**Easiest paths:**

1. **Email or Signal** — send files, scans, links, or "here's a box of stuff." Don files them.
   No GitHub required. You said you're a lightweight; we believe you and plan around it.
2. **GitHub drop repo** — when you're ready: a **private** repo where you upload into
   `incoming/` and Don helps sort into readable markdown later. Personal account is fine; Don
   can create the repo and add you.
3. **Nothing right now** — the room stands as-is.

Don will not mansplain you into a workflow. Ask when you want an explanation; otherwise he just
does the fiddly parts.

### The Fall Guys (and other unpublished work)

Don has a **fictional draft, "The Fall Guys,"** that Tom wrote and asked Don to review. Copy
came through **Dave Tristram** (Santa Cruz, ex-SGI, music and synths with Tom). Don has **not**
published it and will not without your say-so.

If you want it shared:

- Don can reformat into readable markup, chapter it, add a table of contents, illustrate if that
  helps.
- Or keep it private forever. Your call.

**Question still open:** publish? partial? never? under what name?

### What else might you have?

Drafts, email, photos, Berkeley Daily Planet pieces, Housing Advisory Commission work, climate
writing, Guile or arch notes, hard drives, boxes. Don does not know what else exists. You do.

### What Don will do with material you send

- Preserve Tom's words verbatim unless you ask for light proofreading.
- Mark provenance (who sent it, when, family-supplied vs public archive).
- Hold unpublished work privately until you approve release.
- Never put show-bound content on air without your explicit OK.

## For everyone else

Colleagues from the Guile, arch, Berkeley, and FSF years: corrections and memories welcome.
Same rules: no inventing Tom's inner life, no fake quotes, family consent gates anything that
leaves the archive for performance.

See [memorial standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode).
