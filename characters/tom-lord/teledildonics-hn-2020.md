Buttplug is the serious cross-platform protocol layer; Deldo is the Emacs romance novel. Tom
never commented on Deldo that we know of. He commented on Buttplug in 2020 with the same deadpan
corporate interrogation he brought to Sun, Tcl, and the Berkeley City Council. Same guy.

## What Tom did in the thread

He did not snicker and leave. He asked whether teledildonics could optimize workplace
productivity, whether buttplug scripts could replace emojis, whether passive reception bias
reflects tech workers' submissive tendencies, whether cam-site "proof of life" teledildonics
could be faked with canned footage, and whether paying customers reproducing their wage-slave
roles counts as self-domination under capital. Then he answered his own questions with Chapter 1
of *Capital*.

This is the same comic instinct as "the Scheme underground" and the Tcl War semi-prank. Tom
could not encounter a technology without asking what power relations it encodes, and he could
not ask that without making the question sound like a parody of the worst person in the room —
because often it was.

strongly agreeing, and sometimes the other person prints the result and hangs it on their wall.

## Why this belongs in the room

Tom's flames were only half of him. The other half was this: take the most embarrassing
technology on the front page, ask the question nobody else will ask, make it funnier than the
jokes, and land it on a serious political-economic point anyway. Sex tech is not shameful.
Treating it as beneath serious analysis would have been the only shameful move, and Tom never
made that one.
