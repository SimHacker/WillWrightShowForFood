# The Pervert's Guide — and why Don introduced W. Watson to Tom

Tom argued about programming languages as ideology, desire, power, and substrate. So does
**W. Watson** (Wavell Watson) in *The Pervert's Guide to Computer Programming Languages* (2017):
Lacanian psychoanalysis applied to why we pick languages for irrational libidinal reasons, in the
tradition of Slavoj Žižek's film series.

## How Don found it (April 2020)

A Facebook thread about Žižek's *Pervert's Guide to Ideology* reminded Don of Jonathan Rees'
à la carte menu of OO features ("Because OO is a moving target, OO zealots will choose some
subset of this menu by whim and then use it to try to convince you that you are a loser"):

http://www.paulgraham.com/reesoo.html

Don wished aloud for Žižek to do "The Pervert's Guide to Objectology" — then ran the Rule 34
query. Rule 34 delivered Watson's SXSW talk.

Don emailed Watson; **dang liked the video so much he posted it to HN himself under a sock-puppet
account**, and Don talked Watson into joining the discussion, where he answered questions under
his own name:

https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22910702

## The introduction to Tom (June 2020, documented)

On June 7, 2020, Don sent Watson his Žižek "German Stool Hermeneutics" riff (from an HN thread
about smart toilets) — **cc: `lord@basiscraft.com`**. Watson replied the next day with Tom still on
the thread. That cc is the introduction, preserved in Don's mail archive: Don saw two people who
would provoke each other productively, and wired them together.

Why: the intersection of philosophy and programming languages was exactly Tom's territory, and
Watson's tone was Tom-compatible — serious argument wearing a ridiculous title. Tom would have
read the Pervert's Guide and argued with it at full strength, not flinched at the name.

menu is the text; Lacan is the lens; Don has forty years of objectology (NeWS, HyperLook, Self
envy, prototypes vs classes) as the material. Tom would have heckled from the front row, and the
heckling would have been the best part.

## The Žižek stool connection

Don's long-running HN bit about **German Stool Hermeneutics** (Žižek on inspection-shelf
toilets) is the same comedy-truth axis as Watson's Lacanian language critique: the unconscious
is structured like a language, and some technology choices are really about what you're willing
to look at. It's also, not coincidentally, the exact email that carried the Watson↔Tom cc.
