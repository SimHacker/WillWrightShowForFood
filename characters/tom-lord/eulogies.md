# Eulogies and memorials

What the world said when Tom died, June–July 2022. Collected so none of it gets lost.

## The obituary

Thomas Lord 1966–2022, by Trina Pundurs, Berkeley Daily Planet:

https://berkeleydailyplanet.com/issue/2022-06-26/article/49837

The primary biographical source for this room: Pittsburgh, western Massachusetts, Johns Hopkins,
Carnegie Mellon and the Andrew Project, the FSF years, Guile, arch, Berkeley, the Housing
Advisory Commission, SR15 and the climate emergency, Writer Coach Connection, People's Park.

## Berkeley's Loss

Becky O'Malley's editorial in the same issue of the Daily Planet:

https://www.berkeleydailyplanet.com/issue/2022-06-26/article/49843?headline=Berkeley-s-Loss--Becky-O-Malley

> "Tom Lord was one of the last of the real Berkeleyans... a data-driven free-range public
> intellectual who was not hampered by conventional thinking. He knew a lot about a lot of
> things that really count, and he wasn't shy about telling you so... Outrage was his signature
> style. It made officials mighty uncomfortable, as well it should have."

## The free software world

Tom Lord RIP, LWN, July 2022 — with remembrances in the comments from arch contributors
("He was a great mind, precise and dedicated. He was always supporting and caring with
contributors... If we have distributed version control systems such as Bazaar (first) and git
(later), we owe it in no small part to him."), including one reader who patched `tla` to build
in a modern environment and rescued every arch repository still findable on the open net, as an
homage:

https://lwn.net/Articles/901807/

Thomas Lord, an LtU regular, dies at 56 — Lambda the Ultimate:

http://lambda-the-ultimate.org/node/5653

Tom Lord passing — guix-devel, noting he was an early (or the first) maintainer of Guile:

https://lists.gnu.org/r/guix-devel/2022-07/msg00227.html
