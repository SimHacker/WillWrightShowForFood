# Tom Lord — remembrance show ideas 🕯️

*Memorial mode: a remembrance **about** Tom, never an interview **of** him.
[Memorial standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode). Family consent
(Trina Pundurs) comes before any of this leaves the workshop.*

## Segments a remembrance could draw on

1. **The Scheme Underground** — Don tells the Tcl War from both sides at once: Tom fighting it,
   Don shipping SimCity on Tcl/Tk through the middle of it, the heated arguments that never
   dented the friendship. Primary text: Tom's own published account, read straight — brilliant,
   funny, hard on himself, fair even to the people who did him wrong.

2. **The universal engine, vindicated late** — GEL in 1993 to Guile 3.0 under Guix. The argument
   Tom made with insufficient resources, finished decades later by other hands. What it means to
   be right early and uncredited, and how the field should score that.

3. **tla and the argument that won** — GNU arch pioneered distributed version control years
   before git. The tool got eclipsed; the argument now runs the entire software industry. A
   meditation on legacy-by-influence, paired with segment 2.

4. **XML vs S-expressions, 1998** — the preserved email debate (Don and Tom, RMS cc'd), performed
   as a two-sides reading by living participants only. Same substrate argument as Guile, aimed at
   documents. Twenty-five years on, who won? (Trick question: YAML.)

5. **Full strength in public** — the Berkeley chapter: Daily Planet, Housing Advisory Commission,
   the climate emergency after SR15, coaching young writers at Longfellow. The same fire that lit
   the Tcl War, aimed at his city. This segment belongs to the people who shared that work with
   him, if they want it.
