## The epic threads

**An Account of the Tcl War** — his own published history of free software's formative
flamewar, and the single best thing to read in his voice: brilliant, funny, hard on himself,
fair even to the people who did him wrong.

https://web.archive.org/web/20110102015130/http://basiscraft.com/0800-0100-the-tcl-war.html

**"named patches and 'Does Linus matter?'"** (gnu-arch-users, October 2003) — peak Tom: the
list wants him to chase Linus and BitKeeper; he answers that "Linus, as you're using him here,
is a pretty abstraction" and that the way to win kernel developers is real, experience-driven
engineering, not chatting about a man who isn't on the list. Written two years before git
existed, and he was right about how that story would end.

https://lists.nongnu.org/archive/html/gnu-arch-users/2003-10/msg00104.html

**Reply to Linus's bitkeeper comments** (gnu-arch-users, September 2003) — Linus's two wishes
for revision control, answered point by point in arch terms, ending with a design sketch and
then a page of honest open questions about his own sketch. That closing habit, interrogating
his own proposal harder than anyone else would, is the most Tom thing in the archive.

https://lists.nongnu.org/archive/html/gnu-arch-users/2003-09/msg00488.html

**Subversion through the goggles of the arch architecture** (gnu-arch-users, September 2003) —
"tla doesn't chase BitKeeper": his taxonomy of the whole DVCS field, years before the field
knew it was a field.

https://lists.nongnu.org/archive/html/gnu-arch-users/2003-09/msg01306.html

**"Why do we need modules at all?"** (Lambda the Ultimate, 2014) — Joe Armstrong's famous
brain-dump, with Tom running through the comments testing the idea from every side.

http://lambda-the-ultimate.org/node/5079

**XQuery language design issues** (Lambda the Ultimate, 2007) — Tom on standards-body language
design, with "my ridiculous opinions about current proposals" offered up front. The same
substrate argument as GEL and the 1998 S-expressions email, aimed at query languages.

http://lambda-the-ultimate.org/node/2511

**"Bumping into Stallman, again"** (Hacker News, 2016) — Tom defending the free software
movement as a liberation movement, twenty-two years after the Tcl War, with none of the fire
banked: "Proprietary software is all about gaining power over users. It is not a theory
anymore."

https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=11024200

**The teledildonics thread** (Hacker News, 2020) — because the flames were only half of him.
Six comments on Kyle Machulis's [Buttplug](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=23094477) launch:
corporate product vision, cam-site "proof of life," Chapter 1 of *Capital*, commodity fetishism
with a conventional-fetish pun for icing. Same comic instinct as "the Scheme underground." Ten
years earlier Kyle made [Deldo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D1sXuHnf_lo) for Emacs; Tom never
commented on that one, but it's the same lineage. dang shared an anonymous reader email calling it
"spun gold from snark"; Kyle said he'd print it and hang it on his wall. Full preservation:

[teledildonics-hn-2020.md](teledildonics-hn-2020.md)
