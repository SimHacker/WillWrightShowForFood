## Who

**Tom Tjon A Loi** is a Rotterdam-based creative strategist and concept
designer working at the intersection of narrative, design, visual systems, and
AI ([portfolio](https://www.tomtjonaloi.xyz/)). He studied DesignLAB at the
**Gerrit Rietveld Academie** ([final work, 2012](https://oldschool.rietveldacademie.nl/en/final-works/2012/designlab/tom-tjon-a-loi))
and Political Spatial Design at the **Sandberg Instituut**. In the summer of
2009, as a Rietveld student, he joined Don's Oosterpark studio — and turned
into much more than an intern: a co-designer of the Micropolis social vision.

## The Micropolis work (2009)

While Don was deep in engine code, Tom was imagining what open source SimCity
could *feel* like as a social, multiplayer, creative medium. From Don's
internship assessment and recommendation letter (November 2009):

> "Made storyboards, mock-ups and concept illustrations about multi player
> Micropolis and social networking, for a talk I gave at HAR 2009…"

> "Presently working on the user interface design and multi player dynamics
> for Micropolis, an open source multi player version of SimCity in
> development. Ongoing work redesigning the user interface and graphics,
> translating English text to Dutch, writing new documentation and
> educational courseware."

That concept art — the social layer, the multiplayer dynamics, cities as
places where people meet — is the work Don proudly showed at
[HAR 2009](https://donhopkins.medium.com/har-2009-lightning-talk-transcript-constructionist-educational-open-source-simcity-by-don-3a9e010bf305)
and used to present the project to Kennisnet, IBM, and other collaborators.
Tom also brought his own world with him: he introduced Don to the Amsterdam
art and architecture scene ([Mediamatic](https://www.mediamatic.net/en/page/18702/tom-tjon-a-loi) ·
[Amsterdam Biennale 2009](https://www.mediamatic.net/en/page/18705/tom-at-amsterdam-biennale-2009)),
and in November 2009 pitched Micropolis to the **Architectuurfonds** open call
*"Stedenbouw voor de volgende generatie"* — urbanism for the next generation.
A design student proposing an open source city simulator to an architecture
fund, seventeen years before it was obvious.

Don's recommendation, verbatim:

> "I would not hesitate to recommend Tom for challenging projects requiring
> creativity, design skills and technical abilities."

## The work now

Tom's current positioning: *"Most brands know what they make. Few know what
they mean."* He looks for the cultural tension, reframes the value, and builds
a visual and narrative system around it. His stated focus is **"AI not as
output, but as instrument."** His [REmemory](https://www.tomtjonaloi.xyz/work/rememory)
project treats AI-generated reconstructions as **interpretations rather than
objective memories**.

Those are the facts, and they align point for point with this production:

- "AI as instrument, not output" is the MOOLLM stance — LLMs as orchestration
  instruments in a LambdaMOO-descended world, not content dispensers.
- REmemory's ethic — reconstructions are interpretations, not ground truth —
  is the same principle as this repo's
  [portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md).
- His 2009 storyboards (social, multiplayer, cities as shared creative
  spaces) describe the current federation + MOOLLM roadmap.

## Why he matters to the show

Tom is the original proof of the
[educators' track](../../repo-shows/ideas/themes/educators-track.md) model: a
student who joined a real open source project as coursework, produced real
design work, presented at a real hacker conference, and pulled the project
into new communities. He imagined the social layer before it was buildable.
It's buildable.
