## Who

**Tom Tjon A Loi** is an Amsterdam-based designer and a graduate of the **Gerrit Rietveld
Academie's DesignLAB** ([final work, 2012](https://oldschool.rietveldacademie.nl/en/final-works/2012/designlab/tom-tjon-a-loi)).
In the summer of 2009, as a Rietveld student, he became Don's intern at the Oosterpark studio —
and turned into something much more than an intern: a co-designer of the Micropolis social vision.

## The Micropolis work

While Don was deep in engine code, Tom was imagining what open source SimCity could *feel* like as
a social, multiplayer, creative medium. From Don's internship assessment and recommendation letter
(November 2009):

> "Made storyboards, mock-ups and concept illustrations about multi player Micropolis and social
> networking, for a talk I gave at HAR 2009…"

> "Presently working on the user interface design and multi player dynamics for Micropolis, an
> open source multi player version of SimCity in development. Ongoing work redesigning the user
> interface and graphics, translating English text to Dutch, writing new documentation and
> educational courseware."

That concept art — the social layer, the multiplayer dynamics, cities as places where people meet —
is the work Don proudly showed at [HAR 2009](https://donhopkins.medium.com/har-2009-lightning-talk-transcript-constructionist-educational-open-source-simcity-by-don-3a9e010bf305)
and used to present the project to Kennisnet, IBM, and other collaborators. Tom also brought his
own world with him: he introduced Don to the Amsterdam art and architecture scene, and in November
2009 pitched Micropolis to the **Architectuurfonds** open call *"Stedenbouw voor de volgende
generatie"* — urbanism for the next generation. A design student proposing an open source city
simulator to an architecture fund, seventeen years before it was obvious.

Don's recommendation, verbatim:

> "I would not hesitate to recommend Tom for challenging projects requiring creativity, design
> skills and technical abilities."

## Why he matters to the show

Tom is the original proof of the [educators' track](../../repo-shows/ideas/themes/educators-track.md)
model: a student who joined a real open source project as coursework (a Rietveld internship),
produced real design work, presented at a real hacker conference, and pulled the project into new
communities. Everything he storyboarded in 2009 — the social layer, multiplayer play, cities as
shared creative spaces — is now the federation and MOOLLM roadmap. He imagined it before it was
buildable. It's buildable.
