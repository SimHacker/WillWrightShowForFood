## Who

**Dr. Phil Cook** was director of research at the **Plessey Automation Research
Laboratories** in Poole, Dorset, in 1967. He survives here through one document:
[Heinz Lemke's origin story](../heinz-lemke/sources/1967-back-to-the-roots-part1.md).

## Why he's here

Because he twice changed the course of the story. In Heinz's telling, Cook called
him in over a librarian's accusation that he was a **German spy** (his crime:
working 5–10pm in the library on computer modelling), heard him out, believed him,
and asked him to write up his ideas. Then Cook **drove him to Cambridge** to meet
**Maurice Wilkes** — and later agreed to **fund his PhD** as a Plessey investment.
Without Cook, there is no PIXIE.

He's a small, essential hinge in someone else's memoir. This room keeps him
findable; the facts stay honest about how little is independently known.
