## Who

**C. J. Cheney**, of the **University of Cambridge Mathematical Laboratory**, gave
computing **Cheney's algorithm**: the non-recursive, breadth-first **stop-and-copy
garbage collector** ("A Nonrecursive List Compacting Algorithm," *CACM* 13(11), Nov
1970). Split the heap in two, copy live objects with a `scan` and a `free` pointer,
discard the old half — no recursion, no fragmentation. It underlies copying and
generational garbage collectors to this day.

## Why he's here

Roy Eagleson named "Cheney" in the Cambridge/TRE storyline. The **RAINBOW** paper
(Wiseman, **Cheney**, Etherton, Hiles, Lemke, 1972) puts a **C.J. Cheney** in the
same lab, same years, as a CAD co-author — so this is almost certainly the same
person, and the identification is anchored by that shared byline (still worth
confirming with Heinz). A lovely detail for the show: the person who solved
**how to manage a growing tangle of list structure in memory** was in the room while
PIXIE was building exactly such structures from light-pen drawings.
