## The hooks

### 1. The host in 32K

PolyForth, master Z80, extra 16K for buffers, ~four tasks (Paul, ~20
years later). 24 channel Z80s with 2K EPROM each. What did the host
actually do while Paul burned the channels?

### 2. Deskmate, not lecture

Paul: factoring WORDs, vectors, "OOP without knowing it." Coco: they
never taught that in school. What was Moore like in that room?

### 3. A visual mixer as a Forth machine

Faders, Mustang-stick bumper, hero take to an 8-inch floppy, then the
optical printer. Unusual Forth, Inc. contract — or not, from his side?

### 4. What survived

Host code, listings, disks. Stanford's *Body Music* laserdisc (Box 28.7)
— Coco tied it to Forth; confirm.

### 5. Leo Brodie

Coco named him as a HOMER associate. Forth, Inc. orbit. Confirm.
