### 8. The espresso machine, and the floors
At 545 Tech Square, a large espresso machine stood in the Publications Department, the
office that published the AI Lab memos and had chairs and couches for reading. Anyone
could use it. Don remembers it as the AI Lab's or LCS's, and thinks Marvin may have bought
it, possibly bringing it back from a trip to Europe; he isn't sure of any of that. In his
2022 HN comment ([33356932](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33356932)) he quotes a grad
student: "I came here because of LCS's cappuccino machine!" The machine shows up in Don's
[urchin confession](../kent-pitman/elevator-and-door.md#the-urchins-confession-from-don).

Don's notes of what Henry said on 31 October 2026, written up in
[the door story](../kent-pitman/elevator-and-door.md#the-tenth-floor). Henry has not
reviewed it.

The relay is the elevator hack. Hillis and others went to the 10th floor, put a relay
in, and covered it with dust. Bill Silver remembers Hillis saying he dirtied the new
wires so they would look original. James Turner found that relay room in the summer of
1980. The TV-11 code is dated 13 February 1979.

The 7th floor was the AI Lab. People who had been in Europe had an espresso machine sent
back. By 1991 AI had the 7th, 8th, and 9th (AI Working Paper 335), and there was a
playroom on both the 7th and the 8th. The publications office, where Don remembers the
machine, was room 818, off the 8th-floor playroom.

The 8th floor in the 1970s had orange shag carpet and plants, because Marvin wanted a
common space. The 7th copied it, partly. The piano was there. Yoshiaki Shirai (1971–72)
and Mark L. Miller (2013) both put a piano on that floor, and both remember Marvin
playing it.

Still unresolved, from the same sentence as the espresso machine: two names heard as
"DNA" and "Huntlocker," and Gregor.
