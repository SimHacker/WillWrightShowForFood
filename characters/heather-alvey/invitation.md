# Repo Show invitation — Heather Alvey 🧟

*Heather may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time. [Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md)*
[Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

**Today I'm sending two matching invitations** — yours and **Steve's** ([Steve's invitation](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/steve-alvey/invitation.md)) — for one **pair show**. Each letter is written for the person receiving it; both describe the same show from your two angles, and each of you can read the other's.

Heather —

I'd love to do a **Repo Show** with you and **Steve** — a live, friendly conversation whose stage is
a public GitHub repo people can clone, run, and build on. Mostly I want to celebrate **26 years of
SimFreaks** and send **real fans and subscribers** your way.

You've been doing the thing this whole project is about since the beginning: **content that carries
its own behavior and story** — object suites, deep SimAntics, the magnum-opus packs like **Zombie
Sims**, and the community you've led with such care. I'd love to show the catalog live, hear how you
build it, and point people straight to your store and subscriptions so they can directly support the
work that's kept the lights on all these years.

I'd also love to talk about keeping it thriving: the **Micropolis content-management & publishing
system** I'm building is for exactly this — your back-catalog and new content, the **SimProv Wedding
Playset** we made together (with SimBabes and others), and reincarnated tools (SimShow,
Transmogrifier, RugOMatic, body/head/wig editors), optionally AI-orchestrated, open to fans.
