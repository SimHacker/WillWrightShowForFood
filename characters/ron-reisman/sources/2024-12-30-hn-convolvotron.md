## convolvatron's reply

No. The name is from the early 90s, after a paper microcoding a **CM-2** for high-throughput convolutions with spatially varying kernels, **Hubble** image correction, before the eyeglasses servicing mission. "I decided I could be the hero or anti-hero of convolution." He wanted the references anyway.
