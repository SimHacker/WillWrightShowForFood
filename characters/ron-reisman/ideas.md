# Memorial show hooks — Ronald J. Reisman

*Represent + discuss only. Living guests tell stories; we do not speak as Ron.*
[Memorial standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode)

## 1. Safety-critical engineering vs "move fast and break things"

Don's 2023 HN defense when the FAA NOTAM system failed — not as excuse-making, but as respect for
**methodological ATM work** Ron and colleagues spent careers on. Contrast with glib startup slogans
when **lives and fortunes** are at stake.

## 2. Controllers who program

After Reagan fired **PATCO**, Ron hired fired controllers and taught them to program — domain experts
first, programmers second. A Repo Show segment on **who should write safety software** (pairs with
NeWS/FAA threads and [`../david-rosenthal/slots-all-the-way-down.md`](../david-rosenthal/slots-all-the-way-down.md)
engineering culture themes).

## 3. Ames tour — simulators, AR tower, Convolvotron

USEnix 1989 talk → Don's tour. **Vertical Motion Simulator**; **Marvin Minsky** in early AR (video).
Rebuild a toy **Convolvotron** or ARTT-style tower view in the browser — tie to
[`../don-hopkins/body-electric-bounce-vr-stack.md`](../don-hopkins/body-electric-bounce-vr-stack.md)
and **Rebounce**.

## 4. "What is I?" — dolphins, Apple ][, intelligence testing

Ron at **Dolphin Research Center** (1982 video). Don's line: answer **What is AI?** only after
**What is I?** Natural memorial pairing with **Marvin Minsky** (video co-star) and AI-music
**David Levitt** (Minsky PhD).

## 5. People who loved him

**Coco Conn**, **David Levitt**, **Dave Tristram**, Don — invite as discussants on a **Remembering Ron
Reisman** episode (not a roster read-aloud; their stories, his artifacts).
