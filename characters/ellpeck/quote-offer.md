# Quote offer — Don Hopkins on Tiny Life (2026 refresh)

*Don's own words, offered to Ell for the [Praise page](https://tinylifegame.com/#reviews), Steam, or
anywhere else he likes. **Permission granted in advance** — no need to ask, no need to check back,
edit for length if the layout needs it. The 2024 quote already on the site stays valid; this is a
sharper one, written after playing more and after the Little Computer People work.*

**Supersedes nothing.** Existing quote: [`sources/don-praise.md`](sources/don-praise.md).

## The offered quote

```
Tiny Life is an homage cover of The Sims, played in the key of The Sims 0 — Little
Computer People, 1985, one person and his dog living on one floppy disk. It has in
soul what it lacks in resolution, and soul was always the hard part.

— Don Hopkins, designer on The Sims
```

## Shorter, if the layout is tight

```
Tiny Life has in soul what it lacks in resolution — and soul was always the hard part.

— Don Hopkins, designer on The Sims
```

## Unglossed, for interviews and posts where the lineage is understood

```
An homage cover in the key of The Sims 0.

— Don Hopkins, designer on The Sims
```

## Notes on the wording

**"The Sims 0"** is Little Computer People (Activision, 1985) — the ancestor Will Wright cites,
where the house had room for exactly one person and each floppy held a different one. Calling Tiny
Life a *cover in that key* says the thing the long version of this quote used to take four stacked
adjectives to say: small on purpose, made by one person, and closer to the original spirit than
scale ever got.

**"Soul over resolution"** is a compliment about where the effort went, not a hedge about the
pixels. The gloss is in the quote so a stranger reading a praise page gets it without homework.

**Deliberately not included:** the bone-saw line about EA removing the Exchange from the Legacy
Collection. It's true and it's Don's to say on the show — it does not belong in a quote on Ell's own
storefront, where it would make his page political on someone else's behalf.
