Trygve —

I'm **Don Hopkins**. I'm producing a **Repo Show** about interactive systems history and living
software — with **Alan Kay**, **Will Wright**, and others. Your visit to Xerox PARC that produced
**Model–View–Controller** is one of the clearest named gifts from that era; Kay's public history
traces the *idea* of multiple views as cameras back to **Ivan Sutherland's Sketchpad**, and your
naming made it teachable.

We'd like you on record: what MVC was *for* in Smalltalk, what people flattened later, and how
**roles / DCI** continue the same concern for meaning in interaction.

## What I'm asking

1. **MVC at PARC (1979)** — the problem you were solving; what Smalltalk already had.
2. **Sketchpad → camera → controller** — does Kay's lineage match how it felt?
3. **DCI / roles** — what MVC lost in the web framework era, and what you're repairing.
4. Optional: pair with Kay, or with **Alan Borning** (ThingLab / constraints) on structure vs view.
