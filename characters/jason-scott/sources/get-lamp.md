## One sentence

The oral-history film of interactive fiction — from Colossal Cave and Infocom through the hobbyist renaissance — shot while the living makers could still sit for the camera.

## What’s on the discs (cream)

| Piece | What |
|-------|------|
| **GET LAMP** (main) | Genre history: caves → home computers → commercial crash → modern IF |
| **Bedquilt** | Tour of the Kentucky cave system behind Crowther’s Adventure |
| **Infocom** | ~50 min company portrait (players + employees) |
| Bonus shorts | Dozens of short subjects |
| **It Is Pitch Dark** | MC Frontalot music video (Jason Scott cut) — disc 2; see [`../../mc-frontalot/sources/it-is-pitch-dark.md`](../../mc-frontalot/sources/it-is-pitch-dark.md) |

## Why it belongs on this stage

Repo Show / MOOLLM claim the same lineage Montfort maps and Knuth museums: **parser + world model + rooms**. GET LAMP is the *people* documentary of that lineage — the feelie you watch before you invite Scott Adams, Meretzky’s Infocom cohort, or Brewster-scale preservation talk.
