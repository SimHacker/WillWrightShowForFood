# Jason Scott — conversation hooks

*Public hooks only. Not Jason Scott.*

## Preservation and emulation

- From JSMESS (2011) to 250,000 programs in the browser (2023): what made click-to-play work at
  library scale, and what still doesn't.
- Vector displays in the browser: the Internet Arcade's hard cases in 2014, and the cabinet's Type
  340 (PIXIE, DUEL, Spacewar).
- Software with its paper: the cabinet maps every word in core to its scanned listing page. Is that
  a pattern for Archive items?
- Link rot in the middle of a restoration: the Cambridge PIXIE films, digitized in 2019–2020,
  unreachable on Cambridge's site by October 2026.
- HyperCard stacks at the Archive (2017) and their contemporaries HyperTIES and HyperLook.

## Adventures, MUDs and MOOLLM

- MOOLLM treats a repo as a text adventure: directories as rooms, files as objects, characters
  playable by people or LLMs. Its adventure skill follows Colossal Cave, Zork, MUD and LambdaMOO.
- The Infocom source repositories he put on GitHub in 2019, and what they teach about parsers and
  world models.
- *GET LAMP*'s interviews as design advice for LLM-driven play.
