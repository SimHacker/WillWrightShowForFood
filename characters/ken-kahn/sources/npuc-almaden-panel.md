# The NPUC panel photo — Don and Ken on stage at IBM Almaden

![NPUC speakers panel, IBM Almaden](../media/npuc-panel-almaden.png)

**Left to right (per Don):** Marc Davis, Ken Kahn, Don Hopkins, Fred Lakin,
Danny Bobrow, Tim Brown, John McCarthy, Ted Selker (standing).

Don, from the original Facebook post: "Ted Selker's legendary New Paradigms
for Using Computers (NPUC) workshop, at IBM Almaden Research. I was on the
panel, and wore my tie dye Terrapin Station t-shirt to represent the
University of Maryland and the Grateful Dead."

## Which year? (the comment thread dispute, preserved)

- Don's caption: "First Annual New Paradigms for Using Computers Workshop,
  IBM Almaden Research Center, June 15, 1993."
- **Marc Davis**, in the comments: "The year was 1996. NPUC Speakers
  Roundtable of NPUC 1995 Speakers. Danny Bobrow is in the blue shirt."
- **Ted Selker**, first: "YES 1993, I was blown away by the presentations..
  have transcriptions, videos and audios of 7 years of heros speaking about
  the future then!" — then later: "Marc is right, I believe Lakin wore
  sweatpant in 1993."
- Adjudication note: the [archived 1993 program](https://web.archive.org/web/20060210092923/http://www.almaden.ibm.com/almaden/npuc97/1993.htm)
  doesn't list Marc Davis, which supports Marc's account — the **photo** is
  most likely the 1996 speakers' roundtable of 1995 speakers, while Don's
  and Ken's **talks** below are firmly the First Annual, June 15, 1993.
  Both things are true; the caption fused them.

## The First Annual NPUC program (June 15, 1993, from the Wayback Machine)

- Henry Lieberman (MIT) — Smart Interfaces to Smart Machines
- Ted Selker (IBM Almaden USER lab) — Ways I Want to Work with Computers
- **"Don Hopkin, VISEX" — Self-revealing Gesture Interfaces** (the program
  clipped the s off Don's name; VISEX got it in full)
- Don Norman (Apple ATG) — Things That Make Us Smart
- Fred Lakin (Performing Graphics Company) — A Visual Agent for Group Graphics
- Terry Winograd (Stanford) — Charting the Information Wilderness
- **Ken Kahn (Animated Programs, Inc.) — Sketches and Animations as Programs**
- Panel, "Marketing vs. Innovation": Danny Bobrow (Xerox PARC), Carl Hewitt
  (MIT AI Lab — Ken's PhD advisor, on the same afternoon's panel), John
  McCarthy (Stanford)

Source: [almaden.ibm.com NPUC 1993 page, archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20060210092923/http://www.almaden.ibm.com/almaden/npuc97/1993.htm)

## Why it matters for the show

One photograph holds the seating chart of a show segment: Ken's 1993 talk
title — *Sketches and Animations as Programs* — is his 1979 thesis, his
1990s ToonTalk, and his 2026 chatbot experiments in five words, and Don is
sitting one chair away in tie-dye. Ted Selker says he has "transcriptions,
videos and audios of 7 years" of NPUC — a whole archive-fishing expedition
for the repo.
