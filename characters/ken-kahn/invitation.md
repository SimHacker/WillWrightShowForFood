## What is a Repo Show?

The center of gravity is a **GitHub repo**, not just an mp3 or mp4. I
announce a show and open the repo: questions, artifacts, code, papers,
links, videos. The audience participates and learns *before* the
conversation, follows along live or time-shifted, and keeps contributing
forever after. **Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the
