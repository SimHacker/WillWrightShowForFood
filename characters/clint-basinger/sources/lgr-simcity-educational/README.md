## Why it's a show topic

The **School Edition Lab Pack** is the classroom branch of the same game Don wrote for Unix / NeWS /
HyperLook (multiplayer SimCity) and later open-sourced as
**[Micropolis](https://github.com/SimHacker/MicropolisCore)**. Clint rescued this one off eBay and
put it on camera — Maxis's *"software toys that teach"* — which makes it a perfect artifact to react
to live: **educational SimCity → Micropolis in the browser → the preservation ethos.** In the video
Clint opens the teacher's guide, the copyable student worksheets, a Spanish addendum, the Maxis
Maxims sheet, and the 1996 educational software catalog, then boots **SimCity v1.4C** on an LC575.
**Don Hopkins is in the comments** on this very video.

And the [comments](comments.md) are half the treasure: fifteen years of teachers and students
saying *"we played SimCity in school"* and *"please scan these catalogs,"* plus Clint answering in
his own voice. That crowd testimony is the documented-demand evidence for approaching educators,
libraries, and institutions for support.
