## Logo

Margaret Minsky learned Logo as a child and went on to work on it. She was an assistant teacher at
the MIT Logo Group's 1972 demonstration at Exeter, with turtles, the music box and children; was on
the Logo Group's staff until 1979; helped Marvin Minsky, Seymour Papert and Danny Hillis design and
build General Turtle's TT2500 turtle graphics system; and was on the LCSI team that made Apple Logo
(1981–83). She is a co-author of the History of Logo (HOPL, 2020;
[open access](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/133952)), whose author notes are the source for
this, with Cynthia Solomon's [account of the 1970s](https://logothings.github.io/logothings/The70s.html)
for the TT2500. The cabinet's [Logo turtle plan](../../packages/cabinet/LOGO-TURTLE.md) would value
her memory of those turtles.
