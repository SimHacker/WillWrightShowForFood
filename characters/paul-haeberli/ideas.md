## The hooks

### 1. ConMan, 1988 — "a dynamic live performance that is orchestrated by the user"
Visual dataflow programming for interactive graphics on the IRIS: disintegrate applications into
components, connect them live, let a connection manager route the data. The abstract's closing line
is the Repo Show thesis avant la lettre. Segment: read the 1988 paper on air, then walk the
[patch-cord trail](../../process/trails/visual-programming-patch-cord.md) — Hookup, Body Electric,
Bounce, Max/MSP — and ask what the lineage still hasn't shipped.

### 2. GRAFICA Obscura — research in public, 1994
Unpublished SGI research converted to HTML because Dave Brown inspired him. An open notebook that
taught a generation — proto–Repo Show, proto-blog, proto-"build in public." What made him open it;
what he'd publish now.

### 3. Resurrection hour — DynaDraw, the text fountain, F.U.N.
Paul on Don's animated text fountain (2010): *"I want to resurrect that code someday. it would be
F.U.N."* Do it on air: DynaDraw's physical brush, the text fountain, maybe a ConMan panel — old
code running again, PRs welcome. The show's cauldron loop, applied to graphics history.

### 4. SGI stories — 1983 to 1999
"Working with Tom and Rocky and Dave and Kurt and Jim and Henry and Mark and Marc." The
accumulation buffer (with Kurt Akeley), Paint By Numbers, texture mapping as fundamental drawing
primitive (with Mark Segal), the .rgb image format era, demos and toys. Sixteen years inside the
machine that made 3D real.

### 5. Lamina Design — computation becomes atoms
Precise free-form structures from planar material (2004→). Pair with
[Jared Tarbell](../jared-tarbell/README.md) — Levitated's laser-cut computational gems mined the
same vein. Bits & atoms two-hander.

### 6. The 2010 email as a teaching artifact
`fillRectangle()` in the body of the email; "draw everything, let the hardware cull"; 2M triangles/sec
on a 2010 iPhone; the levCAWorm pointer. Send-code-not-commands — a whole pedagogy in one thread.
Ties to [`send-code-not-commands`](../../process/trails/send-code-not-commands.md).
