**Who:** **Paul Haeberli** — computer graphics researcher at **Silicon Graphics from February 1983
to August 1999**, and one of the field's great toolmakers and note-sharers. His
**[GRAFICA Obscura](https://graficaobscura.com/)** (online since 1994, inspired by Dave Brown) turned
years of unpublished SGI research into an open web notebook that taught a generation of graphics
programmers.

**Greatest hits (start here):**

| Work | Why it matters |
|------|----------------|
| **[ConMan](https://history.siggraph.org/learning/conman-a-visual-programming-language-for-interactive-graphics-by-haeberli/)** (SIGGRAPH 1988) | Visual **dataflow programming for interactive graphics** on the IRIS — connect live components with a connection manager. His abstract's closing line is basically this show's thesis: *"we replace the usual user-machine dialog with a dynamic live performance that is orchestrated by the user."* |
| **The Accumulation Buffer** (SIGGRAPH 1990, with Kurt Akeley) | Hardware-supported motion blur, antialiasing, depth of field — how high-quality rendering got real. |
| **[Paint By Numbers](https://history.siggraph.org/learning/paint-by-numbers-abstract-image-representations-by-haeberli/)** (SIGGRAPH 1990) | Abstract impressionist image representations — painting as sampling. |
| **DynaDraw** (1989, GRAFICA Obscura) | Calligraphy with a physically modeled brush — mass, drag, spring. Still getting reimplemented today. |
| **Texture Mapping as a Fundamental Drawing Primitive** (1993, with Mark Segal) | The paper that taught everyone textures were for *everything*, not just decals. |
| **Lamina Design** (2004→) | Fabricating **precise free-form structures from planar material** — kin to [Jared Tarbell's](../jared-tarbell/README.md) laser-cut computational gems. |

**The Don thread:** In August 2010 Paul sent Don his iPad app **Explore 3D**, then patiently walked
him through rendering cellular automata fast with OpenGL ES — send-the-code style, a
`fillRectangle()` right in the email — and pointed him at **Jared Tarbell's levCAWorm** for mapping
CA onto 3D. Don's animated **text fountain** ("little fluffy clouds") came up; Paul: *"I want to
resurrect that code someday. it would be F.U.N."* — which is precisely the kind of resurrection
this show does on air.

**Why him, why now:** ConMan is a founding document of the
[visual programming patch-cord lineage](../../process/trails/visual-programming-patch-cord.md)
(Hookup, Body Electric, Bounce, Max/MSP) — *dataflow orchestrating a live stage*, 1988. GRAFICA
Obscura is proto–Repo Show: research shared as browsable artifacts, decades before "build in
public." And Lamina makes him kin to the bits-&-atoms thread.
