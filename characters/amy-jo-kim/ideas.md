## What Amy Jo has done

Social game designer, community architect, Game Thinking coach. Designer/consultant on **The Sims**,
**Rock Band**, **Ultima Online**, **eBay**, **Netflix**, **Covet Fashion**, and more. Author of
*Community Building on the Web* (2000) and *Game Thinking* (2018). PhD behavioral neuroscience.
Co-founder **Shufflebrain** with Scott Kim.
