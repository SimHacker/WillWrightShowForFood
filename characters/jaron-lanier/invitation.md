# Repo Show invitation — Jaron Lanier

*Public draft — not sent. Jaron may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.yml)

Jaron —

You are, and I say this with a straight face and a huge grin, the **superheroic rock-star legend
Body Electric programmer supreme.** We've had some of my favorite conversations ever about **VPLs**,
**Bounce**, and **Body Electric** in particular — the **Swivel 3D** integrated 3D transform-tree data
type (Swivel save files as the skeleton), its parameters **projected in real time** by **UDP packet
modules** driving **two SGI renderers, one per eye**; the whole VR nervous system — Polhemus,
DataGlove, headsets, the **Convolvotron** — orchestrated live from one running, editable patch. It was
the grooviest groove I've ever programmed in.

I'd love to put that in public, on a **Repo Show** — a live, friendly session whose whole stage is a
public GitHub repo people can clone, run, and build on.

The first one writes itself: **[Rebounce](../../repo-shows/rebounce/README.md).** I want to **check in
the old Bounce source** and invite a crowd to **rewrite it together — by hand and with AI, competing
and cooperating, exchanging ideas** — chasing that live-coding groove in a modern web stack.
**[David Levitt](../david-levitt/README.md)** and I will seed the design discussion; you'd be the
guest of honor for the **origin story** — where Body Electric came from, and what it was really for.
(I wrote down the stack as I remember it, for you to correct and enrich:
[Body Electric / Bounce VR stack](../don-hopkins/body-electric-bounce-vr-stack.md).)

And there's more I'd love to explore whenever you like, your pick:

- **VR/input devices as live musical instruments** — your rare-instrument world; a generative
  performance thread with Brian Eno and Laurie Anderson.
- **Data dignity / *Who Owns the Future?*** — the intellectual godfather of this whole repossession
  idea: creators owning their data and their forks.

**Format:** live (thinking Twitch), announced ahead, audience follows along and can contribute. Warm
and curious, never gotcha. **Zero homework** — I do the setup. What we record and publish is entirely
your call: solo riff, a one-on-one with me and David, or the full Rebounce jam.

No pressure at all — accepted, delayed, declined, or no reply, all honored gracefully. I just really
love your work, and always have.

— Don Hopkins *(the User Interface Flower Child)* 🌀🥽
