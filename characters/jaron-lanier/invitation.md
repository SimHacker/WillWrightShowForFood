# Repo Show invitation — Jaron Lanier

*Public draft — not sent. Jaron may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.yml)

Jaron —

You co-founded **VPL** and drove the vision behind **Body Electric** — and I always make sure
**Chuck Blanchard** gets credit as its lead designer/programmer. We've talked a lot over the years
about **VPLs**, **Bounce**, and **Body Electric** in particular: the **Swivel 3D** transform-tree
as integrated data (Swivel save files as the skeleton), parameters **projected in real time** via
**UDP** to **two SGI renderers, one per eye**; Polhemus, DataGlove, headsets, the **Convolvotron** —
one running patch you could edit while it ran. That work stuck with me.

I'd like to put some of that in public, on a **Repo Show** — a live session whose stage is a public
GitHub repo people can clone, run, and build on.

The first episode that wants you is **[Rebounce](../../repo-shows/rebounce/README.md).** I plan to
**check in the old Bounce source** and invite people to **rewrite it together — by hand and with AI,
competing and cooperating** — in a modern web stack. **[David Levitt](../david-levitt/README.md)** and
I would seed the design discussion; you'd be welcome for the **origin story** — where Body Electric came
from, and what it was for. (I wrote down the stack as I remember it, for you to correct:
[Body Electric / Bounce VR stack](../don-hopkins/body-electric-bounce-vr-stack.md).)

**Why a repo, not just a video.** The artifact and the stage are the same thing — clone it, run it,
fork it. Contributions land as **signed commits**; authorship travels with the work. That matches a lot
of what you've argued about **data dignity** — no siren server in the middle, instruments you can fork
rather than apps you rent. It's also close to **"user interface all the way to the bottom"**: the world
and its knowledge base as one navigable thing, which was Body Electric's direction. I'm not claiming the
show fully implements your ideas; I'd like to discuss whether the medium fits.

Other threads whenever you have appetite — your pick:

- **VR/input devices as live musical instruments** — your rare-instrument world; a performance thread
  with Brian Eno and Laurie Anderson has been floated.
- **Data dignity / *Who Owns the Future?*** — how repos, forks, and attribution relate to what you wrote.

**Format:** live (thinking Twitch), announced ahead, audience can follow along and contribute. Warm
and curious, not gotcha. **Zero homework** on your side — I do the setup. What we record and publish
is your call: solo riff, a one-on-one with me and David, or the full Rebounce jam.

No pressure — accepted, delayed, declined, or no reply, all fine.

— Don Hopkins *(the User Interface Flower Child)* 🌀🥽
