# Jaron Lanier 🥽 *(dream guest)*

*Sniff:* [`GLANCE.md`](GLANCE.md) · [`ideas.md`](ideas.md)

**VPL Research** co-founder; **Body Electric** visual programming; VR pioneer (he coined "virtual
reality"). Connected to this repo through **David Levitt** and **Bounce** — not through Don's employment
at VPL. Beyond the patch-cord lineage, he's a **dream guest** on two more counts: a **performing
musician** who treats **VR + input devices as expressive musical instruments** (a natural
generative-performance ensemble with [Brian Eno](../brian-eno/README.md),
[Laurie Anderson](../laurie-anderson/README.md), and [Nina Hagen](../nina-hagen/README.md)), and the
author of the **"data dignity"** / *Who Owns the Future?* argument that prefigures the show's
**repossession** thesis. Hooks: [`ideas.md`](ideas.md).
