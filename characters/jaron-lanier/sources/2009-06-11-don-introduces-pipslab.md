# 2009-06-11 — Don introduces PIPS:lab (Amsterdam) to Jaron

*Network receipt. Don’s outbound reply to Jaron’s gig list.*
[Portrayal standards](../../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

## Context

Jaron mailed friends about a free **Grace Cathedral** labyrinth concert with harpist Diana Stork
(12 Jun 2009, San Francisco) — string instruments while recovering from Bell’s palsy. Don was
living in Amsterdam and could not attend.

## Don’s ask

Introduce Jaron to Amsterdam **video performance art** people using **Quartz Composer**, yearning
for something as fun and powerful as **Body Electric**. Pointed at:

http://www.pipslab.nl

(That collective is **PIPS:lab** — Keez Duyves and co-founders including Steye Hallema.)

## Show relevance

Bridge from **VPL / Body Electric** (Rebounce dream-guest headline) to **Amsterdam participation
media** — same instinct as Rebounce: live instruments / patchable worlds, not sealed apps.
