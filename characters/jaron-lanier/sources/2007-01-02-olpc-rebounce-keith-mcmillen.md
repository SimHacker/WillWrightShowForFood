## Jaron Lanier (2 Jan 2007)

**To:** Don Hopkins  
**Subject:** Re: Hi Jaron! Just got SimCity running on OLPC!

> super ultra cool- have got to get you together with mary lou jepsen, the main designer of the olpc- she's a trip

> let's converge sometime in the next week or two- i'd like to include keith mcmillen, who did the robot dance piece at will's party

> keith and i are working together on the project of furthering the kind of performances i used to do with body electric- and i'm frustrated with some issues in max/jitter- maybe we can cook up a scheme to make a best-of architecture- i think it's important for the future of culture
