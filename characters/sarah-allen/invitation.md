# Repo Show invitation — Sarah Allen

*Draft — warm path via a twenty-year email thread and the 2006 OpenLaszlo video contract.
Sarah Allen may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time.*  
[Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md) — also covers direct repo access and easy onboarding.

Sarah —

David Temkin resurrected OpenLaszlo — 5.0, Java removed, compiler in TypeScript, running
in the browser, all the old demos alive — and your name was on the cc list where it
belongs. Which means something specific and slightly absurd: the video components you
contracted me to build in 2006, and code-reviewed line by line, can plausibly **run again,
on air, with the original reviewer watching**. I would like to arrange exactly that.

**The ask:** join the **OpenLaszlo reunion Repo Show** — a friendly recorded conversation
staged in a public GitHub repo, carried through to working code and credited ideas anyone
can browse without an account. **Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the
[premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md)
and more.** Cast so far: Temkin, Oliver Steele, Henry Minsky, me — with David Ungar
invited too (he told the LinkedIn world this month he's still programming in Self daily;
his episode's Option B is this reunion).

**The receipts are already in your guest room:** the July 2006 contract (three milestones,
FMS *and* Red5), the laszlo-dev API review where we argued `stream` → `mediastream`,
`mediadevice`, and `capturing` ("'deaf' and 'blind' would be more descriptive but that's
just too anthropomorphic for me"), the YouTube player Jim Grandy wanted on openlaszlo.org
the day he saw it — and, for the blooper reel, the stolen laptop, the ten-fingered
biometric login theory, and the DreamMachine that nearly gave Max a Pokemon-style seizure.
