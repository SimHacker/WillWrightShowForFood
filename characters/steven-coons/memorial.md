# Remembering Steven Anson Coons (1912–1979)

**Steven Coons** gave computer graphics its classic surface patch — interpolate a smooth sheet from
boundary curves. The **Coons patch** is why Utah-era interactive CAD could talk about *surfaces*
instead of only lines.

In this show's living memory: **Andrew Armit** built Coons-patch interaction on a Cambridge/Utah
**PDP-7**; **Alan Kay** was in awe; **Henri Gouraud** toured with the Multipatch film. We cite Coons;
we do not speak as him.
