# Daan van West 🥁🎚️

*Portrayal of a real invitee, written by Don — not Daan.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

**Daan van West** (Daniel van West, b. 1971, Amsterdam) is the
composer / sound designer / audio engineer of
[**PIPS:lab**](http://www.pipslab.org/) — a member of the collective
since June 1999, trained in audio recording at the Royal Conservatory
in The Hague. He is the Max/MSP-and-composition half of the PIPS:lab
shows already threaded through this repo: **DieSpace** (he scored and
programmed the sound of the afterlife social network that
[Keez Duyves](../keez-duyves/) fronts — Oerol, De Parade, Pittsburgh,
Oldenburg), the **TEDxAmsterdam closing acts** (2013; 2015 **Selfie
Symphony**), **Shadows In The Cloud** (Perth Fringe 2016), Lowlands
installations, the Tropenmuseum Lumandulator, motion-capture music
(The Potator), and the **Kilio Cha Haki** hiphop record he recorded
and mixed in Nairobi. Also a drummer — The Scallymatic Orchestra and,
delightfully, a band called **Moody Allen**.

His own words (2017): *"I am more of an audio engineer slash composer
slash sound designer, with the ability to connect to and understand
technology."*
