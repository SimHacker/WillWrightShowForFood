# Invitation — Daan van West · PIPS:lab audio brain × Moody × the Keez route

*Draft, Aug 2026, not yet sent.*

Dear Daan,

It's Don Hopkins. We last talked in 2017, when I pitched you to Jaunt
and you sent me your CV. I owe you a follow-up, and now I have a good
reason to write.

I'm building the **Will Wright Show For Food**. It's a "Repo Show": a
live, free-form show that runs out of a git repository instead of a
studio. Every guest gets a directory, every episode gets design docs
and primary sources, and the audience can read and fork everything the
panel talks about. It grew out of the content creation tools I built
for The Sims at Maxis. Will Wright is in; he's agreed to do the
premiere and more. Other guests range from Sims-era Maxis people to
indie life-sim developers to interactive artists. That's where
PIPS:lab comes in.

I also want to explain **Soul City**, since it connects to your work.
It's a design I'm building in the open: a waystation where a game
character's "soul file" waits between worlds, and each game re-renders
it in its own medium. Sims 1 characters can leave the EA-owned
property and become citizens somewhere friendlier; literary and
AI-driven characters live next door. The reason I mention it to you:
its neighbor is **DieSpace**, the social network for people who have
already passed away, and you composed and programmed the sound of that
afterlife. Soul City takes the same joke seriously from the other
direction: not chatting with the dead, but giving the simulated a
place to live between lives. I want to put those two ideas on stage
together.

So: **would you be a guest?** In your own right, not as anyone's
plus-one. Some threads I would enjoy hearing you on, though what you
want to talk about matters more:

- **The sound of DieSpace**: what it takes to score an afterlife, and
  25 years of Max/MSP live systems where the audience is the
  instrument (Selfie Symphony, Short Mass Upload, Lumasol nights).
- **Moody**: an old unshipped Sims idea I finally wrote up. Music
  carries a machine-readable mood track, so a room full of simulated
  people reacts to what's playing. The design doc is public
  ([MOODY.md](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/designs/MOODY.md)),
  and there's a panel brewing called *Moody: MIDI for Mood*. You
  drummed in a band called **Moody Allen**, so you have to be there.
- **Meaning for hire**: you've priced meaning per minute your whole
  career, in jingles, theater scores, installations, and a hiphop
  record in Nairobi. The Moody design depends on what annotation
  actually costs, and you know.
- **What have you been up to?** Your 2017 CV ended with a visual
  portfolio in progress. I'd like to hear the 2026 chapter.

One favor: **help me reach Keez.** His room in the show is ready, Die
Space front and center, but he hasn't answered on Facebook and the
phone number I have for him is expired. Do you have a current address
or number for him, or could you nudge him?

Last thing: I was just at NDSM and thought we should all be sitting at
the **Noorderlicht café**, you, Keez, and me. Any time, no agenda.
I'll bring my **ebike-safari app**: it records a place-and-time
synchronized track of a ride (music, GPS trace, photos, spoken
impressions) and treats the city map as a playable record, streets as
grooves, the bike as the needle.

Warm regards,
Don
