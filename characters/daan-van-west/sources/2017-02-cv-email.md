# Daan van West — CV email to Don, February 15, 2017

*Primary source digest. Private email addresses and phone numbers
stay out of this repo; the CV content below is professional record.*

## Provenance: the Messenger pitch, February 9, 2017

The CV didn't arrive out of the blue — Don recruited first. On
February 9, 2017 (3:44 PM) Don opened a Messenger thread: **JauntVR
has an office in Amsterdam and is looking for software developers**
— "I love all the stuff that pips:labs has done!" — describing the
JavaScript-programmable 360° interactive video player he was
building in Unity for iOS, Android, Mac, Windows, and PS, and the
lead platform engineer opening.

Daan's reply is the best one-line bio he has:

> Hi Don, thanks for considering me. Although I use Max/MSP and some
> openFrameworks now and then (and still learning) I don't consider
> myself a software developer. I am more like an **audio
> engineer/composer/bridge builder between sound and technology**,
> mostly with pipslab. What is your email? I'll send you my cv.

Don sent his address; the CV email below followed six days later.

## The email

Subject: **cv Daan (PIPS:lab)**. Daan sent his CV, noting a visual
portfolio was in progress:

> I can imagine it's hard to look through the list and imagine what
> I've done. Therefore I'm working on a visual portfolio, but it's
> not finished yet. ... My friend and ex-pipslabber Steye Hallema is
> film director at Jaunt. From their site i can see they are looking
> for a software engineer. My coding skills are somewhat limited, I
> am more of an audio engineer slash composer slash sound designer,
> with the ability to connect to and understand technology.
>
> Thanks for making the effort to help me, kind regards! Daan van West

(Don was at Jaunt's orbit via Arthur van Hoff at the time; the Steye
connection is the same one that later became the Smartphone
Orchestra thread.)

## CV digest

**Daniel van West** ("roepnaam: Daan"), b. November 22, 1971;
Amsterdam (IJburg).

**Education:** HBO Muziekregistratie (audio recording), Royal
Conservatory, The Hague, 1993–1998. VWO, Cornelis Drebbel College,
Alkmaar. Languages: Dutch, English (both spoken/written), German
(spoken).

**Tools:** Max/MSP/Jitter, Arduino, openFrameworks, Pro Tools, Logic
Pro X, Reaper, Final Cut Pro.

**Continuous:** Multimedia collective **PIPS:lab** since June 1999;
self-employed since January 1999; before that jingle production at
Top Format Productions.

**Selected work by thread:**

- **DieSpace** — Max/MSP programming and compositions: Oerol and De
  Parade 2007; DieSpace remix at Paradiso 2010; Pittsburgh Cultural
  Trust 2012; Oldenburgisches Staatstheater ("DieSpace") 2014.
- **TEDxAmsterdam** — closing act 2013 (Max/MSP programming,
  production, part of composition); closing act 2015: **Selfie
  Symphony** (composition, performance).
- **PIPS:lab shows** — Wortal Combat III (Lowlands 2005), Archie and
  the Bees (De Parade 2009: concept, Max/MSP, compositions,
  performance), Luma2solator (2004, compositions), Short Mass Upload
  (United Nations 2014; Tempo Team 2016), Shadows In The Cloud
  (Perth Fringe Festival 2016: Max/MSP programming, composition),
  Ossobuco (Winterparade 2016), PIPS:lab 10-year CD/DVD (2009).
- **Installations** — Lowlands: Spoohkuis (2013), Wisselkantoor
  (2014, concept/production/composition); Tropenmuseum: De
  Lumandulator (2006); Scheepvaartmuseum: Koningssloep soundscape
  (2015); CineKid: HindSight (2016, interactive sound, Max/MSP +
  Arduino); Maker Faire Twenthe: The Potator (2015, music via motion
  capture); Trampolina (2011); RadarFunk (2009); ClipFanger (2006).
- **Records & studios** — **Kilio Cha Haki** hiphop CD recorded and

- **Bands** — drums in **The Scallymatic Orchestra** (2007–08) and
  **Moody Allen** (2006) — yes, a band called Moody Allen.
- **Commercial** — jingles and spots at Top Format (LIDL campaigns,

  Zonnet website sound (2000).
- **Theater composition** — with Mariecke van der Linden (Paradise

- **VR-adjacent** — CineKid masterclass "VR storytelling" (2016,
  audio production).

## Why it's filed

Daan is the audio half of the PIPS:lab material already in the show
(Die Space seed, Keez's room), a Max/MSP live-systems veteran, and —
via Moody Allen and a career of pricing meaning-per-minute — a
natural chair on the
[Moody: MIDI for Mood](../../../repo-shows/moody-midi-for-mood/moody-midi-for-mood.yml)
panel. The [invitation](../invitation.md) is the follow-up Don owed
this email anyway.
