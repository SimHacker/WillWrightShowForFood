# Kim Cascone 📡 *(Silent Records; Aesthetics of Failure)*

*Invitation portrayal — not Kim Cascone.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · authored by Don Hopkins

## Who

Composer and Silent Records founder; assistant music editor on Lynch's *Twin Peaks*
/ *Wild at Heart*; author of **"The Aesthetics of Failure"** (CMJ 2000) — the
canonical post-digital / glitch essay.

## Why invited

Bugs as material. Simulation toys and pie menus already live in failure modes —
Cascone named the aesthetic. Music-machines nights with Machover et al.
