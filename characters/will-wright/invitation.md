Hey Will,

You said yes — so let me say, in public, what this is, and cheekily lay out the things **you've already
agreed to help me pull off.** (No pressure on any of it; the show stays loose. This is the map, not the

The spark: I dug Stanford's recording of your **1996 talk** to Terry Winograd's class out of the
archives, put it on YouTube, and cleaned up the transcript. It's the one where a student asks what
you're working on, you say **"Oh, God…"**, load a SimCity file into **Dollhouse**, and give the first
public preview of what became **The Sims**. *That talk is the reason I went to Maxis.* Now it's the seed
of the whole show.

## What a Repo Show is

Like a blog is a *web log*, a **Repo Show** is a show whose whole stage is a **public GitHub repo you
can enter, fork, and improve.** It's the opposite of AI slop: instead of letting a model regurgitate a
flattened echo of people's work, I go straight to the people who **actually invented this stuff**, we
talk in public, and the AI does the part it's good at — turning ideas into **working code, live**.
**Show, don't tell.** (The medium, in full: [`VISION.md`](../../process/VISION.md).)

**You're the first guest — and there's no assigned topic. You're the topic.** We start from that 1996
video and follow wherever you want to go.

## The things you've (bravely, cheekily) signed up for

- **The post-mortem 30 years in the making.** In 1996 you post-mortemed three games — SimAnt (too
  simple), SimEarth (too complex), SimCity 2000 (just right) — then pivoted to *preview* The Sims. Now,
  decades later, we finally do the **post-mortem on The Sims 1**, using your own 1996 words as the seed:
  what you nailed (Simlish as Peanuts "mwa-mwa" adult-talk, plug-in objects that "advertise," user
  content posted to the net, storytelling…) and what you'd call differently now.
- **The crown jewel, finally built.** The thing you nailed in '96 that *still* isn't really shipped:
  **data portability** — characters, homes, objects, and memories moving between games and universes
  (your train-set hobby model). We build it, live: a **Proxi ⇔ Soul City ⇔ The Sims** bridge — *both
  ends are your games*, Soul City (the liminal heaven where souls lay over between worlds) is the
  waystation. Your **soul-file travels**; each world re-renders it in its own medium. We've even got a model for *how* a character
  accretes worlds without losing itself — **serial endosymbiosis** (Margulis — who, delightfully,
  co-wrote the Gaia hypothesis with your SimEarth advisor Lovelock): each game a character passes
  through is kept as a nested organelle with its own DNA, all running in parallel, the soul-file the
  source of truth. This is the [**RepoShow Soul**](../../process/VISION.md) idea: *you repossess your
  character from the walled gardens.* ([crown-jewel theme](../../repo-shows/ideas/themes/data-portability-crown-jewel.yml).)
- **Reincarnate Slats.** **Slats** — the robot brain I programmed at your **Stupid Fun Club**, who
  showed up in the One Minute Movies (*Servitude*, *Empathy*). I still have the code (and a laptop that
  might run it — emulation if not). We make **bringing Slats back** a live quest: read the old code
  together, revive him, have him **call into the chat**, interview him, reprogram him, interview him
  again. **RoboResurrection** — constructionism as theater.
  ([slats-reincarnation.yml](../../repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/slats-reincarnation.yml).)

## How it works — and the small, real ask

The ask is one word: **join.** You don't "appear on" a Repo Show — you *join* it. You join the repo, the
call, the chat; the show **is** the joining. (And yes — the repo's called **SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood**:
"will work for food" → "Will Wright Show For Food." I couldn't resist, and I figured you'd forgive me.)

- **Zero homework — as much *homefun* as you like.** The audience is invited to actually *build* the
  ideas we raise, AI-assisted, on the open engine, and check their work into the repo; we look at the
  best ones together on later episodes. "Homefun," not homework.
- **No slides — let the AI make the visuals *with* you.** If you want illustrations, slides, even little
  videos, we author them live in Cursor, screen-shared, as your offhand *"what if it could…"* becomes
  running code. AI as your co-author, not a scraper. That's the whole point made flesh.
- **Warm, not gotcha.** Live (thinking Twitch), announced ahead, audience participates — Phil-Donahue-
  working-the-room, not ambush-podcast.

## The bigger mission (none of it required for show one)

1. **Bridging characters between worlds** — your 1996 dream, for real: Proxi ↔ The Sims ↔ Stardew and
   beyond, a character picking up a little Timothy-Leary-*Mind-Mirror* read along the way (run as the
   actual 1986 EA binary in an emulator, in the browser). Powers-of-ten across your own universe of
   games — home (Sims) → city (SimCity/Micropolis) → planet (SimEarth) → cosmos (Spore) — with Proxi as
   the soul that moves through them.
2. **A browser-native platform for Sims tools** — the whole content stack (render, read, write, edit,
   *generate*) reborn web-native: Transmogrifier, RugOMatic, the WigOMatic.
3. **Reviving the Sims Exchange** — now that The Sims 1 is on Steam *without* the Exchange, rebuild the
   community save-and-story-sharing site: the piece that made The Sims a **medium**.
