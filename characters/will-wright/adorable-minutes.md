# The Adorable Will Wright Minutes — short-form Q&A format

Will Wright's **one-minute lesson** series (2008, celesta920 YouTube) — random facts, Care Bears
mythology, Russian space stations, Kubrick, battle bots, advertising history.

Playlist anchor: [The Adorable Will Wright Minutes!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f7G6c097oWU)
(celesta920, 2008 · ~2,159 views · 9 one-minute lessons stitched together, ~9:49 total)

## Episode index + highlights (mined from the transcript)

| 1 | Aug 11 | **Russian Space Station** | Almaz-class manned spy satellite carried a **bore-mounted 20mm cannon**. Firing it de-orbited the station (equivalent to firing the reaction thrusters), so they tied it into the RCS. Destroyed a target ~3km away in tests. Undisclosed until the Berlin Wall fell — "in violation of every space treaty." | 0:13 |
| 2 | Aug 18 | **2001: A Space Odyssey** | Kubrick, trained as a photographer, broke every filmmaking rule — 70mm camera used like a still camera, minute-long static scenes. Couldn't get/find a dead zebra for the savannah vulture scene, so **the "zebra" is a horse painted with stripes.** | 1:28 |
| 3 | Aug 20 | **Batman** | The lone yellow bat symbol was a **red herring / target** — criminals instinctively aim for it, and that's exactly where the armor is thickest. Will used the same trick on his **battle bots** (doll's head decoy over thickest armor). | 2:34 |
| 4 | Sept 2 | **Care Bears** | Care-a-Lot floats in the heavens and **enslaves the stars**; below is the Forest of Feelings where the cousins (lions, raccoons — not bears) live *without* the particle-beam Care Bear Stare, which meshes together **like the Death Star**. Implication: Care Bears near heaven, Earth near hell. | 3:37 |
| 5 | Sept 3 | **Russian Breakfast** | A Moscow cab driver's saying: **"Breakfast is for yourself, lunch you share with a friend, dinner you give to your enemies."** Will loves that Russians work *enemies* into health advice. | 4:26 |

| 8 | Sept 5 | **Ants** | Pheromone trails are *local* storage; **trophallaxis** (mouth-to-mouth food sharing) is *global* storage. The queen injects a chemical into the social stomach; if she dies it dries up in ~48h and the whole colony knows and changes behavior. | 7:42 |
| 9 | Sept 25 | **Buy More Stuff** | Post-WWII, war factories converted to washing machines & cars. The mismatch between production capacity and consumption **birthed the modern advertising industry** — convincing Americans to consume ~2x. | 8:56 |

## The Will Roll (cutaway library)

When **someone else** is infodumping (Don on STAR FORTS, a guest on their SpIn), **Will** supplies
the cutaway bank — not to shut the speaker down, but to carry the social layer so the sermon never
apologizes for itself.

Full spec: [`will-roll.yml`](will-roll.yml)

**On-brand (Don, witness):** Years at Maxis and after — Will rolls his eyes, chuckles kindly, listens
attentively again the millionth time, and **always** has something new and insightful to say back. The
reaction clips are beats one and two; the insight is beat four. That's why the joke is his kindness.
