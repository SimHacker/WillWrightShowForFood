# Ant Farm Revolution — Fry's shelf find

*Retail archaeology — Uncle Milton × Will Wright × Stupid Fun Club.*

![Ant Farm Revolution box — Uncle Milton, from the mind of Will Wright, Stupid Fun Club badge; gel habitat with ceiling projector (Don Hopkins archive)](ant-farm-revolution-frys-box.png)

**Ant Farm Revolution** — Will Wright designed with **Uncle Milton**: live ants dig **3D tunnels** in nutrient gel; an **LED projector** casts **giant ant shadows on your ceiling**. Stupid Fun Club badge on the box. Break-resistant, escape-proof, no food or water needed.

Don found this on the shelf at **Fry's Electronics** — cowboy-themed Silicon Valley big-box retail, now extinct. Sharks 2012–13 box edge visible in the photo; dark wood counter; peak SV toy archaeology.
