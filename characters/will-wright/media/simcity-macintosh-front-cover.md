# SimCity — original Macintosh box (1989)

*Control-panel aesthetic — disasters as red buttons, SF skyline on the monitor.*

![SimCity — The City Simulator — original Macintosh front box cover — beige control console, disaster buttons, Transamerica Pyramid with tornado (MobyGames / filfre.net)](simcity-macintosh-front-cover.jpg)

**SimCity — The City Simulator** — Macintosh retail box art (1989). Beige plastic **control console**: dials, gauges, disaster buttons framing a central monitor. Nighttime **San Francisco** skyline (Transamerica Pyramid) struck by a **tornado**. Zoning dials — residential / commercial / industrial — budget, energy gauge, city selector (San Francisco selected / New York).

## Awards on box

- **Computer Gaming World** — SimCity **1989 GAME OF THE YEAR**
