## Why the restoration matters

Design Plunder is unreadable without slides — it's *"somewhere between a PowerPoint slideshow and a low framerate movie."* The talk is a visual raid: chairs, Bronx Development Center, Eames House of Cards, fitness landscape diagrams, Japanese garden photos, Sims fan-site screenshots. Cory's edit is the **canonical watch experience** for production and for Don's Winograd article slide citations.
