# Summary — *Design Plunder* (GDC 2001)

**Will Wright** · Game Design Keynote · [Cory Martin + slides](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c91IWh4agzU)

Jack London club quote → raid other design fields for game inspiration. Prequel to 2005 Spore rhetoric and post-1996 Winograd Sims community arc.

## Elevator pitch

PowerPoint-as-movie keynote: chair design, Christopher Alexander, Eames crossover, **interaction onion**, Calvin bulldozer, Sims as **sexually transmitted disease** (male gamers → girlfriends vector), happiness **fitness landscapes**, Japanese gardens ↔ Miyamoto, **80/20 processor flip** (imagination vs computer), Schwarzenegger prank-call AI, **SimFreaks** ecology, story exchange, Truman Show story director. Close: only **SimCity** and **The Sims** were ideas everyone said sucked.

## Quotes worth clipping

**On failure:**
> Failure is bad when it's repetitive — too often, too little variety, or inexplicable.

**On disease:**
> The Sims was quite similar to a sexually transmitted disease — hardcore male gamers spread it to girlfriends; then lateral explosion in players who'd never touched a computer game.

**On imagination:**
> It used to be 20% on the computer, 80% in our head. Now it's almost exactly reversed.

**On AI (2001):**
> We could probably build an AI that talked roughly at that level — the key was she thought she was talking to a real person.

**On determination:**
> The two games where everybody told me my idea sucked were SimCity and The Sims.
