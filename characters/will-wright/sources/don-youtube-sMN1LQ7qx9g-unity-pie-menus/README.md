# Unity3D Pie Menu Demo

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sMN1LQ7qx9g

Don's Unity pie menu prototype — continuation of Sims/NeWS pie menu work. Referenced in [2023-unity-tools-portfolio](../2023-unity-tools-portfolio/README.md) and [2022-cam-6-pie-menus-visual-programming](../2022-cam-6-pie-menus-visual-programming/README.md).

These are the pie menus David Ungar critiqued on foveation grounds — see
[fitts-and-foveation.md](../../../david-ungar/fitts-and-foveation.md) ("motion is a
foveation summons"). Notable in the demo: the label-comes-to-the-cursor move (0:49),
the webcam head in the menu center looking at the selected item, Sims-style (1:37),
and a disabled Pac-Man that explains *why* it's disabled (1:05).
