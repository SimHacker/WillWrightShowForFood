## How Proxi works (PC Gamer recap)

1. Player **types a memory** (e.g. college trip with a friend).
2. Game turns it into an **animated scene**.
3. Scenes accumulate in a **"mind world"** — explorable 3D environment with minigames.
4. People from memories become **"proxies"** to interact and play with.

> "I don't remember when I first thought of [Proxi]," Wright said. "I think it's more like an idea that was **percolating for a long time**. Obviously it has a lot of relationship to The Sims."

## Design philosophy

> "I found myself getting continuously closer and closer to the **player**."

> "No game designer has ever gone wrong by **overestimating the narcissism of their players**."

> "It goes to figure that the more I can make a game about **you**, the more you'll like it."

*(PC Gamer: Will said this playfully, chuckling — design philosophy, not a cynical pitch.)*
