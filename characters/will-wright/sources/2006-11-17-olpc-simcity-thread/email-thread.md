I'm apprehensive about it because in all likelihood, based on previous experience, I will end up wasting a lot of time on it, then EA will drop the ball and nothing will actually happen. While I would be happy to help if something were to really happen, I absolutely cannot afford to throw away any more of my precious time and money and energy on a project that will never go anywhere.

Last time I asked you about this (in early 2002), you suggested I write up a proposal and talk with Lucy about it, because you said EA was pressuring her to do something in support of education.

Columbia University's Earth Science Department told me they were redesigning their curriculum around using SimCity to teach students, funded by an NSF grant. They simply wanted a scriptable version of SimCity that they could control, instrument and collect data from, to export as spreadsheets, so students could perform experiments and analyze the data.

So I wrote this proposal, printed some illustrations, made an appointment to talk with Lucy about it, and she seemed quite positive and enthusiastic about the proposal at the time. I thought it would be more likely to be approved if I framed it as a commercial product that EA would profit from, with an educational discount, and I reassured her I'd take every step to protect EA's intellectual property (opening it up and making it scriptable, but not releasing the source code). But of course I'd rather publish all the source code as open source, if that were possible, but that seemed like far too much to ask for.

As I left, she said the ball was in her court, and that she would try to write up a contract, and let me know how it went. Then I never heard back from her. She stopped returning my phone calls and ignoring my email soon after that. Even after I explained the problem to her administrative assistant, who assured me she would call me right back, I never got any calls or emails back.

I don't know why Lucy dropped the ball, cut me off, and never let me know to stop wasting my time. Probably there is some reason it's impossible, but she never told me why. By now of course I have given up my hopes and dreams of an open source educational version of multi player SimCity, and written off all that work as a waste of time. So this whole topic is somewhat depressing.
