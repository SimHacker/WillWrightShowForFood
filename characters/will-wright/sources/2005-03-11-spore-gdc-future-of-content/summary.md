# Summary — *The Future of Content* (GDC 2005-03-11)

**Will Wright** · Game Developers Conference · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofA6YWVTURU)  
**Don Hopkins** — audience notes ([Medium](https://donhopkins.medium.com/the-future-of-content-will-wrights-spore-demo-at-gdc-3-11-2005-9eab0cedc583), reviewed by Will)

First public **Spore** reveal. Use this doc for retrospective episodes: promise vs shipped game,
procedural content rhetoric, and Don's witness account.

## Elevator pitch

Will **lied about the title** so EA wouldn't know he was about to demo Spore. His real argument:
AAA content costs are winning a losing battle — **give players ownership and procedural leverage**
instead. Live demo climbs from tidepool to planet-buster, with creature editor, procedural mating,
and a **T-shaped** design (evolution ladder + sci-fi meta-games). The talk that launched a thousand
"what could have been" comments.

## Quotes worth clipping

**On the title lie:**
> This year when they asked me what I was going to talk about I just flat out lied.

**On the demo:**
> It's very unfinished — kind of held together with baling wire and duct tape.

**On compression:**
> Something like a five thousand to one compression.

**On Care Bears geography:**
> They collectively refer to this as the kingdom of caring… Earth is the kingdom of people who don't give a shit.

**On planet-busting:**
> There go our friends, and there goes my reputation as a non-violent game developer.

**On imagination:**
> The biggest obstacle I truly had was making myself believe that we could build the game.
