Will wanted the **game developer community first** — not E3 marketing. He sent EA executives a fake
title and slide deck; the real talk was the first public **Spore** reveal: procedural content as an
alternate future to mountains of hand-authored AAA data.

## The lie about the title

Will's opening beat: GDC usually gets a vague title a week late — **this year he flat-out lied**.
The published title *The Future of Content* hid the Spore demo from EA review. A more honest title:

> What I Learned About Content from The Sims, and Why it's Driven Me to Procedural Methods, and What I Now Plan to Do With Them

## Core thesis (one paragraph)

**Content cost scales non-linearly; player value does not.** Give players **ownership** over what they
make and you expand the value surface without hiring an army of artists. **Algorithmic compression**
(genotype → phenotype) lets one kilobyte of creature DNA explode into megabytes of mesh, texture, and
animation — the same knobs the player turns can be turned by generative systems. Spore is a **T-shaped
game**: climb evolution (cell → galactic god) as tutorial, then surf sideways through sci-fi meta-games
and vertically back down into any editor layer. Hub concept: **compression** — creative leverage,
portable content, unbounded worlds.

## Act structure

### I — Content economics (0:00–12:00)

- Sims 2 content mountain; team size vs content effort diverging.
- **Utility theory** — 101st apple on a desert island.
- Player-made content (17k characters, 100k objects on fan sites).
- **Ownership** dimension — Moe in GTA, player stories beat cutscenes.
- CD-ROM as death knell for the algorithm; **demo scene** as counter-tribe (Helsinki, 64k → CD-ROM).
- Accurate title reveal; recruit "elite team" in hidden facility.

### II — Spore demo: evolution ladder (12:00–39:00)

- **Held together with baling wire and duct tape** — unfinished, developer-first honesty.
- Tidepool (2D Pac-Man), egg → editor (Mr. Potato Head × Etch-a-Sketch × clay).
- Ocean → land: **procedural walking** from skeleton analysis; drag corpse while walking = new verb.
- **Procedural mating** — crowd goes wild; currency = reproduction; brains = college fund.
- Creature parade: buttface, **Ford Explorer** SUV creature, hang dog, **carnivorous Care Bear**.
- **Care Bears** digression — greeting cards, cousins, Care-a-Lot; Earth = "kingdom of people who don't give a shit."
- **Artist in a box** (Ocean Quigley) — Pixar ↔ Giger; **creative amplifier** + **5000:1** decompression.
- **Creature clipboard** (Don's label) — pollination / async genotype handoff between worlds.

### III — Tribe → space (30:00–55:00)

- Monkeys with weapons; procedural dance; Populous / SimCity / Civ homages — **break the don't-mix-genres rule**.
- **Brain palette** → sentient → first-person eater mutates to RTS.
- Player as Lucas/Tolkien/Dr. Seuss — story as side effect.
- **UFO / MOOFO** — content-creation tool; **Mutual of Omaha's Wild Kingdom** → tranquilizer gun from helicopter.
- Solar system sandboxes; drop abducted creature → **explosive decompression** (no atmosphere).
- **Kid Pix** terraforming; Genesis device; interstellar drive; **SETI tool** on star map.
- First contact → planet-buster → *"there goes my reputation as a non-violent game developer."*

### IV — Design frame + close (55:00–1:02)

- Meta-games (War of the Worlds, Close Encounters, uplift, diplomacy).
- **T-shaped game** — goal ladder trains editors; space layer = storytelling sandbox.
- **Powers of Ten** × **Drake's equation** — Wired secret diagram (Feb 2004) published without explanation.
- **Bacteria to galactic god** — 200+ prototypes; **prototyping has become a religion**.
- **THE dream quote** @ [1:00:57](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=3657) — biggest bottleneck was Will's own imagination; once he believed, persuading EA was easy; revisit weird outside-the-box ideas. Don remembered this advice and applied it decades later.
- Can't talk again until E3.

## Retrospective hooks (show mining)

Use Don's notes + this video alongside shipped Spore and the [Long Now 2006](../2006-06-26-long-now-playing-with-time-eno-wright/README.md) demo:

- **Demo vs game** — much of the GDC setpiece was scripted; still the cultural promise that sold a generation.
- **Scope cuts** — aquatic stage, city depth, procedural mating minigame, genre-mixing Civ layer.
- **Prophecy** — "next generation titles incredibly expensive" (2005) → 2024 industry shape.
- **Compression hub** — still the Repo Show through-line (Micropolis, MOOLLM, generative minutes).
- **Founding advice** — flagship clip `revisit-weird-outside-the-box` — the Spore GDC close Don carried forward into present work.
