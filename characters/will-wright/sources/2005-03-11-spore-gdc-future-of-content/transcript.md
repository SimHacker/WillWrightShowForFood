## I — Content economics {#content-economics}

**[2:10]** Sims 2 content mountain vs Sims 1. Content effort scaling **non-linearly** vs team size. Twice the content ≈ twice the effort; player value is not twice as much — basic utility theory (101st apple on a desert island). — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=130)

**[3:05]** Players love making content — Sims 2 site: ~17,000 characters, 10,000 houses. Not all great, but even 5–10% good is huge. Phantom fan site: 100,000th uploaded object celebration. Metrics useful: what they buy, verbs they choose. Players hate invisible walls. Emergent games need iteration; content costs fight back. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=185)

**[5:06]** Missing dimension: **ownership** over content. Pull that lever and you add tremendous player value — maybe you don't need as much hand-authored content if the player made it. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=306)

**[5:23]** GTA example: **Moe** — slacker on a bike, cute underwear and Barbie watch in cutscenes taken seriously. Will tells his daughter and wife stories about what Moe did; never retells cutscenes. *"Player stories will always be more powerful than scripted stories."* — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=323)

**[6:42]** Apple II: six times faster than a teletype; half memory code, half data. Today ratio ~**10:1** code to data. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=402)

**[7:19]** **CD-ROM** — couldn't conceive filling it. Trend toward piling data into games. *"Kind of like the meteor that sounded the death knell for the algorithm."* — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=439)

**[8:07]** **Myst** — elaborate HyperCard slideshow; immersive but *"I wish I could have made this world — that was the fun part."* — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=487)

**[8:46]** Counter-tribe: **demo scene** — Helsinki ground zero. 64k procedurally generates animation, music, visuals. Square 64k six times → CD-ROM. Evolved from crackers signing their work. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=526)

**[10:31]** Recruited elite team; hidden state-of-the-art facility. Telescope vs microscope tangent — daughter was five; couldn't find a good microscope anywhere. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=631)

**[11:05]** *"This is just kind of Will's personal information you might find valuable."* If he had to pick telescope or microscope, pick the microscope — especially with a kid. eBay makes them easy; want stereo, overhead lighting, **low power** (counterintuitive). Put your hand under it, spend an hour obsessing — *"it's like a religious experience. Anyway, back to our story."* — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=665) · [return segue](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=699)

**[11:43]** So we recruited this elite team of developers… — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=703)

## III — Ocean, land, mating, creature parade {#creature-stage}

**[14:39]** 2D → 3D ocean. Entire world procedural; player creature's animations, behaviors, textures generated from what you built. **Other creatures come from other players** — asynchronously delivered to fill a balanced ecosystem. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=879)

**[15:58]** Creature editor: Mr. Potato Head × erector set × clay. Skeleton underneath; sculpt with mouse wheel. Functional parts — graspers, weapons, legs, feet. Tripod example. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=958)

**[17:48]** Lost fins → doesn't swim; walks onto land. **Procedural walking** from skeleton analysis. Every verb procedural. Fight using tail-mounted weapon. Kill, eat, **drag** corpse while walking — mixed verbs invent new behavior. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=1068)

## IV — Compression, pollination, brains {#compression}

**[26:37]** **Artist in a box** (Ocean Quigley) — automate what artists do; simple controls, creative leverage. Stylistic range Pixar ↔ Giger / Dr. Seuss ↔ scary. Player manipulates small system; computer generates mesh, textures, animations, behavior. **Creative amplification** → **creative amplifier** on what the player did. — [amplifier](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=1683) · [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=1597)

**[28:18]** Creature representation ~**1k bytes** → ~**5 MB** with animations and textures — **5000:1 compression**. Enables huge local database on CD, low-friction peer/server move, generative systems using same knobs. Genotype → phenotype. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=1698)

**[29:15]** **Creature clipboard** (Don's label) / **pollination:** creatures sent to server, categorized by behavior, size, diet; your world requests content to build ecosystem around you. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=1755)

**[30:08]** **Brain palette** — save funds while playing; college-savings-account metaphor. Highest brain → **sentient** → game mutates from first-person eater to **RTS**. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=1808)

## VI — Civ, UFO, solar system {#civ-ufo}

**[37:50]** Entering **civilization game** — simple Civ/Risk. Pull back: entire world; cities exaggerate to icons when zoomed out (aesthetic + functional micromanagement level). Conquer continent. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=2270)

**[39:55]** Homage across levels; *"don't mix genres"* rule — *"I've always wanted to break that rule really badly."* ~20% of best SimCity/Civ; ~40% aesthetic building. Stevenson drew Treasure Island map first; story emerged. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=2395)

## VII — Space, meta-games, close {#space-close}

**[55:21]** Space-level **meta-games** — War of the Worlds, Day the Earth Stood Still, uplift/2001, first contact, abduction/cross-breeding, diplomacy (Star Trek races as landmarks). 1930s–40s sci-fi comic covers as aesthetic north star. Overt browsing: steal creatures/vehicles into your library; zoo planets. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=3321)

**[57:27]** **T-shaped game** — goal-oriented ladder (bottom) trains editors and simulation dynamics; top is broad sci-fi sandbox. Opposite of usual: sandbox-as-tutorial here; goals teach tools first. Fractal surf: horizontal across planets, vertical down into any editor layer. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=3447)

**[58:45]** **Powers of Ten** × **Drake's equation** — terms map to scale levels. Wired (Feb 2004) published secret Spore diagram without knowing what it was. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=3525)

**[59:53]** Player story compressed: **bacteria to galactic god** — replay history and future of life. Hub: **compression** (leverage, generative libraries, portable content, unbounded worlds). — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=3593)

**[1:00:50]** **Prototyping has become a religion** — 200+ prototypes; many shown over the years without telling you what they were. — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=3650)

**[1:00:57]** **THE dream quote** (Don remembered this; applied it decades later): as we were working on this game, biggest obstacle was making myself believe we could build it; my own imagination was the bottleneck. Once I truly believed, persuading staff and executives was easy. Revisit totally weird outside-the-box ideas you think could never work — you just never know where they might lead. — [jewel clip](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=3657)

**[1:01:43]** Can't discuss outside this hall until **E3**. *"You can keep clapping."* — [watch](https://youtu.be/ofA6YWVTURU?t=3703)
