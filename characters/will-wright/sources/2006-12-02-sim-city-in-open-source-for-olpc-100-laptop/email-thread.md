        I have some exciting news about the version of multi player SimCity I developed for Unix! 

John Gilmore suggested donating SimCity as open source to the One Laptop Per Child project. 
Will Wright liked the idea, and has suggested it to some people at EA. 
He's endorsed the idea strongly:

"I'd really like to see this happen, it seems like a very worthwhile project at no risk to us. Please let me know what I can do to move it forward if it hits a snag." -WillW

I am hoping that EA will decide to open source SimCity, and I would appreciate any advice on convincing them to go through with it, please. 
Do you have any advice on explaining the benefits of Open Source software and supporting the OLPC project to the corporate executive types at EA? 

John's perspective is that Open Source software is quite fashionable and much easier to justify to corporate America, now that Sun has made Solaris and Java open source! 
I'd love to know: what line of reasoning finally convinced Sun to open source Solaris and Java? 

    -Don
