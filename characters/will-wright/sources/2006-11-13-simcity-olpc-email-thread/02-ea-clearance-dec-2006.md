*(History summary: DUX–Maxis contract 19 Jul 1991, 10-year term expired 19 Jul 2001; Don–DUX contract 18 Feb 1992; HyperLook/NeWS and X11/TCL/Tk versions; InterCHI'93, Electric Carnival, Columbia OPTIMUS, FC6 port ready.)*

> The SimCity source code already compiles and runs quite nicely on the latest version of Fedore Core, which is the Linux operating system the OLPC is using, so people could start playing with it immediately after it was released.
> I can show you a demo and give you a copy of a VMWare virtual machine image, which you can run on Windows or Linux, including the source code, instructions for building it, and a roadmap I've written telling what all the files are for and how it works.
>
> Making SimCity open source will have many tangible benefits to EA and the Sim franchise (and it'll make Will and a lot of other people happy, too).
>
> -Don

### CONTRACT BETWEEN MAXIS AND DUX SOFTWARE (excerpt)

> THIS AGREEMENT is made and entered into this __July_19th_1991__ between, Sim-Business, a California Corporation, doing business as Maxis [...] and DUX Software Corporation [...]
>
> Licensor has developed the computer software described in Exhibit A [...] and is interested in granting to Publisher an exclusive license to convert and translate to the Unix/X environment [...]
>
> (a) "Original Work" the English language version of the computer program described in Exhibit A [...]
> (b) "Program": A Unix/X Windows Version of the Original Work. The Program shall remain the property of the Licensor.
>
> [...]
>
> 11. Terms and Termination.
> (a) Terms. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the date hereof and shall, unless sooner terminated, continue for a term of ten (10) years.
>
> EXHIBIT A: The program is the program currently shipping in the United States on the Macintosh computer, called SimCity.
>
> EXHIBIT B: Ten and one half percent (10.5%) of Sales Receipts [...]

**Signed:** Jeff Braun (Maxis), Robert B Adams (DUX), 19 July 1991.

### CONTRACT BETWEEN DUX SOFTWARE AND DON HOPKINS

> 18 February 1992
> To: Donald Hopkins, SimCity Project Engineer
> From: Bob Adams, President DUX Software
>
> 1. Don Hopkins is recognized as the developer for SimCity UNIX-HuperNeWS version.
> 2. Don has worked on the project as "Work For Hire" and Don assigns all rights to DUX and MAXIS [...]
> 3. DUX acknowledges that a balance due exists which will be paid to Don through royalties [...]
> 5% of all revenues paid to DUX for the sale of SimCity until the balance of contract is paid.
> 2.5% of all revenues paid to DUX from the sale of SimCity for ongoing support [...]

**Signed:** Don Hopkins, Robert Adams, 18 Feb 1992.
