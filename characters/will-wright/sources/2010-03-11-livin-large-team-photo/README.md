## What the thread establishes

**The props are named characters.** The chicken is **Dr. Strange**. The gnome is **Gnomingo** — gnome
crossed with flamingo, fusing the two canonical Sims lawn ornaments into one mascot. A team that
shipped an expansion pack full of joke objects kept joke objects of its own, and named them, and
speaks of the chicken in the present tense a decade later: *"Dr Strange says to say hi to all of
you."*

**Both props still existed in 2010, in Bob King's possession** — the chicken *and* the chicken-print
Hawaiian shirt. That is a live preservation lead, not a memory: **two physical objects from the Sims
1 expansion team, held by a named person.** Worth asking after. It would make a good segment to put
Dr. Strange on camera.

**Alex Zvenigorodsky is the photographer**, confirmed by both Eric Hedman ("hitting up Alex Z to
shoot it") and Alex himself. Charles London notes it was **a whole series** — so more frames exist.

**"The original crew"** (John Brown) is the claim to check: which six, and were they the Sims 1 core
or the Livin' Large team specifically? **Faces are not identified here** beyond what the thread
itself gives: Bob King is the man with the chicken and the shirt Jonathan Knight is teasing him
about. Everyone else is unassigned on purpose.

Also worth noting: the two Erics in one comment thread — **Eric Bowman** ("Bobo") and **Eric Hedman**
("Irk") — a recurring hazard in this archive. They are different people with separate rooms.
