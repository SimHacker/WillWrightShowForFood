Electric Carnival


The [Electric Carnival](http://web.interval.com/projects/ecweb/ec.html) was touring around the country one summer with the Lollapalooza '94 concert. It was a tent full of outrageous computer stuff, sponsored by [Interval Research Corporation](http://www.interval.com), owned by Paul Allen. 

In [SimCity Without Walls](http://web.interval.com/projects/ecweb/i.simcity.html), you and another person can share in a game of [SimCity/Net](README.md), running on an [SGI Indigo](images/SimCity-Indigo.gif) and an [NCD X Terminal](images/SimCity-NCD.gif).

You can create live computer music and graphics in the [MIDI Zoo](http://web.interval.com/projects/ecweb/i.midimenagerie.html), by playing with a wild collection of computers and MIDI instruments, that interact with a visual data flow language for "VR" programming called [Bounce](bounce.md)!
