Hello, Doreen. It's great to hear from you! I'm glad to know you still have the curriculum guide and that it's still relevant. That's wonderful news!
Congratulations on the California Golden Bell award. It would be great to apply SimCity and the curriculum guide to more of that kind of stuff.

The Unix version of SimCity is essentially the same classic version as the guide is written for, but we have the opportunity to revisit it, open it up, and make changes to support more educational uses.
For example, educators at Columbia University's Earth Science and Environmental Engineering department are interested in making SimCity more scriptable, so it could capture and export data from the simulator as spreadsheets, so college students could perform experiments and analyze the results.
What kinds of game enhancements and additional courseware do you think would be useful for using SimCity in education?

I think it would help EA to decide, if they could see the SimCity courseware material you and Leslie Stoltz developed, and any information about who supported its development, who owns the rights, how it was used, etc.
Charles Normann (cnormann@ea.com) is the guy from EA who's helping us to make this happen, so please tell him about what you've done!
