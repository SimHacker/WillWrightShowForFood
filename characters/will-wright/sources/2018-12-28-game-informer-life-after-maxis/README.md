# Will Wright on His Life After Maxis — Game Informer, 28 December 2018

*Source:* Ben Hanson, **The Game Informer Show** — excerpt posted **28 December 2018**.
**Video:** [YouTube — `SlLtSNydkdY`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SlLtSNydkdY) · ~30 minutes · 17,530+ views.

Will's first long-form public interview in years at the time — life after Maxis, **Proxi** (then ~10 people, targeting
release ~one year out), the **MasterClass**, **The Sims Online** as *"glorious failure,"* the **Strong Museum**
notebooks, empirical **astrology** in original Sims personality design, and **Shigeru Miyamoto** naming Dr. Wright.

## Why this one matters for the archive

It is the **earliest on-camera Proxi pitch** in this repo's cluster — predating the Reddit AMA (2024), T1D stream
(2024), VentureBeat crisis piece (2025), and Dev Diaries (2025). Will describes the same core idea those later
sources elaborate: **memories → a landscape of your psyche → AI finds patterns you didn't know you had.** Here he
calls it *"a mirror into your own kind of subconscious"* and says release is *"roughly like a year from now"* (~2019).

It also connects three Maxis-history threads that usually live in separate rooms:

1. **Design notebooks at the Strong Museum of Play** — Will gave them his Maxis notebooks without re-reading them;
   Game Informer filmed pages showing page-after-page of **astrology breakdowns** during early Sims design.
2. **Sims Online** — Will's own post-mortem: missing player customization layer, 100+ person team stress, players
   taking it to sex-chat territory, *"Frankenstein gets up off the table."*
3. **Ocean Quigley's "all of Will's ideas sound terrible"** quote — Will confirms it and frames persistence through
   failure as the job.

## Proxi (early pitch)

> "What I'm working on now is very psychological — you put **memories from your life** into it and it tries to build
> kind of a **map of your psyche** but in a game kind of format."

Team of **~10**. Memories become **graphic objects** in a **memory scape**; the system finds associations — emotions
linked to people, places linked to activities — *"maps of like the people you know on a landscape and the way
they're grouped actually makes sense."*

Privacy framing (2018): mostly personal, sharing optional — *"it's like a mirror into your own kind of subconscious."*
Applied AI angle: not marketing/credit scores — *"using the computer as kind of a **microscope** on"* the mind.

**Release estimate:** ~one year from interview (~2019). *(Proxi/Gallium shipped later; see
[Proxi cluster index](../proxi-gallium-public-index.md).)*

## MasterClass (game design course)

~**20 lessons**, ~15–30 minutes each. Will organized decades of talks into one cohesive whole — *"really understand
something you have to teach it."*

Content beats:

- **Fundamentals** of game design as **player psychology** — *"how you tweak little parts of their brain"*
- **Prototyping**, **system dynamics**, **emergence** — network theory, cellular automata
- **Charles Eames** definition quoted: *"**Design is constraint**"* — maximize result within technical,
  psychological, marketing, or economic limits
- **Can't teach everyone** to be a great game designer — need designer mindset first; can teach a good designer to
  become a great *game* designer
- Favorite game: **Go** — highest ratio of simple rules to complex strategy; emergence is discovered, not engineered
- Pricing mentioned: **$90** one course / **$180** all courses

Pairs with [VentureBeat Proxi piece](../2025-04-29-venturebeat-proxi-funding-crisis/README.md) (pitch difficulty)
and [Dev Diaries 2025](../2025-12-will-wright-dev-diaries/README.md) (heirloom / Bartle on camera).

## Sims design — empirical astrology

Ben Hanson (via Strong Museum visit): early Sims notebooks are **page after page of astrology breakdowns.**

Will's explanation — not belief, but **empirical aesthetics**:

> "I wanted to have the astrology in the Sims be **empirical** — I took these different personality types, put them
> together and saw which ones got along well, and **mapped it to the real astrology signs.**"

Original Sims: **5–6 personality dimensions** → think of as **6D space** → subdivide into **12 equal regions**
(zodiac). Test which regions border each other; map compatibility to real-world astrology descriptions. *"Nothing I
would expect anybody else to enjoy but I really enjoyed visualizing all this."*

Also: Will had no math interest until he learned to **convert any math to geometry** — *"once I could convert it to
geometry and visualize it, it all made perfect sense."*

## Strong Museum notebooks

Game Informer visited the **Strong National Museum of Play** (Rochester) — Will's Maxis notebooks on display.
Will: they asked if he had material; *"I did and I just gave them to him. **I didn't even really look at them**"* —
once used, set aside, never referred back. Notebooks mix Sims brainstorming with grocery lists and phone numbers.

Related: [2020 Stanford archives / Strong Museum correspondence](../2020-stanford-archives-will-wright-lectures/README.md).

## The Sims Online — "glorious failure"

> "Probably termed a **glorious failure** — if it's successful you're gonna be running that thing for **years and
> years and years**, so it's kind of bittersweet."

What was missing: **customization tools** — players customizing objects, a whole layer absent.

Team stress: **100+ people** — *"so much management involved"*; inverted ratio vs small teams (Spore: *"easily spend
three hours meeting for an hour having worked"*).

EA pressure: **not** the stressor — *"I think they were a little more upbeat about it; I was a little more
skeptical the whole time thinking it's not quite ready."*

Player direction: sex-chat-room reputation — Will's frame: communities thrive when players craft what it becomes;
*too much control and it evaporates* — *"Frankenstein… the monster gets up off the table and goes where it wants."*

Post-TSO: some team went to **TirNua** — Will says *"Journey"* / **Luc Barthelet** ran it *(transcript:
"Luke where Lee" — almost certainly [Luc Barthelet](../../../luc-barthelet/) of Maxis)*.

## Small-team nostalgia → early Maxis feel

Working on Proxi with ~10 people feels like **first five industry years** — small teams, doing what they wanted.
Large teams (Spore, TSO): management overhead dominates. Will prefers **3 hours work : 1 hour meeting** over the
inverse.

Cross-link: [Jim Mackraz Memorial Foosball Table](../../../jim-mackraz/memorial-foosball-table.md) (CTG lead on small
Sims core); [1996 newsletter](../1996-03-what-newsletter/README.md) (203 employees, growth stats).

## Miyamoto and Dr. Wright

On early **SNES SimCity**: Nintendo asked if Will minded them naming the help character **Dr. Wright** — green hair,
advice giver. Will honored; Miyamoto *"has actually got to be the best game designer ever and also a good friend."*

Will on Miyamoto's secret: *"**Never lost his childhood** — not like he remembers it fondly, he **kept it with him**.
A good bit of him is still the six-year-old boy playing in the garden."*

Will's version: an **eight-year-old son** helps him see through kid eyes (Minecraft, Kerbal Space Program).
