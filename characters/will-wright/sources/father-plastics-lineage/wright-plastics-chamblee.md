# Wright Plastics — where it was

Seabrook (2006) says Will's father started a successful **plastic packing materials** company in **Atlanta** in the 1960s. He does **not** name it. Will (2026) says the childhood factory was **in Atlanta** and that the drum was **polyethylene pellets** for wrap and bags.

A Georgia company that used the name **Wright Plastics Co. / Wright Plastics Company**, made **blown polyethylene film and bags**, and sat in the Atlanta metro is documented from at least **1974** through **2001**. That is the plant we can put on a map. It is the economical match for Bill Wright, Sr.'s firm. It is **not** a 1960s primary that prints his name as founder.

Do not confuse with **Wright Plastic Products** (Sheridan / St. Clair, Michigan — injection molding) or other Wright Plastics shops.

McGaw Drive is in the industrial park by Peachtree-DeKalb Airport. Help-wanted ads and later corporate filings use this street. The 1974 state directory only says **Chamblee (DeKalb County)** — no street in the index line.

**1960s street is not found yet.** The weekend Will jumped in the pellet drum (age ~5–6, ~1965–66) was "father's factory in Atlanta." Same site, or an earlier building that later moved to McGaw, is unproven.

---

## What it made

- Will, 2026: pellets "melted down and stretched into **plastic wrap and plastic bags**." [Vulture](../2026-03-25-vulture-proxi-profile/README.md)
- SC&H / Bemis (2001): **blown polyethylene film and bags**
- Business-directory leftover: "Packaging Materials"

That is the same process. Polyethylene resin arrives as pellets; blown-film lines melt and extrude it. The drum story is a blown-film plant, not a Bakelite press.

## Who named it Wright Plastics

- [will-wright.com](https://will-wright.com/willshistory1.php) (fansite, no citation): Bill Wright, "owner of Wright Plastics Company"
- Seabrook / 2007 Wikipedia: company unnamed
- 1974 Georgia directory: **Wright Plastics Co.**, Chamblee

On-air: "his father's Atlanta plastics company — later listed as Wright Plastics in Chamblee" is safe. "Bill founded Wright Plastics at 3315 McGaw in 1958" is not.
