## Bill Wright, Sr. (sketch)

- **Profession:** Chemical engineer; developed a new way to manufacture plastic **packing
  materials** in the 1960s; ran a successful company in **Atlanta** (Seabrook does not name
  "Wright Plastics" — verify separately if needed for on-air).
- **Family:** Wife **Beverlye Wright Edwards** (amateur magician, actress). Children: Will,
  younger sister **Whitney**.
- **Personality (press):** Excellent golfer; porch conversations with young Will about stars,
  NASA, Apollo, space colonies — Will planned to be an astronaut; father encouraged it.
- **Death:** Leukemia when Will was nine; mother returned to Baton Rouge.

## Will's plastics-adjacent childhood

### Polyethylene vat (primary)

Weekend at the Atlanta factory; large drum of **polyethylene pellets** — whitish, tactile,
"like a ball pit." Will jumped in; **quicksand** behavior; father rescued him when only his
arms showed above the rim. Will: could easily have died if he inhaled the pellets.

→ Full quotes: [2026-03-25-vulture-proxi-profile](../2026-03-25-vulture-proxi-profile/README.md)

### Model-building obsession (primary)

Ships, cars, planes — "I loved to do that." At ten, balsa **Enterprise** flight deck won a
Star Trek convention award. Avalon Hill **PanzerBlitz**. Seabrook explicitly ties tactile
model-making to digital system-building; Wright later warns The Sims may deprive kids of the
hands-on experiences that inspired him.

→ [New Yorker excerpts](../2006-11-06-new-yorker-game-master/excerpts.md#model-building)

### Montessori → SimCity (primary)

"Montessori taught me the joy of discovery… SimCity comes right out of Montessori — if you
give people this model for building cities, they will abstract from it principles of urban
design."

→ [New Yorker excerpts](../2006-11-06-new-yorker-game-master/excerpts.md#montessori)

## Bridge to game design

| Childhood material | Game-design echo |
|--------------------|------------------|
| Plastic pellets, factory floors | Material literacy; industrial packaging as invisible infrastructure |
| Snap-together models, ship kits | **Hobby model** tradition — extensible parts, systemic assembly |
| Montessori blocks → abstract theory | **Constructionist play** — discover rules by manipulating a microworld |
| Porch space-colony talks with father | Spore's closing image: interstellar sandbox, "as young Will had promised his father" (Seabrook) |
| Raid on Bungling Bay — building > bombing | SimCity genesis — Forrester + Conway's Life |
| SimCity box Bakelite appliance art | Packaging as **simulation console** metaphor — knobs, gauges, disaster buttons |
