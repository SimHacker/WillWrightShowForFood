## How it surfaced: Jim Mackraz asked for it, in 2015

*Source:* [Maxis Alumni thread screenshot](../../../eric-hedman/media/2015-02-06-jim-mackraz-shtick-thread.png),
Jim Mackraz, **6 February 2015**, 6 comments.

The recording has a request behind it. **Jim Mackraz** ([room](../../../jim-mackraz/)) asked the
group:

> "Does anybody have **the comedy shtick the Sims V1 voice talent put together** (*'I'm going to
> pull a present out of my butt!'*)?"

**Don Hopkins:** *"I've got that somewhere — I'll try to pull it out of my butt! \*POP\*"* — followed
by the link to the WAV on his catalog, and then a second quotation from it:

> **"Don't make me scratch my ass. I'm idling!"**

**Jim Mackraz:** *"Outstanding."*

Three things this fixes:

1. **The session's own name, in-house: "the comedy shtick the Sims V1 voice talent put together."**
   Not a VO session that happened to be funny — a bit, assembled deliberately.
2. **Two verbatim lines to check the transcript against** — *"I'm going to pull a present out of my
   butt!"* and *"Don't make me scratch my ass. I'm idling!"* Both are the performers **narrating
   Sim behavior in the first person**, which is exactly the interest of the recording: the second
   line is a voice actor speaking the *idle animation loop* as a grievance. Add both to
   [`TRANSCRIPT-DRAFT.md`](TRANSCRIPT-DRAFT.md) as anchors.
3. **A filename question.** The 2015 link renders in the screenshot as
   `…/catalog/sounds/Steve_And_**Jerri**.wav`, while the live and verified file is
   `Steve_And_**Gerri**.wav`. Either a low-resolution misread, or an earlier spelling later
   corrected — worth confirming, since old copies of the link may be dead.

**In the same thread**, Don posted the design-document bundle —
[`TheSimsDesignDocuments.zip`](../the-sims-design-documents-zip/README.md) — and **Eric Hedman**
([room](../../../eric-hedman/)) replied with his 2002 concept sheet for an unbuilt Sims arcade

## Why it matters

- **Simlish origins on tape.** Will's mandate was emotive gibberish with no fixed meaning; Kearin
  proposed the improv game *Foreign Poet* and brought Lawlor as his female counterpart. This
  recording is them **doing exactly that in English** while the game runs — thought bubbles,
  personality meters, disasters, and bugs as prompts.
- **Not SimAntics programming** — voice/session artifact, not Edith. Thematic link: objects advertise
  behavior; **voice carries feeling** while icons/thoughts carry legible meaning.
- **Show segment:** pair with [language simlish](../../../../schemas/language-simlish.yml),
  Claire Curtin / Jerry Martin guest arcs, [`sims-play-along-narration`](../../../../repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/sims-play-along-narration.yml),
  and the [**Performance Space**](../../../../process/performance-space/README.md) design doc.

## Ethics

Gerri Lawlor died **January 2019**. Discuss and cite with memorial respect; **do not voice her as a
character** on air. See [`RULES-AND-ETHICS.md`](https://github.com/SimHacker/DonHopkins/blob/main/projects/willwrightshowforfood/strategy/RULES-AND-ETHICS.md).

[**Simlish voice video, 23 Aug 2001**](../2001-08-23-simlish-voice-video/README.md) — Kearin and
