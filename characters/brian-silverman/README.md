## Who

**Brian Silverman** — Canadian computer scientist, **Logo** lineage (LCSI, MIT Media Lab), co-inventor
of **Scratch** and **LEGO programmable bricks**, president of **Playful Invention Company** (Montreal).
He invented the **Brian's Brain** cellular automaton rule (and **Seeds**, **Wireworld**) — the
community named it *Brian's Brain*; he did not name it after his own brain. Both rule and inventor are
on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian%27s_Brain).

**Henry Minsky** and **Milan Minsky** visited Brian in **Canada** (July 2026) — old friend of the
Leela / Logo / CA orbit. Don's witness: Brian **programmed PDP-7s** and could read **Heinz Lemke's
PIXIE** listing — rare bridge between Cambridge 1969 satellite code and the Logo/CA preservation party.
