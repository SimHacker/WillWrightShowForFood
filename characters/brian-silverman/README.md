## Who

**Brian Silverman** — Canadian computer scientist, **Logo** lineage (LCSI, MIT Media Lab), co-inventor
of **Scratch** and **LEGO programmable bricks**, president of **Playful Invention Company** (Montreal).
He invented the **Brian's Brain** cellular automaton rule (and **Seeds**, **Wireworld**) — the
community named it *Brian's Brain*; he did not name it after his own brain.

**Henry Minsky** and **Milan Minsky** are in the Leela / Logo / CA orbit — a warm path when timing
works. Don's witness (pending Brian's confirmation): Brian **programmed PDP-7s** and could read
**Heinz Lemke's PIXIE** listing — rare bridge between Cambridge 1969 satellite code and the Logo/CA
preservation party.
