**Will Wright** has already accepted the first invitation — the [premiere show](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md).

Daniel —

We've been talking for **more than ten years** now. I still have that first Facebook thread from
August 2013 — you pitching **FreeTA**, asking whether a full-city GTA-with-no-missions was possible,
whether The Sims 1 had three stories or only the original, naming **SWRSgaming**, dreaming about
your **own engine**. You weren't performing fandom; you were **building toward something**.

I'd love you on the **Repo Show** — not as a prop, as a **peer on the ladder** Will Wright has talked
about for decades: players at the bottom, modders and tool makers near the top, each lift making the
game better for everyone below. You're on that climb. So are [Jeff](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/jeff-adkins) (SimObliterator),
[Heather](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/heather-alvey) & [Steve](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/steve-alvey) (SimFreaks / SimSlice). Your angle is
different and just as real: **city-scale open worlds**, **studio identity**, and the offer to help
drag **Transmogrifier** into the modern browser — the same toolchain thread I've been on since
Maxis days.

---

## What we'd explore (your pick)

- **FreeTA** — the pitch then and now: open world, no missions, full New York; what you'd build
  today (WebGL, Unity, Godot, your own engine).
- **SWRSgaming** — naming as world-building; founders' initials; what the studio is *for*.
- **Fan → builder → tool maker** — your path next to Jeff's and the SimFreaks story; what you'd
  tell someone starting where you started in 2013.
- **Sims lore you actually care about** — e.g. the three-stories question; we can ask Will on air
  if you want the canonical answer filed in the repo.
- **TMOG / browser pipeline** — your modernization offer and where it meets Soul City,
  SimObliterator, and the soul mover.

---

## Format — low pressure

Same as everyone else who's busy:

- Record a **solo video** whenever you like — show your stuff, tell a story, demo a dream.
- Or a relaxed **one-on-one** with me, edited together.
- Or just **keep corresponding** — the repo already grows from our threads; going on camera is
  optional, not a toll.

If nothing here fits right now, say so. The friendship isn't contingent on the show.
