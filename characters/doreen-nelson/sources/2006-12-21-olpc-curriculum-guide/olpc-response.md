# Doreen Nelson × OLPC — sourced response (Dec 2006)

**Doreen Nelson** · 21 December 2006 · *public excerpt*

From the OLPC SimCity **introducing the players** thread (John Gilmore, Dec 2006). Doreen's reply when the cast was introduced — offered to help surface the Maxis curriculum guides.

---

💬 **Doreen Nelson** (21 Dec 2006, to John Gilmore):

> AMAZING that anyone even knows that there is a curriculum guide. I looked at what Leslie Stoltz and I wrote recently and found that it is still relevant. It would be wonderful if there was a way for it to surface. I head an MA Degree program called Design-Based Learning at CalPoly Pomona and lead an effort to train teachers who don't need the MA Degree at Art Center College of Design in Pasadena. We are starting a Charter Hi around Design-Based Learning which just won the California Golden Bell award in the area of Curriculum and instruction. Sim City would be very useful to everyone.
>
> Let me know what I can do to help.

---

## Context

John Gilmore had cc'd the OLPC SimCity cast — Walter Bender, Jim Gettys, Don Hopkins, Chuck Normann (EA), Mitch Bradley. Don followed up the same evening asking about **courseware rights** and educational enhancements for open Micropolis. Full private thread: Don Hopkins private archive.
