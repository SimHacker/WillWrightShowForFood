## Who

**Doreen (Gehry) Nelson** created **Design-Based Learning (DBL)** — students learn any subject by **designing and building model cities and worlds**, then iterating ("backwards thinking"). She is **Professor Emerita** at Cal Poly Pomona, founded the DBL master's program (1995), and trains teachers at Art Center College of Design. The *New York Times* named her one of **30 top U.S. education innovators** (1991).

She contributed to original **Maxis SimCity** and wrote **teacher guides** (with Leslie Stoltz). She is the younger sister of architect **Frank Gehry**.

## SimCity × OLPC (Dec 2006)

When John Gilmore introduced the OLPC SimCity cast, Doreen responded that the curriculum guides were **still relevant** and offered to help surface them. See her [sourced Dec 2006 response](sources/2006-12-21-olpc-curriculum-guide/olpc-response.md) and the [introducing-the-players article](../will-wright/sources/2006-12-simcity-olpc-introducing-the-players/README.md).
