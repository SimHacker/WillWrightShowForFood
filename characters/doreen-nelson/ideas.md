# Ideas to explore with Doreen Nelson 📐

*Conversation hooks for a Repo Show — grounded in documented public work and the Dec 2006 OLPC primary source.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.yml)

## What Doreen has done

**Design-Based Learning (DBL)** — originally "City Building Education": students learn any subject by designing and building model cities/worlds, then iterating. **Professor Emerita**, Cal Poly Pomona; teacher training at Art Center College of Design. **Maxis SimCity teacher guides** (with Leslie Stoltz). *NY Times* top-30 innovator (1991). Sister of **Frank Gehry**.

## Shared ground

Dec 2006: John Gilmore introduced the OLPC SimCity cast; Doreen replied that curriculum guides were **still relevant** and offered to help — see [`olpc-response.md`](sources/2006-12-21-olpc-curriculum-guide/olpc-response.md). Don's Micropolis fork and **Micropolis Home** continue that open-courseware arc.

## The hooks

### 1. DBL live — build a lesson on air
Start from a model city; derive math, history, science. Contrast physical DBL with digital SimCity/Micropolis.

### 2. Free the teacher guides
What EA/Maxis courseware should accompany open Micropolis? Rights, updates, Leslie Stoltz co-authorship, classroom stories.

### 3. OLPC roadmap revisited
Don's 2006 ask: scriptable simulators, spreadsheet export, Sugar integration — what's worth building now?

### 4. Constructionist reunion
Papert lineage, city models, microworlds — joint segment with [`remembering-the-constructionists`](../../repo-shows/remembering-the-constructionists.yml).

### 5. Frank Gehry's sister builds cities too
Architecture, physical models, and why SimCity was always an education product — not just a game.
