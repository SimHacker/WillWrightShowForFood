# Ralph Abraham 🌀 (1936–2024)

**Memorial** — [memorial.md](memorial.md) · we represent and discuss his documented work; we do
not speak as him. [Standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

Mathematician of dynamical systems and chaos; UC Santa Cruz from 1968. *Foundations of Mechanics*
(with Marsden); *Dynamics: The Geometry of Behavior* (with Shaw) — **phase space made visible**,
the ancestor of [Will Wright](../will-wright/README.md)'s possibility-space design vocabulary.
Published the camera-at-monitor loop as mathematics ("Simulation of cascades by video feedback,"
1976) — the demo [Jim Crutchfield](../jim-crutchfield/README.md) formalized a generation later:
[the lineage](../jim-crutchfield/abraham-video-feedback-lineage.md). Santa Cruz counterculture
icon; *Trialogues* with McKenna and Sheldrake; *Chaos, Gaia, Eros*.

**On the show:** memorial segment in the
[Jim Crutchfield show](../../repo-shows/jim-crutchfield/README.md) — Jim knew him from the UCSC
years and can tell the real stories.
