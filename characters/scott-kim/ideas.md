## What Scott has done

Scott Kim (b. 1955) — puzzle designer, visual artist, computer scientist. Co-discoverer of
**ambigrams** (*Inversions*, 1981). Stanford PhD *Viewpoint* (Knuth advisor). Silicon Graphics
logo; Heaven & Earth puzzlemaster; Martin Gardner orbit. Puzzle designer on Tetris, Bejeweled,
Lumosity. Married to **Amy Jo Kim**; co-founder **Shufflebrain**.
