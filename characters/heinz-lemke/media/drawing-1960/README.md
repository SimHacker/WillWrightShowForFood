## Why this matters

The physical prehistory of radial menus. Heinz worked as a **technical draftsman** in German
companies through the 1950s and early 1960s. Drafting workflow: pencil in one hand, **symbol
template** (circles, squares, arrows, valves, nuts and bolts) in the other, on a vertically
positioned, slightly tilted drawing board.

> In PIXIE, I simply translated this into the light pen with the templates being simulated
> by a radial menu of symbols (PIXIE's control light buttons) that can be dynamically adapted
> to the technical content of the drawing.

The earliest known radial menus are a **digitization of the draftsman's symbol template** —
the tool in the non-dominant hand became an adaptive on-screen ring, freeing that hand
for the TTY.
