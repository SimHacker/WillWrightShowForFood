## Heinz Lemke → Don Hopkins (13 Aug 2026, 15:22)

**Cc:** Roy Eagleson, Leo Joskowicz (josko@cs.huji.ac.il), Mario Cypko, and the **IFCARS Think Tank** on Model Guided Medicine (full list below).

Thanks Don for supporting a presentation and demonstration on the theme **"HCI and Modelling -- where we come from and may be going to"** for the CARS 2027 session **"IA and AI: pearls and pitfalls"**. Keep **Roy Eagleson's and students**, **Mario Cypko**, and **Leo Joskowicz** in the loop for planning.

Most Think Tank members have never heard of PIXIE — no harm if they learn "where we come from" as motivation for "and may be going to."

### a) Where we come from

Two PIXIE-like workstation references since the 1960s:

1. **1979 TUB technical report** — *A Network of Medical Work Stations for Integrated Word and Picture Communication in Clinical Medicine* (15 pp, OCR'd at [pacshistory.org](https://www.pacshistory.org/documents/index.html#misc)). IEEE 1979 cranial-CT paper; derived from the **Cambridge Ring** (Wilkes, Sendai 1975). Republished SIIM 2003 as anniversary PACS article. Naive cost estimates in 1979 — but virtually all hospitals now have PACS; origins forgotten.

2. **PIXIE II (2014)** — *3D++ Visualisation of MEBN Graphs and Screen Representations of Patient Models* — Lemke, **Mario Cypko**, **Dave Warner**, Leonard Berliner; MMVR 21, IOS Press 2014. Based on Warner's **ANTz** visualization tool.

### b) May be going to

Example scaffold: **Hospital of the Future — the Good, the Bad and the Ugly** (CARS '95 Berlin) — interdisciplinary experts imagining futures.

For HCI and Modelling going forward: expand the IFCARS Think Tank to include **social and cognitive sciences** alongside computer scientists and physicians. The **Good** scenario: a **PIXIE III** designed as **inspectable and interactive graph models you can see and poke** — with **Model Science** and a **Model Identity Certificate** for inspectable AI models.

Move from **PIXIE II/ANTz → PIXIE III/XYZ** — perhaps ANTz + Google Maps + a graph manipulator for processes. Human-AI interfaces should preserve human complex-problem-solving and give transparent views of machine reasoning steps.

If Don, Roy, Leo, and Mario agree: Heinz will contact other TT members for a **2.5-day Think Tank** meeting, probably **after CARS 2027**, autumn 2028, **Black Forest**.

## Leo Joskowicz (14 Aug 2026, 15:07)

**Reply-To:** josko@cs.huji.ac.il

Delighted to be part of the Think Tank. Agrees **Good/Bad/Ugly** is an excellent structure — he has a 2000s CAOS presentation with *"how to be very precisely wrong"*, *"a fool with a tool remains a fool"*, *"a tool with a tool remains a tool"*.

**"The bottleneck of AI is now the human"** — our capacity to absorb AI-produced information has not changed. The Bad: AI promotes a **false sense of understanding**. Sees this with undergraduate Data Structures students — they think they know until the closed-book exam with no AI.

Re-read the 1995 paper — remembers it from his first or second CARS conference; liked the conversational presentation format.
