Dear Don,

the bad news is -- the CARS office is closed in August and I have to handle some 150++ IJCARS manuscripts during this month myself (mainly in the mornings) with only little help from the CARS team.

The good news is -- in the afternoon I will be able to look at the contents of your very extensive and insightful email, particularly at the Contents #6: Model Science, PIXIE, and transparency.

By the end of August, I should be able to let you have some suggestions on work items that may form the basis on how we can cooperate for CARS 2027 and possibly beyond. In particular, I am thinking about a joint project of IFCARS with you on the theme somewhat like:

**"HCI and Modelling -- where we come from and may be going to"**

with first presentations and demonstrations at CARS 2027. This is in line with the philosophy with which CARS started in 1985, stating:

> "Ultimately. however, the capabilities and acceptance of MWSs (Medical Work Stations) will depend on MODELLING and MAN-COMPUTER INTERACTION techniques. Both these topics are still very 'basic' research oriented. In the symposium, they are therefore discussed in special workshops."

https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-52247-5

For next year's CARS 2027 in Berlin and future CARS Congresses (2028 Barcelona, 2029 Freiburg, 2030 Kobe), we plan to give the theme HCI and Modelling the attention it deserves and hope you can assist us in this endeavour. If some type of contract could be made between you and IFCARS, some token costs, travelling expenses and so on, could be supported by IFCARS. Also, if some of the results coming out of this cooperation are of interest to the Computer History Museum or ACM, the better.

Best wishes,
Heinz
