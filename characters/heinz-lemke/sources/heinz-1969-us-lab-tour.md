## Context

After Cambridge work on PIXIE, Heinz toured US research labs in **summer 1969** with a **3-month
Greyhound bus ticket for $99**. Intensive discussions — especially with **Doug Engelbart** — helped
the US realize graphics and HCI were not invented only on the American coasts.

> *"The Cambridge Computer Graphics activities were totally ignored in the US at those times.
> Andrew Armit's graphic system on Coon's patches is another example of this."*

Heinz had originally intended to immigrate to the US after visiting the UK in **1965**; received
**several job offers** on this trip, including one from **W.R. Sutherland at MIT**.
