## Summary

Heinz documents PIXIE's development arc for Buxton: October 1967 start (data structures first),
1968–1969 ergonomics pivot, Wiseman collaboration, Wilkes demonstrations, **Electronics** article
(April 1969) → 20+ US lectures, rediscovered PIXIE film. Points Buxton to thesis **Appendix 4**
(PIXIE User Manual) and **Chapter 5** (architecture). Offers **5000+ instruction** assembler
printout. Extended PIXIE (1971, thesis fig 9.4) described as thin/thick clients + server — years
before common networking vocabulary; led to first PACS paper (1979) and current TIMMS surgical work.

Invited Buxton to **CARS 2009 Berlin** keynote on creativity and physician-computer "Window"
metaphor. Met Bill Newman in Cambridge. Buxton spoke at CARS 2009.
