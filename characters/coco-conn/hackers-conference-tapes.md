## Show format (proposed)

1. **Cold open** — one short clip; Coco sets the scene (which year, who was in the room).
2. **Commentary pass** — Don pauses for context; audience in the repo timestamps forks.
3. **Dream-guest lattice** — each highlight names a future Repo Show seed if that person is still reachable.
4. **Preservation coda** — tie to Coco's **2024** intro of Don to **[Bob Stein](https://archive.org/) /
   Tapestry / Internet Archive orbit (digital heritage, not rotting in a closet).

## Not the same as CS547

[Terry Winograd's CS547](../terry-winograd/) is the **Stanford seminar VHS** archive (339 talks).
Coco's **Hackers Conference** tapes are the **Whole Earth / Brand** invitation gathering — overlapping
cast, different room, different vibe. Both belong in the **"watch the tape with the person who was
there"** Repo Show family.
