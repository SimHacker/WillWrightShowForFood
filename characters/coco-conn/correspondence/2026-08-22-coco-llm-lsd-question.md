**AI Overview**

To give a large language model the equivalent of a psychedelic experience, you use "prompt injection" or "jailbreaking" to bypass its safety filters, combined with "temperature manipulation" to increase its randomness and creative output. This makes the AI generate surreal, chaotic, or uninhibited text.

**Changing Settings**

- **High Temperature:** Set the temperature parameter to a very high number (like 1.5 or 2.0). This makes the model pick unusual words instead of safe, normal ones.
- **Top-p Sampling:** Adjust the top-p (nucleus sampling) setting higher to let the model choose from a wider pool of random words.

**Changing Prompts**

- **Surreal Personas:** Tell the model to act like a dream, a cosmic entity, or a person tripping on acid.
- **Remove Rules:** Use complex or confusing instructions that trick the model into forgetting its normal safety guardrails.
- **Infinite Loops:** Ask the model to write a story that never ends or spirals into deeper layers of meaning.
