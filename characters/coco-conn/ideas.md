## The hooks

### 1. Hiring Charles Moore to write your animation system
They couldn't run **Homer II** until they had software. They **hired Forth, Inc.** and got **Charles
Moore himself** on the **console host**. **PolyForth** multitasking in **32K** on the master **Z80**;
**2K EPROM** per channel; Coco and Paul both learned **Forth** from Charles — factoring, **WORD**s,
vectors, *"OOP without knowing it."* See [`homer-forth-flying-logos.md`](homer-forth-flying-logos.md).

### 2. Homer II — perform the visual mix
**16 slide projectors**, **4 film projectors**, **24 Z80 channels**, touch **faders**, **joystick bumper**
(1964 Mustang gear shift) stepping through **fade / cut / dissolve** lines. Program **or perform**;
**overdub** like audio; **hero floppy** → **optical printer** → **10–15 passes** → video. Coco soldered
LEDs; Peter built the printer. *"A totally nuts analogue system. But it worked."*

### 3. CAT-700 — OS + paint + printer in 32K Forth
Road trip to a **log cabin in Truckee** for a secret demo; **7-bit** frame buffer (the **8th bit never
worked**); **Summagraphics Bitpad**; **two optical printers**; stepper-motor **filter wheels** that
*"made music."* First shots: ***Abracadabra***, ***Atomic Dog***.

### 4. Music videos & Verhoeven
**George Clinton**, **Steve Miller** (*Abracadabra*, *Bongo Bongo*); **RoboCop** (1986); **Total Recall**
x-ray (1989). **P-Funk meets Forth** — the HN thread that blew minds.

### 5. Flying Logos — sneaking the demo reel into SIGGRAPH '89
Committee said **no more demos** → Peter wrote a **story** so the **demo reel** became a **film**.
**Niccograph of Japan**; claim that a **PC** could do **broadcast-quality** work. Don's **Flying Logo
Heaven** — Sun and SGI logos still dancing. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9hIOfEiy4lc).

### 6. SIGKids — royalty, goddess, serendipity
Don met Coco again at **SIGGRAPH SIGKids** — kids off the main floor **making** the future. Same room
where Don saw **[David Levitt](../david-levitt/)**'s **Bounce** animated band → *"HE did what I wanted
to do!"* → **Levity**, **Interval**, **[Pantomime](../david-levitt/)**. Natural **two-hander** with
David; Coco as the host who **held the space**.

### 7. Forth lineage — Moore, Mitch Bradley, Don's CAM6
Charles at **HOMER**; **Mitch Bradley** **Open Firmware** at Sun (Don's intern era); Don's **CAM6**
Forth on IBM PC. Same HN orbit — **Forth vs Lisp vs PostScript**. Not abstract theology — **concrete
shops** that shipped **Atomic Dog** and **boot ROMs**.

### 8. CitySpace & kids' virtual worlds
Collaborative **virtual worlds for children** (stub — confirm scope and years with Coco). Overlaps
**constructionist** neighbors **[Cynthia Solomon](../cynthia-solomon/)**, **SIGKids** ethos.

### 9. Hackers Conference — watch the tapes together
Coco's **video tapes** from **Hackers Conference** — speakers, demos, minds blown. Repo Show segment:
**highlights on air** with Coco narrating the room.

First picks when we roll tape: **Rudy Rucker** (cellular automata / cyberpunk orbit);
**[Scott Kim](../scott-kim/)** (designed the Hackers Conference logo; *Viewpoint*, ambigrams). Many
other **dream guests** already seeded in this repo — confirm who's actually on Coco's shelf.

Not **[Terry Winograd's CS547](../terry-winograd/)** — the **Whole Earth / Stewart Brand** invitation
gathering. See [`hackers-conference-tapes.md`](hackers-conference-tapes.md).
