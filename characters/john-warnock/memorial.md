# Remembering John Warnock (1940–2023)

**John Edward Warnock** took Utah graphics seriousness into industry — Xerox PARC, then Adobe
with Chuck Geschke. **PostScript** treated pages as programs; fonts and layout became a medium
you could reason about, not only paint.

Utah classmates and neighbors in the show's Kay line: [Alan Kay](../alan-kay/),
[Ivan Sutherland](../ivan-sutherland/) (citation), [Dave Evans](../dave-evans/).

We cite him; we do not speak as him.
