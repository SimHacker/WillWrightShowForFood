# John Hiles 🖥️ *(PIXIE & RAINBOW co-author, Cambridge)* — status to confirm

*Portrayal from published record only — not John Hiles. **Living-or-passed status
unknown**; not memorialized and not contacted until confirmed.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · authored by Don Hopkins

## Who

**J.O. Hiles** — a member of the **University of Cambridge Mathematical Laboratory**
and the third author on the founding **PIXIE** paper (Wiseman, Lemke, Hiles, 1969),
and again on the **RAINBOW** CAD-system paper (Wiseman, Cheney, Etherton, Hiles,
Lemke, 1972). Beyond that co-authorship, his biography isn't yet confirmed.

## Why he's here

Roy Eagleson named "John Hiles" among the Cambridge people whose storyline he wants
collected. He's a genuine PIXIE co-author, so he gets a room — a placeholder to fill
as **Heinz Lemke** helps identify him and confirm whether he's living or passed.

> **Note:** there is a well-known *American* **John Hiles** associated with
> agent-based simulation (Maxis Business Simulations / Thinking Tools). Given this
> project's Maxis adjacency that coincidence is tempting — but it is **almost
> certainly a different person**, and is logged only as a question to rule out, not
> a claim.
