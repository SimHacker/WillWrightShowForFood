## Don's design-doc position (1998)

From [donhopkins.com/home/TheSims/](https://donhopkins.com/home/TheSims/):

- Slap on same-sex kiss was unacceptable; audience would rightly call it out
- Proposed modeling interest with two 0–100 numbers per Sim
- Retrospect: **Patrick's emergent model is better** — no fixed trait, pure player agency, easier role-play, aligns with Will's "optimize for gameplay"

Sequence Don documents after talking with Patrick:

1. Prototype lacked same-sex support → Don wrote reviews
2. Later docs promised broader romance; Will to recommend implementation
3. Patrick hired; production state may not have reflected reviews yet
4. Patrick implemented anyway — behavior-based, not personality-enum

## Patrick's implementation (press)

- Handed **outdated doc** that still included same-sex unions (team had debated dropping it)
- Tagged romantic social interactions; tracked same- vs opposite-sex over time
- *"Magical moment when my first same-sex Sims couple kissed"*
- Will approved; E3 kiss; EA let it ride

Sources: [New Yorker — Simon Parkin](https://www.newyorker.com/tech/annals-of-technology/the-kiss-that-changed-video-games) · [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-story-of-how-i-the-sims-i-got-same-sex-relationships---in-1999)
