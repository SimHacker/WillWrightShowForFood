# Source: MeetPlayLive LLC — About page

Captured 2026-08-21 from [meetplaylive.com/about.php](http://meetplaylive.com/about.php)
(company's own public bios; page footer © 2026). Sister sites named in the
footer: LiveGLBT.com, Bananax.com.

## Why this source matters here

Three Sims-community principals, one company — the trio stayed together:

- **Patrick J. Barrett III** — President/CEO and founding partner
- **Heather Castillo** — Art Director ([her page](../../heather-castillo/README.md))
- **Steve Alvey** — founding-era partner, Director of Staff Member Services
  ([his page](../../steve-alvey/README.md))

And the headline resonance: the programmer who implemented The Sims'
same-sex romantic interactions went on to found **LiveGLBT.com** — which the
page describes as "the only dating website offering an alternative method of
meeting people which is through games. No other, either for Straight or GLBT
people, offers this service." Meeting through play, as a product thesis,
from the person whose Sims code let preference emerge from what you do.

## Extracts (verbatim from the page)

**Company:** "MeetPlayLive LLC was formed as a Limited Liability Company in
January 2012 by Patrick J. Barrett III, Arnold Rodriguez, and Steven P.
Sereno." Distributes LiveGLBT games on iPhone, iPad, Android, and Xbox ONE.
Based in Modesto, CA.

**Patrick J. Barrett III (President/CEO):** started programming games as a
high-school sophomore; founded Star Gaming Network as a junior, "producing
games for the first home computers and the first generation console
systems." Computer Science at Brookdale Community College, Software
Engineering at Monmouth University. At EA: "He led the Engineering and
Design Teams for many of The Sims' Expansion Packs and 'The Sims Online.'
On 'The Sims 2,' he was the lead designer." After EA, "teamed with Heather
Castillo to produce many mobile and Xbox 360 games under the Star Gaming
Network and Strange Games Studios banners."

**Heather Castillo (Art Director):** Air Force electronics associate's
degree, five years, honorably discharged; Journalism and French at
University of Central Oklahoma; fourth-generation artist. "After the
release of The Sims by Electronic Arts (EA), Heather designed and created
SimFreaks.com... managed a world-wide team of twenty-eight artists...
they grabbed the attention of EA. In turn, they contracted SimFreaks for
expansion packs and other popular titles. **The content from SimFreaks.com
was used by EA to create the prototype for The Sims: Hot Date.**" Also:
"she attended a focus group at PayPal to help develop a system that would
allow her to deliver files to the Online community through subscription and
individual downloads." Later Star Gaming Network work for mobile, Xbox 360,
and PlayStation 4.

**Steve Alvey (Staff Member Services Director):** Clinical Psychology BS,
George Mason; World Bank, then American Red Cross national HQ (supervised a
national plasma-recall system), then Baxter Biotech LA (shipment-discrepancy
system, plasma scanning machine); ESL teacher for Berlitz in Vienna, VA;
built businesses including a rescue-friendly pet boutique in DC and
Arlington. "He is also known for building Simslice.com... maintained a
customer base in every industrialized country in the world. His work has
been featured in news articles around the globe, including the BBC and an
interview by Wired Magazine." At MeetPlayLive: HR director and "liaison to
the Chinese companies that manufacture our Electronics products."

**Others:** Steven P. Sereno (CFO, CPA, Hamilton & Company / FSG Tax);
Arnold Rodriguez (CCO, psychology BA + child-development MA, real-estate
background, club DJ network); Alex Piccininni (sales/marketing, ex-PepsiCo).
