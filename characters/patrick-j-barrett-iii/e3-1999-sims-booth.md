# The Sims at E3 1999 — hidden behind the EA Arena

*Source:* Patrick J. Barrett III, Maxis Alumni —
[post](media/1999-e3-sims-booth-facebook-post.jpg). **His own photograph.**

> "The **E3 1999 booth for The Sims**. **Hidden behind the EA Arena.** This was the closest I could
> get to it, lol."

## What the picture is evidence of

**The Sims shipped in February 2000 and made EA billions. Eight months earlier it was a kiosk behind
the arena.**

Everything in frame is small: a curved kiosk, not a stage. Two CRTs, not a wall of screens. A painted
sky, a picket fence, and some plants — the whole visual identity is *suburban yard*, which is correct
for the game and unhelpful in a hall built for spectacle. The **EA Arena** was the company's big
attraction; The Sims was behind it. Patrick, who **implemented same-sex relationships in this game**
([the record](same-sex-relationships-the-record.md)), reports that as a member of the team **this was
as close as he could get.**

Two things worth holding onto:

1. **It contradicts nothing and confirms everything** about the received story that EA did not know
   what it had. It is not an anecdote — it is a photograph with a sightline. The booth's placement is
   a floor-plan decision someone made, and this is what that decision looked like at eye level.
2. **The crowd.** The kiosk is *mobbed*. Whatever the floor plan said, people were standing three
   deep in front of two monitors showing a woman walking around a red carpet. The audience found it
   behind the arena.

Note also what's on the screens: an ordinary interior, mid-Live-Mode, no explosions. A trade show
where every other booth was selling a spectacle, and this one had **a room**.
