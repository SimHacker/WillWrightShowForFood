# Neil Wiseman — GLANCE 🕯️

🌈 **Neil Wiseman** (1934–1995) — *in memoriam*: **memorial mode**, represent + discuss, never interview.

- Cambridge's quiet founder of interactive graphics: **PDP-7 + Type 340 ↔ Titan** high-speed link — *"probably the world's first distributed system"*
- Grew the **Rainbow Group**; the CAD project that produced **PIXIE** and **RAINBOW**
- Anticipated the pop-up menu (pen-following screen menu), early screen editors, TV camera on the PDP-7
- **Heinz Lemke's supervisor** — took on a university-less research student in 1967; Heinz remembers him on air
- PIXIE 1969 co-author (Wiseman, Lemke, Hiles)
