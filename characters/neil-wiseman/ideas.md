# Neil Wiseman — show hooks & threads

*Memorial mode: prompts for a remembrance **about** him, not an interview of him.*

## Segment hooks

- **"The world's first distributed system."** The PDP-7 ↔ Titan data-link Wiseman
  designed — walk it with [Lars Brinkhoff](../lars-brinkhoff/) as the emulation
  target, and let Heinz describe what it felt like to use.
- **Pen-following menu → pop-up → pie menu.** Wiseman's screen menu as documented
  radial-menu prehistory (see the Buxton "Wiseman Notes on Radial Menus in PIXIE"
  scan). Bridges directly to Don's pie-menu lineage.
- **Rainbow as an *integrated* CAD system.** Electronic design + graphics + data
  structures + change control — the RAINBOW paper (1972) with Cheney, Etherton,
  Hiles, Lemke. A remarkably modern combination.
- **The supervisor.** Heinz's memory of being handed a solo paper to write by the
  end of 1967 — Wiseman's way of making researchers.
