*Invitation portrayal — not Brian Fox.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · authored by Don Hopkins

## Who

**Brian J. Fox** — first employee of the Free Software Foundation (1985); original
author of **GNU Bash** and **GNU readline**; early US interactive online banking
(Wells Fargo, 1995); co-founded Universal Access / Meta-HTML with Henry and Julie
Minsky; open-source elections work; Orchid Labs.

## Why invited

This whole repo's shell is his instrument. Minsky-family startup partner; free-
software ensemble with RMS / Cygnus threads; music-family adjacency via brother
Donal Fox.
