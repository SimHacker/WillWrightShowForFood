## Glenn Reid (reply)

Hey Don,

Cool link that you dug up. That's not the original copy of TouchType, that was after I sold it to Adobe and they rebranded it and started selling it, but close enough.

I couldn't be any less interested in virtual reality, I have to tell you. I am firmly grounded in the real world these days, hacking on washing machines, believe it or not. Appliances. Motors. Solenoids. So much fun!

I'm sure you could whip up 3D TouchType in a 3-day weekend. The only idea in it was that each letter had its own X,Y location (you can add Z :) and you could move them around easily with various direct-manipulation tools. You probably have all those pieces lying around, but just haven't instantiated **"128-point string with 1 glyph"** as one of the objects.

Thanks for touching base, and have fun with it!

Glenn
