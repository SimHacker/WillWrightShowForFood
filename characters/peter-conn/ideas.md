## The hooks

### 1. The printer as the instrument's other half

The desk performed. The printer committed. 10–15 passes to IP film.
Peter at the passes until it was right. What did that feel like in the
room, night after night?

### 2. Flying Logos

Demos banned. Write a salesman. Sneak the reel in. PC/AT, Definicon,
Targa, Digital Arts DGS — hardware the later blurbs skip. Corey Burton
on the phones. Niccograph; Monitor Award. [Video and catalog copy](../paul-rother/sources/1989-flying-logos.md).

### 3. Stanford M2262

31.5 linear feet. Gift 2017. Nothing digitized. *Flying Logos* paper
press release still Box 1.22. What should a researcher pull first?

### 4. Date disagreements (his to settle)

Shop closed 1996 vs 1997. HOMER 1973 / Levi's 1974 / company 1977 vs
Coco's 1978–1981 console build. *Atomic Dog* 1982 vs 1983. Homer I vs II.

### 5. Who else was in the shop

Michael Kory, Jim Hillin, Leo Brodie (Coco named him — unconfirmed).
The engineer who moved to England.
