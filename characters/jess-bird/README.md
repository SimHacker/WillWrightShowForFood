## Who

**Jess Bird** is building a **broadcast documentary** on **The Sims, Will Wright, and Maxis** —
deep archival research, veteran interviews, and community stories told with the patience television
history requires. **Jami Becker** connected her with Don in **2025**; they've been trading sources,
introductions, and long reads ever since.

Jess is also one of the best **real-production connections** in Don's orbit: someone who knows how
a commissioned documentary actually gets assembled, while **Will Wright Show For Food** ships code,
interviews, and repo artifacts in public. The two projects **synergize** — shared credibility, shared
interviewees, shared primary sources — without either pretending to be the other.
