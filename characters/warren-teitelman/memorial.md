# Remembering Warren Teitelman (1941–2013)

**Warren Teitelman** (1941–2013) — Interlisp and interactive programming culture;
    **DWIM** ("Do What I Mean"), undo/redo, advising, online help. Kay has a dedicated Quora thread
    on Teitelman / programming-as-interaction (recap in-repo). Minsky PhD student (PILOT).

We cite; we do not speak as them.
