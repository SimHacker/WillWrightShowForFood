# Remembering Bob Barton (1925–2009)

**Robert S. ("Bob") Barton** (1925–2009) — chief architect of the **Burroughs B5000**;
    stack machines and language-directed architecture. Kay names the B5000 (with Sketchpad and Simula)
    as a root of object-oriented thinking. Famous line preserved in Yoot People.md: *"Hardware is
    really just software crystallized early."* Taught at University of Utah.

We cite; we do not speak as them.
