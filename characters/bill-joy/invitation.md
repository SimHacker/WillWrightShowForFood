# Repo Show invitation — Bill Joy

*Bill Joy may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time. [Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md)*
[Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

We would respectfully invite **Bill Joy** to a **Repo Show** — a Repo Show Class conversation whose stage is [this public repository](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood), following through to working code and credited ideas in public.

## Why Bill

You co-founded **Sun**, shaped **Berkeley Unix** and **vi**, and gave the industry a memorable yardstick:

**S = 2^(Year − 1984) MIPS** — [Joy's law](https://handwiki.org/wiki/Joy%27s_law_(computing)).

Don Hopkins collected highlights from your **February 1991** talk in a Medium piece:

**[Bill Joy's Law: 2^(Year-1984) Million Instructions per Second](https://donhopkins.medium.com/bill-joys-law-2-year-1984-million-instructions-per-second-3a8c92165cfe)**

That article is the front door for this invitation: **C++++-=**, open systems, nomadic devices, and the line Eric Schmidt passed along — you are **"almost always qualitatively right, but the time scale is sometimes wrong."**

We also remember **Educom 1988**: Don demoed NeWS, pie menus, and HyperTIES to a stranger who turned out to be you (beard shaved). You said you **"used to use Emacs on ITS!"** — see [quotes.md](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/bill-joy/quotes.md).

In **2006** you called Don about **pie menus** and information visualization for a serious UI opportunity. You saw **SimFaux**, came to Sand Hill Road, and later cheered on **open-sourcing SimCity for OLPC** — *"perhaps inspire another Will!"* (sourced excerpts in [quotes.md](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/bill-joy/quotes.md); no private mail reproduced here).

## Proposed focus

**Joy's law meets microworlds** — from **2^(year−1984)** to **2026**:

- What the 1991 predictions got right (and what Schmidt's time-scale caveat meant in practice)
- **Open systems** vs closed platforms — then and now (Java/Oak, NeWS, the web, AI tooling)
- **Pie menus**, attention-friendly UI, and simulation games as telescopes (SimCity, Micropolis, The Sims)
- **Venture + constructionist software** — when the interesting UI work lives in small teams again

## What we'd explore together

- The **1991 talk** (C++++-=, 3G machines, designed spaces) vs today's stack
- **Sun logo lore** (Vaughan Pratt; Mark Anderson's 45° rotation) and the culture of "yes by default"
- **Micropolis / OLPC** lineage — open SimCity, Sugar, classroom microworlds
- **Educom '88** and the through-line to Repo Shows (demo culture, live code, audience as participant)
