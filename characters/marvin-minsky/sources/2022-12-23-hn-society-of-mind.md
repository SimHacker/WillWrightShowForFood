**Winston's point (as Don quoted):** AI is not Kirchhoff/Maxwell — no single unifying law; build systems
from **diverse** components (connectionist *and* symbolic), each with its own justification.

**Wikipedia neat/scruffy gloss (Don linked):** neats seek one formal paradigm; scruffies solve many
unrelated problems with many methods. (The wiki's "neats won" claim is contested in-thread — jacquesm
on hindsight — keep as debate fuel, not gospel.)

**Drescher bridge:** schema mechanism from Piaget — constructivist AI that *implements* pieces of the
developmental story Minsky/Papert cared about. Living discussant: [`../../gary-drescher/`](../../gary-drescher/).
