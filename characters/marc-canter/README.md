# Marc Canter 🎬🕺 (an esteemed guest — and old friend)

*Invitation portrayal — **NOT** Marc Canter, and not claims about his views. Old friend of Don + David
Levitt; consent not formally asked (Don expects a joyful yes).*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

**Who:** **Marc Canter** — co-founder of **MacroMind** (1984), the company behind **VideoWorks →
Director** that became **Macromedia**; a founding figure of desktop **multimedia authoring**. Later a
loud, early champion of the **open social web** with **Broadband Mechanics** and **People Aggregator**
(an open social-networking platform / "digital lifestyle aggregator").

**Why him, why now:** two reasons. First, he's the **Macromedia/Director** node the show keeps touching —
the multimedia runtime that **Bounce** and the **Macromedia Player (MMP)** thread grew out of — and the
open-social-web node (People Aggregator). Second, and just as important: **he's a born performer.** Marc
is unabashed about **singing and dancing in public** and being the center of attention (he has an
**animated reggae dancing cartoon character of himself**), so he will **love the puppets** — and we'd
love to hand him some.

**Old friends in the room:** Don, **David Levitt**, and Marc go way back — friends and sometime
colleagues (including **People Aggregator**, gently ribbed as "**People Aggrivator**"), with a trove of
outrageous stories, many from **Amsterdam**, where Marc loves to visit.
