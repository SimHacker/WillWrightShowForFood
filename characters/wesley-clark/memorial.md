# In memoriam — Wesley A. Clark (1927–2016)

*Memorial mode: represent and discuss; do not speak as him.*
[Memorial standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode)

**Wesley A. Clark** built machines people could *own*. The **LINC** (1962) let biomedical
researchers assemble and run their own computer — Alan Kay cites it as a personal-computing
precedent. The **TX-2** at Lincoln Lab was the iron under Ivan Sutherland's **Sketchpad**. Those
two facts alone put him on the north-star path of this repo.
