# Tim Niblett — memorial

**5 June 2022** · aged 66 · cancer  
Announced to the Turing orphans by **Peter Mowforth**, 13 June 2022.

This room does not speak as Tim. It holds what colleagues said, and what the work still shows.

## Who he was (to the Institute)

Tim cofounded Glasgow's **Turing Institute** with **Donald Michie** and **Peter Mowforth**. After a clash with another organisation already using "MIRA," Tim suggested the name that stuck: **The Turing Institute**.

Pete's notice said what the room should keep: Tim's contributions in original thinking, project success, and research direction were numerous — and he **rarely took credit** for innovations that were his. He kept his illness quiet; until the end he was thinking of meeting friends and colleagues again.

Funeral: noon, Wednesday 29 June 2022, South Oxfordshire Crematorium near Wantage. Family: father Bryan, sister Mary, stepmother Daisy. Contact then: Mary Blackmore.

Photos Pete gathered: [album](https://photos.app.goo.gl/iz1NTruLKgiGBfhR9) · [after the service](https://photos.app.goo.gl/1LuWbmRHaPoF3RJp7)

## Work that still names him

- **GoodNeWS** user guide (Tim Niblett, Sept 1989) — NeWS window environment at Turing.
- **HyperNeWS** slides and system — with **Arthur van Hoff** (and Claude Summer in the founding story): HyperCard-like UI in PostScript, client links to C and Prolog.
- **PdB** — C-like language → PostScript (with debugging hooks and optimizer); Leigh Klotz later used it on a Xerox "paper web browser" project.
- **WiT** — structured editor under HyperNeWS; SGML I/O via Tolk.
- HyperNeWS 1.4 README — Jim Rudolf, Arthur van Hoff, **Tim Niblett**, Danny Pearce.

**Arthur van Hoff:** mentor, friend, Scrabble opponent; MIRU and Turing; ski trips; "I learned so much from him."

**Peter Clark (Allen AI):** first met Tim in the pub for his TI job interview — brilliant, scruffy, full of humor, kind; speed chess at 15 minutes vs Tim's 2.

**Ross King:** huge respect; enthusiastic seminar on Prolog meta-interpreters.

**Danny & Pauline Pearce:** ski trips; intellectual genius at work; great mentor on the journeyman programme.

**Doug Scoular:** kind when Doug was starting out; Christmas Trivial Pursuit — new rules needed against that intellect.

**Jonathan Shapiro:** kind man, quick wit; ski trips; squash court.

**Phil McKell:** big brain and big heart; never heard anything negative from him.

**Paul Siebert:** wine and music; hillwalking and cycling; "the whole Turing experience with Tim binds the time and memories of us all."

**Leigh Klotz (to Don):** great help with PdB on the crazy paper-based browser; "Tim was patient with my requests."

**Don:** forwarded Pete's mail as "Sad NeWS" to John Gilmore and Leigh — the pun Tim would have groaned at and deserved.

## HyperLook hunt (2019)

When Don and Arthur looked for HyperLook source, Tim said he didn't think he ever had it — but he still had SCSI DAT tapes from TI2. He found two Fujifilm DG-90M DDS cartridges (December 1994, labelled `/project` and `/usr/people`), posted them to Arthur for John Gilmore's working DAT drive. Low hope, high character: still trying to help twenty-five years later.
