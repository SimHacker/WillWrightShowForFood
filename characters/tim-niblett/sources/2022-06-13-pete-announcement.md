# 2022-06-13 — Pete Mowforth announces Tim's death

**From:** Peter Mowforth to Turing Institute orphans list  
**Subject:** Tim Niblett

## Facts Pete stated

- Died of cancer, Sunday **5 June 2022**, aged **66**.
- Father Bryan, sister Mary, stepmother Daisy saw him shortly before and said goodbye.
- Funeral: noon, Wednesday **29 June 2022**, South Oxfordshire Crematorium near Wantage, Oxfordshire.
- Contact for attending: Mary Blackmore (then phone/email in original mail — not recopied here).
- Pete planned a Facebook memorial account and to close Tim's LinkedIn.

## Pete's characterisation (keep this)

Tim's contributions in original thinking, project success, and overall direction of research at The Turing Institute were numerous. Although Tim was often responsible for many of the key ideas that shaped the Institute's work, he **rarely took credit** for many of the innovations and ideas that were his.

He went out of his way not to let others know how ill he was; right up to passing he was thinking of meeting family, friends, and colleagues again once medication had things under control.
