*Al may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time — or take direct edit access. [Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md) — also covers quiet mode and onboarding.*
**Subject:** A bar reader, tested on your Turing Institute tape

---

Al —

You told me in 2023 that `bar` came with SunOS 4. I finally wrote a reader for it, in TypeScript, so
it runs anywhere including a web page, and ran it over the Turing Institute dump you sent. It reads
all 1,130 entries, decompresses `compress(1)` files, and found the one block the dump had lost, in
the middle of a HyperDraw drawing; the copy on your other tape confirmed the rest was intact. Code
and notes on the format:
[`@wwsff/sunos-bar`](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/packages/sunos-bar/README.md).
Use it, or tell me what's wrong with it.

I've also been building on your scans. The 340 Display Programming Manual, the PDP-7 documents and
the MACRO-9 manual from bitsavers are what let me write a PDP-7 and Type 340 emulator that runs in a
web page, and restore Heinz Lemke's 1969 PIXIE from its printed listing:
[the cabinet](https://hyperties.org/cabinet/) ·
[how it was built](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/packages/cabinet/README.md).
Thank you for that.

**Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the [premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md) and more.**

If you'd enjoy it, I'd like to invite you to a **Repo Show**, a recorded conversation whose stage is a
public GitHub repository, on whatever you'd like to talk about. Possible threads:

1. How bitsavers started, and what's still missing.
2. Reading old tapes: drives, formats, and what fails.
3. What a museum would want from an emulator that runs in a browser.
4. The Software Preservation Group and the museum's software collection.

Or none of these. No preparation, and you see anything before it's published.

— Don
