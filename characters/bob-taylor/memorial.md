# In memoriam — Bob Taylor (1932–2017)

*Memorial mode: represent and discuss; do not speak as him.*
[Memorial standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode)

**Robert W. Taylor** funded and protected people. At ARPA he helped start the network that became
the Internet; at Xerox PARC CSL he hired talent (including the Berkeley cohort) and stayed out of
the way — Alan Kay credits that freedom for the Alto and Smalltalk. Later DEC SRC continued the
same ethos. Draper Prize 2004 with Kay, Lampson, and Thacker.
