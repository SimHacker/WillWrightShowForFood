## The 1997 debate, and this week

At the IUI 97 debate between Ben Shneiderman and Pattie Maes, Pattie pointed to Henry in the
audience and cited his teach-by-demonstration work, while Ben asked from the stage for graphical
macro tools. This repo argues that programming by demonstration was the synthesis both were
describing, though no one named it in the debate: [`AGENTS-DEBATE-1997.md`](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/designs/AGENTS-DEBATE-1997.md). In October 2026 Henry and
Pattie were at the same event, where she showed her Jan 97 "Agent UI solutions" slide again:
[`../pattie-maes/agent-ui-solutions.md`](../pattie-maes/agent-ui-solutions.md).

## NPUC

1993 First Annual NPUC (IBM Almaden), opening talk: *Smart Interfaces to Smart Machines*.
Same program as Don Hopkins (VISEX), Ken Kahn (*Sketches and Animations as Programs*),
Don Norman, Terry Winograd. [Archived program](https://web.archive.org/web/20060210092923/http://www.almaden.ibm.com/almaden/npuc97/1993.htm).

## Why invited

This repo traces a line from *Tinker* ("show the machine what you mean") through programming by
demonstration to pie menus and MOOLLM skills. The NPUC reunion draws on Ted Selker's seven years
of recordings, and Henry gave the opening talk at the first one.
