# Heather Castillo 🧟

As a 3D artist and game designer best known as the founder of **SimFreaks**, Heather Castillo’s creative practice bridges the pioneering days of behavioral game modding with modern real-time 3D pipelines. For over 26 years, Heather built custom content, object suites, and collaborated on total expansion overhauls for *The Sims* original. From crafting sprite assets for classic engines to building stylized low-poly environments in Maya & Blender, her work continues to center on a passion to create immersive environmental storytelling and pushing simulation engines past their limits.

## The Journey & Creative Path

Roots in classical art run deep for this **fourth-generation artist**, who spent her formative years drawing and painting directly in her grandmother's art studio. Before entering digital art and game design, five years were spent serving as a Navigational Aids Electronics Specialist split between the U.S. Air Force (USAF) and the Oklahoma Air National Guard (OKANG)—a background that instilled a deep appreciation for technical systems and complex logic.

In 2000, SimFreaks was established, quickly expanding into one of the largest and most respected custom-content houses in the Sims community. At its peak, the site managed a collaborative global network of up to 28 contributors. During this era, Heather modeled the original 3D environmental sprite assets utilized by Patrick J. Barrett III to pitch the initial concept for *The Sims: Hot Date*, directly leading to its official development at Maxis. This period also included participating in an early PayPal focus group tasked with conceptualizing creator-economy infrastructure for per-item content delivery.

Transitioning to Owner and Creative Director of Star Gaming Network (SGNGames.com), Heather partnered with Patrick to shape game design, user experience, and asset optimization across a diverse catalog of mobile, Xbox, and educational games. These industry ventures later included a collaboration with Strange Games Studios to develop the indie title *Zombie Turkey Outbreak*, along with mobile releases like the *Pirate Monkey Series* and various digital card and slot games.

## Reconnecting & Collaborating with Steve

The professional paths of Heather and her partner **Steve Alvey (SimSlice)** first crossed in the early 2000s, collaborating from opposite coasts alongside Don Hopkins and SimBabes on a shared project. Years later, around 2011, a casual inquiry regarding contact info for SimBabes led to an unexpected reconnection. Steve mistakenly assumed he was texting a former employee and they spent hours playing catch up on their life journeys. Steve realized the next day that he may have overshared with someone he had a more professional relationship with in the past, but the humorous interaction sparked a year of non-stop daily calls and texts.

Steve relocated to the West Coast in late 2012 to live closer to Heather. The two eventually married in 2019, right before the pandemic hit. Going into the 2020 lockdowns as newlyweds, they channeled their isolation into a massive, year-long collaborative development marathon to create **ZombieSims**—an extensive overhaul pack that transforms *The Sims* engine into a strategic, object-driven apocalypse survival sandbox.

## Recent Work & Gallium Studios

From 2022 to 2024, Heather joined Will Wright and Lauren Elliott at **Gallium Studios** as an Artist in Residence and Technical Mentor for their AI life-sim title, *Proxi*. Responsibilities included creating stylized low-poly environment assets and props in Maya, texturing cel-shaded models, and mentoring fellow asset artists on clean UV mapping, topology, and asset optimization for Unity.

Today, Heather and Steve continue to build and preserve high-concept simulation projects, combining her distinct art direction with his signature game-within-a-game programming logic.
