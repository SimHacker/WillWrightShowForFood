# Ideas to explore with Caroleen Beatty 🎤

*Conversation hooks for a Repo Show — **Don's proposed topics**, grounded in the public record of
the album and her own quoted account on saucefaucet.com. Not quotes, not claims about what she
thinks.* [Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · invitation guest · consent not_yet_asked

## The hooks

### 1. "Let's do the whole album!"
The whole-album cover was her idea, mid-session, recording two Eno covers. What made *Taking
Tiger Mountain* the record worth a year of nights in a practice space?

### 2. Singing Eno warm
Eno's 1974 vocals are cool, composed, machine-adjacent. Carrying those melodies with a rock
band's warmth — phrasing choices, what she kept of Eno's delivery, what she threw away.

### 3. The handclaps and the rules
Doug's no-keyboards constraint shaped everything. How constraints read from the singer's side.

### 4. Eno's phone call
He called it "beautiful music" from his "cold experiments" and wrote the liner notes. What that
blessing felt like from inside a project made "just for our friends."

### 5. ENORCHESTRA — the transformation, live
Performing the whole album on stage; what changed again in the third medium (studio original →
hand-made cover → live band).

### 6. Bay Area scenius
Bedlam Rovers, Waycross, the Mekons orbit with Jon Langford — the scene that makes
for-love-not-money projects normal.
