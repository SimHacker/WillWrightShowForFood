🎤🐅 **Caroleen Beatty** — the voice of Doug Hilsinger's 2004 track-for-track cover of **Brian Eno's *Taking Tiger Mountain (By Strategy)*** — and the one whose idea it was ("Let's do the whole album!"). Bedlam Rovers frontwoman, Waycross singer, Jon Langford collaborator, ENORCHESTRA performer. Invited as a guest in her own right and as a warm path to Doug.
