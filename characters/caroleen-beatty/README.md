# Caroleen Beatty 🎤🐅

Invitation portrayal — **not** Caroleen Beatty. [Standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

**Field:** San Francisco Bay Area singer. The voice of **Doug Hilsinger's** 2004 track-for-track
cover of **Brian Eno's *Taking Tiger Mountain (By Strategy)*** (DBK Works #111) — vocals and
handclaps on every song. The whole-album idea was hers: during a covers session she said
*"I love this record… Let's do the whole album!"* Eno heard it within weeks of completion and
called it *"beautiful music"* made from his *"cold experiments."*

Scene veteran: fronted **The Bedlam Rovers**, sang in **Waycross** with Doug, collaborated with
**Jon Langford** (Mekons), and performed the Tiger Mountain album live as the **ENORCHESTRA**.

## Why invite Caroleen

- The cover exists because of her — the idea, the voice, the handclaps.
- The show's central theme — **honoring a work by transforming it** — sung rather than argued.
- Reunion dream: Caroleen + Doug + Brian + Will + Scott on covers, constraints, and love.
- Warm path: she and Doug are longtime collaborators, and Doug's site is offline — she may be
  the friendliest bridge to him.
