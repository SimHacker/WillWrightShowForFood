Caroleen —

In 2006 a bartender at The Eagle put on a record without telling me it was his, and I fell for it
before I knew — and the first thing I fell for was **your voice**, carrying songs I knew by heart
somewhere warmer than Eno ever took them. Doug gave me the CD that night, and I've been telling
the story for twenty years.

I'm producing a **Repo Show** — recorded conversations staged in a public GitHub repo — called
**Will Wright Show For Food**.
**Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the [premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md) and more.**
The show's central theme is **honoring a work by transforming it**, and your *Taking Tiger
Mountain* is its living proof — a record Eno himself blessed as *"beautiful music"* made from his
*"cold experiments."* By Doug's own telling, the whole-album madness was **your idea**.

I'd love your voice in the episode — the story from your side, the singing choices, the
ENORCHESTRA, the Bay Area scene that makes for-love projects normal. Solo, or with Doug, or the
full reunion dream: you, Doug, Brian, Will, and Scott Draves. And a favor, only if it's easy:
Doug's site is offline and I'm not sure my messages reach him — if you're in touch, I'd be
grateful for a nudge in his direction.

**Zero pressure, zero homework.** A phone call, an email, an audio recording at your own pace, a
video chat — every level of participation counts, and you approve anything with your name on it
before it ships.
