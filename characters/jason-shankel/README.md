## Who

**Jason Shankel** — **Maxis** engineer (**SimCity**, **The Sims**, **Spore**); **first programmer on Spore**
([CNET](https://www.cnet.com/culture/behind-the-prototyping-of-spore/)). Built **ParticleMan** (gravitational
particle sim for Spore's space phase) and published [Spore prototypes](http://www.jasonshankel.net/wiki/index.php?title=Spore_Prototypes)
for download.

**Stupid Fun Club** and **Syntertainment** alumnus (Will Wright orbit). Spoke at **AWE 2013** on Syntertainment
**Connected Play** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ztSvzKyVV-4)).

**Teacher:** recurring guest at **Sonoma State CS Colloquium** (public bios: since **1997**) — **17+ talks on
YouTube**, spanning game dev, generative storytelling, quantified self, AR psychology, VR, monads, and gaming life.
Full index: [`sources/TALKS-INDEX.md`](sources/TALKS-INDEX.md).
