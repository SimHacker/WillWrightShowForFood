## Maxis (1994–2008/2009)

- Programmer on **SimCity**, **The Sims**, **Spore** ([MobyGames ref in sims-series roster](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/will-wright/media/sims-series-maxis-people.yml))
- **First programmer on the Spore team** (CNET)
- Prior project: **SimMars** — Mars terraforming simulation, NASA-supported before cancellation; planetary climate tech reused in early Spore work (CNET, Jason quote in article)

## ParticleMan

Internal Maxis tool by **Jason Shankel** — gravitational particle-cloud simulation (orbits, nebulae, pulsars) used in Spore's space phase. Built on GLUT / GLUI (Wikipedia *Development of Spore*).

## Prototyping culture
