# Ideas to explore with Eric Hedman 👤

*Conversation hooks for a Repo Show — **Don's proposed topics**, each grounded in Eric's
public work and documented connections to this repository. Things Don would love to follow
**with** Eric Hedman; not quotes, not claims about what they think.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · invitation guest · consent asked (invited Aug 2026)

## What Eric has done

Eric Hedman ("Irk") — animator, artist, and game designer. Character animator on the original The
Sims; design lead on expansion packs (House Party, Livin' Large, Hot Date, Unleashed, and more).
Pantomime Corporation with David Levitt and Don; later Disney, Henson, Nickelodeon; now indie
creator ([@irkinteraction](https://www.youtube.com/@irkinteraction), **Aether HeXXen**).

## The hooks

[`repo-shows/eric-hedman/`](../../repo-shows/eric-hedman/README.md) — planted, several episodes'

### 3. Vitaboy & hand-tuned animation
Walk cycles, idles, gestures; broad pantomime under frame-budget constraints; the **canWiggle**
legend.

### 4. The object-prototyping pipeline
Simplified Max stand-ins → artist → animator → programmer → same-day sandbox iteration. The template
that made expansion packs possible.

### 5. Expansion design stories
Mechanical bull ("my baby"), love bed, hot tub — designing big "magic" party objects with state
machines that read on a CRT.

### 6. Drew Carey Show tie-in
Animating Sims vignettes + laser blaster; Marion Ross dance-floor story. Pairs with dream
[`drew-carey`](../../repo-shows/drew-carey/README.md) episode.

### 7. ★ Blendshapes — Doppel, and the practitioner's half of the ECG thread
His **[Doppel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6L7jtgRD5rs)** demo puts a monkey face on the **full
ARKit morph-target set** and performs it live with the applied weights on screen — an ARKit answer to
Animoji, published by IMVU.

**This is the hook Don did not have before.** The repo already has an ECG/Mouther show seeded with
[Tom Ngo](../tom-ngo/README.md) (who invented Embedded Constraint Graphics) and
[Golan Levin](../golan-levin/README.md) (who built Mouther on it) —
[`repo-shows/tom-and-golan-ecg-mouther/`](../../repo-shows/tom-and-golan-ecg-mouther/). Eric is the
**third seat: the working professional who has actually shipped this pipeline.** Inventor,
artist-researcher, practitioner.

And it is the demo [Faceball Construction Set](../../designs/faceball-construction-set/README.md#what-it-looks-like-in-the-hands-of-a-great-artist)
is designed around — Faceball's argument is *seeds, not showpieces*, and Doppel is what the ceiling
looks like when someone who knows the whole pipeline performs the rig himself.

Questions worth asking: how many of the 52 ARKit shapes actually earn their keep? Where does the
blendshape basis fight you? What does a hand-built viseme set get you that a solver does not — which
is the same question [Mouther](../golan-levin/README.md) answered in 1995 from the other direction.

Eric + David Levitt + Don — Sims animation craft meets AR/VR character work.

### 9. Indie creator spotlight
**Aether HeXXen** visual novel; hand-made 2D/3D pipeline; Patreon/Ko-fi support.
