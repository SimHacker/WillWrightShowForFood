# Arcade Seal Clubber 2: Barky's Revenge — Eric Hedman, 22 Jan 2002

*Source:* Eric "Irk" Hedman, posted to a
[Maxis Alumni thread, 6 Feb 2015](media/2015-02-06-jim-mackraz-shtick-thread.png), with the caption
**"Barky's Revenge!!!!!"**

![Concept sheet titled Arcade Seal Clubber 2: Barky's Revenge, dated 01/22/2002. Top left, a pencil sketch of an arcade cabinet. Panels show a cartoon seal smoking a cigar against a blue sky, a figure in a yellow parka on the ice above a white seal, a grinning seal wearing a seaweed crown seated on a yellow throne against a rainbow background, and a cabinet-art panel with a seal above an audience of red silhouettes. Left column, four small pixel-art gameplay screens with scores. Signed Eric Hedman 99-03. A note reads: use coinslots from whack-a-will for the front of the cabinet](media/arcade-seal-clubber-2-barkys-revenge-2002.png)

**A design sheet for a Sims arcade machine that was never built** — the sequel to an existing one, in
which the seal is the protagonist.

## What's on the sheet

- **Title and date:** *Arcade Seal Clubber 2: Barky's Revenge. 01/22/2002*
- **Cabinet sketch**, top left — the object's 3D form
- **Cabinet art panels:** a seal smoking a cigar; a hunter in a yellow parka standing over a seal on
  the ice; a seal enthroned in seaweed against a rainbow; and a marquee panel with a seal above an
  audience of red silhouettes
- **Four pixel-art screens** — the game-within-the-game, with a lives-and-score HUD (`3 · 452`,
  `3 · 453`, `0000 3-2`), a yellow-clad figure, a thrown club in flight, and a white seal
- **Signed:** *Eric Hedman '99–'03*
- **Production note, arrowed to the cabinet front:** *"use coinslots from **whack-a-will** for the
  front of the cabinet"*

## Why it's worth keeping

**It documents an object that exists only as art direction**, and it documents it the way Sims
objects actually got made: cabinet form, marquee art, *and the fake game's own pixel screens*, all on
one page. Every arcade machine in The Sims needed a whole second game invented for its screen. This
sheet is that job, visible.

The joke has a structure worth naming. *Seal Clubber* — the premise is already a dare — and then
**the sequel inverts the moral position of the player**: *Barky's Revenge*, seal on a throne, hunter
now the target, crowd of red silhouettes watching. Eric drew the redemption arc for a seal-clubbing
arcade game as a Sims decoration. It is the same instinct as the
[NPC Satan wedding guests](../don-hopkins/sources/2019-05-12-weird-sims-objects-20th-anniversary/)
and the [Spinning Arrow Of Shame](../will-wright/sources/2021-05-31-brain-ui-beta-screenshots/README.md):
Maxis art staff using shipped-object slots as a venue.

**"use coinslots from whack-a-will"** is the load-bearing detail. It names a **prior Sims arcade
object called *Whack-a-Will*** — a whack-a-mole cabinet aimed at Will Wright — and treats it as an
asset library to cannibalize. Neither *Whack-a-Will* nor *Seal Clubber* appears anywhere else in this
repo yet. **Both need to be tracked down**: shipped object, cut object, or in-house joke build.

**Dating:** the sheet is stamped 01/22/2002 and signed '99–'03, so it lands in the Sims 1
expansion-pack years — *Vacation* (Mar 2002), *Unleashed* (Sep 2002), *Superstar* (2003) — exactly
when new lots meant new arcade cabinets.
