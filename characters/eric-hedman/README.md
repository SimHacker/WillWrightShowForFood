# Eric Hedman ("Irk") 🎞️

Invitation portrayal — **not** Eric Hedman. [Standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

**Field:** Animator, artist & game designer — character animation on the **original The Sims**;
design lead on Sims expansion packs (House Party, Livin' Large, Unleashed); **Pantomime Corporation**
(w/ David Levitt & Don Hopkins); later **Disney, Jim Henson, Nickelodeon**; now an **independent creator** making visual novels, games, and VR/AR/MR.

## Watch / support his work

Eric is an independent artist — go subscribe and follow:

- ▶️ **YouTube: [@irkinteraction](https://www.youtube.com/@irkinteraction)** — warm, funny, detailed Sims-development stories (vitaboy animation, the expansion packs, the Drew Carey Show tie-in, and more). Don is a happy regular in the comments — the conversation that led to this invitation started there.
- 🐵 **[Doppel — Character Modelling with Blendshapes for Animation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6L7jtgRD5rs)** — a monkey face on the full ARKit morph-target set, performed live by Eric with the applied weights on screen; an ARKit answer to Animoji, published by IMVU. Deservedly the centrepiece demo of [Faceball Construction Set](../../designs/faceball-construction-set/README.md#what-it-looks-like-in-the-hands-of-a-great-artist), which argues that a facial rig only gets funny when someone who knows the whole pipeline performs it.
- 🎨 **Portfolio: [erichedman.artstation.com](https://erichedman.artstation.com/projects/8wJDgw)** — "Maker of Stuff." Game dev, effects, animation, character modeling, object UI, 3D printing and real-world fab, and a modified tarot deck.
- 🎮 **Aether HeXXen** (his 'space witch' visual novel): [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4033750/) · [itch.io](https://irkinteraction.itch.io/aether-hexxen-death-space-requiem-act-1)
