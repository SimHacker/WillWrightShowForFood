## Dave Crocker (author)

> Worth a reminder, every now and again.

On the viral "Mother of All Demos" / Engelbart article:

> The article does not note that the tone of his team was profoundly collaborative. A version of
> **"Come on in, the water's fine."**

**Personal NLS path:**

- Visited brother at **ARPA**; got access to NLS; played with **text commands** (not mouse-based).
- ARC monitored access → invitation to a **class in Menlo Park** on the mouse-and-keypad system.
- They said if he could tolerate the text interface, he'd probably do OK on the "full" system.

**On interfaces (reply to Dirk van Nouhuys):**

> Given the joys of learning the sequential alphabet encoding of the keypad, I'm not sure I agree
> [that mouse+screen was easier than text], in terms of just the surface user interface. The more
> interesting challenge, for both, was learning the underlying data and association models.

> Having used 'classic' text formatting tools, I was fascinated by the **'paragraph' oriented model
> of NLS**. And even used emulating it as a programming exercise for learning **PL/1**, using it to
> create a **Counselor's Manual** for the student crisis hotline I ran.
