# Viral Engelbart "$10,000" piece — straw man the Facebook thread answered

Circulating July 2026 social posts (exact origin URL varies by share). Dave Crocker's Facebook
thread ([`facebook-engelbart-collaborative-tone-2026.md`](facebook-engelbart-collaborative-tone-2026.md))
opens against this genre of write-up.

## The piece, in brief

A 43-year-old engineer (1968) demoed mouse, hyperlinks, video call, collaborative editing, GUI
ideas in ~90 minutes — "Mother of All Demos." Funding dried up; team hired to PARC; Jobs/Gates
got rich; Engelbart's mouse royalty **$10,000** once; patent expired before mass market; spent
decades reminding industry computers should help solve hard problems, not sell ads; died 2013.
Closer: "He got $10,000. Everyone else got the internet."

## Why this room keeps it

Useful as the **feature-list + bitter-royalty** frame Bret Victor and living witnesses push back on:

| Viral frame | Counter-thread in this repo |
|-------------|-----------------------------|
| Laundry list of inventions | Bret: *what world was he trying to create?* ([worrydream.com/Engelbart](https://worrydream.com/Engelbart/)) |
| Lone genius robbed | Dave Crocker: ARC tone **"Come on in, the water's fine"**; PI helped a stranger in the Hilton ballroom |
| Bitterness as the story | Mary Hodder (same FB thread): Doug curious/gentle 2005–2013, not bitter |
| Mouse as the money | Dave Maynard: keyset patent already 1860s telegraph; *"keyset in left hand whenever mouse in right"* |

**Show use:** open with the viral paragraph, then let Crocker / Maynard / Ted / Bret displace it —
not to deny the royalty fact, but to refuse it as the *point* of Doug.
