## Thesis (KK)

Latent space is extreme compression of culture into a continuous, multi-dimensional map of the real *and* the possible. Style transfer, counterfactuals, and “prospect in the gaps” are navigation in that map — not just Q&A.

Compression beat echoes Don/Will/Eno: genotype→phenotype, 64K demos, GDC algorithmic compression, Long Now generative systems.

## Epistemic caveats (keep)

**Tom Bombadil (Substack):** “correctness / truth” in latent space tracks **truthiness** (corpus consensus density), not correspondence. Medieval-medicine model → “healing” points at bloodletting with full confidence.

**HN (chroma_zone, invictati, luisln):** “all human knowledge” / “oracle” overclaim; Shakespeare is overrepresented; truth≠direction without grounding in reality.

**HN (accrual):** “words are the thinking” challenged by Anthropic global-workspace / J-space work — models can hold content without emitting text.

**HN (ux266478):** Latent/embedding space is only a piece of the pie; unembedding matters; continuous computation between functions is broader than LLMs (web-browser-for-computer fallacy).
