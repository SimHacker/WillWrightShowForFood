my mind: the compiler lies for speed, then un-lies on demand so you debug the program you
actually wrote. Simplicity AND performance AND honesty. JavaScript borrowed the speed and
forgot the simplicity; I'd love to say that on the record with the person who built the
speed sitting next to the person who designed the simplicity.
