## The invitation

Hi Kees!

I've been building something you'd like: a show-and-tell format called
Repo Shows — Will and I and old friends going through source code,
prototypes, and stories together, on the record, in a git repo that
doubles as the show's memory. Warm, unhurried, and everything gets
archived properly instead of evaporating into a feed.

You belong in at least three of them, and you get to pick your poison:

**The Spore reunion.** You, Will, Chaim — the prototyping era. The
creature editor when it was three engineers and a genetic algorithm
learning to walk. I want to hear about evolving nervous systems in IL,
the L-system body plans, and how the any-number-of-legs walk that Will
demoed at GDC 2005 actually came together. Chaim's been on about the
editor lineage; you're the missing engineer of record.

**Language design.** I've been deep in a design conversation growing
out of David Ungar's Self and his newer Korz — subjective,
context-dependent slots, no privileged receiver. And every time I
explain it I keep thinking: Kees has been *living* this. Two languages
of your own, and IL is "based on intensional logic" — evaluation
relative to context, as a daily art practice since Electrogig. I'd
love a show where language designers compare the languages they had to
build to say what they meant.

**Music.** Bohlen–Pierce by continued fractions, Sonology, Koenig,
real-time synthesis in PDP-15 assembly in 1977 — we have music shows
brewing (Jerry Martin, tuning theory, instrument-building feedback
loops) and nobody else on the roster has a scale-adjacent unit named
after them.

Format is whatever suits you: a video call, a solo clip from your
studio, or just email threads that become show notes. No deadlines, no
production pressure — the repo waits patiently.

-Don
