# OOP trinity — Sketchpad · Simula · B5000

Alan Kay's public history (Yoot People.md, Early History of Smalltalk, Quora) repeatedly names
**three** 1960s roots of object-oriented thinking — not one:

| Root | Person | Room | Idea Kay takes |
|------|--------|------|----------------|
| **Sketchpad** (1963) | Ivan Sutherland | [`../ivan-sutherland/`](../ivan-sutherland/) | Master/instance drawings; constraints; "objects" before the word |
| **Simula** (1966–67) | Ole-Johan Dahl + Kristen Nygaard | [`../ole-johan-dahl/`](../ole-johan-dahl/) · [`../kristen-nygaard/`](../kristen-nygaard/) | Classes and objects as language |
| **B5000** | Bob Barton | [`../bob-barton/`](../bob-barton/) | Hardware as "software crystallized early"; stack / descriptor machines |

FLEX (Kay + [`../ed-cheadle/`](../ed-cheadle/)) sits on the Utah side of this story; Smalltalk + Alto
(Lampson/Thacker/Ingalls/Goldberg) are the PARC synthesis.
