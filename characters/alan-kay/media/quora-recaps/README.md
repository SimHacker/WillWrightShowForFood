# Alan Kay — Quora recaps (reformatted for readability) 💬

*Guest hub:* [`../../README.md`](../../README.md) · *Media hub:* [`../README.md`](../README.md)

Alan Kay writes long, generous answers and follow-up discussions on **Quora** — the *content*
is excellent, but Quora's threaded UI is **atrocious to read**: you have to click to expand every
nested comment, replies collapse, ordering scrambles, and long exchanges are nearly impossible to
follow end-to-end.

So Don captures the best of them and **reformats them into readable Markdown here** — flat,
top-to-bottom, quotable, linkable — with the original Quora URLs preserved.

## Why these live here (and what they're for)

These recaps aren't just an archive. They're **discussion fodder**: a clean, readable recap is the
artifact we bring *back* to Alan and ask "what do you think now?" — run through the
**post-order-traversal review protocol** described in
[`../../../david-rosenthal/slots-all-the-way-down.md`](../../../david-rosenthal/slots-all-the-way-down.md)
(§ *Messaging over objects — Alan Kay, the dream guest*):

1. Reformat the Quora thread into a readable recap here.
2. Resolve the leaves (specific claims/quotes) before the summary (children-first).
3. Show Alan the composed recap; ask what he thinks.
4. Fold his reply back in, same order; iterate to a fixed point.
