## Graduation timing (1969)

Kay and **John Warnock** both received PhDs end of **summer 1969** graduation — *"right about that
time."* Kay → **Palo Alto / Stanford** for post-doc year. Warnock worked at **E&S** for a while.

## Utah near-miss with Heinz

Heinz visited University of Utah **12–14 September 1969** ([Heinz email 12 Jul](../heinz-lemke/sources/2026-07-10-pixie-storyline-thread.md)). Kay had already left after PhD.

Andrew Armit was the Utah visitor Kay remembered — months on PDP-7 Coons patches.

## London today

> *"I live in London — and have health problems — so can only travel for short distances. But can
> take short train rides."*

Roy's Royal Society lecture question: Kay did lecture at Royal Society (October — Roy's date); this
email doesn't say London move was *because* of Wilkes, only that they became friendly.

## Wilkes ↔ Xerox PARC

> *"Wilkes and I became friendly (he was a special hero of mine), and there was quite a connection
> between his legacy and Xerox PARC (mainly through Chuck Thacker, etc.)."*

**Show hook:** Cambridge (Wilkes, PIXIE) ↔ PARC bridge episode — Thacker line, not just Utah/Sutherland.
