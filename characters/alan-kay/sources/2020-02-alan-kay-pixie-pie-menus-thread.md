## Engelbart 1962 conclusions (Alan Kay sketch, Feb 2020)

Doug Engelbart's early proposal — Alan's sketch attached in mail:

1. Look **more down** on a display than across (human acuity)  
2. Point/mark **down** not across; light pen on **"floater scaffold"** — grab when needed, out of the way when released  

Don linked to Golod et al. hand-gesture microinteractions on kitchen table + PIXIE 1969 film.

## Alan Kay on pie menus (Feb 2020)

> This is like the "halo of handles" scheme we came up with for Etoys in the late 90s.

> Dan Ingalls came up with what he called "pie menus" (I think for his "Fabrik" system in the 80s) but using strokes in directions… These all seem to be **independent inventions**.

> William Newman was very good at coming up with efficient display picking schemes… when the Alto started working in 1973.

On PIXIE 1969 film (PDP-7, light pen): surprised they used light pen vs RAND tablet — fatigue, button twitch.
