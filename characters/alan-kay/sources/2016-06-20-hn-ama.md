# HN — Alan Kay AMA (20 Jun 2016)

Thread: [Alan Kay has agreed to do an AMA today](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=11939851). 1401 points. Kay posted through the day. Dang closed it: one of the best threads HN has seen, north of 250 of Kay's replies.

**Not a dump of the thread.** Other people's comments stay on HN. This page is the map into rooms that already exist, plus the two comments Don made on it. Kay's lines below are short quotations from that public thread, marked, so a later invite can find them without re-reading 900 comments.

Don's OLPC reply is long enough to have its own receipt:
[`../../don-hopkins/sources/2016-06-20-hn-kay-ama-olpc-sugar.md`](../../don-hopkins/sources/2016-06-20-hn-kay-ama-olpc-sugar.md).

## Don on the thread

He asked what succeeded and failed at OLPC, and what Sugar got right, after icarito asked Kay whether Sugar was the right interface for the Children's Machine. Kay did not answer either comment. Don's answer to his own question is the Sugar/SimCity retrospective in the receipt above: Stone Soup, the GPL, Mary Lou Jepsen's display, Mitch Bradley's Open Firmware, Hippo vs Cairo, and why a thin X11 wrapper was the honest integration.

## Answers that already have rooms

| He said | Where it lives |
|---------|----------------|
| "I love Bret Victor's work." HARC principal investigators include Bret. YC Research set HARC up; it is not "my group." | [`../../bret-victor/`](../../bret-victor/) |
| Engelbart's group wanted human augmentation, not only education. The web was a weak gesture next to Engelbart. Intel and Motorola ignored PARC's high-level-language hardware, and the 1980s got 1950s programming back. | [`../../douglas-engelbart/`](../../douglas-engelbart/) |
| "What if data is a really bad idea?" Objects are ambassadors: for a serious negotiation you send a person, not a telegram. Meaning needs a process. TCP/IP is the small universal thing that lets everything else happen. Lincos is the analogy for sending meaning to a stranger. | Same argument as the live-objects trail: [`../../../process/trails/live-objects.md`](../../../process/trails/live-objects.md) |
| The Rich Hickey exchange. Hickey: data is facts, immutable, older than programming. Kay: how do they even know the bits are numbers? | Clojure's idea of data, argued in public. No Hickey room. |
| Smalltalk-78 image resurrected for Ted Nelson's 70th. "I liked Self. Good OOP is still waiting for a much better notion to replace Class." Simula, especially Nygaard, cannot be praised too highly. Inheritance was left out of the first Smalltalk because Simula's was not semantic enough. | [`../../ted-nelson/`](../../ted-nelson/) · [`../../david-ungar/`](../../david-ungar/) · [`../../dan-ingalls/`](../../dan-ingalls/) |
| Dan Ingalls bootstrapped new Smalltalks out of old ones and made the system safe to debug itself. Chuck Thacker is the hardware half of "art of the possible." PIE (Goldstein and Bobrow) is a favorite meta-system. Alex Warth's Worlds paper goes past PIE. | [`../../dan-ingalls/`](../../dan-ingalls/) |
| "I'm not a big Unix fan — it was too late on the scene for the level of ideas that it had." A tiny kernel and processes-as-objects decayed into dependencies. Parsing non-command messages in each process never grew a real language around the messages. The same thing happened to HTTP. | [`../../james-gosling/`](../../james-gosling/) is the NeWS side of that argument, not Unix's |
| Jerry Bruner's MACOS, *Man: A Course of Study*, for fifth graders. "Real anthropology." Bruner had died a few weeks before the AMA, at 100. | No Bruner room. The curriculum is the education hook in [`../ideas.md`](../ideas.md) |
| Marvin is not in the AMA as a topic. Don's OLPC comment and the Convolvotron thread are the Ames-simulator pairing. | [`../../marvin-minsky/`](../../marvin-minsky/) |
| "Television is the last technology we should be allowed to invent without a Surgeon General's warning on it." Children need to learn how to use the 21st century or they will lose it. Neil Postman, *Amusing Ourselves to Death*. McLuhan, Innis, Ong. | Media-ecology hook, not a new room |
| Pop culture reinvents the flat tire. "Better and Perfect are the enemies of What-Is-Actually-Needed." The ARPA/PARC funders funded people, not projects. Bob Taylor cannot be praised too highly. Sam Altman is named as a rare funder in that line. | [`../../process/hn-harvest/alan-kay.md`](../../../process/hn-harvest/alan-kay.md) |
| Seymour Cray: three weeks at Chippewa Falls before Kay realized Cray was not the janitor. The official CDC software team produced nothing for the 6600. Cray's own multitasking OS and graphical debugger were written in absolute octal. | No Cray room |
| Steve Jobs: "pretty friendly" until Jobs's death, intertwined through Apple and Pixar, and through Kay trying to get education back onto Apple's goals. The iPad has the Dynabook's size and not its services. "I wish they'd allow people to program the darn thing." | Hardware form vs service idea |
| "Object oriented to me has always been about encapsulation, sending messages, and late-binding." Java: "You tell me." Erlang is fun and too absolute to be "the only real OOP." | The definition this repo already uses |
| Most ideas are mediocre down to bad, including his. He writes them in notebooks to get rid of them. Einstein, in the Princeton tea joke: "I don't know, I've only had two." | |
| dang, closing: mine the thread; every Kay encounter produces a list of references. Kay gave a Chinese editor at *Programmer* permission to translate the answers and make them clearer. | |
