## Wes Clark lineage (reply to Heinz/Roy on PDP-7 vs PDP-8)

IIRC:

- **PDP-8** — **12-bit** machine; highly successful commercial follow-on to Wes Clark's classic **1962 LINC** (Alan's vote for earliest real "personal computer").
- **DEC-tapes** — originally invented by Wes for the LINC (had to be foolproof; used for years).
- **PDP-12** — additionally LINC-compatible (Alan believes).
- Wes most famous for main design of **TX-2** (Sketchpad machine) and inviting **Ivan Sutherland** to have it for several hours a day for the year Sketchpad took.
- *(Actually* Wes might be most famous for proposing the **IMP** — first router — that made the ARPAnet practical.)

**Corrects Heinz's same-day memory** that the PDP-8 was 16-bit; Heinz had already noted PDP-9 (18-bit) as the compatible PDP-7 extension.
