## Conversation hooks

1. **Repo Show vs YouTube comments** — his essays end where the fight starts; a repo continues with
   sources, issues, and PRs instead of drive-by toxicity.
2. **Retrofit Unnecessary Analysis** — pick one video (Sims or Stardew); build a mini Repo Show
   around it: transcript, sources, discussion threads, "what should happen next" issues.
3. **1996 "Oh, God…" / $5-billion question with Will** — student asks what Will's working on →
   data portability → Dollhouse. (EA later said The Sims franchise passed **$5B** lifetime sales,
   Oct 2019 — hence the nickname.) Blake as co-host / reactor.
   Link: https://youtu.be/nsxoZXaYJSk?t=4099
4. **Meet Ell / Tiny Life** — introduce Blake to [Ell (Ellpeck)](../ellpeck/), solo queer author of
   [Tiny Life](https://tinylifegame.com/). Don's bet: Blake will *love* it. Natural Unnecessary
   Analysis subject + bridge partner.
5. **Soul City ⇔ Tiny Life bridge** — stack with Ell (+ Don): soul-files as the liminal layover;
   Tiny Life export already almost a soul-file; Sims characters evacuate EA property into a
   project of love. Spec:
   [`game-bridge-sims1-tinylife.md`](../../repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/game-bridge-sims1-tinylife.md)
   · Ell's [bridge doc](../ellpeck/bridge/soul-city-bridge.md).
6. **"War Crimes of the Tiny Life Community"** — Unnecessary Analysis episode / Repo Show.
   **Thesis (Don):** the real criminals are the Gamergate / incel / MAGA trolls and detractors
   who harass a queer indie life-sim and its community — not the Tinies. Blake has watched some
   of that unfold on Twitter; Ell has lived it. Frame solidarity, not both-sides.
7. **Creator Network exodus stack** — optional later with Plumbella / lilsimsie / James Turner.
8. **Steve Bannon / Warcraft rabbit hole** — Blake's essay
   [The War Crimes of the World of Warcraft Community](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OGV5uPmFzrE)
   ([Bannon beat ~40:04](https://youtu.be/OGV5uPmFzrE?t=2404)): gold-farming business → "before
   Steve Bannon became the monster he is today." Don already offered: *"I have other pointed
   comments on your World of Warcraft video about Steve Bannon, so if you want to know more,
   you may pull my finger."* 👆 — dish only if Blake wants that beat on record / off-record /
   green-room.
9. **MDMAyonnaise** — icebreaker from Don's Stardew comment, not a segment.
10. **The record, corrected** — the [same-sex relationships record](../patrick-j-barrett-iii/same-sex-relationships-the-record.md)
    is a case study in exactly what Blake's essays do: a widely-repeated story (New Yorker →
    Game Developer → Kotaku → a decade of retellings) that the primary sources contradict.
    Not a gotcha — Patrick's account of his own work was accurate; he'd been hired into the
    middle of a process whose paper trail he hadn't seen. Good material on how games history
    gets written and how it gets fixed.
11. **Procedural rhetoric without the jargon** — [Bogost's frame](../ian-bogost/roles-not-characters.md):
    the kiss-slap rule was never written in any design doc, so the *code* made an argument no
    human on the team endorsed. Blake reads game systems as arguments for a living; this gives
    him the academic handle if he wants it.
12. **Recording policy, decided together** — first call off the record. Then choose: audio-only,
    screen-share walkthrough, written Q&A committed to the repo, or a green-room doc that
    becomes public when he says so. The repo can hold any of those, and consent is per-artifact.
13. **The economics question** — Don asked in July and still has no answer: YouTube monetization
    vs Patreon vs GitHub Sponsors for funding open-source bridges (paying Ell for a Tiny Life
    bridge, Galium for a Proxi bridge). Blake lives in this economy; Don doesn't.
