## Who

**David Gerrold** sold *"The Trouble with Tribbles"* to Star Trek in 1967 **as a college student**,
and it became arguably the most beloved episode the franchise ever aired. That would be a career;
for Gerrold it was a debut. Over 50 books and a dozen-plus TV episodes followed: *Star Trek
Animated* ("More Tribbles, More Troubles," "Bem"), *Babylon 5* ("Believers"), *Land of the Lost*,
*Twilight Zone*, *Tales from the Darkside*.

Two of his books matter enormously to this show:

- **When HARLIE Was One (1972)** — an artificial intelligence argues for its own personhood,
  navigates corporate boards that want to unplug it, and (along the way) delivers one of the
  earliest fictional descriptions of a **computer virus**. Hugo and Nebula nominated. Every
  question this repo's [portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) tries to
  answer about simulated beings, HARLIE asked first — in 1972.
- **The Martian Child (1994)** — the autobiographical story of adopting his son Sean. Won **both
  the Hugo and the Nebula**, became the 2007 John Cusack film, and remains one of SF's great
  acts of turning private love into public art — the same move this show's family-album,
  memorial, and portrayal ethics try to honor.

Also in the canon: *The Man Who Folded Himself* (the time-travel-identity classic), the *War
Against the Chtorr* septology, the *Star Wolf* trilogy, and *"Blood and Fire"* — his 1987 AIDS
allegory written for TNG, shelved by the studio, and finally **directed by Gerrold himself** for
the fan-made *Star Trek: New Voyages*. He has been an out gay pioneer in American science fiction
for half a century, and he still publishes essays and fiction near-daily on
[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/DavidGerrold), where Don is a follower.

## Why this show wants him

| Thread | The conversation |
|--------|------------------|
| **HARLIE, 1972 → LLMs, 2026** | Machine personhood was his novel's whole plot. What did he get right? What did we all get wrong? |
| **Tribbles vs. soul hydraulics** | Tribbles are born pregnant — a conservation-law catastrophe played for laughs. Our [soul bridges](../../repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/game-bridge-soul-hydraulics.yml) enforce strict conservation. The panel writes itself. |

| **The Martian Child** | Turning family into art, ethically — the exact needle this show's portrayal and memorial standards try to thread. |
| **SF authors wing** | With [David Marusek](../david-marusek/) — the writers who predicted the digital-souls terrain the repo now builds on. |
