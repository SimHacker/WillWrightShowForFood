# Repo Show invitation — Tommaso Toffoli

*Tom may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time — or take direct edit access. [Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md) — also covers quiet mode and onboarding.*
**Subject:** Your CAM-6, still running, and a conversation about reversible computing

---

Tom —

I'm Don Hopkins. I worked on pie menus with Ben Shneiderman at Maryland, on NeWS at Sun, and on
SimCity and The Sims with Will Wright, and I later released SimCity as free software,
[Micropolis](https://micropolisweb.com/). Your and Norman's *Cellular Automata Machines* is one of
my favorite books, and I've been working from it for a long time.

At the MIT AI Lab I got to hold Norman's CAM-6, and he lent a friend and me one for a weekend at a
science fiction convention, where people watched it for hours. Later I wrote a CAM6 simulator,
first in C with Mitch Bradley's Forth compiling the rules from your book into lookup tables, and
then in JavaScript, which runs in a web page:

- [The CAM6 simulator, live](https://donhopkins.com/home/CAM6/) ·
  [source](https://github.com/SimHacker/CAM6)
- [A demo I recorded for Norman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LyLMHxRNuck), going through the
  rules from the book

It runs Critters, the dendrite rule from §15.7, the gas rules, Tron, the spin-glass rule from
§17.3, Life, Brian's Brain and others, with the same lookup tables your Forth produced. Norman has
given me permission to turn the book into interactive courseware, a chapter at a time, so that
someone can read a section and run and modify its rules on the same page. I'm planning that with
him ([his page](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/characters/norman-margolus/README.md)).

I've also been building a PDP-7 and Type 340 emulator for the browser that runs Forth, early UNIX
and the 1969 PIXIE graphics system, along with devices no PDP-7 had: a Turing machine engine
running Minsky's universal machine and Christopher Cherniak's busy beavers
([live](https://hyperties.org/cabinet/turing/)). A cellular automata machine is the obvious next
device to give it.

**Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the [premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md) and more.**

I'd like to invite you to a **Repo Show**: a recorded conversation whose stage is a public GitHub
repository, where what we talk about can be built and run afterward. Norman's show covers playing
with the CAM6 and designing the courseware. Yours could cover the parts of the story that are
yours:

1. The Toffoli gate and reversible logic, and how they got from Endicott House in 1981 to quantum
   computing.
2. Conservative logic and the billiard-ball computer with Edward Fredkin, and remembering him.
3. Lattice gases versus cellular automata, "the whole story at last."
4. The CAM machines from Squareland to the CAM-8, and why you programmed them in Forth.
5. Action as a measure of computation, and "Entropy? Honest!"

Norman could join if you'd both like that. Remote is fine, or in person in Boston. No preparation
needed.

— Don Hopkins
