# Tommaso Toffoli — conversation hooks

*Public hooks only. Not Tommaso Toffoli.*

## Reversible computing

- The Toffoli gate (1980): three bits, reversible and universal. How it went from a technical memo
  at MIT to a basic gate of quantum computing.
- Conservative logic with Edward Fredkin (1982): computing with billiard balls and mirrors, and
  what "losing no information" buys physically. Fredkin died in 2023; a chance to remember him.
- Endicott House, 1981: the Physics of Computation conference, Feynman's talk, and who was in the
  room.

## Machines

- From Squareland (1982) through CAM-2 to CAM-5, the CAM-6 board (1985-86) and the CAM-8 (1992):
  what each one made possible.
- Why Forth: the CAM-6 rules were Forth words compiled to lookup tables. Don's simulator still
  produces the same tables.
- Running the book: Critters and the billiard-ball computer running backward, the dendrite rule
  (§15.7), the spin-glass rule (§17.3), live in Don's
  [CAM6 simulator](https://donhopkins.com/home/CAM6/).
- A cellular automata machine as a device for Don's PDP-7 cabinet, next to its Turing engine.

## Physics

- "Lattice gases vs cellular automata: the whole story at last" (Automata 2008).
- "Action, or the fungibility of computation" (1998): action as a count of computation.
- "Entropy? Honest!" (2016).
- simp/step with Ted Bach, and programmable matter.
