## Why you

I've been archiving the old email, and you keep showing up in the best parts:

- **The LGATE receipt chain** — five documents from 1988 to 1990 tracking your NeWS war-game
  front ends at Los Alamos, from the SUG Southwest program to David Rosenthal reporting that the
  Joint Chiefs were driving Cray simulators from your control panel, to Hugh Daniel's Usenix
  booth manifest ("LGATE-SDI, MPM (Tanks & AirPlains)").
- **The recruitment coda** — my April 1990 email, the week Gosling set up your Sun interview:
  "Please talk to him and tell him about the **peaceful things** he could be doing at Sun!!"
  It worked. As I keep telling Hacker News: Sun was lucky to steal you away from LANL to work
  on NeWS instead of WWIII.
- **The interpreter** — you rewrote the NeWS PostScript interpreter from Gosling's original design.
- **owm** — our Open Window Manager: X11 and NeWS windows integrated under **tabbed frames**,
  **pie menus**, a **scrolling desktop**, **multiple rooms**, all in object-oriented NeWS
  PostScript. I still have source:
  [owm.ps.txt](https://donhopkins.com/home/archive/NeWS/owm.ps.txt) ·
  [design notes](https://donhopkins.com/home/archive/NeWS/i39l.txt) ·
  [tab.ps](https://donhopkins.com/home/archive/NeWS/win/tab.ps) —
  and I've posted about it on HN a lot.
- **The ICCCM death match** — the whole 1991 TNT window-management fight is preserved in the
  archive, including your 3 Sep 1991 email quoting Chuck Price and Rosenthal's 1987 white paper.

## What we'd explore (your pick)

- **The NeWS reunion** — with Gosling, Densmore, Rosenthal, van Hoff: "send a program, not a data
  structure," and what it was like to rewrite the interpreter under everything
- **owm, resurrected** — walk the PostScript together; tabbed frames wrapping X11 windows; maybe
  get it running again (the robots are surprisingly good at old code these days)
- **From WWIII to window systems** — LGATE, MPM, the Pentagon demo, and the "peaceful things"
  recruitment; what NeWS could do that nothing else could
- **General Magic** — Magic Cap Core Technology with Atkinson and Hertzfeld; what Telescript and
  NeWS had in common (I claim: everything that matters)
- **Or just war stories** — the ICCCM fight, the death of NeWS, what survived
