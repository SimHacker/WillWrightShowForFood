# Summer 2026 — memorial rehearsal and messages

Digest of the July–August 2026 exchanges between Don Hopkins and Craig Latta, kept as
context for the memorial show. Light on the personal chatter — the point is the timeline.

## 2026-07-20 — The memorial rehearsal (and the prestoration)

Don and Craig recorded a rehearsal screencast for the Vanessa tribute: reading her SqueakJS
paper (DLS '14, Most Notable Paper 2024) together and talking through it. The rehearsal
exposed the problem that every public copy of the paper still carried her deadname — which
became the founding case of the
[prestoration project](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/designs/prestoration):
a [memorial edition](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/designs/prestoration/sources/Freudenberg-2014-SqueakJS-memorial-edition.pdf)
with her correct name, the original preserved bit-for-bit beside it, every change hashed and
disclosed — and the discovery of her own 2021 Hacker News comment asking not to be deadnamed
in citations. The session was later lifted into the reusable
[change-name skill](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/change-name) via
[cursor-mirror](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/cursor-mirror).
