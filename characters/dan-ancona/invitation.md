# Repo Show invitation — Dan Ancona

*[Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md) — edit, decline, delay, or request removal anytime.*

Dan —

**You almost certainly started this.**

On **26 June 2006**, at the Long Now *Playing with Time* evening (Brian Eno + Will Wright, Herbst
Theatre), Stewart Brand read your written question from the pile — open-source **SimCity** so people
could see and change the **political assumptions in the rules**. Will answered on stage that he was
**very open to the idea**. Brian also loved the idea. I was in the room, mind blown. I was *not* the asker and didn't plant the question (I joked about it outside after the talk and in later email with Will, which he saw through immediately). It was your question and another about Spore that got Will on record and the ball rolling.

About five months later the OLPC thread cleared EA to release the original SimCity source as
**GPLv3 Micropolis** (2008). We're porting it to the web
([MicropolisCore](https://github.com/SimHacker/MicropolisCore)) and building **Will Wright Show For
Food** as a Repo Show whose audience questions work like your Long Now card: **attributed, permanent,
on the record**.

I spent the last few days on a slightly embarrassing archaeology binge — transcripts, Stewart's
pile, UCSB ADEPT papers, democracy-dashboard breadcrumbs — and landed on **you**. When the pieces
clicked, the honest reaction in the room was: *no fucking way, PERFECT.*

**Facts:** **Will Wright is in** — he's signed on to do the
[premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md)
and more. Your 2006 question is one of the load-bearing beats in our documented lineage from that
night to runnable open-source Micropolis today.

**The ask (pick any temperature):**

1. **Reaction clip** — watch the
[clip](https://youtu.be/Dfc-DQorohc?t=3929) with us (remote or in person) and tell us what you
remember: did you know that moment helped set Micropolis in motion?
2. **Mark Live** — Stewart also read a related card from **"Mark Live"** about Spore's political
assumptions and generative politics (Will called the fascism-sim idea *brilliant*).
"Mark Live" is a phonetic translation of a name Stewart wasn't sure how to pronounce, so it may be spelled differently. We have not found Mark publicly. Was that a mispronunciation on the recording? A nickname? Someone you coordinated with that night to tag-team Will and Brian on the open source issue (I could only dream)? Even *"nope, no idea"* helps — it proves we did the homework.
3. **Face to face (optional)** — you're in **Oakland**; Will's not far. If you're up for it and
schedules align, a coffee-shop or cool-scene conversation — your question, Will's answer, Brian's
reaction, what happened after — could be extraordinary on camera. **Whether Will joins in person is
entirely his call**; we're not promising a celebrity drop-in, only that the show exists and he knows
the saga.

Your democracy-tools and visualization work and our rules-as-politics obsession are the same
picture at different zoom levels. We'd love your brain on **generative politics in simulations**
— then and now.
