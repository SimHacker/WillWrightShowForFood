## Who

**Dan Ancona** — political organizer and technologist in **Oakland, CA**. Builds and evangelizes
online tools that strengthen democracy ([Democracy Dashboard](https://www.crunchbase.com/person/dan-ancona);
[Charting the Way Forward](https://chartingthewayforward.substack.com/) on Substack). Deep voter
research for coalition ads; visualization and strategic communications background.

He is also — we are fairly certain — the person who **asked Will Wright to open-source SimCity**
on **26 June 2006** at the Long Now [*Playing with Time*](https://longnow.org/talks/02006-eno-wright/)
talk with Brian Eno. Stewart Brand read Dan's written question from the pile; Will answered on stage
that he was **very open to the idea**. Don Hopkins was in the audience.

That question helped set in motion **GPLv3 Micropolis** (2008) and today's
[MicropolisCore](https://github.com/SimHacker/MicropolisCore) — documented in the
[SimCity open-source saga](../will-wright/sources/simcity-open-source-saga/README.md).
