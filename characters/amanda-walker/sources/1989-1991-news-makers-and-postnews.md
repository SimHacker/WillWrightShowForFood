## Signature beats (her words, paraphrased for the digest)

**NeWS UI factoring (6 Jan 1990)** — the show-stopper argument: NeWS separates UI code from
clients so look-and-feel is decided at *invocation*, not compile/link. She rewrote liteUI
PostScript on a 3/50 to look like a Mac (custom color icons per window) without touching a
byte of client code; same binaries looked like vanilla liteUI on coworkers' machines.
*"This is the gem behind all of Sun's marketing hype."* X does not permit that flexibility.

**Sun shooting NeWS in the posterior (2 Mar 1990)** — blunt: Sun marketed NeWS as academic/OEM
only while X ate the world; *"Who are the most visible proponents of NeWS? Don Hopkins and
SGI. Sun is starting to be seen as an obstacle to the success of their own product!"*
Suggested putting NeWS in the public domain. Signed *Disgusted*, with the Obi-Wan epigraph.

**Non-proprietary NeWS (13 Mar 1990)** — even high Sun commitment isn't enough if NeWS stays
proprietary; C-script isn't the only PostScript interpreter; a good Lisp programmer could add
the datatypes and LWPs; *"of similar scale to stuff GNU is sponsoring."*

**Adobe "but that's not how we planned to use it!" (16 Feb 1989)** — Display PostScript vs NeWS:
Adobe's cleanliness/extensibility means PostScript *can* be a window system whether Adobe
likes it or not. Same pathology as AppleTalk-for-TCP/IP.

**PostNews (Mar–Apr 1990)** — don't Solve Multimedia Mail; ship something: compress + btoa
PostScript over 7-bit ASCII; Type-3 font / Distillery nits; Times Small Caps font she posted;
*"like good whiskey, it should get better the second time through"*; reading is only half —
creating messages must stay as easy as ASCII or PS you already have.

**Pinned menus / Here Menu! (Dec 1989)** — Mac-style floating pinned menus; window-manager
depth; X11/NeWS vs vanilla X.

**forall bind tip (Aug 1989)** — teaching NeWS PostScript on the makers list (reply to Don).

**Toy withdrawal (Nov 1989)** — email to Don: miss wandering one room down to a Sun 4 with a
big color screen; InterCon nifty but odd; OSU paid little but the toys were fun; how to find
HCIL at UMCP without forced entry.

**Pizza Tool + Galaxy (18 Jun 1991)** — *"most excellent pizza tool"*; NeWS into a commercial
product; left-coast stint brewing; *"I've already got one. It's very nice…"* (Monty Python)
when Don asked if she was looking for a job.
