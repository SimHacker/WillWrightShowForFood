# Amanda Walker 🌌

*Invitation guest — consent not yet asked. [Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md).*

**Old friend. Speaker-To-PostScript.** Before Galaxy, she was the InterCon NeWS junkie on
`NeWS-makers` who wrote the pitch Don still quotes: NeWS factors UI into the *server*, so
you can remake liteUI into a Mac desktop without recompiling a single client. She roasted
Sun marketing for shooting NeWS in the posterior, named **Don and SGI** as its most visible
proponents, and co-ran the **PostNews** experiment — ship PostScript over compress+btoa
instead of waiting for the perfect multimedia mail standard.

**Visix / Galaxy.** By September 1990 her address was `amanda@visix.com`. In June 1991 she
congratulated Don on **Pizza Tool** and said she was putting NeWS into a "(shh…) real live
commercial product" — Galaxy, the cross-platform framework with a NeWS-like PostScript
interpreter at its core.

**Now:** Sr. Director, Engineering — Privacy, Safety and Security at Google.

**Jul 2026:** found a spiral-bound **Self** manual in a 90s office-move box. Urs and Ungar
answered in public. Same person, same taste for the real object model.
