## The hooks

### 1. Charles Moore as a deskmate

They hired Forth, Inc. and got the inventor. Paul programmed the 2K EPROM
in each visual channel; Moore programmed the PolyForth host. "I learned
Object Oriented Programming without knowing it" — factoring WORDs, vectors.
What was Moore like in the room? What did school never teach?

### 2. Homer II as an instrument

16 slides, 4 movie projectors, 24 Z80 channels, Allison-ish touch faders,
joystick bumper like a 1964 Mustang shifter. Perform the crossfade, record,
overdub, hero take to an 8" floppy, then 10–15 optical-printer passes to IP
film. Live visual mixing before anyone called it a DAW.

### 3. 32K that held everything

CAT-700 7-bit frame buffer (the 8th bit never worked). Optical-printer
control + paint system + OS + compiler + debugger in 32K. Summagraphics
Bitpad. Two printers: digitize vs. record. Filter wheels on steppers —
"it made music." First uses: *Abracadabra*, *Atomic Dog*.

### 4. Truckee, Van Gogh traces, Lisa

Recycler find → Reno → log cabin in Truckee, sellers wouldn't show the
controlling program → Palo Alto to meet the French designer of the curvy
hand-routed board → night at a Stanford friend's working on a secret Apple
project called Lisa.

### 5. Flying Logos / SIGGRAPH '89

Peter wrote a story to sneak the demo reel into the Electronic Theater
after demos were banned. Niccograph of Japan. PC as broadcast-quality
production. Don's Flying Logo Heaven: Sun and SGI logos still dancing.
[Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9hIOfEiy4lc).

### 6. What survived

Code, EPROMs, schematics, hero floppies, the Mustang stick, the analog
slider box from the Millimeter Magazine shoot. Paul offered a writeup.
Stanford has the [Peter Conn papers](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/c8n303pn/entire_text/).

### 7. Forth thread across shops

Paul + Moore at Sunset Gower. Don + [Mitch Bradley](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=21822840)
at Sun. [CAM-6 Forth](../don-hopkins/cam6-cellular-automata-machine.md).
Same glass-box religion, different metal.
