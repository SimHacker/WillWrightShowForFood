# Flying Logos — Homer & Associates, 1989

Watch: [youtube.com/watch?v=9hIOfEiy4lc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9hIOfEiy4lc)
(Peter Conn's channel, uploaded 30 Sep 2006). Live 2026-08-26.

## Why it exists

The year before SIGGRAPH '89, the Electronic Theater committee stopped taking
straight demo reels. **Coco Conn** (public, 2021): Peter wrote *Flying Logos*
to sneak Homer's reel in by turning it into a story. It worked; it was a show
favorite.

The story: a client calls **Flying Logos, Inc.** to have a logo visualized.
The house salesman walks the catalog of 1980s CG clichés. The client then
orders the entire cosmos. The salesman says they can't do that — then they
do it anyway.

Peter's own description (YouTube, and still on
[milesconsulting.org/service.html](https://www.milesconsulting.org/service.html),
live 2026-08-26): voiceover soundtrack written by Peter; animations assembled
from productions of the previous year; juxtaposition of the technical versus
the anticipated. Claim: debut of the **PC as a fully capable production
system** for broadcast-quality work that until then lived on expensive
workstations.

Awards named in those same texts: **Niccograph of Japan**; Stanford finding
aid adds **Monitor Award for Best Showreel**.

Credits (Peter's site): computer animation **Peter Conn, Michael Kory, Jim
Hillin**. Voice (YouTube comments, later logo-wiki consensus): **Corey
Burton**. Moviefone lists Burton as narrator, Conn as director, Homer &
Associates as production company, theatrical date 20 Jun 1989.

**Don Hopkins** (same 2021 thread):

> I truly believe that in some other alternate dimension, there is a Flying
> Logo Heaven where the souls of dead flying logos go, where they dramatically
> promenade and swoop and spin around each other in pomp and pageantry to
> bombastic theme music. It would make a great screen saver, at least!
> Somewhere the Sun Logo and the SGI Logo are still dancing together.

## What the pictures are

People still come for the **DiC** "extracted from a star" ident (the "kid in
bed" closing logo). Peter on YouTube: Homer did a DiC logo circa 1989; he
did the logo itself, not the show. 007 animation ~1987, placement forgotten.
World Access Television closed before their animation was even complete.

## Transcript

Proofread 2026-08-26 from YouTube auto-captions (`en.vtt`) against the
soundtrack. This is **not** a studio script. ASR errors corrected in
brackets where the spoken word is obvious; remaining guesses marked.

Speakers: **House** = Flying Logos salesman (Corey Burton). **Client** =
the caller.

**House** [0:04]: Flying Logos here, may I help you?

**Client:** Uh, yes — do you do chrome?

**House:** Do we do chrome, sir? Every piece and polish to a brilliant
luster. But may I interest you in a plastic chrome?

**Client:** Well I don't know…

**House:** We do bump mapping, texture mapping, reflection mapping, road
mapping, you name it mapping. You a real-time heart with floor texture,
multiple wood grains with inlaid reflected mirror finish, crystalline glass
with a tinge of blue—

**Client:** Huh, you think so?

**House:** I got it. Marble and glass is your cup of tea.

**Client:** Well I'm not sure about that. Could it be marble and chrome, or
is that plastic and chrome? Uh, what else you got?

**House:** Hot fiery chrome. Okay. Pulsating red throbbing balls moving in
unison before your very eyes, of course with chrome. Maybe you're a
dive-through-the-hole kind of guy.

**Client:** I am.

**House:** How about a write-on, a type-off, a shoot-on, pulled out of an
envelope, extracted from a star. You can blister it, translate it, tumble
it, explode it away, lift it, or do your basic split. We do exteriors and
we do interiors too.

**Client:** Well you know I was thinking — could we go like this: pull back
until we see that it's like a whole Earth, and once we get out there it
like drops away and it's just one of hundreds of screens out in space, and
there's these beams and they start going around turning on all the screens,
and then there's the real Earth, and we see that there's a satellite, we go
right down the beam of the satellite, there's this big metallic dish
sitting in a sea of diamonds, it's all gold and silver, and it unwraps
magically into my logo. Could we do that?

**House:** Uh… um… I'm sorry. I don't think we can do that.

[music; they do it anyway]
