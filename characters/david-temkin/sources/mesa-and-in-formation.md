## Mesa — spatial canvas shared with AI

**Essay / demo:** https://davidtemkin.com (LinkedIn: "A spatial canvas that you and your AI share" — `lnkd.in/gAvFxNB5`)

Thesis: the future can't be "everything is a chat window." Four months building **Mesa** — zoomable spatial canvas; files/media visible at every scale; AI agent sees the same surface, uses it as context, edits like you. Visual mode and conversational mode share one surface instead of fighting.

**Show hook:** Mesa ↔ Declare calendar zoom ↔ Micropolis map zoom ↔ Soul City liminal space — same anti-chat, pro-spatial grain. Pair with Elliot Winard's "wormhole" comment on the OL 5.0 thread.
