# Ted Selker — Stanford CS547 lectures

> *Six guest lectures over fourteen years in Terry Winograd's "People, Computers, and Design" seminar — IBM to MIT Media Lab, TrackPoint to context-aware computing.*

Ted was a repeat guest in [Terry Winograd's](../../terry-winograd/README.md) Stanford **CS547** HCI
seminar — the recorded, open, one-luminary-a-week class that is the direct forebear of the Repo Show
(the same series that holds Will Wright's 1996 *Interfacing to Microworlds* talk). Five of Ted's
lectures are digitized and streamable from Stanford; the earliest — the **TrackPoint** lecture — is
still tape-only.

a decade of **context-aware / considerate computing**.

## The missing TrackPoint lecture (1993)

The lecture people keep asking Ted about — how the **TrackPoint** was created — is listed in the
Stanford finding aid ([SC1217, OAC](https://oac.cdlib.org/findaid/ark:/13030/c82b926h)) as:

> T. Selker, IBM, "Trackpoint II: Making a better mouse" (CS547 Winograd 7), 1993-5-12 —
> 1 videotape (VHS), Box 1

It does **not** appear among the digitized SearchWorks records. Henry Lowood (Stanford's curator for
History of Science & Technology and Film & Media Collections) confirmed in January 2023 that he
found six streamed Selker videos but not the 1993 lecture, and offered to ask the Curator of
University Archives. **Rescue target:** the Box 1 VHS is the primary source for the TrackPoint
origin story — a natural show segment.

## The January 2023 preservation thread

Part of Don's effort to crowdsource the resurrection of the whole CS547 videorecording archive
(339 talks, 1990–2012) — the thread that recovered Will Wright's 1996 talk. Ted's role:

- Ted asked after his 1993 TrackPoint lecture and his other talks in the series; Henry Lowood
  replied the same day with the SearchWorks results — *"Ask and you shall receive, Ted!"*
- Ted volunteered practical help: he'd found someone to cheaply back up old **BayCHI** talks to
  YouTube over the previous year, and offered the same for the CS547 collection.
- Ted on the stakes: *"I hope we can save all the amazing historical treasure that Terry Winograd
  created by holding his historic class."*
