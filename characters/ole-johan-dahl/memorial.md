# Remembering Ole-Johan Dahl (1931–2002)

**Ole-Johan Dahl** (1931–2002) — with **Kristen Nygaard** created **Simula**
    (Simula I 1966; Simula 67), the first language with classes and objects. Kay names Simula as
    one of the two main roots of Smalltalk (the other being LISP) — Yoot People.md. Turing Award
    2001 (with Nygaard).

We cite; we do not speak as them.
