# Severe Tire Damage

Mark Weiser was the drummer of Severe Tire Damage, a band that called itself "the first band on the internet, the first band on the
MBone." In June 1993 it broadcast live video and audio worldwide over the Internet, and then kept
broadcasting weekly "to demonstrate that technology can be both loud and annoying." Its web site,
`www.std.org`, survives in the Wayback Machine (600 captures, 1997–2026).

This folder keeps what can be recovered of it, cached from the Wayback Machine.
