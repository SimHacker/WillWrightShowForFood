SEVERE TIRE DAMAGE WEEKLY MBONE SHOW

Besides being the band that first opened the Internet to live music, Severe Tire Damage has also made a series of broadcasts on the MBone every week for 15 to 30 minutes. Viewers can tune in to the Severe Tire Damage channel on the MBone every Wednesday at 9pm PST.
What is the MBone? Glad you asked.
