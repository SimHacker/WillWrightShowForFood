Problems?

There's a lot of stuff going on when you download this demo.
First is, of course, the download: it is likely that elements will download out of sync. Just wait for all of the parts to finish downloading, then start the audio at the beginning of the "video" loop.
If the frame rate on the "video" loop is too slow, try loading the version for slow computers regardless of your processor speed. This is not full motion video, so don't expect more than a few frames per second. (It's actually a Hi-8mm copy of the view from STD's remote-cam, digitized on a non-linear editor, single-framed to a series of 640x480 gifs, and munged into a Netscape/IE-friendly sequential gif loop.)
If the frame rate is still sucky, try saving the gif locally and/or enlarging your disk cache.
If the frame rate is still not making you happy, view the gif in a dedicated paint program; not all programs correctly interpret gif animations though.
If the frame rate is still not joy-inducing AND you're editing on deadline for broadcast television, use the "DT" function on that BVW-75.
If none of these solutions work, just get yourself an MBone feed and watch it for real.
Or try Severe Tire Damage technical support at (techsupport@std.org )

And if for some reason the audio isn't appropriate, perhaps the lyrical content or the muddy, distorted on-camera microphone doesn't suit your use, try either the .wave clips available on the Custom CD page or the RealAudio full songs on the Jukebox.
