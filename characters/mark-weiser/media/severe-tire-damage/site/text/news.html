WINDOWS REFUND DAY

Monday, 15 February 1999 is Windows Refund Day. STD will be performing on a flat bed truck at Microsoft's Foster City campus at noon for a parade of Linux, BSD, and VMS users who can honestly say they don't use "Windows" software and are entitled to a refund for bundled Microsoft software. Be there to find out if Microsoft has a sense of humor.

BRAZIL ROCKS

STD's "Who Cares" CD is reviewed in Brazil's finest news organ: O GLOBO. In retaliation, STD performs that Brazilian classic bossa nova tune, "The Girl With Emphysema" -sometimes called "The Girl From Ipanema". A musical tribute.

THEY MIGHT BE GIANTS

TMBG just released their live tour CD, "Severe Tire Damage". This, of course, means that STD's next CD may be entitled, "They Might Be Giants". Call out the lawyers! Actually, STD's bass player used to date TMBG's manager's sheep or something back in the days of "Lincoln".
