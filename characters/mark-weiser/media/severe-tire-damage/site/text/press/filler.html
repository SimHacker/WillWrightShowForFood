SEVERE TIRE DAMAGE IN THE PRESS




The first band on the Internet has the first live interactive multimedia show. STD's commitment to originality is demonstrated in both the music and the medium of transmission.

Severe Tire Damage
...is the first live band on the Internet
...has the first "official website" for a band
...has the first remote-controlled live video camera on the Internet
...has the first remote-controlled audio mixer on the Internet
...has the first remote-controlled for machine on the Internet
...has "rock's smartest drummer" -Smithsonian Magazine
...had the first "live worldwide pantsless broadcast"
...had the first bruise-cam
...had the first live worldwide interactive multimedia show on the planet
...is the first rock band to present an invited technical talk

The biggest flurry of attention was in November 1994 when STD opened and closed for the Rolling Stones during a live broadcast on the Internet's MBone.

STD has also been on TV a number of times. Not on public access, either: PBS and the Discovery Channel.

Occasionally, there are virtual sightings of Severe Tire Damage in movies, print, and television. Some of them actually refer to this band. Which ones? You decide.

And as the "House Band of the Internet" there are many online references to Severe Tire Damage.
