Q: What was the first rock group to go on-line on the Internet: the Rolling Stones,Severe Tire Damage, or Aerosmith?
A: Severe Tire Damage; The group performed Nov. 18, 1994, on the Internet 20 minutes before the Stones began their first-ever on-line concert.
