THE SEVERE TIRE DAMAGE WEB SITE

Severe Tire Damage, the band with the most bits.
Currently well over a gigabyte online and skyrocketing.

The main server at STD is averaging 25,000 hits per month (first quarter of 1997). This doesn't include hits to the remote webcam (at 100 per hour), any of the movies, and most of the music.

The Severe Tire Damage web pages are always in development. Whenever there's a new web product in beta testing that might be interesting, STD will be among the first to test it out. Because of this, there are occasionally problems. The STD site uses some features that may not be possible in your browser and plug-ins that aren't made for your hardware. Here's some of the stuff you may need to make the STD pages run on your computer.

AUDIO: STD has audio in AU, WAV, and many RA formats. For quick downloading, try the .ra 14.4 format. The (beta release) stereo RA formats seem to take at least 80586 processing power to decode without glitches. The STD "Who Cares" Compact Disc for sale elsewhere in this site is a standard audio CD, playable in any CD player.

IMAGES: STD has what may be the largest inline GIF ever made, at somewhere near 10 megabytes, and many QuickTime movies of >50 megabytes. But these images only load if you select them, otherwise, the site is somewhat "bandwidth-friendly" and only downloads animated images once as a header to the frames pages.

CODE: The underlying JavaScript for the audio mixer, camera control, and fog machine (among others) works with a beta version of Navigator version 2.something. It may not work under MSIE always. Don't know. Don't care.

STD LIVE: Nearly every Wednesday at 8:00pm PDT, Severe Tire Damage broadcasts live on the internet's MBone. When this is happening, you will be able to make the remote camera work, make the audio mixer work, and play with the fog machine. IF YOU DON'T HAVE MBONE CONNECTIVITY YOU CAN"T SEE THE SHOW. Check out the MBone page elsewhere on this site for details. The STD Camera Control Page will display a live frame from the remote cam for those without the MBone. Frequently the camera is turned on and working during the week either in the basement where STD rehearses or in Lance Berc's office.

RealAudio from Progressive Networks

STD uses version 3.0 ra and ram files. If you're not hearing the music in the jukebox or audio clips, you may not have RealAudio installed. Not all platforms currently have support for ver3.0 files.

Most of the pages on the high-bandwidth side of the STD site use some JavaScript. You'll need a JavaScript-enabled browser to make it work. The scripting was tested using beta versions of 32 bit Netscape products.

VRML

There are many browsers and plug-ins that support VRML (Virtual Reality Markup Language). Try either Netscape, MSIE, or any of the fine stand-alone products by other companies.

inline GIF Images, AVI movies, WAV audio, AU audio

While these formats were new and cool back in the old days (a few years ago), they're ubiquitous now. If your browser is having problems with these formats, contact AOL to cancel your account.

ABOUT THE SITE DESIGN:

Back in the old days, before anyone thought of building an HTML editor, there was Severe Tire Damage. Back in the days before numbers were added to the HTML specifications and it was just "html" and not "html2.1" or "!DOCTYPE htmlplus PUBLIC "-//Internet/RFC xxxx//EN" there was Severe Tire Damage. Back when this site was considered "the first official band website" by some media person and you had to tell people "aitch-tee-tee-pee-colon-forwardslash-forwardslash-doubleyou-doubleyou-doubleyou-dot (that's a period)-..." --you get the idea-- there was STD.

The site has grown a bit since then. And with every new goodie available for website design, STD has added new features. Remember when being able to have a colored background was cool? Then when background images were first supported, STD was among the few who didn't throw a huge picture under the text (the tire tread design underlying the STD pages is only one kilobyte). Then inline GIFs were finally supported (though they had been in the specification for the format for years before). Then audio plug-ins, RealAudio, VRML browsers, frames, embedding, JavaScript...

Every time there's going to be something new on the 'net, STD tries to be the first to abuse it. This usually means some STD clone spends hours slaving in front of an ASCII text editor trying to get the "hot new goodie" to work. All of the pages contained in this site have been created by hand. As such, they don't always work across different platforms. There's also no coherent map to the site. Too many weird little ideas and niches keep popping up. Hopefully the index is current and you'll find what you need.

All of which is just an excuse for not keeping the site up to date. And if it starts looking a bit dated, you can assume STD is busy designing a whole new suite of pages for your edification. Remember, Severe Tire Damage is the only band that really cares about you.

Now that you've made it to the bottom of the page, if there's any problems or suggestions that haven't been adequately addressed, please contact webmaster@std.org or techsupport@std.org. If you're currently at AOL, Prodigy, CompuServe, or WebTV and having problems, it's not our fault.
