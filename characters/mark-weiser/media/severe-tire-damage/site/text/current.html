WEBCAM OF THE DAY

The remote controlled STD WebCam and SuperMegaBandCam are up and running.
The Digital Camera Network (dcn.com) picked STD's super cool remote-controlled camera as "WebCam of the Day". Unfortunately, it wasn't working on the day they picked. So the friendly gnomes at STD got out the power tools and fixed it. To celebrate, DCN was kind enough to reschedule the honor for a whole weekend. To celebrate the honor, STD has exhumed and recharged the rarely-seen SuperMegaBandCam --a high-resolution camera designed for solar observation. If you watch on Wednesday night at 9:00pm PST, you'll see live snapshots of STD's MCast. If you have MBone access, the WebCam controls will change the live video image. Click on the cartoon image of a band member and the camera will point at him. The rest of the time, you get to see Lance at work in Palo Alto. He knows you're there, too. Every time you grab a fresh image or move the camera, a little voice says, "Cheese!" to let him know you're there. (STD's camera control code is used by many WebCams on the internet. It's free, y'know. Why? Because STD cares.)

STD SPREADS WORLDWIDE

Ernst goes to Silicon Valley
This week, STD hosted a Deutsche freelancer based in New York City ("New York City?"). With any luck, he'll write flattering pieces about STD for three German-language publications. He was just interviewing Drummer Weiser until discovering STD. And what's more fun than sharing STD with your countrymen?

Slow Burn for Stern
German magazine "Stern" should be coming out with an article on STD this month or so. A camera crew and reporter spent an evening with the band. Like all journalists of excellence, they bought dinner and beer for the whole band; which that night numbered twelve or so... ("Stern" and "burn" don't rhyme.)

The Guiden in Sweden!
Welcome, readers of InterNet Guiden. Sweden's hip to STD. An article with photos was published recently. Swedish speakers can check the English language translation for errors.

Korea is Kool!
The Korean TV series STD did has been in broadcast for a while now. Recently the band got a tape of the four hour series. Pretty cool. The email from Korea has been a lot of fun, too.

THE STD COMPACT DISC AND THE UNABOMBER.

The title track, "Who Cares" , was written and performed long before the alleged Unabomber was caught.
"...millions die, you all go numb.
But kill just one, the whole world stares... who cares..."
-the repeated phrase, "Who Cares," became a mantra for STD as the album project dug a bigger and bigger hole for itself. Most of the members of Severe Tire Damage were good candidates for the Unabomber's list. What else would you expect from the band that brought you the first worldwide live pantsless broadcast? A very small portion of the profits of album sales will be donated to the Kaczynski Defense Fund.
