FORMATS

These songs were first recorded onto audio cassette in various studioesque environments. They were then sampled in stereo at 44.1ksamps (ksamps==kilo-samples/sec) into SGI-Indy files, and from there down sampled to the various formats. The formats are:

Sun: 8ksamps mulaw Sun/Next format. This is one of the most widely used formats, there are shareware players for macs, and of course suns.

Macintosh: 11ksamps 8-bit unsigned integer mono Macintosh sound format files, in Stuffit archive form. Grab the file (by clicking on the hyperlink), and then use your favorite unstuffing program to convert the .sit to a sound file. Your MacMosaic reader may even do this automatically for you.

PC: 22.05ksamps 16-bit integer mono WAVE format. This is the MS-Windows universal audio format. This is a widely used format, but beware, not all programs that claim to accept WAVE format actually can do the full conversions.

AIFF: the original Indy file, in AIFF format (Audio Interchange File Format), 16-bit integer, big-endian, two channel, 44.1ksamps. There are AIFF players for Macs and PCs.

MPEG: the mono MPEG encoding of the original, at 128kbps output rate.

Real Audio: These samples use the 28.8 encoding in version 2 of Progressive Networks' Real Audio encoding standard. There are players for Macs and PCs available at realaudio.com.
