Radio Loves STD

SEVERE TIRE DAMAGE ON
KFJC (89.7 FM)

aired November 21, 1997

Pictures from the studio:

Severe Tire Damage is invited to play live from the studios of KFJC radio (89.7 FM in the San Francisco Bay Area). After 35 minutes of nonstop chatter and music, the band leaves the airwaves and goes to work (hey! this happened at 9:00 in the morning). And since the station keeps play logs, here's proof that STD played.

And as if it wasn't enough to play live, KFJC replayed some of this show on December 12.
