# Mark Weiser — GLANCE 🕯️

- *In memoriam (1952–1999)* — **memorial mode**: represent + discuss, never interview
- Coined **ubiquitous computing**; **calm technology**; 'The Computer for the 21st Century' (1991)
- Xerox PARC **CTO**; tabs / pads / boards
- **CHI'88 pie-menu paper** co-author with Don, Callahan, Shneiderman
- Drummer, **Severe Tire Damage** — first band live on the Internet (1993)
- **Told Don that PKD's *Ubik* inspired the name ubicomp** — seed of the [`pkd-lem-ai-sf`](pkd-lem-ai-sf.md) reading group (PKD·Lem·SF·AI)
- **Living legacy** — the [Mark D. Weiser Excellence in Computing Scholarship](legacy.yml) (UC Berkeley EECS) + torchbearers Gregory Abowd & Amber Case
- Discussants: [Don Hopkins](../don-hopkins/README.md), [David Rosenthal](../david-rosenthal/README.md), [Ben Shneiderman](../ben-shneiderman/README.md), [Brad Myers](../brad-myers/README.md); Vicky Reich & Will Wright welcome
