## Who

**Zenon Walter Pylyshyn** (25 August 1937 – 6 December 2022) was a Canadian
cognitive scientist and philosopher. After Engineering Physics at McGill and a PhD
in experimental psychology at Saskatchewan, he taught at **Western University (UWO)**
from 1966 to 1994 across Psychology, Computer Science, Philosophy, and Electrical
Engineering, directing the UWO Centre for Cognitive Science. In 1994 he founded and
led the **Rutgers Center for Cognitive Science (RuCCS)**. He asked foundational
questions about **cognition and computation**, argued against the pictorial view of
mental imagery, and developed **visual indexing ("FINST") theory**. FRSC; Jean Nicod
Prize (2004); Hebb Award.

## Why he's in this cast

He enters through [Roy Eagleson](../roy-eagleson/), who **began his PhD under
Pylyshyn at UWO** and foregrounds him as the root of his own lineage in the
[9 July storyline thread](../heinz-lemke/sources/2026-07-09-pixie-storyline-thread.md).
Roy also notes Pylyshyn's own postdoc advisors were **Allen Newell** and **John
McCarthy** — a direct line from symbolic AI's founders into the classroom where PIXIE
now gets taught. (Newell, McCarthy, Hinton, and Fodor are context here, not
characters in this show.)
