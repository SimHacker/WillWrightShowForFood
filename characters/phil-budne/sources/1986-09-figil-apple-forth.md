# FIGIL, September 1986: Forth for an Apple IIe

FIGIL was a Forth Interest Group mailing list moderated from the Weizmann Institute over
BITNET, gatewaying Usenet's net.lang.forth. Don received it at `A2DEH@mc.lcs.mit.edu`. Public
list; Don's copies of the August 1986 mailing (sent 12 September 1986) and the October 1986
mailing (sent 2 October 1986).

## Phil's post

Date: 6 Sep 86 00:53:48 GMT · From: budd@bu-cs.bu.edu (Philip Budne) · Boston U. Comp. Sci.
· Subject: Request: FORTH (or basic compiler) for Apple IIe

> I have a friend doing experiment control / data acquisition with an
> apple II but finds basic too slow (big surprise).
>
> What are some forths for the apple and their pros/cons/prices.
> Or alternatively a compiler for basic.
>
> Thanks
> Phil Budne
> Boston University / Distributed Systems

## Replies, in brief

- **Steve Schlaifer, JPL (8 Sep):** Mountain View Press's Forth-79, public domain; source
  listing or disk, about $20.
- **Jeff Loucks, R&D Associates (8 Sep):** Mountain View Press too; a friend's Apple Forth with
  a symbolic debugger ("a little guy on the screen moving registers and memory pictures").
- **Steve Hawley, Bellcore (11 Sep):** Paul Lutus's GraForth, no floating point; BASIC
  compilers gain 2 to 3 times; his assembly knight's tour ran 400 times faster than compiled
  BASIC.
- **Lee Brotzman, NASA Goddard (12 Sep, October mailing):** he wrote the public-domain UNIFORTH
  Sampler, Forth-83 for ProDOS with a 6502 assembler, floating point and a screen editor; free
  for postage.
- **Bruce O'Neel (13 Sep, October mailing):** UNIFORTH Sampler for ProDOS, public domain;
  United Software Systems' address.

## Why it's here

Forty years later the 340 display Phil modeled runs Mitch Bradley's PDP-7 Forth in a browser.
