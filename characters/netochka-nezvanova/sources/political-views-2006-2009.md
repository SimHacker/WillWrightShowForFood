## 2006 US midterms (November)

After the Democratic sweep, Don celebrated Berkeley **Measure H (impeach Bush)** and midterm results. NN replied (9 November 2006):

- Welcomed tech news for Don's OpenLaszlo agenda  
- On the Dem win: "almost impossible to believe"; Rumsfeld stepping down "even more impossible"  
- Damage already done — bad laws, plans "almost certainly never been undone"  
- Hope: maybe things will start being **QUESTIONED**, less intimidation  
- Not returning to the US soon — "at least til I know it's gotten a bit better"  
- "I have hopes, but little faith… faith is something that I will require some help getting back"

Earlier same thread (3 November): lost faith in US politics; pan-Australasian region; nihilist self-question; asked how Don kept spirits high.
