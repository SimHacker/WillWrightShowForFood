## Smalltalk / Squeak

Traveling in the **pan-Australasian** region, NN picked up **Smalltalk again briefly** — by way of **Squeak** — after meeting a Brazilian programmer in a backpackers' hostel. She expected good programmers to emerge from the region; missed the days when **Bulgaria** "had the clever ones."

## Lua

Don pointed her at **Lua** (PUC Rio) and the Debian language shootout; NN said she'd check it out. Don had switched pocket-PC pie menu scripting from JavaScript to Lua for speed/memory and SWIG integration.

## OpenLaszlo / Flash contrast

Same month Don sent **OpenLaszlo** streaming-video demos (Flash player). His note: Flash scales and mixes alpha graphics well but **cannot manipulate video pixels like NATO** — a fair split between Don's web stack and NN's Max/NATO world.

## Don near-miss: After Effects host for NATO

Don once discussed building an **After Effects plug-in host for NATO** with NN; intrigued, he **declined** to take the gig — scope and temperament. (Don was also deep in live video on PowerPC Mac, AE plugins for CA/Warpomatic/8-bit colorization — Subutai Ahmad / Jim Crutchfield lineage.)
