## Who

**David A. Tristram** ("Dave") — Don's friend **since the mid–late 1980s** — they met at **USENIX
Monterey 1989** (Dave's **Panel Library** paper; Don's **PSIBER Space Deck** / visual PostScript
debugger for **NeWS**). Dave sent Don **Electropaint** source; Don hacked it on an **SGI at UMD** into
a **3D Pseudo Scientific Visualizer** over recursive **s-expr** trees. Dave co-founded **Raster Masters**: **Onyx**
rigs, **Shoreline Amphitheatre**, **Grateful Dead** tours, MIDI-reactive live graphics (*Infrared
Roses* captures some of the Dead **Drums/Space** era). Full write-up:
[`raster-masters-panel-library.md`](raster-masters-panel-library.md).

Dave's generative stack runs through the same obsessions as this repo: **reaction-diffusion**,
**video feedback**, **cellular automata** (he has publicly credited Toffoli & **Norman Margolus**'s
*Cellular Automata Machines*, alongside CelLab and *Chaos*). Don has been carrying that torch in
**CAM6**, **HyperLook**, and **Musical Gas**.

## Why a Repo Show

Because Dave already **performs** generative systems — he doesn't just render them. A Repo Show is
**Raster Masters for the browser era**: clone the repo, open WebGPU/CAM6, and **improvise live
graphics to music** on stream — the Dead-at-Shoreline energy, forkable.

- **Electropaint → web** — resurrect the spirit of the Panel Library for **`AudioWorklet` + WebGPU**.
- **CA + graphics jam** — Don's **CAM6** beside Dave's performance rule-sets; natural co-guest for
  [Norman Margolus](../norman-margolus/README.md)'s **CA looping fest**.
- **Feedback family** — same room as [Jim Crutchfield](../jim-crutchfield/README.md) (video feedback),
  [Scott Draves](../scott-draves/README.md) (generative art), [Brian Eno](../brian-eno/README.md)
  (generative ambient).
