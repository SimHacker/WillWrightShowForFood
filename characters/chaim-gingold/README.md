# Chaim Gingold 🏗️

**Chaim Gingold** is the designer-historian who put SimCity under a microscope and drew what he
found. His ***Building SimCity: How to Put the World in a Machine*** (MIT Press, 2024) traces the
game from Vannevar Bush's analog computers and system dynamics through cellular automata to the
rise and fall of Maxis — Stewart Brand called it "one of the best origin stories ever told."
His famous **reverse diagrams** are engineering archaeology: SimCity's actual loops and maps,
drawn with enough care that programmers cite them like source code.

He isn't only a historian. He apprenticed to **Will Wright** on *Spore* and designed the
**Spore Creature Creator**; built **Earth: A Primer**, a science book made of simulation toys;
co-created the falling-sand toy **Sandspiel**; and holds a Ph.D. in *Play Design* (UC Santa Cruz).
He studies microworlds by building them.

## Why he's here

- **The book is the map** of the territory this repo runs: Don's Micropolis is the code Chaim's
  diagrams describe. Historian's drawing meets executable source.
- **He connected the cast.** Chaim introduced [Sam Arbesman](../sam-arbesman/README.md) to Don
  (July 2024) — the intro that seeded the Maxis Labs 2 thread.
- **He and Will already work on air.** ROMchip's July 2024 stream had Will interviewing Chaim
  about the book — [sources/romchip-will-wright-2024.md](sources/romchip-will-wright-2024.md).
- **Pacing:** new parent — async-first, tiny asks, no deadlines.
