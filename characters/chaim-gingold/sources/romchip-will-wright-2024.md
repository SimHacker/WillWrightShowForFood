## The event

| | |
|--|--|

**Will Wright** interviewed **Chaim Gingold** about *Building SimCity: How to Put the World in a
Machine* (MIT Press, 2024) — the history of simulation, SimCity's design, and the rise and fall
of Maxis. Chaim announced it in his [HN AMA](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40699204):
*"We thought it would be fun to turn the tables and have him interview someone else for a change."*

Phil Salvador (Video Game History Foundation) later described Will's opening on the
[VGHF podcast](https://gamehistory.org/episode-125-romchip-whats-next/): trickster-god energy,
first question — *"So Chaim, if you were a tree, what kind of tree would you be?"*

## Why it matters here

- **Proof of format** — Will + Chaim already did a live-streamed book conversation with a real
  audience. A Repo Show weave with the same pair is a proven combination, not a hope.
- **The lineage** — Chaim apprenticed to Will on *Spore* (designed the **Spore Creature Creator**);
  the book is his scholar's return to Will's first microworld.
- **Same month as the Sam intro** — Chaim connected [Sam Arbesman](../../sam-arbesman/README.md)
  to Don on **9 July 2024**, ten days before this stream. One July, two threads.
