# Nicholas Negroponte 🏛️💾 *(MIT Media Lab; Being Digital; OLPC)*

*Invitation portrayal — not Nicholas Negroponte.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · authored by Don Hopkins

## Who

Architect who founded MIT's **Architecture Machine Group** (1967) and co-founded /
directed the **MIT Media Lab** (1985); author of *Being Digital* (1995); early
**Wired** investor/columnist; founded **One Laptop per Child**. Margaret Minsky's
PhD advisor (haptics / Sandpaper). Public record also includes his 2019 remarks on
accepting Jeffrey Epstein's Media Lab donations — omit that and the portrayal is
gloss; ask about institutional ethics on air rather than pretend.

## Why invited

Institution-builder who put a roof over Marvin Minsky, Seymour Papert, Muriel Cooper,
and Opera of the Future. Bits-vs-atoms prophecy, OLPC constructionism at scale, and
the hard questions about how labs fund themselves.
