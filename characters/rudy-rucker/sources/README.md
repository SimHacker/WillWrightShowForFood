## Symbiotic programming on the CAM-6 (1989)

[`wolfram-03-1-6.pdf`](wolfram-03-1-6.pdf): Rudy Rucker, ***Symbiotic Programming: Crossbreeding
Cellular Automaton Rules on the CAM-6***, *Complex Systems* 3 (1989) 79–90, Complex Systems
Publications. Written at San José State.

Two or three existing rules are run whole, each in its own CAM-6 bitplanes, in an arena where they
can see each other, instead of splicing halves of programs together as genetic programming does.
From Conway's Life, Gérard Vichniac's Vote and Brian Silverman's Brain he makes **Brainlife**,
**Votelife** and **Ranch**. Rules are given in "pidgin Forth", the CAM-6's rule language. It
includes his tricks for slowing Brain down, and colour plates of Brainlife evolving.

These rules are direct candidates for the cabinet's CAM-6 cartridges and for Don's CAM6 simulator,
which runs CAM-6 Forth rules.
