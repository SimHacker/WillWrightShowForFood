# Rudy Rucker — show hooks & threads

*Prompts and dreams for a Repo Show with Rudy. His pick, his pace.*

## The big three

1. **Design a MOOLLM cellular-automata skill together.**
   Seed it from **CelLab's rule catalog** (Rudy + John Walker's spectacular manual),
   cross-referenced with **CAM6** (Toffoli & Margolus) and the **Movable Feast
   Machine**. Rudy classifies and narrates rules better than anyone; turn that into
   a living, runnable skill — rules as K-lines, the manual as semantic YAML.

2. **A new "commercial plug" for cellular automata.**
   The original CA Lab demo ends with a deadpan **1-800 order number** — pure late-'80s
   infomercial. Invite Rudy to record a fresh plug: *"Order your cellular automata
   now."* House-style retro treatment (B&W grain, motion-tracked credits) optional.

3. **"Why not 3D?"**
   Pose Dave Ackley's question to Rudy — and put the answers side by side across
   Ackley, **Norman Margolus**, **Scott Draves**, and Rudy. Rudy has opinions about
   dimensionality (he wrote *The Fourth Dimension*, after all).

## More threads

- **CAs are too UPTIGHT.** Rudy's delight in the MFM loosening rigid synchrony /
  determinism — a great, funny way into robust-first computing for a lay audience.
- **Zhabotinsky as a "fifth class."** Reaction-diffusion, async wave motion, "zhabo
  scrolls," near-sync update parameters — a live experiment worth running on air.
- **Cyberpunk ↔ ALife.** *Software*/*Wetware*'s boppers, *The Hacker and the Ants*,
  and BOPPERS the Autodesk product — fiction and code feeding each other.
- **The Wolfram hinge.** A Wolfram interview turned Rudy into a CS professor; worth
  hearing him tell it (bridge to the [Stephen Wolfram](../stephen-wolfram/) room).
- **Gnarl.** Rudy's term for the sweet spot between order and chaos — the aesthetic
  target of every good CA. A unifying theme for a CA-art jam.
