## Through-line

**Andrew (CMU) → SunDew → NeWS** — PostScript-programmable, network-transparent windows.

At the **1985 Alvey Methodology of Window Management** workshop, Gosling published **SunDew** (NeWS precursor) and co-authored the **Andrew window manager** paper with **David Rosenthal** — same proceedings as **Warren Teitelman's** ten-year retrospective. Both **published and participated** in architecture working-group discussions that shaped window-system vocabulary and UK MMI planning.

→ Full workshop context: [David's window-systems lineage](../david-rosenthal/window-systems-lineage.md#alvey-workshop-1985)  
→ Proceedings: http://www.chilton-computing.org.uk/inf/literature/books/wm/index.htm

## Gosling papers

### SunDew

**Title:** *SunDew — A Distributed and Extensible Window System*

Renamed NeWS; programmable WM — cited in Alvey architecture WG discussions. Venue: Alvey Workshop (1985); *The NeWS Book* (1989).

### Andrew window manager

**Title:** *A Window Manager for Bitmapped Displays and Unix*  
**Co-author:** David Rosenthal  
**Venue:** Same Alvey workshop; CACM *Andrew* paper (March 1986)
