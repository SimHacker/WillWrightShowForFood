## What Jamie has done

Jamie Doornbos was a lead programmer on The Sims at Maxis and one of the principal architects of the game's Sim behavior and AI — the system that made smart objects and autonomous Sims feel alive. He has spoken publicly about the design and engineering of the Sims' simulation. (Don's colleague on The Sims.)
