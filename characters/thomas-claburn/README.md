# Thomas Claburn 📰

*Press contact — not an invitation portrayal.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · consent: warm correspondent · authored by Don Hopkins

**Thomas Claburn** reports for **[The Register](https://www.theregister.com/)** from San
Francisco. In April 2023 he wrote
**["What if someone mixed The Sims with ChatGPT bots? It would look like this"](https://www.theregister.com/2023/04/11/sims_ai_generation/)**
— the piece on the Stanford/Google *Generative Agents* paper (Park et al.): memory streams,
reflection, and planning bolted onto ChatGPT to populate a Sims-like Smallville with 25 agents,
at thousands of dollars of tokens for two simulated days.

That article is why he's here: **MOOLLM** is a working answer to its question — characters with
personality, memory, and relationships as plain text files in git, human-authored and governed,
running in ordinary developer tools. And his *"This won't end well"* section maps directly onto
this repo's [consent and portrayal rulebook](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) —
impersonation forbidden, simulation labeled, subjects holding edit/delete rights.

**[Liam Proven](../liam-proven/README.md)** forwarded Don's February 2026 brief to him in June
2026; Tom replied that he'd pitch an editor. **The story has since grown:** Will Wright accepted,
**WillWrightShowForFood** became the flagship application, with
[MicropolisCore](https://github.com/SimHacker/MicropolisCore) as running code and
[Soul City](../../catalogs/soul-city/README.md) as the games bridge — all free and open (MOOLLM
free, WWSFF free, Micropolis GPL). He and Liam get the coordinated first look, publication timed
with the premiere ([quiet mode](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#stealth-mode)).

**The standing offer:** participate, not just report — write about whatever aspects he pleases,
and if it's fun, record the conversation as a show in either direction: Don on a Register show,
or Tom on the Repo Show. The meta layer is aimed straight at him: MOOLLM isn't only the subject
of his 2023 question, it's the **production tool** making this show — invitations, consent
enforcement, and editorial workflow running as skills in the same public repos anyone can browse.
