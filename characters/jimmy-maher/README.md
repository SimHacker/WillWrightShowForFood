**Jimmy Maher** — historian, IF author, and the voice of [The Digital Antiquarian](https://www.filfre.net/).
He lives with his wife **Dorte** near **Billund, Denmark** (formerly Dallas). His site is a
**chronological chronicle** of games and digital culture — free to read, sustained by optional
[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/DigitalAntiquarian) support. Since 2019 he also writes
[The Analog Antiquarian](https://www.analogantiquarian.com/) (world wonders).
