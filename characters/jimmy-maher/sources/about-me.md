Title: About Me — The Digital Antiquarian

Key excerpt (Jul 2026):

Hello! My name is Jimmy, and I live with my wife Dorte in a cozy little village near Billund,
Denmark. (The city of Lego!) Until 2009, I made my home in Dallas, Texas, but that life seems
very far away to me now.

The bulk of the articles here form an historical chronicle of interactive entertainment, digital
culture, and other matters adjacent. Because history is always in the making, a project of this
nature can never be truly completed. But that's fine: I'm enjoying the journey immensely.

I will never require anyone to pay to read the articles on this site, but your support, if you
have the means to provide it, will help to ensure that I can continue doing this for a long time
to come.

Second site (2019): **The Analog Antiquarian** — Wonders of the World.
