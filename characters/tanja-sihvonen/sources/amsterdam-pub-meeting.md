# Amsterdam pub — scholar meets Sims builders by accident

**Circa 2008** · **Sound Garden**, Amsterdam  
*Gezellig. That's just the kind of thing that happens in this cozy magic village.*

---

## The pub

The **Sound Garden** — great place, interesting crowd. Garden and dock on the canal out back; you can
pop by in your **boat**. One of those Amsterdam rooms where the city feels like a small town.

Don Hopkins and **Eric Bowman** (original Sims programmers, both in Amsterdam then — Eric at TomTom)
were out for a casual evening. Video games, life, nothing heavy.

At another table: **Tanja Sihvonen**, PhD student, writing about **user-created content in The Sims**
— the thesis that became ***Players Unleashed!: Modding The Sims and the Culture of Gaming***
(Amsterdam University Press, 2011).

---

## The collision

They talked games. Tanja mentioned her Sims research. Don and Eric mentioned they'd worked on the
**original game**. Mutual head-explosion — the scholar studying player culture had accidentally sat
down with two of the builders, neither side knowing until the conversation unfolded.

Don and Eric offered to answer research questions. Tanja went on to publish one of the field's anchor
books on Sims modding and player negotiation of the code.
