# Cynthia Solomon's videos

Cynthia Solomon's YouTube channel ([@cynthiaso](https://www.youtube.com/@cynthiaso/videos)) holds her own uploads of Logo and AI Lab history: films of the MIT Logo group and its turtles, Seymour Papert and Marvin Minsky clips, the 1982-84 Atari Cambridge Research demo tape, Logo lessons and children's projects from 2007, and a few talks. This index lists all 90 videos on the channel's Videos tab (the channel has no Shorts, Live or Playlists tab) and cross-references Don Hopkins's Hacker News comments that link or describe them. One-line descriptions come from her video descriptions; where she left the description empty, from the title and YouTube's automatic captions. Compiled 2026-10-25.

## Atari Cambridge Research, 1982-84
