## Why Will and I keep re-teaming (the honeybee model)

We wander off in different directions for years, gathering the most interesting and
potent pollen — new engines, new languages, new AI — then get back together in the
hive and brew it into things neither of us could make before. SimCity ports led to
The Sims tools; a coffeeshop iPhone app led back to Stupid Fun Club Storymaker, 
Bar Karma audience scripted TV show, and Urban Safari; open-source
SimCity for the OLPC led to the Micropolis project; and all of it led here. 
That pattern — leave, learn hard things, return, compound — is the career.

## What I want to work on
