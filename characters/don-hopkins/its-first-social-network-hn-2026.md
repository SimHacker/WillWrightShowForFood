## The riff

Henry wants a **forested internet**; benrutter names the [small web](https://ar.al/2020/08/07/what-is-the-small-web/); TFNA says that crowd is a desktop-reading elite. Don's move: **you want artisan? here is the original.**

[ITS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incompatible_Timesharing_System) at MIT was social infrastructure before "social network" was a product category:

| Small-web anxiety | ITS reality |
|-------------------|-------------|
| No algorithm | Real-time **SEND**, **UNTALK**, session linking — social by design |
| Hand-made | **TECO Emacs**, **MACLISP**, PDP-10 assembly — the stack was craft |
| Longform | Mailing lists digested on overloaded mailers ([SF-Lovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SF-Lovers), [Human-Nets](https://www.davidwittenbrook.com/human-nets/)) |
| Moderation drama | [Jordan Hubbard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Hubbard)'s **rwall incident** (1987) — nearly got UCB kicked off ARPAnet |
| History forgotten | [Keith F. Lynch](https://keithlynch.net/history.net.html)'s 1994 memoir — [`../keith-lynch/sources/history-of-the-net-1994.md`](../keith-lynch/sources/history-of-the-net-1994.md) |
