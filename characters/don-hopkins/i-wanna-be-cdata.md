# I Wanna Be <![CDATA[

*By Don Hopkins. Sung to the tune of "I Wanna Be Sedated," with
apologies to The Ramones. First published September 8, 2017
([Medium](https://medium.com/@donhopkins/twenty-twenty-twenty-four-escapes-to-go-i-wanna-be-cdata-cca5e9c1391b));
this is the permanent, self-contained copy.*

The premise: a CDATA section is the one place in an XML document where
text is safe from the parser — nothing gets interpreted as markup,
nothing needs escaping, nothing can touch you. The song is what any
string would sing after one entity reference too many.
