# SimCity NeWS showcase — Carol Adams / Sun field opportunity (Jul–Aug 1991)

Internal Sun ↔ Maxis thread while Don and **Josh Siegel** were on the **NeWS Toolkit (TNT)** team.

## Origin — sun.mac-users (24 Jul 1991)

**Carol Adams** (Sun GIS & Desktop Applications): opportunity to port **#1-selling SimCity** to Sun — differentiate with **ToolTalk**, GIS import/export, networking vs PC version; ESRI + URISA support; seeking field encouragement.

**Don** (24 Jul): *"We *HAVE* to port SimCity to TNT! Today the SimCity — tomorrow the SimWorld!!"*

**Josh Siegel**: wouldn't be hard — vendor would supply simulator + bitmaps; TNT UI **a few days**; very interested.

## Carol's offer (2 Aug 1991)

- **Official engineering commitment**: SimCity port to Sun using **X** (Maxis vendor path).
- **Unofficial NeWS path**: if TNT team wants NeWS port, vendor willing to provide what's needed **plus SimEarth code** for a **showcase NeWS package** (SimCity + SimEarth).
- Requires Ts & Cs for source protection and return of NeWS version to vendor.

**Josh**: will safeguard source and reputation; *"showcase TNT version of SimCity and SimEarth."*

**Don** (3 Aug): *"I would *love* to port SimEverything to TNT!"* — features beyond Mac version.

## Parallel DUX track

Same summer Don was pursuing **DUX Software** Unix license (signed **19 Jul 1991**) — the contract that enabled HyperLook/NeWS SimCity and later open source. Sun's showcase offer and DUX contractor port ran in parallel.

## Josh + Maxis NDA comedy (12 Aug 1991)

Outside group wanted Sun help porting to TNT; required NDA. Josh: Don and he cannot sign without **Sun Legal** — vendor decided they'd **sneak into the meeting** unsigned; Josh promised to route NDA through Legal or *"drink large amounts of beer and kill all associated brain cells."*
