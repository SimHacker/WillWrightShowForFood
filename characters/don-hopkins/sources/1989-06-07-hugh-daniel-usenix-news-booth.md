# The NeWS booth at Usenix — Hugh Daniel's status file (7 Jun 1989)

Hugh Daniel (Grasshopper Group, hugh@toad.com **and** hugh@xanadu.com)
mails the `newsnix.booth` status file to the whole NeWS vendor crew —
Sun, UniPress, MFTI, SGI, TGV, Parallax, Architec, Columbia, LANL
(siegel@hc.dspo.gov), and Don — for the 20-foot island booth at Summer
Usenix 1989 (setup Monday 12 June; floor show Tuesday noon through late
Thursday). The operational sequel to the
[vendor census](1989-05-20-gilmore-news-vendor-census.md) sent three weeks
earlier.

## Why this matters

- **Fourth LGATE receipt — and the demo is named.** The LANL confirmed
  entry: "Contact: Josh Siegel … People: Josh Siegel, Bret Thaeler …
  **Demoing: LGATE-SDI, MPM (Tanks & AirPlains)** … Hardware: Loaner Color
  Sun 4/any … Arrives: Tuesday Night." Adds a second LANL NeWS system to
  the record: **MPM**, tanks and airplanes — plausibly the conventional-war
  panel Rosenthal described. (Spelling drift: "Thaeler" here, "Thayler" in
  the [1988 program](1988-09-14-sug-southwest-lgate-sdi-news.md) — same
  person, spelling unresolved.)
- **Don's booth entry**: "UofMaryland … Demoing: **HyperTIES, PieMenus** …
  Hardware: Sun 4/110 … 3 plugs 18 amps." The pie-menus + HyperTIES double
  bill, itemized down to the amperage.
- **"NeWS WArE" archive collecting**: "If you have NeWS software that can
  be put into the public domain, or freely redistributed, please bring it
  to the show. **Don Hopkins and I will be collecting NeWS WArE for the
  various NeWS archives.**" — the free-software distribution instinct, on
  paper, 1989.
- **GoodNeWS / HyperNeWS in the booth plan**: the Turing Institute entry
  (not attending, but "this software could run on any of the Sun 3's":
  GoodNeWS, HyperNeWS) — the Arthur van Hoff lineage that becomes
  HyperLook, in a June 1989 booth manifest.
- **OVA governance beat**: NeWS/X BOF Thursday 8pm, followed by an OVA
  meeting "where we will decide on how we want to use/run OVA for the next
  year or so … if you want to see OVA help NeWS become a strong force in
  the market place."
- **Ecosystem texture**: Sun loaner machines brokered for six vendors
  (including a MicroVAX for TGV and a 286 MS-DOS box for TAG's NewScript);
  MacNews 1.1.10 on Macintosh A/UX; a NeWS/X 3/50 terminal demo; TGV
  "Developing… might show if it wroks…"; and Hugh's self-assessment:
  "Hugh Daniel is trying to sluff off as much as possible, :r)".

NeWS software that can be put into the public domain, or freely
