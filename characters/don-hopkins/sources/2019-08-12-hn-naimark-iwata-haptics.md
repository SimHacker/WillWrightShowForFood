**3DOF multitouch haptic interface with a movable touchscreen** — Shun Takanaka, Hiroaki Yano, Hiroo Iwata, AsiaHaptics 2016. [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YCZPmj7NtSQ). The Springer chapter URL is in the comment (DOI prefix `10.1007/978-981-10-4157-0`).

When two fingertips are fixed relative to each other on the screen, the hand is a rigid object, and a 3DOF haptic device can push back on both fingers. Prototype: touchscreen, 6-axis force sensor, X-Y stage, capstan drive. It estimates finger force from the sensor and the finger positions, and generates reaction force by controlling static friction between fingertip and glass. Users feel 2D virtual shapes, translate and rotate them, and deform elastic objects.
