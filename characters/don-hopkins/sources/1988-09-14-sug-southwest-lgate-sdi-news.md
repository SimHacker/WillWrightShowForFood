# LGATE receipt — SUG Southwest Regional Conference program (14 Sep 1988)

**The primary-source receipt for LGATE**: Josh Siegel and Bret Thayler's SDI
simulation with a NeWS user interface, built at Los Alamos National
Laboratory "for SDI at the Pentagon." Public conference announcement,
forwarded via the SlugNews mailing list (kccs.com) from Michele Schirru
(LANL), for the Sun User Group Southwest Regional Conference, Albuquerque
Marriott, Friday 30 September 1988.

## Why this matters

- **Names LGATE, dated, with billing**: "This system is using NeWS in a
  large application program. The user interface has been designed for use by
  non-computer professionals. LGATE uses a Raster Technologies board running
  Phigs+. This work was done for SDI at the Pentagon. — Bret Thayler/Josh
  Siegel (Los Alamos National Laboratory)."
- **Corroborates two stories already in the archive**:
  - James Gosling's remark that Operation Desert Storm command-and-control
    ran on NeWS PostScript (Josh Siegel's LGATE at LANL) — k-line
    `desert_storm_postscript` in `DonHopkins/characters/james-gosling/CHARACTER.yml`.
  - Don's HN recollection that Siegel "at Los Alamos wrote MMPORG
    simulations of World War III for the Joint Chiefs of Staff with a
    beautiful interactive NeWS front-end" — see
    [zork-troll-flag-adventure-lineage-hn.md](zork-troll-flag-adventure-lineage-hn.md)
    (same Josh Siegel who rewrote the NeWS PostScript interpreter and
    co-built the PostScript X11 window manager with Don).
- **Documents Don's own 1988 talk billing**: first technical slot,
  8:45–9:45 — "NeWS Presentation — Building User Interfaces Using NeWS.
  Includes a demonstration of HyperTIES, a hypermedia browser running under
  NeWS. — Don Hopkins (University of Maryland)." UMD/HCIL era, five months
  after the [UMD tech-writing proposal](1988-04-10-umd-tech-writing-proposal.md).
- **Supporting cast**: John Gage keynote ("The Future of Workstations"),
  Dick Phillips (LANL Cray-to-Sun visualization workbench), Carl Diegart
  (Sandia neural-network visualization on a TAAC board), Ted Dunning (NMSU,
  autonomous network diagnostics), Sun porting-guide panel.
- **Period color**: $12 registration including deli buffet lunch, dessert by
  the pool, timed the day before the Albuquerque Balloon Fiesta ascension,
  and the LANL bureaucracy punchline: "Fixed costs at the Lab are around
  $50.00 to write a check (even for $12)!!"
