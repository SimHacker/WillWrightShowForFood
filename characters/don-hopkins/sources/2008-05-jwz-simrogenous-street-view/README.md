Jamie Zawinski compares Google's new **Street View face blurring** (Central Park carriage
horse with blurred muzzle) to The Sims **censorship pixelization** — and names Don's
implementation **"SimRogenous zones"** (1×2-pixel areas the shimmer bar hides).

## The hook

> This reminds me of the "censorship" pixelization code in The Sims that prevents you from
> ever seeing their little 1×2-pixel SimRogenous zones. (Don Hopkins once told me a long
> story about how hard that was to implement...)

Google blurred the **white horse's face** but not the brown horse behind it — comment thread
gold: Sarah Jessica Parker lawsuit jokes, Transmet sentient horses, Witness Protection,
**"I call racism"** (romulusnr), **"I'm too equine for G.M.A.P.S."** (relaxing).
