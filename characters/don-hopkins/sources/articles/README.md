# Smooshed articles — deduplicated canon

Don summarizes and re-edits old posts to reflect whatever discussion he is in, fixing
broken links with archive.org substitutes along the way, so the same material exists
in many overlapping versions across his blog, Medium, and years of Hacker News
comments. The method here: smoosh all versions of a topic together, then pull them
back apart into one comprehensive article per topic — best-of text, exquisite detail
where the topic owns it, a summary and a link where a sibling owns it. Pseudo hand
transclusion. Every article ends with a Provenance section listing all merged
versions, so the originals stay citable.
