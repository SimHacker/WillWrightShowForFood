## Working title

**How Inclusivity Saved The Sims** (subtitle: evolution of relationships, marriage, preference, gender expression — **and Soul City / Soul Saver as infrastructure that saves player souls today**)

Alt coinages: *Simclusivity* · *I Sim Therefore I Am* · Ben's *Mister Rogers meets The Sims*

## Main idea

**Then:** Inclusivity was not a late patch — it was **playability + procedural rhetoric** from the substrate
(Motive.c, SimAntics, masking, Simulator Effect) through Patrick Barrett's implementation, community
tools (Family Album, Exchange, Transmogrifier), modding culture (Tanja Sihvonen), and twenty years
of society ↔ game co-evolution across gender, race, sexuality, and expression.

**Now:** Reported EA take-private (Saudi PIF, Kushner-linked Affinity Partners in press — verify before cite)
and Legacy Collection's Exchange removal make **exit infrastructure** the next inclusivity fight.
**Soul City** (namesake: McKissick's NC town, Nixon HUD backing, Helms foreclosure — see
[`soul-city-namesake.md`](../../../../repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/soul-city-namesake.md)) is the
git hub for soul-files; **Soul Saver** / **SoulAngel** relocates characters from oligarch-owned silos
into indie spokes — **Tiny Life** first — where share + story still ship. Procedural rhetoric as
**platform**, not one-off objects ([the thesis](soul-city-soul-saver-thesis.md)).
