## Thesis (for Repo Show / MOOLLM)

Tridge used Claude to clear rsync CVE backlog; regressions followed. Newbury argues the bugs are
**symptom** not cause — **informational density** and **maintenance power (Pmaint)** hit an asymptote.
LLMs subsidize Pmaint temporarily but cannot preserve **architectural intent**; tests verify behavior
not meaning.

**Gabriel Worse is Better** reframed: complexity is **conserved** — MIT Magic moves complexity to
maintainer; UNIX pushes to skilled operator (blacksmith).
