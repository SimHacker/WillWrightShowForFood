# PIXIE papers, films, and Buxton patent roadblock (2008–2023)

Chronology: Dave Fleck → Bill Buxton → David Chapman (Cambridge) → Brad Myers book → Don's 2023 letter.
Email receipts quoted from Don's archive (forwarded 16 Jul 2026).

---

## 2008 — Fleck asks Don; Don brings in Buxton; Buxton promises

**16 May 2008** — Dave Fleck (IEEE) → Don: researching pie menus, can't find the Wiseman
PIXIE 1969 paper anywhere ("I've tried IEEE and IEE and they don't seem to have it").

**17 May 2008** — Don forwards to **Bill Buxton** (Microsoft): "Do you have any idea where to
dig up a copy of Wiseman's PIXIE paper?" (Don's own copy in California storage — he'd just
moved to Amsterdam for TomTom.)

**17 May 2008** — Buxton replies to Don + Fleck:

> I have a copy of Wiseman's PIXIE paper stored in my files… In the meantime, **where I
> learned about Pixie was in Newman & Sproull, 1st edition**… William had an image of a Pixie
> radial menu in the interaction section, a brief overview, as well as the full reference.
> …Happy to scan it and make it available.

So Buxton's own PIXIE knowledge came the same route as Don's — the Newman & Sproull citation —
and he only went to Wiseman's actual papers in 2008.

**18 May 2008** — Don thanks him: "I wonder what other kind of stuff the authors of that paper
did, and what they've done since, having accomplished that work so long ago."

## July 2008 — Buxton's Cambridge visit; promises multiply; Don cut out

**7 Jul 2008** — Buxton email "PIXIE and Radial Menus" → **Kurtenbach, William Newman, Heinz
Lemke**, Cc Fleck + Nicholas Chen — **not to Don**, who had connected him to the request:

> 1. I have attached a transcription of the relevant parts of [the PIXIE paper]
> 2. I have attached notes on what I found in Wiseman's papers in the manuscript room at the
>    university (thanks to William Newman's help)
> 3. The most interesting thing that I found… is that **there was a film made of PIXIE**,
>    showing the user interface. I have started inquiries to see if we can find a copy… I have
>    offered that **Microsoft would pay to have a digital copy made** so that researchers could
>    have access to it.
> 4. I have made contact with **Heinz U. Lemke**… My hope is that he will be able to help.
>
> What I expect I will do is **merge this material into a web page, and link it to my home
> page**… I also plan to **integrate this into my book on input**.

**8 Jul 2008** — Fleck notices Don was left off and kindly forwards it to him. That's the only
reason Don got the transcription and notes at all.

**What Buxton actually delivered:** none of it, as far as Don can tell — no digitized film, no
web page, no book integration, despite Microsoft resources, travel allowance, and multimedia
services. Don's copies of the two attachments are now the public record:
[Wiseman Page 466](../../heinz-lemke/media/from-mail/paper-wiseman-page-466.pdf) ·
[Wiseman Notes on Radial Menus](../../heinz-lemke/media/from-mail/paper-wiseman-notes-on-radial-menus-in-pixie.pdf)

## 2019 — David Chapman (Cambridge University Library) delivers

Don pursued the film independently. Reference librarian **David Chapman** tracked the Rainbow
group copy, digitized **two PIXIE films** + the paper, and published them online. Don then cut
*Flight of the PIXIE* — the Bumblebee edit — because few people will watch unnarrated hour-long
digitized films; he condensed it into a story showing how fast radial menus were even on a
PDP-7. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrqR9XssJI

Don put more work into this than Buxton even promised, let alone did. Librarians rock.

Chapman ↔ Don correspondence also notes **William Newman** unwell in a care home (2019).

## 2023 — Don → Brad Myers (*Pick, Click, Flick!*)

Brad Myers CMU guest lecture 2019 → book Ch. 33 on Don; Don reviewing draft.

Don's thesis: **Bill Buxton** blocked pie menu adoption via marking-menu patents and FUD —
false claims about pie menus (no mouse-ahead, distance-trigger straw man) in the Kurtenbach
demo video and Alias licensing.

> His corporate funded Fear Uncertainty and Doubt marketing campaigns… misleading and
> bullying… 3D Studio Max and Blender away from pie menus.

> Buxton's broken promises… more than a decade of delay in those important papers and films
> finally seeing the light of day.

Straw-man video (Kurtenbach, "Demo of Marking Menus", posted 2008): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtH9GdFSQaw
— transcript + rebuttal: [`kurtenbach-marking-menu-demo-video-straw-man.md`](kurtenbach-marking-menu-demo-video-straw-man.md)
Alias marketing video for the patented marking menus ("smoking gun" — Don): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dKaXJ14BkjA

Don now friends with **Heinz Lemke** (PIXIE coauthor); wants CHM interview facilitated.

## Show policy — Buxton not invited

Per Don (16 Jul 2026): the PIXIE show and its discussion proceed **without inviting Bill
Buxton**. He was no help when it mattered, snubbed Don after Don connected him to the 2008
request and thanked him, and never delivered on the digitization, web page, or book promises.
The story is told from the receipts and from the people who did the work: Heinz, Chapman,
Fleck, Lars, Roy.
