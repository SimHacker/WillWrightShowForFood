# PIXIE papers, films, and Buxton patent roadblock (2008–2023)

Chronology: Dave Fleck → Bill Buxton → David Chapman (Cambridge) → Brad Myers book → Don's 2023 letter.

---

## 2008 — Fleck asks Don; Buxton promises scan

Dave Fleck (IEEE) seeks Wiseman PIXIE 1969 paper. Don forwards to **Bill Buxton** (Microsoft).

Buxton: has copy in storage; will scan; points to Newman & Sproull 1st ed.

**July 2008:** Buxton visits Cambridge manuscript room; transcribes paper; notes **film of PIXIE UI**; offers Microsoft pays digitization; contacts **Heinz Lemke** (coauthor).

Buxton sends notes to Fleck + Kurtenbach + Newman + Lemke — **not to Don** (despite promise). Fleck forwards to Don.

Don's copies: https://donhopkins.com/home/Wiseman%20Page%20466.pdf

---

## 2019 — David Chapman (Cambridge University Library)

Don pursued film independently. Reference librarian **David Chapman** tracks Rainbow group copy; digitizes **two PIXIE films** + paper; publishes online.

Don's Bumblebee highlight reel: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrqR9XssJI

Chapman ↔ Don correspondence also notes **William Newman** unwell in care home (2019).

---

## 2023 — Don → Brad Myers (*Pick, Click, Flick!*)

Brad Myers CMU guest lecture 2019 → book Ch. 33 on Don; Don reviewing draft.

Don's thesis: **Bill Buxton** blocked pie menu adoption via marking-menu patents and FUD — false claims about pie menus (no mouse-ahead, distance-trigger straw man) in Gordon Kurtenbach demo video and Alias licensing.

> His corporate funded Fear Uncertainty and Doubt marketing campaigns… misleading and bullying… 3D Studio Max and Blender away from pie menus.

> Buxton's broken promises… more than a decade of delay in those important papers and films finally seeing the light of day.

Straw-man video (Kurtenbach): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtH9GdFSQaw  
Alias 2000 Z-Ray: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dKaXJ14BkjA

Don now friends with **Heinz Lemke** (PIXIE coauthor); wants CHM interview facilitated.
