# Sims origin — Don Hopkins witness (1997)

**Don:** hired Jan 1997 for Dollhouse / The Sims — character animation, UI, tools, pipelines.  
**Press:** VentureBeat 2025-04-29 (Will compares Proxi pitch to pre-Sims Maxis).  
**Confirmed:** 2026-07-12 (Don, first person).

---

## Beats (on-air safe)

| Beat | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Architecture first** | Dollhouse started as an architecture game; *A Pattern Language* (e.g. church-pew seating) informed smart-object scoring of buildings |
| **People takeover** | Focus shifted — messing with people beat scoring buildings |
| **SimAnt substrate** | Pheromone trails → intelligence distributed in the **environment**; objects **advertise** to comparatively dumb Sims ("if you're hungry, come to me") |
| **Don's hire** | Team got a rec to hire someone for the game — Don started on **character animation** and **UI**, spread to tools and pipelines once green-lit |
| **Focus groups** | Mostly male panels said they wouldn't play it |
| **Mattrick due diligence** | Don Mattrick visited San Mateo before EA bought Maxis; saw prototypes; Don handed him **Scott McCloud's *Understanding Comics*** — "this explains everything" |
| **Mattrick quote** | Buy Maxis for **The Sims**, not SimCity — "oil and water" but mutual respect (Will, VB) |
| **EA** | Acquired Maxis **July 1997**, **$125M**; The Sims shipped **February 4, 2000** |
| **After ship** | Nearly empty office; dog-eared *Understanding Comics* on an artist's desk — well read, satisfied |

---

## Mattrick acquisition (what the beat means)

EA was buying **SimCity** brand and Maxis studio. Mattrick (EA studios head) walked into Dollhouse prototypes and realized the acquisition thesis had flipped: the bet was **the people toy**, not the city toy. EA paid **$125M** July 1997; Sims shipped Feb 2000 under EA marketing crunch while the San Mateo craft culture largely survived.

---

## Kaleida → GDC path (how Don got there)

Years before Maxis: Don met **Jim Mackraz** at Kaleida (ScriptX evangelism for Maxis). Don arrived late with **Coke in one hand, Pepsi in the other** — hedging bets — which blew Jim's mind. At **GDC**, Don ran into Jim, **Jamie Doornbos**, and **Eric Bowman**; hotel-room "software demos"; mutual tolerance of ambivalence (and Bistellar: loves both Star Trek and Star Wars).
