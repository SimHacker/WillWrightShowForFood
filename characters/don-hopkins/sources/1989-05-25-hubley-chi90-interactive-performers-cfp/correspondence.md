# Hubley ↔ Hopkins — CHI'90 Empowered correspondence (Aug–Sep 1989)

Verbatim forwarded mail from Don's archive. Proves Hubley's Usenet CFP was **about Don's CHI'88
demo specifically**, and documents the commission of the **Empowered** performance.

---

## 1989-08-25 — Hubley: Is this Don Hopkins?

**From:** Craig Hubley \<craig@gpu.utcs.utoronto.ca\>  
**To:** don@brillig.umd.edu  
**Date:** 25 August 1989, 19:12 GMT+2  
**Subject:** Is this Don Hopkins ?

If so, please reply. I've been trying to get ahold of you.

Craig Hubley  
CHI'90 Committee

---

## 1989-08-26 — Hubley: CHI'88 demo confirmed; repeat at CHI'90?

**From:** Craig Hubley \<craig@gpu.utcs.utoronto.ca\>  
**To:** don@brillig.umd.edu  
**Date:** 26 August 1989, 22:26 GMT+2  
**Subject:** Re: Is this Don Hopkins ?

Don,

OK, I've got Don Hopkins, now let's see if I've got the right one:

Did you give a demonstration of pie menus in action at CHI'88 ?  
If so, congratulations on one of the most involving demos I think I've seen.  
Did you ever get to trade your Reagan picture for one of Ed Meese ?

Anyway, are you planning to be at CHI'90 in Seattle ? If so, how would you feel about a
repeat performance in front of a larger audience ?

Craig Hubley

---

## 1989-08-29 — Don: Yes; PSIBER; will ask Shneiderman

**From:** Don Hopkins \<don@brillig.umd.edu\>  
**To:** craig@gpu.utcs.utoronto.ca  
**Cc:** don@brillig.umd.edu  
**Date:** 29 August 1989, 23:38 GMT+2  
**Subject:** Is this Don Hopkins ?

*(Replying to Hubley 1989-08-26)*

Yes, this is me. I'm really glad you liked the demo — I sure had a fun time doing it! The idea
of doing it again sounds interesting. I will talk to Ben Shneiderman to see about the prospects
of going to CHI'90. I am developing an interactive visual postscript programming environment,
which uses pie menus as part of the user interface. So I have a lot more neat interactive stuff
to demonstrate, now! I am working on a paper about it for the Usenix Monterey Graphics
conference. If you're interested, I can send you a description.

-Don

---

## 1989-08-30 — Hubley: send pictures; Interface Rodeo; manic narrative; wizard robe

**From:** Craig Hubley \<craig@gpu.utcs.utoronto.ca\>  
**To:** don@brillig.umd.edu  
**Date:** 30 August 1989, 20:34 GMT+2  
**Subject:** Re: Is this Don Hopkins ?

Don,

Please send me a description of your visual postscript programming stuff. You can send it via
email, or if you have fancy pictures, send them to:

Craig Hubley  
700 Spadina Avenue, Unit 2  
Toronto, Ontario  
Canada M5S 2J2

Mark the outside 'printed matter' to satisfy the customs bozos.

Since Ben was so hot on the idea of an 'Interface Rodeo' for CHI, and since the 'performance'
event I am chairing is the closest thing to it at CHI'90, he should be easy to convince. The
event is presently too heavy with interactive music stuff and too weak on real/useful/fun
interface stuff. I thought your CHI'88 demo was sufficiently theatrical to qualify, since I
wanted some 'practical window hacks' or whatever in the program, too. Could you whip up a sample
program of the gee-whizziest stuff you've got, and give it a manic running interactive narrative
as in '88? And if you have a long robe with funny planets and stars all over it... 1/2 :-)

Even the staidest interface styles (let's face it, desktops are getting staid) need to have some
life breathed into them once in a while. Although virtual reality is fun, it shouldn't get all the
attention, and good work is being done in visual programming languages that doesn't require
$100000 of gear, that can be just as exciting, to do *and* to watch.

Looking forward to seeing what you've got,

Craig Hubley  
CHI'90 Performance Chair

---

## 1989-09-05 — Hubley: PostScript inside-out; CyberSpace collapse

**From:** Craig Hubley \<craig@gpu.utcs.utoronto.ca\>  
**To:** don@brillig.umd.edu  
**Date:** 5 September 1989, 13:06 GMT+2  
**Subject:** Re: Is this Don Hopkins ?

Sounds wonderful, Don, do you mean to say you've actually turned PostScript inside-out and are
effectively hacking at it from the *inside* ? I mean, how else do you describe using NeWS
constructs to hack at NeWS internals while it's running ? Is it running while you do this ? What
happens if you change something vital in the interpreter ? Hmm.. could be fun to watch CyberSpace
collapse in on itself, hopefully not with anyone important inside...

I'd love to see some pictures of this in action.

Craig
