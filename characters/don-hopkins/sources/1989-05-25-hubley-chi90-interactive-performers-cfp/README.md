# Source: Craig Hubley — CHI'90 call for interactive performers (1989)

**Usenet:** comp.graphics · rec.arts.int-fiction · rec.games · **Date:** 25 May 1989  
**Mail thread:** Hubley ↔ Hopkins, Aug–Sep 1989 — [`correspondence.md`](correspondence.md)  
**Official CFP:** Steve Armstrong on NeWS-makers, 18 Jul 1989 — [`chi90-official-cfp-news-makers.md`](chi90-official-cfp-news-makers.md)  
**Conference:** ACM SIGCHI CHI'90 · Seattle · **April 1–5, 1990** · theme **"Empowering People"**

The casting call and personal commission that led to Don Hopkins's **"Empowered"** live
performance — pie menus, PSIBER Space Deck, Pseudo Scientific Visualizer, **mousee** trails.

## The receipt chain (confirmed)

Hubley's May Usenet post described an anonymous **pie menu virtuoso** (blindfolded, three layers
deep). In August he tracked Don down by email:

> Did you give a demonstration of pie menus in action at **CHI'88** ?  
> If so, congratulations on one of the most involving demos I think I've seen.

Don agreed. Hubley, as **CHI'90 Performance Chair**, asked for a repeat *"in front of a larger
audience"* — *"manic running interactive narrative as in '88"* — balancing an program *"too
heavy with interactive music stuff and too weak on real/useful/fun interface stuff."*

Don pitched the **interactive visual PostScript programming environment** (PSIBER). Hubley:
*"turned PostScript inside-out… hacking at NeWS internals while it's running"* — *"could be fun
to watch CyberSpace collapse in on itself."*

Conference theme **Empowering People** → show title **Empowered**.
