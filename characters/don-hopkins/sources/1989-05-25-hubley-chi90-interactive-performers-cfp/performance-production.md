# CHI'90 Empowered — production mail (Jan–Apr 1990)

Verbatim excerpts from Don's archived Hubley correspondence. Logistics, travel, Sun/Barco,
program copy, and the commission letters that locked the show.

See also: [`director-run-sheet.md`](director-run-sheet.md) (Hubley's Mar 1990 line-by-line
direction of the act).

---

## 1990-01-07 — Early commission ask (Hubley)

> Can you quickly describe what you would do in a 5- or 10-minute whiz-bang-gosh-what-was-that
> demo of your system? The **blindfolded choices from pie menus in '88 were really terrific** —
> as was the **altered Reagan photo**… what hardware does all this run on, and can it easily
> hook up to a video projector, and perhaps most importantly, can you bring it with you?

---

## 1990-01-18 — Performance contract (Hubley)

> I am perfectly willing to let you set up the most insane demo you can think of, and do it onstage,
> so long as you are willing to **encore the fabulous 'pie-menus blindfolded' part** of your
> demonstration from CHI'88 during the thing. This time, of course, we will give it an
> **appropriate drumroll and setting.**
>
> I am sure there is plenty of other amazing stuff you can do with the **Cyberspace deck**, and if
> you can pack dozens of things into ten minutes, you are my guest. I will not even ask what drugs
> you are on…
>
> Right now we anticipate having a full raft of AV equipment there, but if your stuff runs only on
> Suns we might have to find one and hook it up to the video. I don't think there are supposed to
> be any Suns onsite — **do you think Sun would go for lending us one?**

---

## 1990-01-19–20 — Travel; Don accepts (Hubley ↔ Don)

**Hubley:** Airfare the blocker? Registration free for performers; possible airfare support
(deep-discount, Saturday-night stay, student housing ~$22/night); volunteer + performer combo OK.

**Don (1990-01-20):** Airfare is basically yes — *greatly* appreciates help. Last semester student;
can volunteer (as at CHI'88). Will ask Sun for loaner Sun (they lent one for CHI'88). Mother's
**frequent flier miles**; friend in **Edmonds, WA** (Don lived there 1st–4th grade).

**Don (1990-01-20, to Ben):** Ben happy to have him perform but **find your own support** — time
sheets may yield cash; no UMD travel for undergrad invited on merit of CHI'88 demo + video.

---

## 1990-01-22 — Volunteer + expenses (Hubley)

> Contact **Larry Kuhn** (student volunteer chair). Send expense summary ASAP — airfare
> DC→Seattle, 5 nights lodging, etc. **Four-line blurb** for conditional Final Program inclusion.

**Don (1990-02-03):** Student volunteer application; interactive performance; frequent flier miles;
College Park address.

---

## 1990-03-15 — Show schedule + Sun loan (Don → Lin@Sun)

> The performance is called **"Empowered"**, and it runs **twice, on Tuesday, April 3, 7-8:30 and
> 9-10:30.**
>
> My stuff is all totally **NeWS** based, and runs on **OpenWindows**. The goal of the performance
> is to **knock as many socks off as possible!** The show lasts only **5 minutes**, so I'm going to
> try to do as many things as possible one right after the other and some even at the same time!

**Brent Browning (Sun):** CHI provides 4/60 + Barco for OPEN LOOK tutorial Mon 8–12; will bring
**SS-1** for Don's Empowered slot; coordinate with Craig Hubley; OWN 1.0.1 vs 2.0 beta issues.

---

## 1990-03-15–17 — Program blurb + pacing (Hubley)

**Hubley's program draft:**

> Don Hopkins — PSIBER space deck  
> "Gesture interfaces aren't just for music and dance. By pushing the limits of a mouse-ahead,
> pie-menu interface in a custom visual programming environment, Don can program in PostScript
> with a few quick flicks of the wrist. Cheer him on as he shows us all how it's done.
> **The desktop is not dead!**"

**Hubley (1990-03-17):** Needs Sun confirmed for final program. Barco vs Sony vs NTSC encoder
crisis — 90 MHz Sun refresh vs 67 MHz Barco limit. *"How many levels deep can you do? Or aren't you
telling?"*

---

## 1990-03-27–30 — Barco / NTSC saga

Hubley: pointing a camera at a Sun = strobe unless 60 Hz refresh; Barco rental ~$937/day busts
budget; **no NTSC encoder for Suns?** Sun should provide encoder or Barco.

Don → Gosling/Owen/Brent: *"synergistic combination of pie menus, mousee, psiber, and the pseudo
scientific visualizer!"*

**Lin Brown (Sun):** Sun may help pay for Barco shared between OPEN LOOK tutorial and Empowered;
fax commitment to Seattle Sheraton.

---

## 1990-04-10 — After the show (Hubley)

> Thanks Don! Hope you are safely back in reality once again.
