# CHI'90 official Call for Participation — NeWS-makers (Steve Armstrong, 1989-07-18)

**From:** Steve Armstrong, Bell-Northern Research (NeWS-makers list)  
**Date:** 18 July 1989  
**Note:** Electronic CFP includes **Interactive Performances** and **Interactive Exploratorium**
sections not in the printed Call. Theme: **"EMPOWERING PEOPLE"**.

Don Hopkins saved this alongside Hubley's performer search. Craig Hubley is contact for
Interactive Performances; proposal deadline **Tuesday, August 29, 1989**.

---

## Interactive Performances (excerpt)

> We are looking for innovative interface work that is best expressed in a dramatic, as opposed
> to academic, setting. This could be artistic, theoretical, or practical in nature, and might
> include music, video, or other adjuncts to the computer-human interaction. We are interested
> in, but not restricted to: performance enhancement and extension, interactive accompaniment,
> support for group interaction, theatrical demonstrations of practical systems, and virtual
> realities. Proposals will be evaluated on the basis of their originality, novel use of known
> ideas, novel application to new areas, interest to attendees, fun, feasibility and above all,
> their potential as performance events.
>

## Conference theme (excerpt)

> For CHI '90 the theme is **"Empowering People"** — how interactive systems empower individual
> and group creativity, productivity, and satisfaction while fostering innovation and cooperation.
