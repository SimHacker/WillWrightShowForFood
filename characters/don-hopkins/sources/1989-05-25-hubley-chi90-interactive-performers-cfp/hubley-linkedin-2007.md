# Craig Hubley — LinkedIn recommendation (2007)

Hubley's post-show characterization of Don's methodology, archived from LinkedIn notification
mail.

**Date:** September 2007  
**Context:** Don thanked Craig for CHI'90 organizing; Craig had endorsed Don's HCIL work.

---

> Don's software development and testing methodology is nothing short of **performance art**. I
> literally put him on stage in front of **most of the user interface designers in the world** in
> 1990, doing nothing more than his own standard demo of a visualization of the entire Internet.
> Having seen since then his development methodology firsthand, I realized that he can handle as
> many as **three workstations in play at once**, one merely as inspiration. I have met no person
> who so understands software as an art.
>
> It surprises me not at all that he went on to work on some of the world's most successful
> non-violent games. He's a peaceful and powerful soul.

**Don (2007-09-17 reply):**

> That was a truly amazing experience that I'll never forget, and I really appreciate your help
> organizing my thoughts, focusing my presentation, and giving me the confidence required to go
> up on stage and do all that risky stuff in real time. I would never have been able to pull it off
> without you. Every now and again I run into somebody who was in the audience and remember me from
> that show!

**CHI'92 CFP echo (Hubley, Dec 1991)** — quotes a 1990 performer:

> "I met more worthwhile people here than at SIGGRAPH. And that is amazing when you consider that
> SIGGRAPH is ten times the size."
