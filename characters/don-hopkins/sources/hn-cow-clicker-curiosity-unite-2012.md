# The Unite 2012 cow joke — Cow Clicker, Curiosity, and Molyneux

## The story

At Unity's Unite 2012 conference (Westergasfabriek, Amsterdam, August
2012), Peter Molyneux keynoted about *Curiosity: What's Inside the
Cube?* —
[keynote video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24AY4fJ66xA), his
segment starting at 1:08:21, with the crucifix-pose money quote at
1:48:06:

> Because we can dynamically put on ANY surface of the cube ANY image
> we like. So THAT's how we're going to surprise the world, is by
> giving clues about what's in the middle later on.

Afterward Don chatted him up and joked that the big secret inside the
cube was a **cow** — an ironic reference to
[Ian Bogost's Cow Clicker](../../ian-bogost/sources/cow-clicker.md).
Molyneux didn't get the joke. Don explained the Cow Clicker and
Zynga-satire context. Still didn't get it.

## Curiosity, for the record

[*Curiosity: What's Inside the Cube?*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curiosity:_What%27s_Inside_the_Cube%3F)
(22cans / Peter Molyneux, November 2012 – May 2013). Don's
characterization: the epitome of dopamine-addiction clickers — a
cop-out of game design, nothing original; arbitrary value becomes real
value. The prize (becoming "god of gods" in Godus) famously
under-delivered.
[Quartz caught the irony in 2012](https://qz.com/34024/life-really-is-a-game-with-a-lot-of-clicks-and-then-you-die):

> Just ask Ian Bogost, who created the satirical social game Cow
> Clicker that went on to such absurd popularity that he felt compelled
> to continue developing it, trapping himself in an ironic loop that
> refuses to end.
