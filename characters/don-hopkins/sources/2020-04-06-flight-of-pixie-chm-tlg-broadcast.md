## Flight of the PIXIE

Don published the Yuja Wang edit of Cambridge's 1969 PIXIE films (PDP-7, Type 340, light pen — first known pie menus):

- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrqR9XssJI
- Paper: [PIXIE — Wiseman, Lemke, Hiles (1969 CAD Conference)](https://www.donhopkins.com/home/documents/PIXIE%20a%20new%20approach%20to%20man-machine%20communication.pdf)
- Cambridge archives: https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/library/archives.html
- Pie menu retrospective: https://medium.com/@donhopkins/pie-menus-936fed383ff1
- Yuja Wang audio source: https://archive.org/details/FlightOfTheBumblebeeChaameh/02+Yuja+Wang+-+Piano.mp4

Don thanked **David Chapman** (Cambridge University Library) for digitizing the films. Pitched **Marc Weber / CHM** oral history for **Heinz Lemke** (operator in the film; PIXIE programmer) — recommendation credited to John Gilmore.

## CHM 1996 interview — discovery

Searching YouTube for Marc Weber, Don found:

**Tim Pozar and Brewster Kahle — CHM interview (29 Oct 1996)**  
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u2h2LHRFbNA

Don's summary to the TLG circle:

- Tim: SF's first ISP; couldn't legally "resell bandwidth" but did anyway; kicked off UUNET.
- Brewster: Alexa and Internet Archive.
- **John Gilmore** and **Tom Jennings** named in the conversation.

Don transcribed Brewster's **early dreams** passage at ~45:35 — see [`../../brewster-kahle/sources/chm-1996-early-dreams-clip.md`](../../brewster-kahle/sources/chm-1996-early-dreams-clip.md).
