## Engelbart mouse collector orbit

**Dec 2022** — Facebook [Internet Old Farts Club](https://www.facebook.com/groups/internetoldfarts/posts/681963806784174) thread on old mice. Maynard posted his **50-year-old** SRI-era mouse (leather saddle-stitched look, "A-MP" marking).

**Maynard:** had a **chord keyset**, loved it, **donated to Harold C. Hohbach History of Science & Technology collection at Stanford** — still misses it. Keyset page: https://www.software-artist.com/keyset/
