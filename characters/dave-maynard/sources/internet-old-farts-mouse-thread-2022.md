## David Maynard (public thread)

Posted photo of his **50-year-old mouse** — leather saddle-stitch, "A-MP" marking.

**Don Hopkins:** *"David Maynard, I have one of those, from a friend of mine who worked at SRI keeping Douglas Engelbart's PDP-10 running. Do you have the chord keyboard that goes with it?"*

**Maynard:**

> I had a keyset but I donated it to the Harold C. Hohbach History of Science & Technology collection at Stanford. I loved the keyset and still miss it.

> https://www.software-artist.com/keyset/

Don posted photos of his set (living room table, Netherlands unpack) and shared Valerie Landau / HN Engelbart links.
