## Who

[**University of British Columbia**](https://www.ubc.ca/) (`ksbooth@cs.ubc.ca`),
working in **human–computer interaction** and **computer graphics**.

He enters this project on **9 July 2026** the way many good people do here: someone

[PIXIE storyline thread](../heinz-lemke/sources/2026-07-09-pixie-storyline-thread.md),
naming him *"a friend and mentor … who taught me and inspired my storytelling
nature in HCI lectures."*
