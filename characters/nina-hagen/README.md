## Who

**Nina Hagen** (b. 1955, East Berlin) is the **"Godmother of Punk"** — a classically-trained singer who
turned a multi-octave operatic voice into confrontational punk/new-wave theatre. Stepdaughter of the
dissident songwriter **Wolf Biermann**, she left East Germany in 1976 after his expulsion, and broke
through in the West with the **Nina Hagen Band** (1978): "TV-Glotzer (White Punks on Dope)," "African
Reggae," "Naturträne." She has spent a career being fearless in public — operatic, theatrical, and
politically outspoken.

## Why a dream guest here

Hagen is a living emblem of the show's counterculture thesis: **get the true, taboo thing on the air —
and grin.** On Austria's late-night discussion show **Club 2 (9 August 1979)**, during a debate about
sexuality, she **demonstrated female masturbation positions live on air** to make the point that women
should understand and own their bodies. It scandalized broadcast TV and is now remembered as a landmark
of anti-censorship performance — a feminist bodily-autonomy argument pushed straight through the gate.

She pairs naturally with the rest of Don's avant-garde pantheon — **Bowie, Klaus Nomi, Joey Arias,
Leigh Bowery, Laurie Anderson** — and is an **operatic-punk sibling to Klaus Nomi** (both classically
trained, both weaponizing opera for the avant-garde). She's cited as the twin emblem to **Bowie's video
puppet** in the show's medium framing ("The emblem: Bowie's video puppet" — see
[`VISION.md`](../../process/VISION.md)).

She is one of **Don's long-time avant-garde north stars.**
