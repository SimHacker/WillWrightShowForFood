## How to watch

**We do not embed YouTube players in this file.** GitHub strips `<iframe>` embeds from repo markdown,
and a page full of inline players fights you for screen space anyway.

Every clip below is a **link that opens YouTube in your browser**. Middle-click or ⌘/Ctrl-click for a
new tab — that is the intended experience: read a paragraph here, watch there, come back. Some entries
include a **thumbnail** you can click; it goes to the same place.

Conversation hooks and sourcing live in [`ideas.md`](ideas.md). The public invitation is
[`invitation.md`](invitation.md).

## 2. Drag, Trannyshack, and "Born in Xixax"

That Trannyshack clip is from one of the most punk underground drag nights San Francisco ever had.
[Heklina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heklina) founded it in 1996; "tranny" was reclaimed
insider-style — like "queer" — among drag queens and their friends. Trans women were part of the scene
from the start.

Years later the word's weight changed. Heklina **chose** to retire the name — not censorship. Her own
words: [SFist interview, 2014](https://sfist.com/2014/05/23/exclusive_sfist_talks_with_heklina/).
The party became **Mother**; the venue **Oasis**. Punk reclaiming when it works; graceful retirement
when the word hurts.

On Nina's side of the stage: male persona, cosmic mythology — one place Don has sometimes thought
"double drag" (opera femme *and* male character) and then thought *better ask than label*:

[![Nina Hagen — Born in Xixax](https://img.youtube.com/vi/wOckSjlZTsY/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wOckSjlZTsY)

## 3. The Gorbachev rap — Poland to Biermann to mother to daughter

Wolf Biermann wrote **"Michail, Michail"** for Nina's mother Eva-Maria Hagen — a send-up laced with
Russian folk-song passages; Eva-Maria's own CD notes call it *blackest scepticism and childlike hope*.
Nina cut her mother's song as a **rap** in 1989, the year the Wall came down, and gave it an edge the
gentle version never had.

[![Nina Hagen — Michail, Michail (Gorbachev Rap), 1989](https://img.youtube.com/vi/7e1suuoTs2Q/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7e1suuoTs2Q)

The trail may start earlier: the Russian refrain echoes Andrzej Rosiewicz singing **to Gorbachev's face**
in Poland, 1988 — a pro-freedom wink smuggled past the censors (note the upside-down star on his cap):

[![Andrzej Rosiewicz — Wieje wiosna ze wschodu](https://img.youtube.com/vi/XM2FSf5IQa8/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XM2FSf5IQa8)

Poland → Biermann → Eva-Maria → Nina. Cabaret → chanson → rap.

## 4. Hearing your songs through someone else's ears (Eno ← Doug)

In 2003 Doug Hilsinger and Caroleen Beatty covered Brian Eno's entire *Taking Tiger Mountain (By
Strategy)* — by hand, for themselves — and handed Eno a CDR at a San Francisco lecture. He **phoned**
Doug. Then wrote: *"It's strange and enlightening hearing it through your ears… When I heard your
versions I thought, 'Gosh, they were music after all!' It was a bit like that fairy tale: 'I am a
swan!'"*

Don met Doug tending bar at The Eagle in 2006, fell in love with the record before he knew it was Doug's,
and got the CD from the source. That is the gift a great cover gives the original artist — and Nina has
stood at **both** ends of it (see section 1).

- [The full Tiger Mountain story + Eno's phone message](../doug-hilsinger/taking-tiger-mountain-by-serendipity.md)
- [All ten tracks on YouTube](../doug-hilsinger/youtube-tracks.md)
- [Title track — Taking Tiger Mountain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i-cI1o_wBPc)

## 5. Amsterdam — Herman Brood, *Cha Cha*, Ruigoord

1979: Herman Brood, Nina, Lene Lovich, Les Chappell — the *Cha Cha* soundtrack and a **filmed** wedding
at the Sint-Gertrudiskerk in [Ruigoord](https://ruigoord.nl/) (Simon Vinkenoog officiates on camera). Real romance during the
shoot; **never a legal marriage** — Brood staged the scene for tabloid publicity, and manager Koos van
Dijk confirmed after Herman's death they were married *in the film only*. Van Dijk started civil paperwork
in Zwolle, then deliberately slowed it; Herman moved on before anything was registered. Don still walks
that Amsterdam/[Ruigoord](https://ruigoord.nl/) history locally.

[![Herman Brood, Nina Hagen & Lene Lovich — Crocodile](https://img.youtube.com/vi/xFIjlxl-DLI/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xFIjlxl-DLI)

Open question for Nina on air: did Herman ever **paint** her? Don has a lead that a Brood portrait
exists in a private collection — even a photograph would do.

## 6. The Rickles night — Habanera, gunshot, vegetables

Merv Griffin, 1985. Nina sings the Habanera from *Carmen* in a white wig, ends with a gunshot, and stays
so unflappable she gets **Don Rickles** — Don Rickles! — singing along about not eating animals. Clip
preserved by her bassist **Karl Rucker** (NYC roommate of eight years; RIP 2016):

[![Nina Hagen & Don Rickles on Merv Griffin, 1985](https://img.youtube.com/vi/lagkoVUVPhc/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lagkoVUVPhc)

Her animal-rights anthem *Don't Kill the Animals* (1986 duet with Lene Lovich for PETA) ties the Rickles
thread to the Lovich thread. Same Karl Rucker on bass in the next section.

## 8. Voice as instrument — toy tract, then the real thing

Opera trained, punk deployed. The show runs **vocal-tract toys** alongside real voices — not voice
clones, instruments:

- [Pink Trombone](https://dood.al/pinktrombone/) — Neil Thapen's playable model of the human vocal tract
- [Pink Trombone jam](../../apps/performance-space/pink-trombone-jam.md) — group sing-along activity
- [Zack Qattan — Pink Trombone demos walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B74tKZDVNS8) — taught
  it to listen and sing along

Pink Trombone is a **cartoon** of what Nina does for real. Hold everything else against this:

[![Nina Hagen — Naturträne (Rockpalast, 1979)](https://img.youtube.com/vi/9xi4O4RvlnQ/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9xi4O4RvlnQ)

Ten million views and counting. Opera head voice, whisper, scream, whistle-register seagull tones, chicken
noises at the end — and by the solo she is **singing the same note as the guitar** until you cannot tell
which is which.

Vocal coach [Beth Roars breaks down why](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0baOxEPR7nI) — *"she is not
afraid to shape her vocal tract,"* *"release rather than control,"* *"if you ever wonder what is the
right or wrong way to sing, remember this exists."*

We drag a tongue across a screen and a voice comes out. She **is** the instrument.

Sibling lineage: [Laurie Anderson's Talking Stick](../laurie-anderson/ideas.md) (granular MIDI baton,
[Interval Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interval_Research), 1999; earlier [STEIM](https://steim.org/) 1992 —
[Laurie's *Moby Dick* program notes](https://www.jimdavies.org/laurie-anderson/performances/mobydick/programnotes.html) ·
[Miya Masaoka interview, Oct 1999](https://miyamasaoka.com/writings-by-miya-masaoka/1999/examining-obsession/))
· [Max / NATO / Jitter](../laurie-anderson/ideas.md#8-steim-maxmsp-and-the-natojitter-scene) · [AntiORP](../netochka-nezvanova/README.md)
· [Musical Gas](../don-hopkins/musical-gas-granular-ca-synth.md) · Klaus Nomi.

## 9. Club 2 — education, not provocation

August 9, 1979. Austrian late-night TV. Nina demonstrates female masturbation on live broadcast to argue
women should understand and own their bodies. Scandal; landmark; moderator lost his job. Nina's stance:
**Aufklärung, keine Provokation** — enlightenment, not provocation.

Watch the full broadcast when you have an hour and a strong nervous system:

[![Club 2, ORF — full broadcast, 9 August 1979](https://img.youtube.com/vi/CXp99IrKIF4/hqdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXp99IrKIF4)

Key excerpt with subtitles also circulates:
[clip with French subs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xtQh6Sb9urs)

The Repo Show framing: live, unscripted, uncensored — you can do anything you think the world still
hasn't understood, and **nobody loses their job afterward**. We don't have a moderator to fire.
