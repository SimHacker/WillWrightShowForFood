## Headline quotes (derStandard / APA, Mar 2026)

On Jesus and punk:

> "Jesus war einer der Ur-Punks überhaupt."

> "Er hat sich immer für Gerechtigkeit eingesetzt." Authorities did not impress him; he stood for charity. Not caring much about authority suits Nina too.

On church vs faith:

> Protestant baptism **2009**; professing Christian; separates **personal faith** from **worldly institution**.

> "Ich bin sozusagen selbst eine Kirche."

On the Catholic Church:

> "Gruselverein" — creepy club / horror show.

> Abuse cases, canonizations, relics ("abgeschnittene Fingernägel von Pseudoheiligen" in monstrances) disturb her.

> Membership decline: people may still trust God but want to send a message to the institution.

On identity labels:

> Finds "Godmother of Punk" inaccurate; prefers **"Nina Hagen, the one and only."** Was inspired by London punks.

Biographical (interview):

- Formative **LSD** experience: agony → turned to God → vision of Jesus "as a handsome man who looked at her with love" — describes as "like a baptism Jesus himself performed."
- Decades of mistakes; says God repeatedly pulled her out.
- Long quit smoking and marijuana; lives in **Hamburg**; often unrecognized on the street.
- **"Du hast den Farbfilm vergessen"** — Merkel requested it for her farewell (noted in piece).

## Producer — Charles Normal (not Chuck Normann)

**Disambiguation:** This is **[Charles Normal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Normal)** (Larry Norman's brother; punk/gospel producer, Magnetic Real studio, Salem OR) — **not** Charles **Normann** (EA licensing / SimCity OLPC in Don's mail archive).

Charles Normal lineage (public):

- Larry Norman introduced teenage Charles to **Sex Pistols** / L.A. punk; Charles led Bay Area hardcore band **Executioner** (1982).
- Long collaboration: *Stranded in Babylon*, *Tourniquet*, Solid Rock Records orbit.
- Nina's 2023 **Nina Hagen sings Larry Norman** single includes **Rock The Flock** — explicit Larry Norman tribute before the 2026 gospel push.

Show hook: **punk → Christian rock pioneer → punk guitarist brother → Nina Hagen gospel** — a cover-chain across *spiritual* reappropriation, not just Tubes → TV-Glotzer.
