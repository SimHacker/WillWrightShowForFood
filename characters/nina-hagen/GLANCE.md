🎤⚡ **Nina Hagen** *(dream guest)* — the **"Godmother of Punk"** (b. 1955, East Berlin). A classically-trained, multi-octave voice turned into punk/new-wave theatre; stepdaughter of dissident **Wolf Biermann**, she left the GDR in 1976 and broke through with the **Nina Hagen Band** (1978). Fearless anti-censorship provocateur: on Austria's **Club 2 (1979)** she **demonstrated female masturbation live on air** to argue women should own their bodies — scandal, then landmark. Operatic-punk sibling to Klaus Nomi; one of **Don's avant-garde north stars**, and an emblem of the show's *get-the-true-thing-on-the-air-and-grin* ethos.
