## Who

**Yuja Wang** is a concert pianist whose virtuosity and appetite for cross-media work made her
*Flight of the Bumblebee* the perfect metronome for a piece of computer-history filmmaking.

Born in Beijing (1987), trained at Curtis, signed to Deutsche Grammophon, she is one of the
most visible classical performers of her generation — and unusually open to **technology,
immersive staging, and VR** (including LA Phil's *The Rite of Spring* VR). That spirit matches
what the 1969 **PIXIE** films were doing on a PDP-7: making an interface *perform*.

## The performance we cite

Don Hopkins's *[Flight of the PIXIE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jDrqR9XssJI)* (March 2020)
intercuts David Chapman's digitized **1969 PIXIE demonstration films** with Yuja Wang playing
Rimsky-Korsakov's *Flight of the Bumblebee* — **György Cziffra's arrangement**, from her
celebrated **Verbier Festival** era (featured on the festival's highlights DVD, 2008).

The edit is a **non-commercial archival homage**. Don credits Wang in the YouTube dedication
block alongside Neil E. Wiseman, Heinz U. Lemke, and John O. Hiles (PIXIE, 1969 CAD
Conference, Southampton), and David Chapman (Cambridge University Library digitization).

**Idea:** PIXIE's radial menus move like a bumblebee — fast, precise, impossible to ignore.
Yuja's hands supply the tempo.
