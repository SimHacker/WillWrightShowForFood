# Design by Accretion — with Chris Trottier 🪶 ★

**Flagship Repo Show seed** · solo · host Don Hopkins · guest Chris Trottier · consent not yet asked

> *Building a design by layering, accumulating, and letting it grow — not top-down specifying it.*

## Why this show

Chris gave a great interview on **design by accretion** and **tuned emergence**. That philosophy maps onto this whole project — and almost exactly describes the **cauldron** method: melt many ideas into one growing monolith, let them accrete and brew, then ladle out the parts. Accretion *is* the Phase-1 brewing discipline.

**The meta payoff:** we can *discuss* design-by-accretion *and* **demonstrate it live** — run the cauldron MELT/STIR on the very ideas from the conversation, then ladle them into a MOOLLM skill. The method is the message.

## What everyone gets

| Audience | Payoff |
