# Leaving Maxis — career memories (Jan 2007)

**Chris Trottier** · 10 January 2007 · *career-safe digest*

On leaving Maxis after nine years, Chris sent a wide farewell email — a memoir organized by project. This page extracts the **career and studio-culture** beats for the Repo Show archive. Personal lines from the original are omitted here; the full text lives in Don Hopkins's private archive.

## Arc

Nine years at Maxis — longer than high school and college combined. Hired as an Orinda intern (Claire Curtin, sight-unseen); moved into design (Luc Barthelet); grew through **Sim Safari**, **The Sims**, **The Sims Online** (producer), and early **Spore**. Left January 2007.

**Thanks she named:** Claire Curtin, Kana (Steven) Pettus, Luc Barthelet, Lucy Bradshaw, Will Wright.

## Orinda intern era

- Intern-era hallway folklore about eccentric exec behavior — the kind of stories that make a young studio feel mythic.
- Outdoor meetings at Theatre Square; fountains as meeting quality control.
- First E3: built up as the greatest social event ever; arrival anticlimax.
- The famous tennis-bracelet expense report.
- Finding a SimTower crash bug on final night — pride, then learning you can ship with such things.

## Sim Safari

- Three weeks in, told the kids' product line was cancelled — "the job where I was going to change the lives of children."

## The Sims

- First day in San Mateo: Jeff Mackraz's rapid-fire "how we work" speech — catching one word in twenty.
- Second day: producers cut out pictures of cute outfits.
- Third day: Kana Pettus takes the wheel with Jim — hold on.
- VOX sessions inventing **Simlish** — Claire Curtin and Robi holding consonant cards for actors.
- Charles London animation act-outs "playing to the back of the house."
- Unexpected goodies buried in the Dollhouse couch.
- The toilet object: unprecedented development time and energy for a single Sims object.
- First person publicly yelled at by Will — brief street cred.
- Diagonal walls and ghosts — the eternal design meeting topics.
- Focus group with hardcore gamers: "how long to get married?" — stone faces.
- **Sleeper hit at E3** — adrenaline, blisters, voicelessness.

## The Sims Online

- Producer alter ego **Yoyo Mama**.
- In-game flirtation with Mother Theresa → rushed wedding → turned out to be Will. Wrong on many levels.
- Gordon's first team meeting (paraphrased): "I'm a lovable asshole. You're going to be glad you have an asshole at the helm."
- Synchronous peeing as perennial player gag.
- At a black-tie event honoring Will: Will, sauced, cornering executives — launching TSO as-is would be a "huge mistake."
- Pinata-gate, in-game mafias, every player segment mad six ways from Sunday — producer life.
- Post-launch: kicked out of a TSO house for bad mini-game skills and unfamiliar acronyms.
- The walk-down-the-hall-while-talking-seriously-with-Bob-Simon thing.

## Spore (early team)

- First lunch: "As long as all creatures have sneakers and jetpacks, I'm good." Team exchange glances — long onboarding ahead.
- Second lunch: world history and natural phenomena every day?
- Designated **village idiot**: if Chris can use the interface, anyone can.
- Will gives her a 20-sided die for Checker's role-playing night.
- Design offsite at Stinson Beach — terraforming with birds overhead.
- Guillaume emerging as a fireball tuner — "keep your eye on this one."

## Why it matters here

Chris is Don's anchor for **design by accretion** — layer upon layer until a microworld lives. This farewell is a primary-source map of where those layers came from: intern myth, Simlish invention, TSO community storms, Spore usability testing by the person who'd never pretend to be an expert.
