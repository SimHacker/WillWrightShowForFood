# What is a Repo Show? — and Chris’s place in it 🪶

> *So you have a Repo to Show us?*

## The idea

A **Repo Show** is a show whose **center of gravity is a GitHub repo** — not just an mp3 or mp4. When we announce a show, the repo opens first: questions, artifacts, papers, code, links to other repos, videos, podcasts. The audience can participate and learn *before* the live conversation, follow along in real time or time-shifted afterward, and keep contributing forever after.

**Will Wright** has agreed (enthusiastically!) to the first show and more afterward. The series spans reunions, tributes to people we’ve lost, and guests who knew them — their work and ideas living in the repo.

## Time shifting on purpose

Guests and audiences live in different time zones and busy lives. So we embrace:

- **Solo videos** — you record when it suits
- **One-on-one chats** — edited together
- **Multi-person discussions** — when schedules align
- **Live Twitch** — holy grail: play, develop, discuss in public

GitHub supplies the community tools: PRs, issues, forks, curation. The repo is the stage *and* the artifact.

## How to participate (TicketPR)

Your GitHub account is a passport. Submit a **TicketPR**: create your subdirectory under a show’s audience folder, describe yourself (costume optional — Price Is Right energy welcome), and list your questions. Guests can read them in advance; we edit answers into the record afterward.

*(We’ll also make a video for Will — and anyone — on setting up GitHub and Cursor.)*

## Video playlist model

We collect short and long videos — new and existing — link them, cut excerpts, mix into shows. Guests prime the pump: talk about your work, screen recordings, whatever you like. Viewers pause the interview and watch your clip. Before and after the show, the **YouTube playlist keeps growing** as questions and clips accumulate. When the dust settles, one nicely produced cut combines the shorter pieces.

**Chris Trottier’s “Design by Accretion” interview** is the reference example for this model.
