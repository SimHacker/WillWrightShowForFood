## What is a Repo Show?

The center of gravity is a **GitHub repo**, not just audio or video. I announce a show and open the
repo: questions, artifacts, code, papers, links, videos. People participate and learn *before* the
conversation, follow along live or time-shifted, and keep contributing afterward. The repo is a living
artifact.

**Will Wright** has agreed enthusiastically to the first show (and more). I hope for a whole series —
with him, with other guests, and with tributes to amazing people we've lost, talking with those who
knew them and showing their work in the repo.

Because guests and audiences are busy and scattered in time zones, we embrace **time shifting**:
solo videos when you like, one-on-one chats edited together, multi-person discussions when we can,
live Twitch when we're lucky. GitHub has the community tools.

## Why you

You gave a great interview on **design by accretion** and **tuned emergence** — building a Sims-scale
design layer by layer until it lives. That maps perfectly onto what we're doing here, and onto the
**cauldron** method: melt ideas, let them accrete, ladle out working parts. I'd love a show where we
*discuss* accretion and *demonstrate* it live on the ideas from our conversation.

The Sims is the perfect case study, and you were right in the middle of it.
