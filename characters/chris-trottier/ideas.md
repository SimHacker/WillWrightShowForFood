## What Chris has done

Chris Trottier was Lead Designer at Maxis (1997–2008) — The Sims, Sims Online (producer; Yoyo Mama), early Spore. Don associates her with **design by accretion**: layer upon layer until a microworld lives. Her [Jan 2007 career map](sources/2007-01-10-leaving-maxis/career-memories.md) names the accretion points project by project.
