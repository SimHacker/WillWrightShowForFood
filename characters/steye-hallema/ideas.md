## Hooks

### 1. PIPS:lab split / rhyme
What transferred from PIPS:lab comedy-music-theatre-film into Smartphone Orchestra and cinematic VR? What stayed Keez’s lane (Lumasol / Die Space / Anyways)?

### 2. Smartphone Orchestra as Repo Show cousin
Phones already in pockets = the instrument. Parallel to repo-as-stage: the audience’s own devices are the runtime. *Ancestors* / *The Score* as live demos of participation at scale.

### 3. *Ashes to Ashes* → *Imaginary Friend* → *Ancestors*
POV experiments: urn, intimate friend, collective future-babies. How does “suspension of disbelief” work when the user *is* in the scene ([FilmForward Vrijplaats](https://filmforward.nl/deelnemer/steye-hallema/) framing)?

### 4. Jaunt year (with Arthur / Don)
Public only: creative director EMEA during cinematic VR boom; *Ashes to Ashes* with Submarine / WeMakeVR / Jaunt. Optional three-way with [`../arthur-van-hoff/`](../arthur-van-hoff/) — HyperLook→Jaunt CTO vs PIPS→Jaunt CD.

### 5. Volumetric + 4DR
*Imaginary Friend* partially captured at 4DR Eindhoven ([Playgrounds](https://weareplaygrounds.nl/slot/the-art-department-eindhoven-2024/steye-hallema/)). Mash with Keez’s Gaussian-splat / paint-cam ideas in [`../keez-duyves/ideas.md`](../keez-duyves/ideas.md).

### 6. Invite path
Warm via Keez first. Don’t cold-pitch Rebounce/Jaunt internals. Offer: Amsterdam XR / participation episode, or guest on Keez Die Space show.
