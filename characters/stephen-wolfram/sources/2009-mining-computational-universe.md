## Same title, later transcript (2019 Edge)

Not the Azores talk — same mining thesis, full prose:

| Asset | URL |
|-------|-----|
| Edge conversation | [Mining the Computational Universe (5.30.19)](https://www.edge.org/conversation/stephen_wolfram-mining-the-computational-universe) |
| EdgeCast audio | [edgecast.fireside.fm/540](https://edgecast.fireside.fm/540) |

**Core claim (Edge):** sophisticated computation is cheap/ubiquitous (Principle of Computational Equivalence). Engineering by stepwise foresight is one mode; **mining** the computational universe for short rules that do clever things is another — humbling, often hard to understand after you find them. Hard problem is naming *what you want*; doing may automate, wanting does not.
