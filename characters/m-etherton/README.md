## Who

**M. Etherton** — a co-author of the **RAINBOW** multi-purpose CAD-system paper
(Wiseman, Cheney, Etherton, Hiles, Lemke, *Software — Practice and Experience*,
1972) from the **Cambridge Mathematical Laboratory**. That byline is, so far, the
only firm fact. Even the given name (just "M.") is unconfirmed.

## Why he's here

Roy Eagleson listed "Etherton" among the Cambridge/TRE storyline people, and the
RAINBOW paper confirms an **M. Etherton** in exactly that group and era. This room
is an honest placeholder — the person is real and documented, the details await
**Heinz Lemke** and the archives.
