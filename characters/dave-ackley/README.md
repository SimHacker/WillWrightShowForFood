# Dave Ackley 🧱🦋

> *"A crashed system is infinitely wrong."* — robust-first computing.

**Dave Ackley** (Prof. Emeritus, University of New Mexico) is the patron saint of **robust-first
computing**: the argument that **survival comes before correctness and efficiency**. He builds
**indefinitely scalable** architecture — the **[Movable Feast Machine](https://movablefeastmachine.org/)**
(a spatial substrate where programs are living, self-repairing patterns) and the **[T2 Tile
Project](https://t2tile.com/)** (physical tiles you bolt together edge-to-edge, no global clock, no
single point of failure). Long before that, he co-authored the classic **Boltzmann machine** paper
(Ackley, Hinton & Sejnowski, 1985).

Don has written about his work **extensively on Hacker News**, and his mantra powers the
**robust-first** ambient skill in Don's MOOLLM.

## Why a Repo Show

- **Robust-first computing** — survival > correctness > efficiency; a different value system for machines.
- **Movable Feast Machine, live** — cellular-automata-descended code that heals, diffuses, reproduces.
- **T2 tiles** — indefinitely scalable hardware with only local, asynchronous communication.
- **CA looping fest** — a natural co-guest with [Norman Margolus](../norman-margolus/README.md)
  (locality/reversibility) and [Stephen Wolfram](../stephen-wolfram/README.md): CA as *architecture*.
