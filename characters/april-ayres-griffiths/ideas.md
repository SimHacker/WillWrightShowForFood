# Hooks — April and Melody Ayres-Griffiths

Possible threads, if they want a conversation. Theirs to change or drop.

1. **Upcycling.** What an emulator owes the person using it, beyond running the code. Which
   microM8 feature changed how people use an Apple ][ most.
2. **Live rewind and the reverse debugger.** How the memory recording works, what it costs, and
   what people do with it that nobody expected.
3. **microPAK and the cabinet's cartridge.** Two answers to "what is a program, as it reaches a
   user?" Could the two formats translate, and should they?
4. **turtleSpaces.** Bringing Logo forward without losing Apple Logo II compatibility; teaching
   with it; publishing and remixing; using Claude to explain errors and write Turtorials.
5. **The MCP server.** An AI driving an Apple ][: what it's good at, and what surprised them.
6. **The federation demo.** Forth on an emulated PDP-7 builds a PIXIE ring, tiny-titan translates
   it to Logo, and microM8's 3D turtle draws it in the next window
   ([TINY-TITAN.md](../../packages/cabinet/TINY-TITAN.md#first-demo-pixie-rings-to-microm8s-3d-turtle)).
   turtleSpaces already listens on port 1967 (`api`); would they want the cabinet's ITS turtle
   stream to drive it, and a link back to turtleSpaces wherever it does?
7. **Making a living from it.** microM8 is free and now MIT; turtleSpaces is the product. How the
   two fit together, and how the rest of us can send them users.
8. **Paleotronic Magazine.** Interviews with Doug Carlston, Rob Fulop and 4am; what makes popular
   computing history work.
9. **Logo Adventure.** Paleotronic ported Don's 1980s C64 Terrapin Logo game to Apple Logo in 2018,
   while Alexey Slyusar ported it to LLOGO on ITS. Play both side by side.
