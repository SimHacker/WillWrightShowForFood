# Tod Machover 🎼🧠 *(Opera of the Future; Brain Opera; hyperinstruments)*

*Invitation portrayal — not Tod Machover.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · authored by Don Hopkins

## Who

Composer-inventor; MIT Media Lab **Opera of the Future**; hyperinstruments (incl.
hypercello for Yo-Yo Ma); **Brain Opera** (1996, after Minsky's *Society of Mind*);
*Death and the Powers* (Pulitzer finalist); Hyperscore; teacher of Harmonix founders
(Guitar Hero / Rock Band lineage); City Symphonies.

## Why invited

Society of Mind as a building full of audience-played instruments. Premorializing
opera (*Death and the Powers*) rhymes with Die Space / RAI threads. Music-machines
ensemble with Levitt, Kurzweil, Jerry Martin.
