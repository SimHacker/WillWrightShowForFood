## What Ted has done

Ted Nelson (b. 1937) — coined hypertext and hypermedia, founded Project Xanadu, and wrote Computer Lib / Dream Machines (1974). Champion of transclusion, visible parallel connections (Xanalinks vs jump-only weblinks), and intertwingularity — 'everything is deeply intertwingled.' A direct inspiration for Don's HyperTIES work at Maryland and a north star for the Repo Show.
