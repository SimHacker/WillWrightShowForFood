## Ted on "self revealing" (Media Lab video, transcribed by Don)

Ted rejects "intuitive"; prefers **self revealing** — Pac-Man, mouse, clean interfaces:

> *"The term 'intuitive' is stupid… the moment you see it work, it has revealed itself. So it's 'self revealing'."*

Attributed to **Klaus Landsberg** (misheard spelling).

## Ted's reply (29 Jan 2020)

Not Wikipedia's Klaus Landsberg. Ted's supervisor at **Datapoint 1981**:

**Klavs Landberg** (Denmark) — wrote an OS for **Datapoint 2200** before Datapoint admitted it was a computer. With **Harry Pyle**, OS to "Outdo Unix" (bad idea); Ted documented until Datapoint folded.

> *"Don't know how to put this in Wikipedia."*
