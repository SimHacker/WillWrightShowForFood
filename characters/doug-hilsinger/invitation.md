# Repo Show invitation — Doug Hilsinger

*Draft — Doug may edit, decline, delay, or request removal at any time.*
[Portrayal standards](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

Doug —

Twenty years ago you were tending a nearly empty Eagle, you put on a record without saying whose
it was, and a stranger driving home to Berkeley told you he loved it before he knew you'd made it.
That stranger was me. You gave me the CD that night, and I've been telling the story ever since —
it's written up properly here:
[`taking-tiger-mountain-by-serendipity.md`](taking-tiger-mountain-by-serendipity.md).

I'm producing a **Repo Show** — recorded conversations staged in a public GitHub repo, artifacts
browsable forever — called **Will Wright Show For Food**.
**Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the [premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md) and more.**
Will and Brian Eno's 2006 Long Now talk is the night this whole story orbits: you and I were both
in that audience, the dinner afterward is how I introduced Brian to my friend Scott Draves, and
The Eagle was on my way home from Scott's place.

Your *Taking Tiger Mountain* is the show's living proof of its central move — **honoring a work
by transforming it**. No keyboards, no synths, every instrument by hand, Caroleen's voice — and
Eno's own phone call blessing it: *"beautiful music"* from his *"cold experiments." "I am a
swan!"* I want to sing your praises on air, A/B your tracks against the 1974 originals, play
Brian's phone message, and — the dream — reunite you, **Brian, Will, Scott, and Caroleen** for a
conversation about covers, constraints, and transformation as tribute. We'd drive listeners to
your Bandcamp and the ENORCHESTRA story the whole way.

**Zero pressure, zero homework.** Participate at any level: a phone call, an email with
corrections and stories, an audio recording on your own schedule, a video chat, or just your
blessing while I tell the story. Everything is time-shifted and edited with your approval;
you review anything with your name on it before it ships.
