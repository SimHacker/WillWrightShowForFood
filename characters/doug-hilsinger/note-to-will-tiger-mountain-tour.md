# A note to Will — a tour of the Tiger Mountain document 🐅

*From Don, July 10, 2026 — the morning after digesting our conversation.*

Will —

There's a document in this repo I want to walk you through:
[`taking-tiger-mountain-by-serendipity.md`](taking-tiger-mountain-by-serendipity.md).
Check the date on it: I wrote it on **June 29, 2026** — ten days *before* our July 9 conversation
where you started naming Cunk, MST3K, Bo Burnham, James Burke, and phase space as exactly the
format you wanted. I didn't write it in response to what you asked for. It was already sitting
here waiting for you.

## The quick tour

- **The record.** Fall 2003: Doug Hilsinger re-records Eno's *Taking Tiger Mountain (By Strategy)*
  track for track with Caroleen Beatty — one rule, **no keyboards, no synths** — and turns Eno's
  cold machine experiment warm and alive. Same Chinatown Mao postcard on the cover.
- **Eno's blessing.** Doug hands Eno a CD-R at a 2003 SF lecture; Eno phones back days later:
  *"beautiful music"* from his *"cold experiments"* — *"I am a swan!"* We have the actual
  [phone message](../brian-eno/audio/TakingTigerMountainBrianEnoPhoneMessageToDougHilsinger.mp3).
- **The serendipity.** 2006: the Long Now talk — **you and Brian on generative art**, me asking a
  question from the floor, dinner with you both after, introducing Brian to Spot at Scott's
  apartment — and then, minutes later, on a whim, The Eagle, nearly empty, where the bartender
  puts on music I fall in love with before learning **he made it**. Sincerity all the way down.
- **The thesis.** *Honor by transformation* — Doug covering Eno is the same move as me passing
  your SimCity through new interfaces without spoiling it, the same move as the whole Repo Show:
  original → tribute → whatever we make next. The segment even
  [stands alone](taking-tiger-mountain-by-serendipity.md#the-segment--even-without-doug-) if Doug
  and Brian never answer.

## Why I'm pointing you at the date

When you told me on July 9 what you wanted this show to be, it was exactly what I'd already been
building — and that stopped surprising me a long time ago. It's the **iLoci moment** again:
remember when I showed you [iLoci](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=03ddG3jWF98), the Amsterdam
Coffeeshops guide, and the
[Layar AR coffeeshop viewer](../don-hopkins/media/amsterdam-coffeeshops-layar/INDEX.yml) on the
iPhone, and it turned out to be just what you'd been thinking about? No coincidence then, no
coincidence now — **I shamelessly stole all your ideas and ran with them**, and we've been running
in the same direction for so long that when either of us describes where he's headed, the other
one is already there. Possibility space, honor by transformation, simulation as storytelling:
your ideas, my legs, one direction.

The lineage receipts are in
[`career/lineage.yml`](../don-hopkins/career/lineage.yml) (MUD1 → DreamScape → SimCity → The Sims
→ iLoci → LayAR → MOOLLM) and the [crazy idea jam](../../process/crazy-idea-jam.yml) — the TomTom
trilogy that re-sparked you is documented there too.

So this document is my standing answer to "what do you want the show to feel like": a true story
about loving someone's work so hard you rebuild it by hand, told with the original artist's
blessing already on tape. And it happens to end with a dinner **you** were at.

— Don
