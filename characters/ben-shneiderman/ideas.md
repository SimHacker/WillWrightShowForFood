## What Ben has done

Ben Shneiderman founded **UMD HCIL**, coined **"direct manipulation,"** invented **embedded /
highlighted hyperlinks**, directed **HyperTIES**, pioneered **Dynamic Queries** and info visualization
(*The Eyes Have It*), and co-authored the **CHI'88** pie-menu empirical study with Don, Mark Weiser,
and John Callahan. Berners-Lee cited Hyperties in his 1989 Web proposal. Recent work: **human-centered
AI** — human oversight in high-stakes automation.
