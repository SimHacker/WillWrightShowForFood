Ben —

We co-authored the **CHI'88** pie-menu study with Mark Weiser and John Callahan — the paper that
turned radial menus from a neat idea into something we could **measure** (Fitts, errors, direct
manipulation). I've been shipping pie menus ever since — SimCity, The Sims, NeWS, MOOLLM — always
knowing **PIXIE (1969)** came first.

Since our **January 2022** thread on HyperTIES, Ted Nelson, and self-revealing interfaces, I've been
building **Will Wright Show For Food**: a repo-native talk show whose stage *is* a public GitHub repo
— artifacts, correspondence, and working code anyone can browse. **Show, don't tell.**

**Will Wright is in — he's signed on to do the [premiere](https://github.com/SimHacker/WillWrightShowForFood/blob/main/repo-shows/will-wright-premiere/README.md) and more.**

**What I'd love to explore with you — your pick, any order, solo or spread across episodes:**

**1. Reincarnate HyperTIES — a living emulator (the one I think you'd love)**  
You directed **HyperTIES**; I built the **NeWS/PostScript** version — embedded PostScript applets on
hypermedia pages, years before the web had a word for that. I still have the whole stack: **Forth,
MockLisp, C, the HyperTIES markup language, the docs, the NeWS PostScript**, and I even **converted
the databases to XML**. The plan is pixel-perfect in the **web browser** — not a screenshot museum,
a *running* system. We talked about **documenting our work**; a living emulator is the best
documentation there is.

**2. Light blue links — HCIL → Berners-Lee**  
Your embedded highlighted links (1983–1986), the **CACM July 1988** *Hypertext on Hypertext* electronic
journal, and Berners-Lee citing Hyperties for **"hot spots"** and **link color** in his 1989 proposal —
with your fair read on Ted Nelson (*impact vs vision*) from our 2022 mail.

**3. CHI'88 pie menus — measure on air**  
Run the rhetoric with data: pie-menu slices on the Micropolis command bus. Optional group episode with
**Heinz U. Lemke** (PIXIE 1969) if you're both up for it — spans 1969 → 1988 → today. Solo with you
is plenty.

**4. The Eyes Have It / Dynamic Queries**  
Your CS547 talks with Terry — info viz, sliders that make databases answer instantly. I have the
CHI'88 paper and HomeFinder-era artifacts in the repo.

**5. Remembering Mark**  
Memorial-mode segment if you're willing — you sat at the same CHI'88 table; Mark's ubicomp/calm-tech
story deserves honest discussion from someone who knew him.
