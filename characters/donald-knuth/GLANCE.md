**Dream guest.** Author of *The Art of Computer Programming*, inventor of TeX, father of literate
programming — and the person who took Woods's FORTRAN Adventure and rewrote it as a CWEB essay
you can *read* as a world model.

**Hook for us:** programs as literature for humans; Adventure as the ur-schema of rooms/objects/parsers;
Repo Show as that instinct on GitHub.

**Reality check:** he doesn't do email. Invite = snail mail, someday, if the letter earns its stamp.
Until then this room is a portrayal and a wish with sources attached.
