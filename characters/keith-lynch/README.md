# Keith F. Lynch

Primary-source witness to [ARPAnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARPANET) and early [ITS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incompatible_Timesharing_System) guest culture (1977–1994). Not a show guest — an archivist's anchor.

**Canonical publication:** [History of the Net](https://keithlynch.net/history.net.html) (Jul 1994 email, still hosted by Lynch)

**Full text in this repo:** [`sources/history-of-the-net-1994.md`](sources/history-of-the-net-1994.md)

---

## Why this room exists

In July 1994 [Keith F. Lynch](https://keithlynch.net/) answered [Ronda Hauben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronda_Hauben)'s net-history inquiry with a CC'd memoir now treated as folklore: TIP/TAC dialup, MIT ITS guest hacks, the [Bandykin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FTP_Software) list lineage, [Usenet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usenet)/Internet overlap, the [1980 ARPAnet crash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARPANET#Collapse_and_rebirth), [KREMVAX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kremvax), and a rolodex of people who were *there*. Lynch disclaims perfect memory upfront; the value is texture and cross-references, not court-grade dates.
