## Dec 2022 — Old Farts mouse thread → Maynard → auction

Full chain: Facebook Internet Old Farts → **David Maynard** mouse photo → Don's SRI set from **Mark Lottor** → Maynard keyset at Stanford Hohbach → **$54k** RR Auction alert.

## Jan 2023 — Reproduction pitch (Don → Alan)

Don visiting California **Feb 6–11**; Netherlands house + solar + eBike life update.

**Reproduction scheme:**

- Carefully disassemble; reverse-engineer electronics; 3D scan  
- Print case; **mill** metal wheels for authentic clunk  
- RS232 dongle → Bluetooth HID thumb PC  
- Limited run; maybe Kickstarter  
- Original in vault; replicas increase value of documented prototype  

Asked Alan: sourcing vintage RS232 plugs, boards, manufacturing history, who made how many.

Also: Medium transclusion of Alan's Quora NeWS/Smalltalk answer + Warren Teitelman underrated + David Rosenthal window-systems blog.
