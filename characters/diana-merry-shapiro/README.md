# Diana Merry-Shapiro 🪟 *(PARC Smalltalk; BitBLT; overlapping windows)*

*Invitation portrayal — not Diana Merry-Shapiro.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md) · authored by Don Hopkins

## Who

Xerox PARC Learning Research Group programmer — original **Smalltalk** implementor;
early **overlapping windows**; co-inventor of **BitBLT**. Publicly documented in
Wikipedia, the 1975 BitBLT memo lineage, and a CHM oral history.

## Why invited

Every windowing UI still speaks BitBLT. She is a living primary source on how that
primitive and overlapping windows actually got written.
