# Arcade Blogger — Atari's Mike Jang (13 Feb 2024)

**URL:** https://arcadeblogger.com/2024/02/13/ataris-mike-jang/
**Author:** Tony (The Arcade Blogger)

## Career frame

- San Jose State → Atari Industrial Design mid-70s through 1980s (coin-op Golden Age).
- Focus: cabinet **ergonomics** and bespoke controls/mouldings; many patents in his name.
- Colleague photos: Asteroids Deluxe with **Dave Cook** (1981); RoadBlasters production at Atari
  Ireland (Tipperary) with Cook.
- Process: pre-CAD drafting tables; in-house wood/metal shops for secrecy; field-test → iterate
  (Missile Command large-marquee cut after negative test feedback).
- Jed Margolin: temperature testing → Mike added vents/fans without fuss.

## Star Wars cabinet (Mike's words, via tribute)

Started concepts; another designer detailed plastic in front of monitor. Sketched **hydraulic ram**
shapes (movie / Millennium Falcon ramp); **truss** roof sides (particle board, angled router, painted
black — worried about wood texture; nobody noticed).

## Other cabinets named

Starship 1 (1976 patent), Missile Command, Hard Drivin' (patent), Stun Runner, Starfighter
(unreleased 1984; appeared in the film), RoadBlasters, Firefox (control panel), Hydra, Malibu Grand
Prix open cockpit (shelved laserdisc; commenter **orubin**: started with Ed Logg), McDonald's Kids
Counter kiosk (unreleased).

## Archive

Donated Atari documents → Stanford **Michael Jang collection of Atari materials, 1978–1991**.

## Post-Atari

Hot-rodding; customized **1951 Mercury coupe** (acquired 1989; owned until passing).
