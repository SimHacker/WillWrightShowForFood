# Remembering Mike Jang

Memorial mode — we represent and discuss his documented work. We do not interview him or invent
his voice. [Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md#memorial-mode)

**Primary tribute:** [Atari's Mike Jang — The Arcade Blogger](https://arcadeblogger.com/2024/02/13/ataris-mike-jang/)
(Tony, 13 Feb 2024) · **HN:** [item?id=40260210](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40260210)

Industrial Designer, Atari coin-op — the hands and eyes behind cabinets players sat *inside*. Star
Wars cockpit, Missile Command, Hard Drivin', RoadBlasters, and a long list of patents and field
fixes (vents when Jed Margolin's temperature tests demanded them).

**Archive:** Stanford *Michael Jang collection of Atari materials, 1978–1991*.

**Bridge in this repo:** the same Star Wars cabinet design that fired imaginations worldwide is the
iron [Bill St. Clair](../bill-st-clair/) turned into a turtle-graphics device on a Lisp Machine at
Atari Cambridge Research — Don saw the ribbon-cable mess; Bill confirmed the ROM hack.
