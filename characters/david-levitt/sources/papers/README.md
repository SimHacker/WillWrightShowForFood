# David Levitt -- theses (primary sources)

Local copies of David Alex Levitt's MIT theses, both done in Minsky's orbit at the AI
Lab / Media Lab. Levitt is thanked by name in the acknowledgments of Minsky's
[Jokes memo](../../../marvin-minsky/sources/papers/AIM-603-jokes-cognitive-unconscious-1980.md),
and Minsky's [Music, Mind, and Meaning](../../../marvin-minsky/sources/papers/AIM-616-music-mind-and-meaning-1981.md)
is the intellectual neighborhood both theses live in: music as constraint, description,
and difference -- not magic.

| File | Degree | Year | What |
|------|--------|------|------|
| [`1985-phd-a-representation-for-musical-dialects.pdf`](1985-phd-a-representation-for-musical-dialects.pdf) | PhD, MIT | 1985 | A Representation for Musical Dialects: describing musical styles as constraint systems so that dialects (ragtime, jazz walking bass...) become programmable, parameterized descriptions. |
| [`1981-ms-a-melody-description-system-for-jazz-improvisation.pdf`](1981-ms-a-melody-description-system-for-jazz-improvisation.pdf) | MS, MIT | 1981 | A Melody Description System for Jazz Improvisation: the earlier melody-description work the PhD grew out of. |
