### Bounce reborn — the mythology, SimFaux, and the Faceball Construction Kit

The Bounce **Jesse / Rush / house-fly** demo grew a whole **clearly-labeled-fiction** mythology in
Don's LLOOOOMM tomb — the **Truth Fly** character (born as Bounce's interactive cursor, later
"famous" for the Pence debate), the **Great Transformation** story (Rush and Jesse watching Mr
Rogers), and the rule that characters **comment out old beliefs instead of deleting them**. Real
Interval history vs labeled fiction, distinguished on air.

The plan — and David is the perfect person to brainstorm it with — is to dive deep into that
mythological backstory and **tell the story Bounce was trying to tell**, reimagined and reimplemented
in the **[Faceball Construction Kit](../../apps/performance-space/faceball-construction-set.yml)**:
simulated commentator puppets over live media, closed captions and amplitude scrub as data streams,
Truth Fly landing on heads as the cursor again. (Savor the name: *Faceball* lunges toward *Facebook*,
then veers off at the last minute.)

The bridge demo to show on air: **SimFaux** (2006) — Don's OpenLaszlo **Fox News parody**, pie menus
driving live simulated talking heads with video mixing, PiP, and overlays — inspired by the Interval
work with David, and its DNA (simulated commentators over real media) flows straight into Faceball.
[The video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gRodlxUZ9SQ) ·
[source notes](../will-wright/sources/don-youtube-gRodlxUZ9SQ-simfaux/article.md).

A perfect Rebounce segment. (Sources: [`../don-hopkins/interval-research-pluggers-and-mediaflow.md`](../don-hopkins/interval-research-pluggers-and-mediaflow.md);
