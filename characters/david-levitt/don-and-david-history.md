## MIT — the Music Hacker's Hangout

At MIT, David was **office-mates with [Miller Puckette](../miller-puckette/README.md)** in what their
Lisp machine's location field literally called the **"Music Hacker's Hangout."** This is the
**AI Lab / NE43 / Atari Cambridge Research / Alan Kay / Seymour Papert / Lynn Conway** scene — the
whole constellation.

There's a treasure in **[Cynthia Solomon](../cynthia-solomon/README.md)**'s video trove: **young David,
in full mad-scientist hair, gleefully demonstrating his PhD project** (under **Marvin Minsky**) on his
**musical MIDI Lisp machine, improvising ragtime.** *(Cue that clip on the show.)*

### David's Mac apps at MIT — before Body Electric / Bounce

- **Harmony Grid** — David's own Mac app. *(It blows Don's mind; David explains it — this is his to
  tell.)*
- **Hookup** — David's **visual programming language**. It handled **MIDI I/O and other input
  devices**, and — the part people miss — it **plugged in the MacroMind/Macromedia Director Player,
  the "MMP Library."** (That same MMP later got plugged into **Netscape** and rebranded
  **Shockwave**.) David used MMP as the **multimedia rendering + audio engine for a VPL**: Hookup
  could **load Director files *without* a score** and **procedurally place and animate "cast
  members"** — sprites, text, audio. A visual language driving Director's media engine, at MIT,
  **years before** Body Electric / Bounce.

> **Context sidebar — the Puckette/Max tangle (to confirm on air).**
> Miller Puckette (a person, and David's officemate) created **Max** at IRCAM (named after Max
> Mathews); **MSP** is the audio-DSP extension → **Max/MSP** (the name puns on "Max Signal
> Processing" *and* **M**iller **S**. **P**uckette). His **open-source** baby is **Pd (Pure Data)**,
> written in **C** (the "open-source in Java" memory is likely **jMax**, IRCAM's Java Max successor).
> Around this era **Antiorp / Netochka Nezvanova** made **NATO.0+55+3d** (video for Max) and had
> dramatic battles with the Max world — reportedly furious when Cycling '74 shipped their own video
> extension, **Jitter**. Miller, MSP, and Pd are all his and all cool; Max adopted his DSP ideas.

## California — VPL and Body Electric

David went out to California to work at **VPL Research** (the company that coined "virtual reality").
There he worked on and used **Body Electric** to build VR simulations on the expensive hardware — but
he still wanted the **multimedia** simulations he'd done in Hookup, so he **added MMP to Body Electric
too.** The same instinct, carried into VR.

**The rights chain** *(as Don recalls — confirm with David + [Jaron](../jaron-lanier/README.md)):*
when VPL broke up, **David ended up with the rights to Body Electric** — Don believes as compensation
for giving VPL the rights to **Hookup**. **Sun** also acquired rights to Body Electric, and **Jaron
did some work on that with Sun** — a thread Don and Jaron have discussed and can show and dig into more
with Jaron on the [Rebounce](../../repo-shows/rebounce/README.md) show.

## SIGGRAPH — serendipity at Coco Conn's SIGKids

Don ran into David again at **SIGGRAPH**, in **[Coco Conn](../coco-conn/README.md)'s "SIGKids"** area —
where people demonstrated cool things **to kids** (who weren't allowed on the show floor). *(Coco is
royalty and a goddess — Don's words — invited to talk about SIGKids and much more.)*

There Don saw what David was doing with **Bounce**: a **visual programming language controlling
sprites, sounds, and animation**, with an **animated band of simulated characters** you could
**enable and disable to play their instruments together**, moving the **spotlight** to give each one a
**solo.** *(From memory — David explains; Don thinks David built it for a **contract in New York with a
TV network**.)*

Don's honest reaction: **immediate envy** — *"HE did what I wanted to do!"* — so he **immediately
forgave him.** David asked if Don wanted to **work with him.** Don definitely did. David was heading
back to California to work at **Interval Research** (where Don had just given a talk), and could keep
developing **Bounce on the side with Don's help** — while Don went to **Kaleida** to work on
**DreamScape**, and played with Bounce on the side for David's company **"Levity."**

## Play, and road stories

- **The massage-chair vow (CES).** Don and David once staffed a booth in the **home-automation
  ghetto**, stuck next to the booth with the **massage chairs**. They had **one uncomfortable bar
  chair**; the massage-chair booth had, well, **massage chairs — all day.** Watching the *himbos and
  bimbos* (Don's words) lounge in comfort, they got deeply envious and **swore never to attend a trade
  show booth again unless there were massage chairs.** A vow held to this day.
