## Bounce · Body Electric · Hookup (visual programming for VR & music)

David Levitt's line of real-time, patch-cord visual programming languages — a through-line from MIT to
VPL to the projects he and Don shared. Primary source: [HN 22788773](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22788773) (DonHopkins, on *Csound*).

- **Hookup** — real-time visual language for MIDI/music/graphics by **David Levitt** (who shared an
  office with **Miller Puckette** at MIT); incorporated Macromedia Director's **MMP** player plug-in.
  Per **Mark Coniglio**, Hookup was **the first program to use the "patch-cord" metaphor** — the direct
  ancestor of **Interactor → Isadora**, and the metaphor **Max** also inherits.
  ([Coniglio history](http://www.sdela.dds.nl/sfd/isadora.html))
- **Body Electric** — VPL's real-time visual language for VR/music/hardware (**Chuck Blanchard**; David
  Levitt + **Jaron Lanier**). Original name of **Bounce**. Drove DataGlove, Body Suit, Flock of Birds,
  Polhemus, Spaceball, Convolvotron 3D audio, SGI *Isaac* to EyePhones.
  ([Jaron Lanier / VPL](http://www.jaronlanier.com/vpl.html))
- **Rights chain (Don's best recollection — confirm with David):** David **created Hookup** as a **Mac
  product** → granted **VPL** the rights to market it (and possibly **HarmonyGrid** — unsure); as VPL
  fell apart, **David got the rights to develop Bounce for Mac** (from **Body Electric**) — compensation
  for losing the rights to his own Mac app(s); **Levity** → the vehicle to develop/sell **Bounce for Mac**.
- **Bounce @ Levity** — Don serial-contracted with David; shippable Mac product, dogfooded, reinvented
  with **plugin COM datatypes**, **objects on wires**, **plugin components with COM**, new **multimedia
  video renderer**. Don co-developed; demo **"Space Seed."** Writeups:
  [Medium — Bounce Stuff](https://medium.com/@donhopkins/bounce-stuff-8310551a96e3)
  · [c2 wiki — BounceLanguage](https://wiki.c2.com/?BounceLanguage)
  · [bounce-notes.txt](https://www.donhopkins.com/home/archive/visual-programming/bounce-notes.txt)
  · Don topic: [`../don-hopkins/levity-bounce-space-seed.md`](../don-hopkins/levity-bounce-space-seed.md)
- MMP → later became **Macromedia Shockwave** on the web.
- **"Space Seed"** — the Bounce demo name. *(firsthand — Don searching HN/archives for the link.)*
- **Levity Software / Levity Novelty** — Mac product development (with Don). **Interval Research** —
  later Bounce character sim + MediaFlow with David. *(firsthand)*
- **Reincarnation dream:** Don has the **Bounce source** (originally **Pascal**, machine-translated to
  ugly scoped-struct **C**, Mac heritage) — a candidate for a merciful **TypeScript** reimplementation.
  *(firsthand)*

## ConnectedTV (ConnectedMedia — David + Don)

Primary sources: [HN 7262676](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=7262676) (SimHacker) and
[HN 17105958](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=17105958) (DonHopkins, in the Pie Menus retrospective).

- Finger-controlled **Palm** app (pre-multitouch): a personalized handheld **TV guide integrated with a
  universal remote**, operable one-handed in a dark room — **no stylus** — via **pie-menu buttons** with
  **stroke-vs-poke** (tap + up/down/left/right strokes = up to 5 functions per finger-sized button).
  Remapped Palm hardware buttons to Power/Mute/Vol/Next/Back; TiVo/VCR support; Favorites.
- **"Touch Tuning"** — touch a show's *name* to change the channel (speed-dial for the remote; forget the
  numbers). **"Finger Pies"** — the directional-stroke commands (e.g. stroke left/right = prev/next
  program; up = send-to-TV; down = show-more; thumbs-up/down = favorite/filter).
- Reviews: **Geoff Walker, "ConnectedTV: TV Guide + Remote Control," PenComputing, 2/2/02** —
  [pencomputing.com/palm/Pen44/connectedTV.html](http://www.pencomputing.com/palm/Pen44/connectedTV.html)
  · **PCMag** — [pcmag.com/article2/0,2817,770400,00.asp](https://www.pcmag.com/article2/0,2817,770400,00.asp)
- David Levitt's essay **"iPhone Lovefest — How Don and I Invented Stroking vs Poking"** —
  [facebook.com/note.php?note_id=106220169912](https://www.facebook.com/note.php?note_id=106220169912)
- **Sony** paid for custom **CLIÉ** skins and offered to bundle it pre-installed (before Sony Japan
  dropped Palm OS). "Handheld Computing" called it one of the most impressive Palm OS apps; users called
  it "addictive"; Don frames the directional strokes as a **swipe-to-unlock predecessor**.
- *(To add, firsthand:* XML-based custom-UI editor; IR-code capture & community sharing site.*)*
