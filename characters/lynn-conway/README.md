## Who

**Lynn Ann Conway** — computer scientist and electrical engineer; principal force
(with Carver Mead) behind the **VLSI design education revolution** at Xerox PARC;
earlier IBM ACS architecture work; DARPA Strategic Computing; University of Michigan
EECS; CHM Fellow 2014; public transgender rights advocate after coming out in the
late 1990s.

## Why she's here

Chip design as a **democratized craft** — textbooks, courses, and foundry access —
and the ethical through-line of who gets to keep a career while living truthfully.
Don has discussed her work on Hacker News (see harvest index); this room holds the
sourced biography for shows and intertwingled history.
