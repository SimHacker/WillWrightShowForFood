# Philip K. Dick — threads for a remembrance

*Memorial mode. About his published work; never in his voice.*

## Worlds

- Perky Pat layouts and The Sims: miniature worlds people move into.
- *Ubik*: coin-operated doors, and the opposite of calm technology. Mark Weiser told Don it was one
  of his inspirations.
- The 1977 Metz speech: programmed worlds and alternate presents.
- "Faith of Our Fathers" and many-worlds version control, from the reading group.

## Machines and people

- *Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?*: empathy tests for AI characters.
- "Autofac" (1955): factories that won't stop, as an early warning about self-replicating systems.
- "The Electric Ant": a man who finds his own punched tape and edits it.
- "We Can Remember It for You Wholesale": memory as a product.

## Dick and Lem

- Lem's 1975 essay praising him, and Dick's 1974 letter to the FBI about Lem, read together and
  fairly.
