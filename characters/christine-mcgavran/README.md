# Christine McGavran 🗺️🏙️ *(SimCity 3000 → Apple → Google → Vantor → Mapbox)*

*Portrayal of a real friend and interview candidate, written by Don — not Christine.*
[Portrayal standards](../../schemas/portrayal-standards.md)

Christine worked on and led **SimCity 3000**, the top-selling PC game of its release year. Then she
spent the next twenty-five years building maps for real: mapping and navigation products at
**Apple**, then **Google**, where she pitched putting Google's maps inside car dashboards — the pitch
that became **Google Automotive Services**, now shipping in Volvo, BMW and Ford vehicles — and later
led the work that widened Geo's data intake from a handful of sources to thousands: authoritative GIS
datasets, transit feeds, road incident feeds, EV networks, Waze, and the city and government
partnerships that come with them. Most recently she was **VP of Engineering at Vantor**, working at
the bottom of the stack where imagery arrives from orbit, and led the launch of the **Spatial
Intelligence Platform (Tensorglobe)** — imagery at volume, satellite tasking for fresh
high-resolution capture, and services on top of both. She is now at **Mapbox**.

She has been a software engineer, an engineering manager, a director, a VP, an executive producer and
a game designer, across embedded systems, graphics, applications, services, algorithms and big data.
There are not many people who can discuss a tile pipeline, a ship date and a game design in the same
sentence.

## The sentence this whole room is built on

From her own public summary of her career: SimCity introduced her to the concepts of **mapping and
urban planning**, and that is what led her to a career focus on **mapping and navigation**.

That is the argument of [eBike Safari](../../apps/ebike-safari/design/README.md), made by someone who
lived it instead of theorising it. A city simulator is a map you are allowed to touch; a navigation
app is a map you are mostly not. The project is an attempt to close that gap on a bicycle — OSM data
as the world model, direct manipulation as the interaction rule, and the rider's own senses as
inputs, [bumps included](../../apps/ebike-safari/design/bumps-as-input.md).

## Don and Christine

They met in the early 1990s, when Don was at Sun and Christine was at SGI working with game
developers. They were later colleagues at Maxis/EA — LinkedIn dates the overlap September 1997 to
March 2000, which is exactly the SimCity 3000 stretch.

In March 2024, when Yoot Saito sent Don the **Yoot Tower** code drop for archival and an open-source
browser port, Christine was one of the people Don wrote to. She took access to the management repo,
went looking for the art assets, and offered to think about who could help with the Japanese comments
and strings. Details in [`correspondence.yml`](correspondence.yml); the revival show is
[`repo-shows/yoot-tower-revival/`](../../repo-shows/yoot-tower-revival/).
