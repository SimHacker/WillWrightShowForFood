# Melissa Bachman-Wood

**Maxis / EA, The Sims era.** Named on a 2002 bug report as the previous assignee, which places her
in the production or QA chain during *Deluxe + Unleashed*. She kept a box, and around **2017**
photographed it for Maxis Alumni — then **mailed it to Don**.

Her box turned out to contain the single best naming artifact in this repo: the title page of
**The Sims — Design Script**, listing five working titles including *the Tactical Domestic
Simulator*, *the Total Dollhouse System*, and *What the Hell Is It?*

## Why she matters here

- **She is why the codenames are legible.** `TDSB.pdf`, `TDSBInfo.pdf`, `TDSEditToDo.pdf`, and
  `TDRObjects.pdf` sit in [Don's published design-documents directory](../will-wright/sources/the-sims-design-documents-zip/README.md)
  as cryptic filenames. This title page expands the acronym.
- **She tried to place the collection properly.** In the thread she says she'd *"want to start with
  the Sims folks still at EA, since they own the franchise"* before an outside library — an
  ex-employee doing rights diligence on her own memorabilia, unprompted.
- **She mailed it to Don.** He confirmed receipt: *"Your wonderful package arrived in the mail! I
  will scan it and put the PDF online as soon as I get a chance!"* **That scan is still owed.**
- **She has more.** *"I'll post pics of anything else I unearth (I also had a whole collection of
  Sims 1 Prima guides?!)"*
