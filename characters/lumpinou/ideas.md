# Ideas — Lumpinou

## Why him / why now

The Sims 4's queer features live in the character creator. Lumpinou's live in the simulation. That
distinction is the entire thesis of
[How Inclusivity Saved The Sims](../../repo-shows/how-inclusivity-saved-the-sims/README.md), and he
demonstrates it better than any slide could.

## Conversation hooks

1. **Rules vs wardrobe.** EA gave Sims binders and top surgery scars; Lumpinou gave them
   transition as something that happens over time, with moodlets and sentiments and other Sims
   reacting. Ask him where he drew the line between what a mod should simulate and what it should
   leave alone.
2. **Everything is optional.** The mod's most striking design decision is that every system can be
   switched off, including whether identity affects romance at all. That's a stance about players,
   not about Sims. Worth hearing him state it.
3. **Auto-assign percentages.** He shipped a knob for how queer the neighborhood is. Nobody at a
   publisher would ever ship that knob. What did he learn from how people set it?
4. **1998 and 2022.** Play the [record](../patrick-j-barrett-iii/same-sex-relationships-the-record.md)
   against his work: Patrick's first pass simply did not check sex, and preference emerged from
   behavior instead of an attraction number. Lumpinou split romantic from sexual orientation
   twenty-four years later. Same instinct, opposite direction — get them in a room.
5. **Modding as the real API.** Sihvonen's argument, alive: the mod community is where the game's
   design actually continues. He is doing systems design on a shipped product with no access to
   the source.
6. **The Sims 4 soul bridge.** Don doesn't know Sims 4; Lumpinou does. What would it take to read a
   household out of a save with names, relationships and identities intact, and hand it to
   [Soul City](../../catalogs/soul-city/README.md)? His mod already reasons about most of that
   state.
7. **Exit, generally.** EA's Legacy Collection shipped without the Exchange;
   [Tiny Life](https://tinylifegame.com/) ships export, import and mods as first-class. Under new
   ownership, portability stops being an abstraction. Introduce him to [Ell](../ellpeck/).
8. **Sustaining the work.** Mods for The Sims have to be free under EA's policy. How does that
   actually work economically, and what breaks when a patch lands? Same open question Don has been
   asking [Blake](../blake-jennings/) about funding open-source bridges.

## Show seeds

- **Solo:** a walkthrough of the overhaul's systems, played, with the design reasoning out loud.
- **With Patrick:** 1998 meets 2022 — two people who implemented inclusion without being asked to.
- **With Ell:** what a life sim should model natively vs what should be left to mods.
- **With Heather and Steve:** three generations of Sims modding —
  [SimFreaks and SimSlice](../../repo-shows/heather-and-steve/README.md) from 2000, Lumpinou from
  2022. Objects, then Python.
- Audience seat in [How Inclusivity Saved The Sims](../../repo-shows/how-inclusivity-saved-the-sims/README.md).
