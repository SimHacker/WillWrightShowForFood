## What he built

The [overhaul](https://lumpinoumods.com/2022/06/07/lumpinous-lgbtqia-mod/) (7 June 2022, still
patched against EA updates) gives Sims gender identities — questioning, cisgender, agender,
non-binary, genderfluid, transgender — and plays affirmation and transition out through
interactions, moodlets, notifications, sentiments and relationship statuses instead of sending you
back to CAS. Romantic and sexual orientation are modeled as separate axes. Self-discovery and
coming out are optional systems, not flags. Sims can be queerplatonic partners. Aromantic Sims can
WooHoo without romance. An auto-assign system populates the neighborhood with tunable percentages,
so the world isn't straight by default — and every piece of it can be switched off.

Also on [Mod The Sims](https://modthesims.info/d/668488/) and
[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/lumpinou).

## Why he's in this repo

The Sims 4 shipped the representation and stopped: gender-independent clothing in 2016, custom
pronouns in 2022, an orientation setting in July 2022, binders and top surgery scars in the January
2023 patch. Lumpinou shipped the *rules* underneath. That is the same move
[Patrick Barrett made in 1998](../patrick-j-barrett-iii/same-sex-relationships-the-record.md) by
simply not checking sex — done twenty-four years later, from outside the company, in Python.

He is also the reason a Sims 4 soul bridge would be a collaboration rather than a
reverse-engineering project. Don doesn't know that game; Lumpinou knows it all the way down.
