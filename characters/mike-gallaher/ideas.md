# Mike Gallaher — show hooks

1. **The pie menu wedding** — 1986, discussions and bong hits after Mike's wedding to Liz
   Sommers; theta menus; Mitch Bradley's naming; the lineage to The Sims and OLPC. Both
   co-discoverers, one stage, forty years of radial menus as exhibit A.
   → [pie-menus-chi-88-and-beyond](../don-hopkins/pie-menus-chi-88-and-beyond.md)

2. **Software Hoarder Emacs** — UniPress, Gosling Emacs, NeMACS and the NeWS display driver; RMS's
   epithet as a badge. The free-software culture war from the villain's seat, with affection.

3. **Live adversarial committee** — Maya, Frankie, Joe, Vic, Tammy convene on a real pending
   decision; audience seeds personas and motions via TicketPR; independent evaluator scores.
   The methodology demonstrating itself. → [moollm skills](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/adversarial-committee)

4. **Stories all the way down** — the essay walked through, with the danger section intact
   ("high-entropy bullshit that happens to be locally plausible") and the defenses: adversarial
   review, evidence, testable predictions, humility.
   → [the essay in moollm designs](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/designs/mike-gallaher-ideas.md)

5. **Stories × Made-Up Minds** — crossover with [Gary Drescher](../gary-drescher/README.md):
   ensemble inference over framings meets constructivist schema learning. Does a mind make itself
   up by running a committee? (Minsky's society of mind says: obviously.)

6. **The Sun pilgrimage** — Mike and Don visit Sun; Don reads the Red PostScript book on the
   plane, meets Gilmore/Gosling/Rosenthal/Densmore/Bradley, and promptly crashes SunDew. The trip
   that set the NeWS decade in motion.
