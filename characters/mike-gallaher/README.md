# Mike Gallaher 🥧🧙‍♂️📖

**Emacs hacker, co-discoverer of pie menus, committee methodologist — the "Software Hoarder" who
gave MOOLLM its deliberation chambers.**

*Sniff:* [`CHARACTER.yml`](CHARACTER.yml) · [invitation](invitation.md) · [ideas](ideas.md)

> *"Everything is a story. Math proofs are stories. Legal theories are stories. Decision trees,
> causal analysis, risk assessments — all stories."*

## Who

Mike Gallaher and Don Hopkins go back to **UniPress Emacs** — the commercial Gosling Emacs that
RMS immortalized as **"Software Hoarder Emacs"** (a title Mike wears the way pirates wear their
wanted posters) — and **NeMACS**, the NeWS version of Emacs with its PostScript display driver.

And then there's the origin story that belongs in UI folklore: in **1986, Mike and Don together
arrived at the idea of pie menus** — menus selected by angle, distance as a free parameter —
in intense discussions (and, per the historical record, bong hits) after Mike's wedding to Liz
Sommers. Don prototyped them as "theta menus" for X10, Mitch Bradley named them, and the idea ran
from NeWS windows through HyperTIES, SimCity, and [The Sims itself](../will-wright/README.md) —
see [Pie Menus: CHI'88 and beyond](../don-hopkins/pie-menus-chi-88-and-beyond.md). Every radial
menu in every game you've played descends partly from that conversation.

## Stories All the Way Down

Mike's January 2026 essay to Don — *Stories All the Way Down* — became one of MOOLLM's
load-bearing documents ([archived in moollm designs](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/designs/mike-gallaher-ideas.md)).
The thesis: **LLMs are storytelling machines**, so cast inquiries as stories — and since no single
story is true, run an **ensemble**: adversarial committees of fixed personas (paranoid Maya,
idealist Frankie, continuity-guardian Joe, evidence-prosecutor Vic, systems-thinker Tammy)
debating under Robert's Rules, scored by independent evaluators against rubrics.

> *No single story is "true" — but the ensemble approximates actionable wisdom.*

Four MOOLLM skills implement his methodology directly:
[adversarial-committee](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/adversarial-committee) ·
[roberts-rules](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/roberts-rules) ·
[rubric](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/rubric) ·
[evaluator](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/evaluator) — the deliberation
wing of the skill library is Mike's wing.

## Why the show cares

- **The pie menu origin, first-person** — both co-discoverers on one stage, forty years on, with
  The Sims' pie menus as exhibit A.
- **Emacs archaeology** — UniPress, NeMACS, the NeWS display driver, "Software Hoarder Emacs" vs.
  RMS: the free-software culture war told by someone who was the *villain of the epithet* and has
  been funny about it ever since.
- **Committee methodology, live** — run an adversarial committee on a real decision during the
  show, with the audience seeding personas via TicketPR.
- **Stories all the way down × Made-Up Minds** — Mike's ensemble-of-stories and
  [Gary Drescher's](../gary-drescher/README.md) made-up minds are the same claim at different
  altitudes: minds and decisions are both narrated into being.
