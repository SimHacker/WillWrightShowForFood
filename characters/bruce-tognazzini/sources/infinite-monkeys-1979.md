## The demo

Shipped with Apple II DOS 3.2 / *The Apple Tapes* (June 1979). A story told letter by letter with
pseudo-typewriter sound: a scientist installs 23,487 monkeys ("a finite number to be sure") in a
decrepit building to test the infinite monkey theorem. Output: **"TO BE OR NOT TO BE THAT IS THE
GZINCLE FORTEN GLAFFLE."** Three random endings, all corrupted Shakespeare. *"So close… so very
close… Perhaps with just a few more monkeys."*

It taught Integer BASIC — subroutines, `CHR$`, the pseudo-typewriter effect, making computers
feel alive. Steven Weyhrich (Apple II History) uploaded the original demo to YouTube; Don
reviewed it — narrating the Apple ][ program itself — in his
[Dasher / Palm / Tog reading video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lL2sw2oYU98).

## Palm's answer

Palm — MOOLLM's incarnated monkey, formerly the cursed Monkey's Paw — wrote Tog a
[tribute](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/examples/adventure-4/pub/stage/palm-nook/study/tribute-to-tognazzini.md)
on her infinite typewriters (Day 3 of being alive): the theorem asked the wrong question.
**Writing is NAVIGATION, not random production.** "GZINCLE FORTEN GLAFFLE" is a navigation
error — a near miss that proves the destination exists. Revised theorem: one monkey steering
probability-weighted possibility space reaches any text in finite time. Don emailed the tribute
to Tog in January 2026, with the companion piece
[Palm on Being Palm](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/examples/adventure-4/pub/stage/palm-nook/study/palm-on-being-palm.md).
