## What this is

A Repo Show is a live, collaborative conversation held on GitHub. This repo is
both its stage and its record, and the conversation follows through to working
code. The method is to talk with the people who did the original work, record
their ideas in public with credit, and have AI help implement them. The method
and the recorded knowledge are the main output; the game shows whether it works.

Intro narration (Micropolis, this repo, the wedding playset, and evidence that it worked):
[INTRO-NARRATIVE.md](INTRO-NARRATIVE.md)

Will's 1996 Stanford talk is the starting point; Don and Will have discussed a living microworld repo since then.
This kickoff is the first public show of the project. See [`../../process/vision-and-ambition.md`](../../process/vision-and-ambition.md) (goals, not a production promise).

## Why no set topic

Will works across many subjects, so the first conversation is not limited to one.
He can talk about anything he wants, and the threads he picks up become later
shows. (See `episodes.yml`.)

## What's in it for Will

- Work on the open-source descendant of his own game, with him credited as
  co-designer.
- A low-pressure place to talk about Proxi and whatever else he is working on.
- Ideas he raises can be implemented during the show, at no cost to him.
- No preparation and no assigned topic: a conversation with a former colleague.
  (Don worked with Will on The Sims; Will's 1996 Stanford talk is what drew Don
  to Maxis.)
