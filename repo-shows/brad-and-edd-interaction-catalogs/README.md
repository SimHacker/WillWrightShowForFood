## Topic

Pick, Click, Flick! meets the Game UI Database: the academic taxonomy of interaction techniques and the industry's 73,000-screen visual archive, cross-referenced live. Where the literature and the shipping record agree, where they contradict, and what each catalog is missing that the other one has.

## Beats

1. Show and tell: each guest walks the other through his catalog — taxonomy design, what counts as an entry, what gets rejected
2. The crosswalk: pick one technique (radial menus is the obvious first) and trace it through both catalogs — literature lineage vs shipping record
3. What games knew first — techniques GUIDB has that the academic literature missed or lagged (and vice versa)
4. All the Widgets (1990) → GUIDB 2.0 video (2024): the 34-year arc of cataloging interfaces in motion
5. Second editions: what belongs in the next Pick, Click, Flick! from the GUIDB record; what GUIDB taxonomy could borrow from the VPL/interaction-techniques literature
6. Ethical archives — GUIDB's no-AI-scraping stance meets academic citation culture; who gets credit and who gets paid
