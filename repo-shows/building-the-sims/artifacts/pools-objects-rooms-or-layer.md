# Swimming pools — objects, rooms, or layer?

## Hook

Don's 2007 interview question for Jamie: **objects or rooms — and why!**

## Story

Three framings debated on original team:

| Model | Idea |
|-------|------|
| Objects | Multi-tile placeables, ladders in catalog |
| Rooms | Flood-filled space, own room ID |
| Layer | Per-tile pool layer + pool **pieces** for edges/ladders |

Don recalls **layer + pieces** shipped; team should correct on air.

## Ask on air

- Jamie / Eric: what actually drove swim checks?
- Jim: what reached management?
