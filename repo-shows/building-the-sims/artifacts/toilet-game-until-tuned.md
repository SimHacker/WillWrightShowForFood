# The toilet game — until tuned

## Hook

Pre-tuning Sims: there wasn't much to do except potty loops — hence **the toilet game**.

## Story

Chris Trottier / Don archive: **design by accretion** — layers accumulated (motives, objects,
behaviors) but **tuning** in final months made it fun. EA nervous; Will stamina; SimCity 2000
analogy (fun ~6 weeks before ship).

Not missing content at the end — missing **balance**.

## Ask on air

- Chris: what changed in tuning pass?
- Will: when did you know it was fun?
