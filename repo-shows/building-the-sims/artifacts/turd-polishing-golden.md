# The polished turd — corrected by Jim

## The quote (Jim's version, 2026-08-21)

> Never underestimate the power of a polished turd.

The turd refers to **documents, ppt decks, etc.** — the **SimWorld
Report** being the likely context. Jim, explicitly: *"There is no
concept of polishing turd code. There might be for the related
'lipstick on a pig,' but I don't know that one."*
Source: [Jim's correction](../../../characters/jim-mackraz/sources/2026-08-21-polished-turd-correction.md).

## Don's recollection (the earlier telling)

Don called the code a turd and remembers being handed the word back
as a job description — clean it out, polish it, you'll be golden —
followed by real **de-Macification** work that later affected **Mac
porting**. The cleanup happened; the quote, per Jim, was about
documents. How the two merged into one story over 25 years is a
better on-air beat than the original version was.
