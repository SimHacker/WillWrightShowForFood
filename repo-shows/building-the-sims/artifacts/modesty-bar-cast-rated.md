# Modesty bar — cast-rated, not X-rated

## Hook

The pixel bar wasn't really hiding penises from players — it was hiding the humiliating fact that
Sims had **nothing** to hide.

## Story

Don's intent (engineering memory):

- Underlying meshes: smooth dolls — **cast-rated** anatomy.
- Shimmering bar suggests **functional** Sims with modesty — for the Sims' feelings, not player
  shock prevention.
- Players who *knew* there was nothing underneath would embarrass the Sims (Don's framing).
