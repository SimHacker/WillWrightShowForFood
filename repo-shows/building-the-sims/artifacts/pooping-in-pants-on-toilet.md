# Pooping in pants on the toilet

## Hook

Michael sits still on the toilet in the March 1999 beta — shimmer on the modesty bar — but he never
takes his pants off. Thank God it's censored.

## Story

Sims had **binary** appearance: fully clothed or fully nude. No separate **pants mesh** to swap at
the toilet. Sitting animation + motive relief → bowel movement **in trousers**.

Don's memory: too hard to fix before ship; closed **works as designed**; Sims "had to crap in their
pants."

## Reproduction (beta)

See beta clip in [`dana-michael-beta-march-1999.md`](dana-michael-beta-march-1999.md) — Michael
holds still; pixelation shimmers while paused.
