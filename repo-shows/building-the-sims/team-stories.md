→ [`artifacts/turd-polishing-golden.md`](artifacts/turd-polishing-golden.md)

## Patrick's stories

*As [Patrick J. Barrett III](../../characters/patrick-j-barrett-iii/) told Don over the years —
retold here from Don's chat archive. Patrick may correct, expand, or cut any of these; his call.*

### "Who did grass?"

Patrick wasn't invited to a programmer meeting — he wasn't considered a programmer. He barged in
demanding to know who did grass. The three programmers just stared at him. Finally Bobo
([Eric Bowman](../../characters/eric-bowman/)) said: "Legally, I can't ask that." Patrick meant
the lawn in the game.

### The abortion question nobody designed

An article claimed The Sims supports abortion, and there were heart attacks around the office when
Patrick told them the writer was right. Nobody thought about it while making the game — but the
dialog asks if you want the baby *afterwards*. That's why in The Sims 2 it asked *before* sex.

### Kids downtown (Hot Date)

In Hot Date, children can't go downtown — but they can go if a stranger phones and invites them.
Kids going downtown was planned; it got cut to ship on time, and the team thought they'd found all
the paths in. They missed strangers calling them on the phone. Bigger picture, per Patrick: the
children being in Hot Date at all was an accident caused by downtown existing — the team expected
EA to cut downtown, so dating was the backup plan. They never intended to ship both.

### The McDonald's mishap (Patrick's favorite)

In Vacation, production insisted on a sushi cart. Patrick didn't want it, so when Jake programmed
it, he gave the Sims a chance of getting horrible diarrhea. In the object code it's called
**poopy pants** — blue like the other motive effects, but it keeps them running to the toilet, if
they can make it. All the vacation-island-only food has it.

Meanwhile, EA had sold product placement in The Sims Online. Since nobody bought TSO and the
objects were never made, the sponsors wanted their money back — so EA decided to build them as
free downloads for The Sims and keep the money. The Superstar team built the McDonald's stand from
the sushi cart, not realizing what they had inherited. When they did, Patrick told them not to
patch it: since the McDonald's had to be the best food in the game, he felt they deserved it.

Related, in Patrick's telling: Vacation had a larger team, so they could be more creative.

### The bowl in the couch

Don's own memory, confirmed across the years: [Chris Trottier](../../characters/chris-trottier/)
found Don's bowl in a couch at Maxis, gave it to Charles London, and Charles gave it back to Don.
Lost and found, chain of custody intact.

### The jilted lovers club

Years after The Sims shipped, Don built a **Cupid** object for the **SimProv Wedding Playset**:
a phone-style menu to call up any neighbor, who'd come over, fall completely in love with you
(mutually), and kiss you. Enumerate the whole neighborhood and your jilted lovers would form an
orderly queue to slap you, one after another. Patrick remembered it, and named it:
**the jilted lovers club**.

### The love bed

Patrick's favorite Livin' Large object behavior: Sims may puke after using the love bed.

### How the paper design docs survived

Patrick never saw most of the paper design documents until the Hot Date era — when The Sims Online
team was throwing them all away and Mike rescued them. By then most design flowed through the
production-controlled Access database, not the paper docs. Context for
[the record](../../characters/patrick-j-barrett-iii/same-sex-relationships-the-record.md): the
paper trail and the day-to-day production truth ran on separate tracks.
