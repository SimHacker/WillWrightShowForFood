# Discussion formats

How reunion topics can run — sync and async, solo and group. Pick what fits each guest's
schedule; nothing here is mandatory.

## On-air workflow

1. Open [`artifacts/INDEX.md`](artifacts/INDEX.md) — sort by **heat** or **guest**.
2. Read the card **hook** → open full card for **ask on air**.
3. Parent docs below hold longer context; cards stay bite-sized.

## Formats

| Format | What it is | Good for |
|--------|------------|----------|
| **Async written** | Short answer in repo (issue, markdown, or email → ingested) | Pool question, bug-pile memories, "what shipped from your chair" |
| **Async video** | Self-recorded clip; Don or team ingests with consent | Designers, composers, guests who can't make live blocks |
| **1-on-1** | Don + one guest, recorded | Deep dives (SimAntics, grid, production pipeline, TSO) |
| **Small group** | 2–4 guests + Don | Cross-chair topics (pools, pixelation, pause/time) |
| **Full reunion** | Core team on stream | Compare answers; bug pile; Will line |

## Pool question (objects or rooms?)

Straight engineering question — see [`swimming-pools-objects-or-rooms.md`](swimming-pools-objects-or-rooms.md).

**Suggested flow:**

1. **Async** — each person answers separately (no need to name who they argued with in 1999).
2. **Don on stream** — states what he recalls shipped; invites corrections.
3. **Group** — compare notes; file disagreements as folklore, not verdicts.

Everyone can have a reaction; the show records what people actually said.
