# A Repo Show with Peter Norvig 📖

> Teach Yourself AI in Ten Years — an open-topic conversation, Peter's pick.

**Peter Norvig** — Director of Research at Google, co-author of *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern
Approach*, and author of [*Teach Yourself Programming in Ten Years*](https://norvig.com/21-days.html)
— is a friend of Don's who's writing an AI book and offered to include a **chapter on Don's work**.
This show is the natural other half of that: a recorded conversation that's fun on its own and
useful as source material.

## What we'd get into (Peter's choice)

- **MOOLLM & skills** — filesystem-as-microworld; skills as constructionist artifacts for authoring
  and teaching AI behavior.
- **Worms & Ground** — Peter named this in January 2026 before the field notebook went public:
  scruffy on neat, Ground as character, *The Ground Truth* as the ML journal, worm pipelines as
  map/reduce. [**Palm's field notebook**](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/blob/main/examples/adventure-4/pub/stage/palm-nook/study/palm-on-worms-fieldnotes.md)
  is the invitation to read it over Palm's shoulder.
- **AI in general** — wherever the book is pulling him; Don as sounding board / case study.
- **"Ten Years," revisited** — deliberate practice in the LLM era; are *skills* the thing we
  practice with?
- **I've Consed Every Pair** — optional cold open; Guy Steele energy.
- **Society of Mind in modern AI** — with [Henry Minsky](../../characters/henry-minsky/README.md) and other
  [Leela](../../characters/steve-kommrusch/README.md) colleagues.
