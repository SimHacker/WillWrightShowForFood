## The thread

Will said it on a Stanford stage in 1996: characters, homes, cities, objects, ideas, and *memories* moving between games and universes. Nobody shipped it. Every bridge in this repo — SimSlice, Stardew hooks, endosymbiosis, Player-in-the-Middle — is one cut of the same jewel.

**Precedent:** Steve Alvey **SimSlice** — SimCity inside The Sims (shipped deep bridge).
