# Microsoft Research at SIGGRAPH '96 (press, 6 Aug 1996)

**URL:** https://news.microsoft.com/source/1996/08/06/microsoft-research-displays-latest-innovations-at-siggraph-96/
**Place:** New Orleans · **Angle:** MSR five years old; 10 of 52 accepted papers from Microsoft authors

## Papers listed (including Comic Chat)

- The Lumigraph
- The Virtual Cinematographer *(3-D chat room camera direction)*
- Blue Screen Matting
- Progressive Meshes
- **Comic Chat**
- Automatic Reconstruction of B-Spline Surfaces…
- Talisman: Commodity Real-Time 3-D Graphics for the PC
- Hierarchical Image Caching…
- Consequences of Stratified Sampling in Graphics
- Efficient Generation of Motion Transitions…

Plus Electronic Theater short **"Chicken Crossing"** (Andrew Glassner) demoing Talisman.

## Show hooks

1. Comic Chat wasn't a lonely novelty — it sat in a 1996 MSR graphics push about *how conversation
   and space look*
2. **Virtual Cinematographer** as sibling: automatic camera for chat rooms ↔ Comic Chat's automatic
   panel direction
3. Same conference week as productization path into IE3 / Windows 98
