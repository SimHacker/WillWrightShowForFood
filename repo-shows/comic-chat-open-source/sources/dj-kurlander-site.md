## Creator frame (from his Comic Chat page)

- Proposed and coded initial Comic Chat at MSR (1995); Virtual Worlds Group helped first ship;
  then moved to Internet Division for subsequent versions.
- SIGGRAPH '96 with Tim Skelly + David Salesin.
- Jim Woodring: hand-illustrated real chat transcripts first; challenge was "reverse-engineering
  Jim" into rules for poses/layout.
- Shipped with IE3, Windows 98/NT5, IE4/5; MSN official chat; 24 languages; later renamed
  Microsoft Chat.
- MSN turned off comic chat servers in 2001 — DJ: "boneheaded decision" that destroyed much of
  the community. Still works on standard IRC.
- Emotion wheel, keyword gestures (LOL, ALL CAPS, hi/bye wave, I/You pointing), balloon types,
  algorithmic "hand-drawn" balloons.
- Moral wink: characters are real — keep them extant.
