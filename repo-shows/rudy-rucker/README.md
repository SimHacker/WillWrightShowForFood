## The show

**Cellular automata with the man who wrote the manual.** Async-first: Rudy records what he's up
for, when he's up for it, and Don edits it together. Live or multi-person only if he wants that.

Pick any of these:

1. **A MOOLLM cellular-automata skill, co-designed.** The CelLab rule catalogue is now cached and
   indexed ([`cellab-celdoc/`](../../characters/rudy-rucker/sources/cellab-celdoc/README.md)):
   36 JC rules with their sources and their *Cellular Automata Machines* page references. Rudy
   narrates and classifies; the rules become runnable by name.
2. **Brainlife, Votelife and Ranch, live.** His 1987 CAM-6 rules from the 1989 *Complex Systems*
   paper ([cached](../../characters/rudy-rucker/sources/README.md)), run on Don's CAM6 and on the
   cabinet's CAM device, with Rudy telling how he found them on the board that came in styrofoam
   peanuts.
3. **A new commercial plug for cellular automata**, the sequel to CA Lab's 1-800 ending.
4. **"Why not 3D?"**: Dave Ackley's question, with Rudy's answer beside Norman Margolus's and
   Scott Draves's. CAM-8 ran 3D in 1993, so it's not hypothetical.
5. **Peer review of Palm's *Running on Wetware*** by the author of *Wetware*.
