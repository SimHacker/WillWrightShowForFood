## Logline

The million-dollar question — how do you flatten arbitrary PostScript into something portable and editable? — led to PDF. **Glenn Reid** wrote *The Distillery*; **Brian Reid** wrote the definitive 1985 *laser-lovers* history; **Don** ported and extended distillation on NeWS and ran the PostNews experiments.
