# A Repo Show with Coco Conn 🎛️

> HOMER & Assoc., Forth, Charles Moore — and the performance console behind *Atomic Dog* and *Flying
> Logos*.

**Coco Conn** (with **Peter Conn** and **Paul Rother**) ran **HOMER & Assoc.** at **Sunset Gower
Studios** (1977–1997) — a **Hybrid Optical Montage Electronically Reproduced** console they **played
live**, software by **Charles Moore** in **Forth**, feeding an **optical printer** that composited
***Atomic Dog***, ***Abracadabra***, **RoboCop**, and ***Flying Logos*** (SIGGRAPH **1989** Electronic
Theater). Don met her again at **SIGGRAPH SIGKids** — where **David Levitt**'s **Bounce** band sparked
a decades-long collaboration.

## What we'd get into

- **Charles Moore writes your animation system** — PolyForth in **32K**, **24 Z80s**, **2K EPROM** per channel.
- **Homer II as live instrument** — faders, joystick **bumps**, hero take → film → MTV.
- **Flying Logos** — demo reel as story; Don's **Flying Logo Heaven**.
- **SIGKids & CitySpace** — kids, virtual worlds, serendipity.
