You already said yes to the important part — so this is me writing it down in the open, where your
fingerprints belong. And I want to be plain about something: **you're not a nice-to-have on this
project. You're one of the reasons it exists.**

You built **SimObliterator** (and **VitaMoo**): the tools that actually read, write, analyze, and
render The Sims' saves, objects, and characters. That's not a footnote to this show — it's the
**foundation.** The whole thing turns on one genuinely hard, unglamorous problem that most people look
at and walk away from: getting a character *in and out* of a game — reading a 25-year-old save file,
understanding the objects around it, writing it back without breaking it. I've been calling that the
**soul mover**, and **it does not exist without the work you did.** You didn't skim the easy part; you
went into the file formats, the GUIDs, the objects — the deep, tedious, essential stuff — and you made
it *work.* That's rare, and it's exactly the kind of contribution that usually goes uncredited. Not
here.

Here's why it belongs at the very front. The **first show** is **Will Wright's 1996 Stanford talk** —
the one where he says, out loud, that **porting data between games is the holy grail.** Thirty years
later, **your work is how that holy grail becomes something we can actually run.** You made it
buildable.

There's a bigger frame here that I think you'll appreciate, and it's pure Will Wright. Will has always
talked about the **pyramid of players**: a huge base of casual players at the bottom, and as you climb,
a smaller and smaller number who tinker, then mod, then **build the tools** — and the people near the
top **drastically improve the game for the casual millions below them.** He's said for decades that
those fans are among the **most important people there are.** Jeff, **you climbed that whole pyramid** —
from playing The Sims, to reverse-engineering it, to *building the tools other people need* — and
landed right at the top, in the same company as **Heather (SimFreaks)** and **Steve (SimSlice)**, whose
content brands shaped the community for a quarter century. In Will's own model that's not a hobby
footnote; it's the highest, most valuable thing a fan can become. And now the tools *you* built are
what lift the next people up.

credit**. Each version feeds the next and gets *better* every pass. That's **totally Eno, totally
