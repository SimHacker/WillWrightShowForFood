# A Repo Show with Jeff Adkins — SimObliterator & the Soul Mover 🪓🧬

*The show built on the layer Jeff built. Topic-light by design — Jeff and SimObliterator are the
topic, and the first Will Wright show (the 1996 data-portability holy grail) flows straight into it.*

> *SimObliterator is how the holy grail becomes something you can actually run.*

## What this is

A **Repo Show** with **[Jeff Adkins](../../characters/jeff-adkins/README.md)** (GitHub **DnfJeff**),
creator of **SimObliterator** — the AI-assisted, open tools for reading, writing,
analyzing, and rendering **The Sims 1** saves, objects, and characters. (**VitaMoo**, the in-browser
character renderer, is **Don Hopkins'** — inspired by Jeff's SimObliterator.) SimObliterator is a big part
of the substrate under the show's **character import/export** — the **soul mover** — the thing that
turns Will Wright's 1996 *"data portability is the holy grail"* into a running demo.

## Why Jeff

- **Foundational.** IFF/FAR parsing, GUID/object analysis, catalog inventory — the unglamorous
  plumbing everything else stands on.
- **The feedback loop.** Jeff built SimObliterator **with AI** by studying Don's documented, published
  code and open-source Sims-family reimplementations; Don is now reimplementing it in WWSFF TypeScript,
  **with AI and full credit**. Each iteration feeds the other and improves. **AI copying AI copying
  people** — stone soup, cauldron, cellular automata, Wolfram, Eno — made literal in a codebase.
- **Full credit, unconditionally.** Jeff is credited for SimObliterator's influence and code whether
  or not he ever records a thing.

## How it runs (low-pressure, time-shifted)

Record a **solo video** whenever Jeff likes, and/or a relaxed **one-on-one** with Don, **edited
together** into a longer show. No cross-time-zone live juggling. If Jeff wants to get into the weeds
(he does), there's plenty of weeds.

## The bigger build

A GitHub-native home for decades of **user-created Sims content** — communities like **SimFreaks,
SimSlice, Zombie Sims**, and the **Simprov Wedding Playset** — cataloged, reviewed, analyzed, patched,
and upgraded in the open: **file an issue on a Sims object**, discuss what's wrong, fix it together.
(See [`apps/soul-angel/windows/`](../../apps/soul-angel/windows/README.md) and the
[Sims community landscape](../../catalogs/sims1-community-landscape.yml).)
