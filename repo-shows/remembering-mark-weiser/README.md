## Topic

A tribute to Mark Weiser (1952–1999) — ubiquitous computing, calm technology, PARC, CHI'88 pie menus, Severe Tire Damage — and the PKD/Ubik thread that named the field.

## Memorial mode

**Subject:** Mark Weiser

We represent and discuss Mark's documented work only. We do NOT interview him or speak as him, and invent no inner life or quotes. Any living person on a produced episode requires their own consent — the list below is NOT an invitation.

## Frame

**Read the Intent.** Resist the ubicomp laundry list. Center what Mark was reaching for: computing that recedes into the background — a "quiet, invisible servant" — the opposite of technology that demands your attention. What world was he trying to build, and did we build it or its dystopian twin?

**Pkd Door.** Mark named ubiquitous computing in reference to PKD's Ubik, and inverted its metered, hostile everyware into "calm technology." That naming story is the natural bridge into the sci-fi→AI reading group. See characters/mark-weiser/pkd-lem-ai-sf.md.
