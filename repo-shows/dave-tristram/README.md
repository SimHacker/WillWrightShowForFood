# A Repo Show with David Tristram 🎸

> Live generative graphics — Electropaint, Raster Masters, and CA-inspired performance for the
> browser era.

**David A. Tristram** wrote **Electropaint** (NASA Ames → SGI demo legend) and co-founded **Raster
Masters**, the 1990s **visual-music ensemble** that performed **MIDI-reactive psychedelic graphics**
live behind the **Grateful Dead**, **Herbie Hancock**, and **Graham Nash**. Don met Dave at **USENIX
Monterey 1989** — **Panel Library** beside **PSIBER Space Deck** — then watched Raster Masters haul
**SGI Onyx** rigs like guitars (*Wired*, 1994–1995). His generative work draws on **reaction-diffusion**, **video
feedback**, and **cellular automata** — publicly citing Toffoli & **Norman Margolus**'s *Cellular
Automata Machines*, the same ground Don's **CAM6** stands on.

## What we'd get into

- **Electropaint → WebGPU** — simple geometry, alive motion; forkable live instrument.
- **Raster Masters energy on stream** — MIDI in, generative layer out; the audience clones the repo.
- **CA + feedback jam** with Don's **CAM6** — rules you perform, not just simulate.
- **CA looping fest** weave with [Norman Margolus](../../characters/norman-margolus/README.md) +
  dream co-guests [Jim Crutchfield](../../characters/jim-crutchfield/README.md),
  [Scott Draves](../../characters/scott-draves/README.md), [Brian Eno](../../characters/brian-eno/README.md).
