# Repo Show — John & Brenda Romero 🎮

*Show seed. Will pointed here 2026-07-09 — next gen after current recording.*

**Guests:** [John & Brenda Romero](../../characters/john-romero/README.md) — Romero Games, Galway  
**Host:** Don Hopkins · **Dream co-guest:** [Will Wright](../../characters/will-wright/README.md)

## The pitch

BBC just aired [*The Romeros: Developing digital games*](https://www.bbc.com/audio/play/p0n7xpn6)
(Mar 2026) — 27 minutes inside the Galway studio while John and Brenda ship another ambitious
large-team game. Will's note: **talk to Romero** — game-industry interview from Ireland, not
Silicon Valley nostalgia.

Repo Show angle: **reinvention at scale** — what drives legends to keep building after DOOM,
*Train*, *Empire of Sin*? Pair with Will on possibility space, player worlds, and why the
industry still needs **craft in a shifting landscape**.

## Host hook — exit culture (Don, 2026-07-10)

BBC doc: Romeros left Silicon Valley **exit culture** for Galway — "weird and arty."

**Don's parallel:** left Silicon Valley for **Amsterdam** too. Same allergy to exit culture;
loves it here. The hippies aren't running Silicon Valley any more like when Don started out.
Amsterdam has its own take on **tech culture, gaming, and lifestyle** — not a copy of Bay Area
or Galway, but the same *why leave?* question.

Show beat: three exits, three cultures — SV → Galway (Romeros), SV → Amsterdam (Don), Will's
reinvention arc. What survives when you leave the default map?

Don says friendships. Amsterdam is the hub of Europe, so I can just hang out here, and lots of my old friends pass through and stop by on their way though Europe for conferences or vacations, from many different places and lifetimes ago, from different parts of the US I lived in and know people from, East and West coast and other places. In a lot of ways Amsterdam is like the San Francisco (Haight Ashbury specifically) of Europe, a cultural and technology hub.
