# A Repo Show with the Leela AI Crew 👁️🧠

> Visual intelligence, *running* — a friendly show-and-tell by the people who build it.

**Leela AI** builds **visual intelligence** that reads what's happening in video — carrying ideas
from the MIT AI Lab (Society of Mind, schema learning) into modern, explainable AI for the real
world. This is the crew's open show-and-tell: bring the system up live, see how it learns, and meet
the people behind it.

## The crew

- **[Henry Minsky](../../characters/henry-minsky/README.md)** — co-founder / CTO; Society of Mind in modern AI, OpenLaszlo lineage.
- **[Milan Singh Minsky](../../characters/milan-singh-minsky/README.md)** — VP Product; turning research into a real product.
- **[Cyrus Shaoul](../../characters/cyrus-shaoul/README.md)** — co-founder; computational models of learning.
- **[Steve Kommrusch](../../characters/steve-kommrusch/README.md)** — Sr AI Scientist; program repair + verifiable ML.
- **[Isabella Struckman](../../characters/isabella-struckman/README.md)** — vision-model evaluation/training; AI policy & explainability.
- **[Art Medlar](../../characters/art-medlar/README.md)** — systems builder; WAIS, early Internet Archive, Connection Machines, PARC.

## Ways to do it

- **Group** — the whole crew, a tour of Leela's visual intelligence and the ideas behind it.
- **Couple** — Milan + Henry Minsky together, product & architecture as a partnership.
- **Solo** — individual episodes; each person on what they're passionate about.
- **Leela chat** — a relaxed round-table with Steve, Isabella, Art, and Cyrus (plus Henry & Milan).

## What we'd get into

- **Visual intelligence, live** — reading activity in video, honestly and explainably.
- **Society of Mind, running** — many small agents / schema learning in a shipping product.
- **How it learns** — self-supervised grid-world learning (the 2020 Leela paper) and beyond.
- **Builder's war stories** — WAIS + the early Internet Archive (Art), verifiable ML (Steve),
  vision-model eval + AI policy (Isabella).
