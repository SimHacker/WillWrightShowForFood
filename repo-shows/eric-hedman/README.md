# A Repo Show with Eric Hedman ("Irk") 🕺

> The hand-tuned Sims — walk cycles, idles, the mechanical bull, and a monkey face on the full ARKit
> morph-target set.

**Eric Hedman** was a **character animator on the original The Sims** — the walk cycles, the idles,
the gestures, hand-tuned for the vitaboy pipeline — and then a **design lead on the expansion packs**
(House Party, Livin' Large, Hot Date, Unleashed). Later **Pantomime Corporation** with
[David Levitt](../../characters/david-levitt/README.md) and Don, then **Disney, Jim Henson,
Nickelodeon**. Today he is an **independent creator**, and still shipping.

## What we'd get into

- **Vitaboy animation** — walk cycles, idles and gestures, and **broad pantomime as an engineering
  constraint**: you lost half your frames to unknown CPU speed, so every motion had to read big.
  Plus the **canWiggle** legend (Don checked in the debug; they're still great buddies).
- **The object-prototyping pipeline** — simplified Max stand-in with all its states, handed off
  artist → animator → programmer, iterated same-day in the sandbox. **The template that made the
  expansion packs possible.**
- **Expansion design** — the mechanical bull ("my baby"), the love bed, the hot tub. Big "magic"
  party objects as state machines that have to read on a CRT, informed by his years as a nightclub
  bouncer at raves.
- **The [Drew Carey Show](../drew-carey/README.md) tie-in** — Sims vignettes and a laser blaster, and
  nearly dancing Marion Ross into the floor at a TV-moms ball.
- ★ **Doppel and the face rig** — a monkey face on the **full ARKit morph-target set**, performed live
  with the applied weights on screen. This is the practitioner's half of the
  [ECG / Mouther conversation](../tom-and-golan-ecg-mouther/) with Tom Ngo and Golan Levin, and it is
  the demo that [Faceball Construction Set](../../designs/faceball-construction-set/README.md#what-it-looks-like-in-the-hands-of-a-great-artist)
  is designed around.
- **Pantomime reunion** — Eric + David Levitt + Don, Sims craft meeting AR/VR character work.
