## Frame

**Bret Victor's question** ([2013 tribute](https://worrydream.com/Engelbart/)): *"The least important question you can ask about Engelbart is, 'What did he build?' … The most important question you can ask is, 'What world was he trying to create?'"*

This show asks the important question — together.

**Not a feature list.** Resist the mouse/hypertext/video-conferencing laundry list. Center the intent: collective intelligence, bootstrapping, knowledge workers thinking in powerful new ways to solve urgent global problems.

**Ties to this project.** Engelbart's augment-don't-replace thesis **is** the show's Player-in-the-Middle principle — see [`memorial.md`](../../characters/douglas-engelbart/memorial.md) and [augmentation trail](../../process/trails/augmentation-and-hypertext.md).
