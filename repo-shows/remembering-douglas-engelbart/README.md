## Topic

A tribute to Doug Engelbart (1925-2013) — memories, eulogies, and above all his INTENT: augmenting collective human intellect, not a laundry list of inventions.

## Memorial mode

**Subject:** Douglas Engelbart

We represent and discuss Doug's documented work and invite those shaped by him to share memories. We do NOT interview him or speak as him.

## Frame

**Bret Victors Question.** Bret Victor's 2013 tribute reframes the whole show: "The least important question you can ask about Engelbart is, 'What did he build?' ... The most important question you can ask is, 'What world was he trying to create?'" This show asks the important question — together.

**Not a Feature List.** Resist the mouse/hypertext/video-conferencing laundry list. Center the intent: collective intelligence, bootstrapping, knowledge workers thinking in powerful new ways to solve urgent global problems.

**Ties to This Project.** Engelbart's augment-don't-replace thesis IS the show's Player-in-the-Middle principle (see memorial.md).


## Guests

- **Ted Nelson** — [ted-nelson](characters/ted-nelson) — Engelbart officiated Ted's wedding; Ted delivered the eulogy at the Computer History Museum, 2013-12-09 — the writing he calls his best. 'I feel like I've lost my best planet.'
- **Alan Kay** — [alan-kay](characters/alan-kay) — Shaped by the 1968 'Mother of All Demos'; the PARC lineage that carried Engelbart's vision forward. Recalled the demo's impact.
- **Yoot Saito** — [yoot-saito](characters/yoot-saito) — PERSONALLY INTERVIEWED Doug Engelbart (and Alan Kay) in Japan in the early 1990s, when Engelbart and Kay were visiting together for a Japanese computer-magazine special issue. Not adjacent to this history — a primary interviewer of it. His own Engelbart interview is a centerpiece.
- **David Maynard** — [dave-maynard](characters/dave-maynard) — Internet Old Farts Club (2022): fifty-year-old Engelbart mouse photo; donated chord keyset to Stanford Hohbach collection; alerted Don to $54k auction; attended Ted Nelson's Engelbart tribute.
- **David H. Crocker** — [dave-crocker](characters/dave-crocker) — Personal NLS learner via brother Steve at ARPA — text interface first, invited to Menlo Park mouse/keypad class. Witness to ARC collaborative tone ('Come on in, the water's fine'). Washington Hilton Fall 1972 — Doug across the ballroom on text chat. Emulated NLS paragraph model in PL/1 for a student crisis-hotline Counselor's Manual. RFC 733/822 author; NLS-mail → Internet-mail lineage. Facebook Jul 2026 thread with Don rebutting the viral $10k Engelbart piece + Ted Nelson tribute room.
- **Bret Victor** — [bret-victor](characters/bret-victor) — Wrote 'A few words on Doug Engelbart' (2013) — the definitive plea to read Engelbart's INTENT, not his feature list. The conscience of this tribute's framing.
