# Producer clips

Submit a **clip** via PR — a sourced artifact the hosts may read, demo, or link.

**Where clips live (pick one):**

| You are | Path |
|---------|------|
| **Audience Producer** (TicketPR) | `repo-shows/<show>/audience/<your-github-username>/clip.yml` |
| **Character room** (guest or recurring persona) | `characters/<slug>/sources/` or `media/` — same receipt fields |

**No** `show-notes/clips/` tree. Clips colocate with **your** Producer identity — audience dir for this episode, or your character dir if you have one. See [placement constitution](../../../process/repo-show-format.yml#placement_constitution).

Copy [`clip.yml`](clip.yml). Required: `source_url`, `context`, `why_it_matters`, `license_or_fair_use_note`.

Spec: [`producers-and-show-notes.yml`](../../../process/producers-and-show-notes.yml)
