# Timothy Leary's Mind Mirror

Electronic Arts, 1985 on the Atari 8-bit, 1986 on the Apple II. Made by Futique, Timothy Leary's
studio, and designed by Marc Dawson. You rate a person, yourself or anyone, on a set of qualities,
and the program draws their *Mind-Map*. Then you take that personality into a Life Simulation and
play it through situations, two thousand of them, answered by multiple choice, to see how
consistently you can be who you said you were.

It is Leary the psychologist, not Leary the LSD advocate, though both turn up. The model comes
from his 1957 book *The Interpersonal Diagnosis of Personality*: the
[interpersonal circumplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpersonal_circumplex), a circle of
traits where each one sits opposite its complement. Mind Mirror runs four of those circles, one
for each plane of thought: **Bio-Energy**, **Emotional Insight**, **Mental Abilities**, and
**Social Interaction**. Each describes a person twice, in *Plain Talk* and in *Shrink-Rap*: the
same scale reads "peppy" in one and "hyper-manic" in the other.

## Starting

1. **Press Return** past the disclaimer: the statements attributed to real people are satire.
2. **Joystick?** Press **N** to use the keyboard.
3. **Choose a play level.** Beginner has exercises in thought modification and Mind-Maps;
   Intermediate suggests topics and coaches you; Master leaves you on your own, in Auto-Play or
   Inter-Play; Professional Consulting is for psychologists, personnel managers and teachers.
   Move with the up and down arrows, or **I** and **M**, or the space bar, and press Return.

Click the screen first so the keys go to the Apple.

## Life Simulations

When a simulation is about to begin, the game says *Insert Simulation Disk A in drive 1 and
Simulation Disk B in drive 2, then press RETURN to proceed*. Press **Insert the Simulation Disks**
under the screen: it puts both disks in and presses Return for you. If it asks for the program
disk again, press **Put the Program Disk Back**.

## Keeping a person

The game saves a person you have mapped onto Simulation Disk B, as a file named for them.
**Save disk** under the screen downloads the disk in the selected drive, with your people on it;
**Insert file…** puts it back.
