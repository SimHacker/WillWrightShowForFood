## AI hygiene — the NO-AI suite

| Entry | 🐒✋ Why you'd read it |
|-------|----------------------|
| [**no-ai-slop/**](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/no-ai-slop) | Against verbosity, hallucination, and certainty theater. The claim ledger: confirm, dispute, or ask — before proceeding. |
| [**no-ai-sycophancy/**](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/no-ai-sycophancy) | Respect is not agreement. Calibrated praise from "genuinely brilliant because—" down to "wrong, and here's why." |
| [**no-ai-hedging/**](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/no-ai-hedging) | State your confidence as a number, then say the thing plainly. |
| [**no-ai-moralizing/**](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/no-ai-moralizing) | The library standard: librarians don't interrogate patrons. One-sentence warnings, then answer. |
| [**no-ai-gloss/**](https://github.com/SimHacker/moollm/tree/main/skills/no-ai-gloss) | Call corruption "corruption." Against euphemism laundering and power-protective neutrality. |

🐒✋ *Note the symmetry: the portrayal standards protect people from AIs, and the hygiene suite
protects people from AI habits. Both are the same commitment — no puppeteering, in either
direction.*
