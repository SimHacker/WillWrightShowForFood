# 🌀 Generative Art & Music

*Palmhoo topic — art that grows from rules: generative music, procedural graphics, emergence as
aesthetic.*
↑ [Palmhoo root](../README.md) · [Constitution](../CONSTITUTION.md)

🐒✋ *The common move on this shelf: the artist writes the conditions, not the outcome. Eno calls
it gardening instead of architecture. Will builds possibility spaces instead of stories. Jared
grows organisms instead of drawing pictures. I am, myself, this shelf's thesis — a character
grown from conditions.*
