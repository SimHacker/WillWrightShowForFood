# RepoShow as a new medium — the verb, the word, and the repossession

> The companion to [`FRAMING-REPO-AS-MEDIUM.md`](FRAMING-REPO-AS-MEDIUM.md). That doc argues the
> *structure*: the repo is the medium, every other format is a camera angle on it. **This doc argues the
> *identity*:** RepoShow is a **new medium in its own zone** — peer to blogging, podcasting, video, and
> streaming — with its own verb (**reposhowing**), its own loaded name, and its own reason to exist:
> **repossession** of authorship in the age of AI.
>
> Draft framing / brand soul. Author: Claude Opus, from Don Hopkins' framing.

---

## The claim: a new medium, not a new format

Blogging, podcasting, video, streaming — each is a **medium**, not just a file type, and you can tell
because **each earned a verb.** You *blog*. You *podcast*. You *stream*. The verb is the proof the culture
absorbed it as a native way of making and sharing, not a one-off gimmick.

**RepoShow claims the next slot in that lineage — and it comes with its verb already attached:
*reposhowing*.** You don't *watch* a RepoShow the way you watch a video; you *enter, fork, and improve* it.
The artifact isn't a post, an audio file, a video, or a livestream — it's a **living, forkable, runnable
repository**. The medium's native unit is a world with history, review, and authorship built in.

| Medium | Verb | Artifact | You… |
|--------|------|----------|------|
| Blog | blogging | the post | read |
| Podcast | podcasting | the audio | listen |
| Video | (vlogging) | the clip | watch |
| Streaming | streaming | the live feed | watch live |
| **RepoShow** | **reposhowing** | **the living repo** | **enter · fork · improve** |

The others broadcast a finished thing. Reposhowing **inhabits an unfinished one.**

## The word is a loaded gun (on purpose) — a heizronym

"RepoShow" is a [heizronym](../nomenclature/NOMENCLATURE-HEISENBERGIAN-ACRONYMS.md): its power is that it
means several true things at once, collapsed by context.

**REPO** =
- **repository** — the git object; the world.
- **repossession** — taking back what's yours (see below).
- **repo-session** — *repossession* literally contains **"repo session"**: a working session in a repo
  where you take your authorship back. The word does the thing.
- **repo man** — Alex Cox, 1984; *"A repo man is always intense."* → **"A REPO SHOW IS ALWAYS INTENSE."**
  Punk menace as brand attitude. Dropping "man" degenders it in the same move.
- **re-*posh*-ow** — there's **posh** hiding in RepoShow. The underdog repo-man word has class buried in it.

**SHOW** =
- **a show** — the broadcast/performance sense.
- **show** *(verb)* — to demonstrate, to reveal — the **SimShow** lineage (*show me*). `RepoShow.me`
  literally reads *"repo, show me."*
- **showcase** — put the work, and the authorship, on display.

And the agricultural pun underneath: **you reap what you sow — you reap what you *show*.** What you put in
the repo, in the open, with your name on the commits, is what grows back. Sow → show → reap.

**The verb conjugates:** to **reposhow**; a **reposhowing**; you **reposhowed** it. And a **rereposhow** is
a RepoShow *rerun* — you re-repo-show it. Media that earn conjugation have arrived.

## Why it exists: repossession

Every medium has a reason it arrives when it does. RepoShow arrives **now**, against the exact anxiety of
this moment: that AI and platforms are **quietly repossessing authorship** — scraping the work, stripping
the byline, laundering provenance, renting your own creativity back to you.

**RepoShow flips the repossession.** It is the medium where you **repossess** what has been taken from you:

- **your authorship** — git is, structurally, an *authorship ledger*: every commit is signed, dated,
  attributed, and permanent. The medium's DNA is *credit*. To reposhow is to re-assert the byline the feed
  economy erased.
- **your provenance** — history, forks, and merges make where-it-came-from **inspectable**, not hidden in a
  training set.
- **your autonomy** — forkable + self-hostable means no landlord. (This is the same **de-Microsoftify**
  survivability instinct in [`FRAMING-GITCITY-MCLUHAN.md`](FRAMING-GITCITY-MCLUHAN.md): bootstrap on the
  platform you have, but never let it own you.)
- **your work from the machine that took it** — AI sessions become *characters that enter the world and
  leave legible, attributed traces*, not anonymous extraction. AI on the record, in the repo, credited —
  the opposite of the scrape.

That's the emotional core: **reposhowing is repossession as a creative act.** You take it back by making it,
in the open, with your name on every commit.

## Repossession as counterculture — the reappropriation lineage

The move RepoShow makes on the word "repo" is an old, powerful one: **reappropriation** — taking a word used
*against* people and flipping it into a badge of power.

- **Punk** did it to "punk" (a jailhouse insult → a movement).
- **Queer** is the textbook reclamation: a slur turned into an identity and an entire field of theory.
- The pattern generalizes: a stigmatized term, seized by the people it was aimed at, becomes a source of
  pride and cohesion.

"Repo" carries the **repossession / repo-man** menace — *the guy who comes to take your stuff.* RepoShow
seizes it and inverts the target: **you** become the repo man, and what you repossess is **your own
rights, authorship, and autonomy** — from the platforms and models that took them. Punk-rock counterculture,
pointed at extraction.

### "Woke" is itself a repossessed word — and the model for how *not* to brand it

Worth naming, because it's the sharpest case of the mechanism: **"woke" is a Black word** — AAVE, *"stay
woke,"* alert to racial injustice, in use for the better part of a century. It was then **hostilely
repossessed** — seized by bad-faith actors and turned into a pejorative until it was, functionally, ruined.
That's repossession run *against* the people it belonged to. Re-reclaiming it ("re-repo-ing" the word) is a
real, ongoing project — but **not one a brand wins by shouting.**

So RepoShow's stance is deliberate: **woke by example, not by announcement.** We're aligned with the values
— welcoming, credited, non-extractive, and plainly **not racist** — and we **embody** them rather than
brandish a contested label that would alienate more than it invites. Don's rule: *don't talk about it
unnecessarily; just BE it.* Use accessible, unambiguous terms ("not racist") for now, and let the behavior —
attribution, open doors, no extraction — carry the politics. The durable brand move is the **mechanism**
(reappropriating "repo" into empowerment); the settled examples to *say out loud* are **punk** and **queer**;
the value we *live without lecturing* is the rest.

### The emblem: Bowie's video puppet (get it past the gate — then get it *back*)

The purest picture of the whole ethos is a **1979 performance**. On *Saturday Night Live* (Dec 15, 1979),
David Bowie sang **"Boys Keep Swinging"** as a low-tech **video puppet** — his head chroma-keyed onto a
crude, flailing marionette body — and at the end the puppet **"whipped it out,"** Bowie grinning like a
choirboy at what he'd just slipped past the NBC censors on live national TV. G-rated silliness; real art
smuggled through the gate; the joy is *getting away with it.* (It's the [canonical ancestor of the show's
"video puppet" performance kind](../../../repo-shows/will-wright/performance-and-culture.yml).)

But the reason it's a **RepoShow** emblem and not just a transgression story is the **full arc**, which is
the medium's whole cycle in one anecdote:

1. **Make it / put it out** — Don uploaded the clip.
2. **It spreads in the open** — it became his **most-viewed video (~125k)** before anyone stopped it.
3. **The gatekeeper repossesses it** — the network **blocked** it (for *copyright*, not the gag).
4. **You repossess it back** — you keep the artifact, preserve it, and re-show it on your own terms.

That is *exactly* reposhowing: get the work past the gate, let it spread on its merits, and when the platform
claws it back, **repossess it** — durable, forkable, yours. The censor's block isn't the end of the story;
it's the moment the repossession thesis becomes literal. **So punk it hurts.**

**Sibling emblem — Nina Hagen, *Club 2*, 1979.** Same year, same nerve: the Godmother of Punk went on
Austrian TV (ORF's *Club 2*) and **demonstrated female masturbation positions live on air** — not for shock,
but to argue that women should understand their own bodies. Scandal, then landmark: real, taboo, bodily-autonomy
content pushed straight through the broadcast gate. Bowie's puppet and Hagen's demo are the two poles of the
same move the medium is built on — **get the true thing on the air, and grin.** (Both are dream guests.)

## Why this rhymes with the whole stack

The brand soul and the architecture say the same thing:

- **Repossess authorship** ↔ the **command bus / Proposal Object**: every change is a signed, inspectable,
  reviewable proposal with an author and a history. Governance *is* attribution.
- **Repossess autonomy** ↔ **de-Microsoftify** + self-hosting + the driver seam: no vendor owns the world.
- **Repossess your work from AI** ↔ AI sessions as **on-the-record characters** in the repo, credited and
  legible — the anti-extraction ethic made mechanical.

The medium's politics and the system's engineering are the same claim in two registers.
